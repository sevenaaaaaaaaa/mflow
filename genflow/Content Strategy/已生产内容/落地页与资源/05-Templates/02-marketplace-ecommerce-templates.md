## Pro Tips for Amazon Listing Success

1. **Image Order Matters** — Always lead with your strongest lifestyle image (not a white-background product shot). Amazon's algorithm considers image engagement in ranking.

2. **Mobile First** — Over 60% of Amazon browsing happens on mobile. Test every infographic at 200px wide — if the text is unreadable, simplify.

3. **A/B Test Modules** — Use Amazon's Manage Your Experiments tool. Lovart templates are designed with module-level variability for easy testing.

4. **Refresh Quarterly** — Listings that update A+ Content every 90 days see a sustained 4-6% conversion lift from freshness signals.

5. **Competitor Audit** — Before customizing any template, review the top 5 competitors in your category. Note what they do well and where they fall short — then fill those gaps.

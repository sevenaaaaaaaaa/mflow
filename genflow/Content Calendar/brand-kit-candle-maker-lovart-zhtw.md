## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How do I communicate scent visually?**
Through color, texture, and ingredient illustration. Cedar = deep green + wood grain texture. Vanilla = warm cream + smooth surface. Lavender = soft purple + botanical line art. Lovart's scent palette system maps fragrance notes to visual elements.

**Q2: What label information is legally required?**
Net weight, ingredients, manufacturer information, and safety warnings. In the US, candles fall under CPSC (Consumer Product Safety Commission) labeling requirements. Lovart's label templates include all required fields pre-positioned.

**Q3: How many collections should I launch with?**
Three to four core scents that represent your brand's olfactory identity, plus seasonal limited editions. Too many scents at launch confuses customers. Lovart's collection template scales from 3 to 20 plus scents.

**Q4: What vessel should I choose for my signature candle?**
The vessel IS your brand. Choose one signature vessel shape and use it consistently. The matte black jar trend is saturated — consider amber glass, textured ceramic, or concrete vessels. Lovart's vessel mockup templates let you preview branding on different container types.

**Q5: How do I brand the unboxing experience?**
Tissue paper with your pattern, a scent-sample card (scratch-and-sniff style), a candle care card, and a personalized thank-you note. The unboxing is what gets shared on social media — design it to be photographed. Lovart generates unboxing kit templates.

**Q6: How do I make my candle brand giftable?**
Design gift-ready packaging — no price tags, beautiful box ready to wrap, gift message card included. Create a gift finder flowchart (For the Host, For Relaxation, For the Minimalist). Lovart's gift packaging templates increase average order value through gifting.

## How AI Color Palette Generator Works

### Step 1: Describe your desired mood

Tell AI about your industry, audience, and emotional response: playful children's app vs premium spa vs innovative tech. Be specific about the feeling.

### Step 2: Review AI-generated palettes

AI generates multiple options with hex codes, usage recommendations, and rationale explaining why these colors work for your context.

### Step 3: Validate accessibility

Auto-check WCAG contrast ratios. Green = passes AA. Yellow = large text only. Red = insufficient. Adjust until critical combinations pass.

### Step 4: Save to Brand Kit

Save palette directly to Brand Kit. Becomes default for all Lovart designs — auto-applied to every image, post, mockup, presentation.

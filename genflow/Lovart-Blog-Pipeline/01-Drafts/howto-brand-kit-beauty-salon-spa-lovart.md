# Brand Kit for Beauty Salons & Spas: Elegant Continuity Without Cliché Glam

Salons lose brand trust when every stylist posts a different universe: marble fantasy one day, neon chrome the next, three versions of the same logo by Friday. A Brand Kit is how the room’s real materials, lighting, and service lines become a reusable system—booking page, menus, stories, ads—in Lovart, with a cold handoff a front-desk lead can run.

## Who this is for

Salon/spa owners, beauty marketers, freelancers embedding in clinics, multi-location beauty groups.

## What the kit must encode

Logo lockups · color tokens from the actual interior · type roles (menu vs story vs ads) · photography rules (skin honesty, light) · before/after policy · forbids (fake awards, melted faces, stock marble spam, competitor rhyme) · channel crops · seasonal accents with end dates

## Anti-cliché forbids

Automatic gold foil everything · random “luxury marble” not in the room · poreless plastic skin · invented certification seals · identical soft-pink wellness paste for a bold color bar

## Capture truth

Shoot the reception, chairs, product shelf, and real service finishes. AI should extend continuity, not invent a hotel lobby you do not own.

## Before/after ethics

Get consent. Keep lighting honest. Do not invent results. When using AI cleanup, disclose per your policy. Identity Lock on client faces only with permission and careful edits.

## ChatCanvas weekly rhythm

Load Brand Kit → list promotions → service-line accents → social set with four-take stops → soft-proof on phones → archive autopsies → front desk continues from laminate.

## Flop diary

### Flop: marble cosplay
Kit ignored the plywood-and-plants room. Guests felt bait-and-switch. Fix: sample real interiors.

### Flop: face melt promos
Beauty plastic on models. Comments roasted. Fix: Touch Edit restraint + skin honesty forbids.

### Flop: logo playground
Each stylist regenerated the wordmark. Fix: logo is locked; accents only.

## FAQ

### Is a Brand Kit just a logo zip?
No. It is reusable rules plus working boards: color, type, photo recipe, forbids, channel templates, and cold handoff.

### Do beauty salons need different kits for spa vs color bar?
Often one master kit with service-line accents and end dates beats two drifting brands.

### What size should Pinterest pins start from?
Design for the current recommended pin ratio your channel uses, then soft-proof on mobile. Empty type bands help when overlays change.

### Can AI invent my brand kit in one prompt?
It can explore. You still lock truth from the room/product and write forbids. Exploration is labeled until promoted.

### Where do I start in Lovart?
Brand Kit → MCoT list → ChatCanvas → soft-proof → Touch Edit → lock winners → VA handoff test.

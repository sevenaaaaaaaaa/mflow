# Getting Started with Lovart: Your AI Design Journey (2026)

## What Is Lovart? (30 Seconds)

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Lovart is an AI Design Agent. Not an image generator. Not a design template tool. An agent — meaning it thinks about your design problem across multiple dimensions, maintains context throughout your session, and executes with precision.

**The core idea**: Instead of learning complex design software, you describe what you want in plain English. Lovart handles the execution. Need a logo? Describe it. Need a social media campaign? Describe it. Need to turn a photo into a video? Describe it.

**What makes Lovart different from other AI tools**:
- **ChatCanvas**: An infinite workspace where every generation lives on a persistent canvas. No more "generate, download, forget" — everything stays where you put it.
- **Touch Edit**: Tap anything to change it. Tap the text → retype it. Tap a color → recolor it. Tap an element → regenerate just that element. Direct manipulation, not prompt tweaking.
- **Brand Kit**: Set your logo, colors, and fonts once. Every subsequent design automatically uses them.
- **MCoT (Multi-chain of Thought)**: Lovart's reasoning engine processes your prompt across multiple dimensions simultaneously — composition, color theory, typography, brand consistency, technical specs. You get a design, not a roll of the dice.
- **@commands**: Director-level control. `@cinematic` for video direction. `@brandguide` for style guides. `@colorpalette` for palettes. `@shorts` for short-form video. Command the AI like a creative director.
- **9+ design models**: Access the best AI models (Nano Banana Pro, Recraft V3, FLUX, and more) plus 6+ video models (Seedance 2.0, Kling 1.6, Veo 3). Different models for different tasks — you're never locked into one engine.

## Create Your First Design (3 Steps)

Let's make something right now. You'll have a finished design in under 2 minutes.

### Step 1: Open the ChatCanvas

When you log in, you're on the ChatCanvas. It's literally a canvas — an infinite, zoomable workspace. Type in the prompt bar at the bottom, or use the chat interface on the side.

For your first design, type this prompt:

```
A modern podcast cover art design, "The Creative Spark" 
title in bold typography, warm gradient background amber 
to deep purple, abstract geometric shapes, podcast artwork 
square format 3000x3000px
```

Hit enter. In about 10-15 seconds, your design appears on the canvas.

### Step 2: Use Touch Edit to Make It Yours

Tap any element on your design:
- **Tap the title text** → A text editor opens. Change it to your actual podcast name.
- **Tap a background element** → Options to recolor, resize, delete, or regenerate just that element.
- **Tap the background** → Change the gradient, add a photo, or replace with a different background style.
- **Tap an empty area** → Add new elements — text, shapes, icons, images.

Touch Edit is the feature that makes Lovart feel like design software rather than a slot machine. You're not hoping the AI gets it right. You're iterating directly.

### Step 3: Export

When you're happy, click Export. Choose your format:
- **PNG** (transparent background supported) — for web and social
- **JPG** — smaller file size, good for photos
- **SVG** — vector format, infinitely scalable (for logos)
- **PDF** — print-ready documents
- **MP4** — for video exports

Your design is ready. You just made something.

## Essential Features: Your Daily Toolkit

### ChatCanvas
The ChatCanvas is where everything happens. Key things to know:
- It's **infinite** — zoom out, pan around, keep everything visible
- It's **persistent** — your work stays even when you close the browser
- It supports **multiple canvases** — one for logo exploration, one for social media, one for a client project
- **Drag and drop** images, files, inspiration directly onto the canvas
- **Right-click** any element for contextual actions

### Touch Edit
The "I want to change this specific thing" feature:
- **Tap to select** any element on your design
- **Text editing**: Change font, size, color, alignment, letter-spacing
- **Element editing**: Resize, rotate, recolor, opacity, layer order
- **Spot regeneration**: Select an element, type a new prompt, regenerate just that part while keeping everything else
- **Background editing**: Change the background without touching foreground elements

### Brand Kit
The "never manually set brand colors again" feature:
- **Setup** (3 min): Upload logo, pick colors (or extract from reference), select fonts
- **Auto-apply**: Every new design uses your brand elements automatically
- **Multiple kits**: Agencies can manage dozens of client brands, switch with one click
- **Consistency guardrails**: The Kit prevents off-brand color and font usage
- **Accessibility checking**: Auto-tests color contrast ratios against WCAG standards

### MCoT (Multi-chain of Thought)
This is what happens under the hood when you prompt Lovart. MCoT reasons about your request across:
- **Composition**: Where should elements be placed? What's the visual hierarchy?
- **Color**: What palette fits the described mood and brand?
- **Typography**: What typefaces and treatments match the brief?
- **Style**: What visual style and aesthetic era does this call for?
- **Technical**: What resolution, format, aspect ratio? Print or digital?
- **Brand**: Does this align with the active Brand Kit?

You don't see this happening. You see the result — a design that considered all these factors instead of just putting pixels on a canvas.

## The 7-Day Lovart Challenge

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

Here's a one-week path to go from new user to confident creator:

**Day 1 — First Generation**
- Create your first design (follow the 3 steps above)
- Explore the ChatCanvas: zoom, pan, resize elements
- Use Touch Edit to customize your first design
- Export it and share it somewhere

**Day 2 — Set Up Your Brand**
- Create your Brand Kit (3 minutes)
- Generate a logo (50 concepts, pick your favorite)
- Generate a color palette
- Generate a social media post using your new Brand Kit

**Day 3 — Try Video**
- Upload a photo
- Use `@parallax` to add 3D motion
- Export as MP4
- Try `@cinematic slow push-in` on a different photo

**Day 4 — Go Deep on One Project**
- Pick a real project (presentation, social campaign, product mockup, etc.)
- Use multiple @commands together
- Iterate with Touch Edit until it's genuinely done
- Export in multiple formats

**Day 5 — Templates and Batch**
- Load a template, customize it for your brand
- Try batch generation (`@batch 5 variations...`)
- Create a matching set (post + Story + banner from one concept)
- Explore auto-resize for different platforms

**Day 6 — Advanced Features**
- Try a different model for a different task (Nano Banana Pro for logos, FLUX for photos)
- Use `@brandguide` to create a mini style guide
- Try compound camera moves (`@cinematic slow orbit with gentle push-in`)

**Day 7 — Build Your System**
- Create your essential template library (social posts, presentations, documents)
- Set up a content batch for the week ahead
- Organize your canvases into a project structure
- Write down your most-used prompts as a personal reference

By day 7, you should have: your brand system set up, your essential templates created, at least one video made, and a repeatable workflow for daily design needs.

## FAQ

**Q: Do I own what I create?**
A: Free plan: personal use only. All paid plans ($19+): full commercial rights. You own everything you create. Upgrade before building commercial assets.

**Q: Is Lovart replacing my existing design tools?**
A: It depends on your workflow. Many pro designers use Lovart for exploration and first drafts → then refine in Figma or Photoshop. Non-designers often use Lovart as their sole design tool. There's no wrong way.

**Q: Can I upload my own images and files?**
A: Yes. Drag and drop any image, photo, or reference file onto the ChatCanvas. Lovart can work from your existing assets or generate entirely new ones.

**Q: How do I collaborate with a team?**
A: Team plan ($99/mo) includes shared canvases, shared Brand Kits, and collaborative editing. Multiple people can work on the same canvas simultaneously.

**Q: What if the AI doesn't produce what I want?**
A: Three things to try: (1) Be more specific in your prompt — add style references, composition instructions, or comparables. (2) Use Touch Edit to refine the result instead of regenerating from scratch. (3) Switch models — different models have different strengths.

**Q: Does Lovart work on mobile?**
A: The web app works on mobile browsers. A dedicated mobile app is in development. For now, the desktop experience is more fully featured.

**Q: Can I use Lovart offline?**
A: No. Lovart is cloud-based and requires an internet connection. All your work is auto-saved to the cloud, so you can access it from any device.

**Q: What's the difference between the design models?**
A: Nano Banana Pro excels at logos, typography, and vector-style output. Recraft V3 is best for brand systems and style-consistent variations. FLUX produces the most photorealistic results. Stable Diffusion 3 is strong for creative illustration. You can switch between them mid-project.

**Q: How does the 7-day challenge work if I'm on the Free plan?**
A: Most Day 1-3 activities work on Free. Days 4-7 involve features (Brand Kit, batch generation, commercial rights) that require a paid plan. The Free plan is great for evaluating whether Lovart fits your workflow.

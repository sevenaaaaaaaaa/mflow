**Q: Will the AI avatar actually look like me?**
A: When using reference photo upload, yes — Lovart preserves your key facial features (face shape, eye color, hair style, distinguishing characteristics) while applying professional lighting, background, and styling. When using text-only generation, the output is an original avatar that matches your description rather than a photorealistic likeness. For professional use (LinkedIn, company sites), we recommend uploading a reference photo for the most accurate result.

**Q: Can I use the avatars commercially?**
A: Yes. Every avatar, image, and design you create with Lovart belongs to you with full commercial rights. Use them on your LinkedIn, company website, speaking engagements, book covers, anywhere — no attribution required.

**Q: How many avatar variations can I generate from one reference photo?**
A: Unlimited. Upload your photo once, then generate as many variations as you want — different backgrounds, different outfits, different expressions, different lighting styles. Each variation counts as one design toward your monthly allowance. The Starter plan ($19/month) gives you 500 designs per month — more than enough for unlimited avatar experimentation.

**Q: What if the AI gets my expression wrong?**
A: Use Touch Edit. Tap on your face in the generated avatar and describe the change: "make the smile slightly wider," "softer, more relaxed expression," "more confident and direct eye contact." The AI adjusts only the expression while keeping everything else — face shape, lighting, background — exactly as it was.

**Q: Can I generate avatars for my entire team with a consistent look?**
A: Yes. Upload each team member's reference photo (or describe each person) and specify "same professional studio background, same warm lighting, same navy blazer styling" for each generation. Lovart applies the same visual treatment to each avatar, creating a cohesive team presentation. The Professional plan ($49/month) supports multiple Brand Kits, which is useful for team avatar projects.

**Q: Is my reference photo stored or used for training?**
A: Your uploaded photos are used only for your avatar generation session. Lovart does not use customer uploads for model training. You retain full ownership and control of all uploaded and generated content.

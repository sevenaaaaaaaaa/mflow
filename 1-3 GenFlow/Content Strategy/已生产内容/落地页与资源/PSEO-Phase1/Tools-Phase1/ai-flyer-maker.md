## How the AI Flyer Maker Works

### Step 1: Describe Your Flyer

Type `@flyer` in ChatCanvas and describe what you need. Lovart's MCoT engine processes your description and selects the best image generation model for your flyer type — photorealistic for real estate, stylized for nightlife, clean and structured for service businesses.

The more detail you provide, the more tailored the output:
- Flyer type (event, promo, real estate, food, service, nightlife)
- Business or event name
- Key information (date, time, location, offer, contact)
- Visual style and mood
- Any brand preferences

### Step 2: Generate and Select

Lovart produces 2-4 flyer variations in approximately 30 seconds. Review the options and select your preferred composition. Each variant explores different layout approaches — one might emphasize imagery, another might be typography-forward, a third might balance both.

### Step 3: Refine with Touch Edit

The Touch Edit interface lets you fine-tune the selected flyer:
- **Edit text inline:** Click any text element to modify. Change the date, update the offer, add a phone number — the layout reflows automatically.
- **Adjust imagery:** Regenerate only the background or product image without touching the layout.
- **Apply Brand Kit:** Toggle your saved brand settings — colors, logo, typography — onto the flyer. The composition adapts to your brand identity.

### Step 4: Export and Print

Download the print-ready PDF for professional printing, the PNG for digital sharing, or both. If you are printing at home or at a local print shop, the PDF includes all necessary print marks. If you are posting on social media, the PNG is optimized for each platform.

## Flyer Design Best Practices

**One message per flyer.** The most common flyer mistake is trying to communicate everything. A flyer should have one primary message — the event, the offer, the announcement. Supporting details support; they do not compete.

**High contrast for readability.** Flyers are often viewed in less-than-ideal conditions — dim lighting, from a distance, while walking by. Lovart generates flyers with strong visual contrast by default, but you can specify "high contrast" or "bold" in your prompt for extra emphasis.

**QR codes drive digital action.** Include a QR code for ticket links, menu pages, or contact forms. Lovart generates QR-ready composition space — just add your code. Pro tip: use `@flyer ... include-qr: true` and Lovart generates a placeholder QR code position that integrates naturally into the layout.

**Print on quality paper.** The design is half the flyer. The paper is the other half. Lovart's print-ready PDFs include bleed for full-color edge-to-edge printing — use a professional print service or a good color printer for best results.

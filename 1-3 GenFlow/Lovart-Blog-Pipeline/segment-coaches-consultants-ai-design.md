Coaches and consultants sell expertise. But expertise is invisible — you cannot photograph it, package it, or display it on a shelf. Visual branding makes expertise tangible. A professional logo, a polished social presence, and well-designed lead magnets signal competence before a single discovery call happens.

**Personal brand identity:** *"Personal brand logo for a business coach, 'Maya Okonkwo.' Modern, confident. Abstract geometric mark — an ascending spiral or upward arrow that suggests growth. Clean sans-serif name. Deep teal and warm gold palette. Works as a social avatar and on a website header."*

**Lead magnets:** *"Lead magnet cover design. 'The 90-Day Business Growth Framework.' Professional, clean. E-book or PDF cover format. Bold title. Author name below. Brand Kit colors. Subtle geometric pattern background. Looks premium — worth an email address."*

**Social proof graphics:** *"Client testimonial graphic. Quote: 'Maya helped me 3x my consulting revenue in 6 months.' Client name and title below. Brand Kit colors. Clean, elegant. Professional headshot placeholder."*

**Webinar/presentation slides:** *"Webinar slide deck master. 'Scaling Your Consulting Practice.' Brand Kit colors. Clean, authoritative. Space for data, frameworks, and key takeaways. Not corporate — warm, human, expert."*

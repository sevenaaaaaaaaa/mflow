# How to Create Fully Editable Designs with AI — No Photoshop License Required

[IMAGE 1 PLACEHOLDER — Persona Scenario]

The biggest lie in AI design isn't about quality. It's about editability. A tool promises "professional designs in seconds" and delivers a flattened JPEG — beautiful, but frozen. You can't move the headline. You can't swap the product image. You can't adjust the colors. You can look at the design or you can start over. Those are your options.

This is why AI design tools get used for inspiration and exploration but abandoned for production. The gap between "that looks great" and "I can actually use this" is editability. If you can't modify the output, the output is a preview, not a deliverable. Clients need revisions. Campaigns need variations. Teams need shared working files. A flattened image doesn't support any of those workflows.

Lovart takes a different approach: ChatCanvas for generation, Touch Edit for modification, and PSD export for downstream editing. The combination means you can generate, refine, and hand off without ever opening Photoshop. Here's the complete workflow.

## The Three-Layer Editability System

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

### Layer 1: ChatCanvas (Generation + Prompt-Based Editing)

ChatCanvas is where the design is born. You describe what you want. The AI generates it. But unlike other tools, the AI doesn't output a final, locked image — it outputs an editable project that lives on the canvas and can be modified through additional prompts, direct manipulation, or format export.

After the initial generation, you can:
- **Refine with prompts:** "Keep the composition. Make the headline larger. Change the background color to navy."
- **Replace elements:** "Swap the hero image with [new description] while keeping the rest of the design identical."
- **Add elements:** "Add a secondary CTA button below the headline. Rounded corners. Amber color. 'Learn more' text."
- **Remove elements:** "Remove the decorative line below the headline."

Each prompt-based edit maintains the existing design's structure — you're editing, not regenerating. The iteration happens inside the canvas, not in a separate generation.

### Layer 2: Touch Edit (Direct Manipulation)

Prompt-based editing handles broad changes. Touch Edit handles specific adjustments. This is the tool that makes AI design actually production-ready.

**What Touch Edit can do:**

**Move any element.** Click on the headline, the image, the logo, the button — drag it to a new position. The element snaps to alignment guides (center, edges, spacing to other elements) so repositioning maintains professional layout standards.

**Resize any element.** Click an element, drag its corner handles. Scale proportionally (hold Shift) or freely. The AI re-renders the element at the new size without quality loss because it's working with the element's latent data, not scaling a raster image.

**Change colors.** Click any element. Open the color picker. Choose a new color — from the Brand Kit palette, from a hex code, or from the built-in color wheel. The element re-renders in the new color. This works on text, shapes, backgrounds, and illustration elements.

**Replace text.** Double-click any text element. Type new text. The typography treatment (font, size, weight, color) stays the same. Only the content changes. This is how you go from "Hero Headline Goes Here" to your actual headline in 3 seconds.

**Remove backgrounds.** If you generated an image that has a background you want to remove (for merch, for compositing, for transparent overlays), one click removes the background. The subject stays. The background becomes transparent.

**Apply textures.** Add grain, paper texture, canvas weave, or screen print effects as non-destructive overlay layers. Adjust intensity. Remove or change at any time.

**Erase and inpaint.** Circle an area you want removed. The AI fills it with contextually appropriate content. Remove a stray element, a background distraction, or an awkward detail without regenerating the entire image.

### Layer 3: PSD Export (Downstream Editing)

For teams and workflows that require Photoshop or Photopea (free, web-based), export the design as a layered PSD file. Each text element, each image, each shape exists on its own layer. Open in any compatible editor and you can:

- Fine-tune typography with kerning, leading, and tracking controls that aren't available in Touch Edit.
- Apply Photoshop-specific effects (blend modes, layer masks, adjustment layers).
- Swap in photography from a different source.
- Prepare the file for print with CMYK conversion and bleed setup.
- Hand off to a designer, agency, or printer who requires layered source files.

The PSD export bridges the gap between AI design generation and traditional design production pipelines. Your workflow can start in Lovart (fast, AI-powered, iterative) and finish in the tool your team or client requires (precise, familiar, compatible).

## The Full Workflow: From Blank Canvas to Client-Ready File

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

**Step 1: Generate the Concept (3 minutes)**
Open ChatCanvas. Describe the design: format, subject, style, text content, brand elements. Generate 4 variations. Select the strongest direction.

**Step 2: Refine with Prompts (2 minutes)**
Adjust the selected design: "Make the headline 20% larger. Shift the hero image to the left. Reduce the background pattern opacity by 30%."

**Step 3: Polish with Touch Edit (5 minutes)**
Move elements into their final positions. Replace placeholder text with final copy. Adjust colors to exact hex values from the Brand Kit. Apply texture if needed. Remove any awkward details.

**Step 4: Export for Destination (1 minute)**
- Web/social/email? Export PNG at the target resolution.
- Print? Export PSD, convert to CMYK, confirm resolution and bleed.
- Further editing? Export PSD with all layers preserved.
- Logo/vector? Export SVG (vector format for infinite scalability).

Total time from blank canvas to production-ready design: roughly 10 minutes. The Photoshop equivalent: 40-90 minutes, depending on complexity. The Canva equivalent: 20-45 minutes (if you find the right template). The freelance designer equivalent: $50-$200 and 24-48 hours.

## When You Still Need Photoshop

Touch Edit covers 90% of common editing needs. The remaining 10% where Photoshop (or a PSD editor) is still necessary:

- **Complex photo compositing.** Blending multiple photographs with advanced masking, frequency separation, or channel-based selections.
- **Typography with custom kerning pairs.** Touch Edit offers standard typography controls. If your brand uses a custom typeface with specific kerning requirements, do the final text polish in a PSD editor.
- **Print production prep.** While Lovart exports at print resolution and supports CMYK conversion, some printers require specific file configurations (spot colors, overprint settings, trapping) that are best handled in a dedicated print-production application.
- **Animation or video integration.** If your design will be animated in After Effects or composited into video, export as PSD, organize layers, and import into your motion workflow.

The goal isn't to replace Photoshop for everyone. It's to make Photoshop optional for the majority of design work that doesn't need its full capability.

## FAQ

### Do I need any design experience to use Touch Edit?

No. Touch Edit is a direct manipulation interface — you click on elements and move them, resize them, or change their color. It's closer to using PowerPoint or Google Slides than Photoshop. If you've ever dragged an image around in a presentation or changed text in a document, you have the skills to use Touch Edit.

### Can I edit designs that were generated previously?

Yes. Any design you've created in Lovart remains on your canvas and is fully editable — through prompts, Touch Edit, or re-export. You can return to a design from weeks ago, change the headline, and export a new version in minutes. No need to regenerate from scratch.

### What happens to the quality when I resize elements?

Touch Edit re-renders elements at the new size using the underlying AI model, so quality is maintained when scaling up or down. This is different from raster scaling (enlarging a JPEG), which creates pixelation. The AI knows what the element should look like and redraws it at the new dimensions.

### Can multiple team members edit the same design?

On Professional tier and above, designs can be shared with team members. Each team member has their own editing capabilities within the permissions set by the design owner. Brand Kit Locking (available on Enterprise tier) prevents team members from modifying brand colors, fonts, and assets while allowing them to create within the brand sandbox.

### Does the PSD export work outside of Photoshop?

Yes. PSD is a widely supported format. Photopea (free, web-based, photopea.com) opens and edits PSD files with full layer support. GIMP (free, open-source) imports PSD files. Affinity Photo opens PSD files. Any application in your workflow that accepts layered PSD files will work with Lovart's PSD export.

### Can I combine AI-generated elements with my own assets?

Yes. Upload your own photography, illustrations, or brand assets via ChatCanvas. Place them in the design using Touch Edit. The AI-generated elements and your uploaded assets coexist on the same canvas. Export as a single flattened PNG or as a layered PSD with your assets and AI elements on separate layers.

### How does Touch Edit compare to Canva's editor?

Both are direct-manipulation design tools. The key difference: Canva is a template-driven editor where you arrange pre-made elements. Touch Edit works on AI-generated designs — the elements you're moving, resizing, and recoloring were created by the AI specifically for your design, not selected from a template library. The output is original. The editing is familiar. The combination means you get the creativity of AI generation with the control of direct manipulation, without the template-conformity of traditional drag-and-drop editors.

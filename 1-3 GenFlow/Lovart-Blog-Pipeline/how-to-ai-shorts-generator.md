Short-form video is not optional anymore. TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts collectively reach over 3 billion monthly active users. But creating consistent, high-quality short-form content requires time — filming, lighting, editing, captioning — that most brands and creators do not have.

Lovart's AI video tools generate short-form video from text descriptions. This guide covers how to produce professional shorts in minutes, not hours.

---

## The Short-Form Formula

The most successful AI-generated shorts follow a simple 3-part structure:

1. **Hook (first 2 seconds):** Visual that stops the scroll. Bold color. Surprising motion. Direct eye contact from a character. Text overlay with a provocative statement.
2. **Body (3-10 seconds):** The content. Product reveal. Tutorial step. Before/after transformation. Information delivery.
3. **Close (last 2 seconds):** CTA or brand moment. Logo reveal. *"Link in bio."* *"Try it free."*

---

## Generating Your First AI Short

1. Set aspect ratio to 9:16 in the ChatCanvas settings.
2. Prompt: *"Seedance 2.0: vertical short-form video, 9:16. Hook: a coffee machine appears from darkness with dramatic lighting, steam rising. Body: slow orbit around the machine showing its design details. Close: the machine fades to the brand logo centered on screen. 12 seconds. Audio: ambient coffee shop sounds, soft lo-fi beat. Energetic but not aggressive pacing."*
3. Refine: *"The hook transition from dark to lit is too slow — make it snappier, under 1 second. The body orbit should be more dynamic — weave between close-up and wide shot instead of a smooth circle."*

---

## Platform-Specific Optimizations

**TikTok/Reels:** Fast pacing. Jump cuts. Text overlays are essential (most users watch without sound initially). Generate with Seedance 2.0 for audio-visual sync.

**YouTube Shorts:** Slightly slower pacing. More informational. Can use Veo 3 for smoother camera work and longer shots.

**Product demos:** Generate clean product video first (see our [product video guide](/blog/how-to-create-product-videos-with-ai)), then add text overlays and captions in your preferred editing tool or via Lovart's Text Edit.

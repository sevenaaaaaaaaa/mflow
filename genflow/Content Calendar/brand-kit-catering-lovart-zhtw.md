- **Primary Logo:** Cinzel — elegant uppercase serif with gravitas for wedding and corporate appeal
- **Headlines:** Cormorant Infant — refined serif for proposal documents and menu headers
- **Body Text:** Source Sans 3 — highly legible across print and digital formats
- **Accent:** Great Vibes — decorative script reserved for wedding-specific collateral only
- **Digital:** Rubik — warm geometric sans-serif for social media and website

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How many logo versions does a catering business need?**
At minimum three: a full horizontal lockup for proposals and website headers, a square/social version for Instagram, and a simplified mark for small applications like chafing dish tags and napkin bands. Lovart generates all variants in one session.

**Q2: Should my catering brand look different for weddings vs. corporate events?**
Maintain the same logo but create event-type color variants. A navy/gold core palette can shift to soft blush for weddings or sleek silver for corporate. Never create entirely separate brands — you'll confuse referral sources.

**Q3: What printed materials should I prioritize?**
Proposal/brochure (your primary sales tool), business cards (you network constantly), menu cards (most photographed item by guests), and thank-you cards (drives repeat and referral business). Lovart's template system exports all four from one brand kit.

**Q4: How do I brand my staff's appearance?**
Consistent aprons with your logo mark (embroidery, not screen print — it lasts longer). Name tags in brand font. If servers wear their own black attire, provide a branded tie, scarf, or pin. Document these specifications in your brand guidelines.

**Q5: What's the best way to showcase my food photography?**
Invest in professional photography once and create a systematized image library tagged by event type, cuisine, and format. Lovart's brand kit includes a photo style guide: lighting specs, composition rules, and editing presets for consistency when you hire different photographers.

**Q6: How do I use my brand kit to win more corporate RFPs?**
Corporate clients value professionalism signals. A branded proposal template (consistent headers, fonts, color bars, logo placement) communicates operational maturity before they read a single menu item. Lovart exports branded InDesign/Canva proposal templates.

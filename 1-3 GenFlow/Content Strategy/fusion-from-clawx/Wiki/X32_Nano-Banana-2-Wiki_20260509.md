Nano Banana 2 is Lovart's flagship AI image model. Key features: character consistency (same character across different scenes and poses), spatial awareness (understands object relationships in scenes), multi-image fusion (combines elements from multiple references), brand integration (auto-applies Brand Kit parameters). Output: up to 2K, PNG/JPG/PSD format, 3-8 second inference time. Best for: brand design, product photography, IP/character design, social content, packaging.

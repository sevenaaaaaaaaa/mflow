## The First 2 Seconds: Hook or Die

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

Let's be blunt. If your short doesn't grab attention in the first 2 seconds, it doesn't matter how good the rest is. Nobody sees it.

Lovart's Shorts Generator builds hooks into the template engine. Here's what works:

**Pattern 1: The Question Hook**
Start with a bold text overlay asking a question your audience cares about. The AI generates a visually compelling opening frame with kinetic typography that pops in sync with the audio beat.

*Example*: "Is your skincare routine actually working?" → Cut to product demonstration

**Pattern 2: The Transformation Hook**
Open with the "before" state (cluttered room, dull skin, messy code) and promise the "after." The human brain is wired to crave closure — they'll watch to see the transformation.

*Example*: "This room renovation cost $200" → Timelapse of the transformation

**Pattern 3: The "Wait For It" Hook**
Tease something unexpected happening at the end. Text overlay: "Watch what happens at 8 seconds." This creates a curiosity gap that dramatically improves retention.

*Example*: "I asked AI to redesign famous logos" → Reveal shocking results

**Pattern 4: The Statement Hook**
Lead with a bold claim in text + voiceover. "This is the most important design principle you've never heard of." Now they need to know what it is.

*Pattern testing tip*: Use Lovart's batch feature to generate 3 versions of the same short with different hooks. Post all three. Let the algorithm and your audience tell you which pattern works best for your niche.

## Platform-Specific Tips

### TikTok
- Duration: 9-15 seconds performs best for discovery; 21-34 seconds for storytelling
- Text: Bold, frequent text overlays. Many TikTok users watch without sound
- Trends: Use Lovart's @trend command to incorporate current TikTok visual styles
- Music: Trending sounds matter more than anything. Generate with Lovart's trending audio library

### YouTube Shorts
- Duration: 15-30 seconds (YouTube rewards slightly longer Shorts)
- Format: End with a call to subscribe. The "Shorts shelf" keeps viewers scrolling
- Strategy: Each Short should be a gateway to your long-form content
- Thumbnails: Auto-generate a custom thumbnail hook — it's shown in search results

### Instagram Reels
- Duration: 7-15 seconds for feed discovery, up to 30 for existing followers
- Aesthetic: Instagram rewards visually polished content. Use Lovart's higher-quality rendering presets
- Safe zones: Text needs to clear the bottom 14% (UI overlay) and top 10%
- Hashtags: Generate niche-specific hashtags after your Reel in the first comment

### Cross-Platform Strategy
Batch-generate 12 shorts → Post 3 to TikTok, 3 to Shorts, 3 to Reels → Analyze which platform gives you the best engagement → Double down there. You can afford to test when content costs 5 minutes instead of 2 hours.

## What Is an AI Video Upscaler?

An AI video upscaler uses deep learning models — typically convolutional neural networks (CNNs) or vision transformers — trained on millions of high/low-resolution video pairs. The model learns what "missing detail" should look like. When you feed it a low-resolution frame, it predicts and reconstructs the high-resolution version — not by simple interpolation (averaging neighboring pixels), but by *hallucinating plausible detail* based on learned visual patterns.

The best AI upscalers do three things simultaneously:
1. **Super-resolution** — increasing pixel count (e.g., 720p → 4K)
2. **Denoising** — removing sensor noise, compression artifacts, and grain
3. **Sharpening & detail enhancement** — restoring edge definition, texture, and fine detail

Lovart's model was trained on a diverse dataset spanning faces, landscapes, text, UI, product footage, and archival content — so it generalizes well across content types.

## AI Upscaling vs Traditional Upscaling

| Method | Technique | Quality | Artifacts |
|--------|-----------|---------|-----------|
| **Bilinear/Bicubic** | Pixel averaging | Poor | Blurry, soft |
| **Lanczos** | Sinc-function interpolation | Moderate | Halos, ringing |
| **AI Super-Resolution** | Deep neural reconstruction | Excellent | Minimal |
| **Lovart AI Upscaler** | Multi-model ensemble + Touch Edit | Professional | Near-zero |

Traditional upscaling methods are mathematical interpolation — they add pixels by averaging neighbors. This always produces a softer, blurrier result. AI upscaling reconstructs detail that wasn't in the original — it's generative, not interpolative.

---

## Frame Interpolation: The Smoothness Factor

Resolution isn't the only quality axis. Frame rate matters too — especially for motion-heavy content. Lovart's frame interpolation model analyzes consecutive frames, calculates optical flow (how pixels move between frames), and generates synthetic intermediate frames.

The result: choppy 24fps footage becomes smooth 60fps output. For gaming content, sports, and action footage, frame interpolation can be more impactful than resolution upscaling alone.

You just took a product photo for your online store. The product itself — a hand-poured ceramic mug — looks great. But the photo looks like it was taken in a dimly lit apartment at 10 PM, because it was. The background is a wrinkled bedsheet, the lighting is yellow and uneven, and the overall impression screams "garage operation" rather than "artisan craft." You need this photo to look like it came from a professional studio — diffused natural light, clean background, color-accurate, with a subtle warm tone that makes people want to own this mug. You need effects, not a photography degree.

## Step 1: Import Your Image and Let Lovart Analyze It

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Drag your photo onto Lovart's ChatCanvas or tap the **Import** button. Lovart's **Image Analysis Engine** automatically evaluates exposure, white balance, contrast distribution, noise levels, and subject-background separation. The analysis panel (expandable from the bottom toolbar) shows a diagnostic report: "Exposure: -1.2 stops underexposed. White balance: 3,850K (too warm for product). Noise: medium (ISO-equivalent 3,200). Subject isolation: poor (background within 18% luminance range of subject)." This diagnostic tells you exactly what needs fixing — no guesswork. For the ceramic mug, the AI recommends a 5-action treatment plan prioritized by impact.

## Step 2: Apply Intelligent Auto-Fix as the Foundation

Tap **AI Auto-Fix** to apply Lovart's recommended baseline corrections: exposure lifted +1.2 stops, white balance cooled to 5,500K (daylight neutral), contrast curve adjusted to recover shadow detail without clipping highlights, and a subtle denoise pass that reduces grain without losing ceramic texture detail. The result is a technically correct photo — properly exposed, color-accurate, and noise-reduced. This is your clean starting point. Lovart applies all corrections non-destructively as adjustment layers, so you can dial back any individual fix without losing progress.

## Step 3: Apply Cinematic and Artistic Effect Presets

Now transform the technically-correct photo into a visually compelling one. Lovart's **Effects Library** contains 80+ presets organized by category: Cinematic (teal-orange grade, film grain, anamorphic flare), Product Photography (clean white, lifestyle warm, editorial contrast), Social Media (vibrant pop, muted aesthetic, vintage film), and Art Styles (oil painting, watercolor, pencil sketch, cyberpunk). For the ceramic mug, select **"Product → Lifestyle Warm"** — this applies a subtle warmth to midtones, lifts shadows with a gentle S-curve, adds a micro-vignette to focus attention, and applies a 2px radius background blur to simulate a wide-aperture product shot. Each preset is a starting point — you can adjust intensity from 0% to 200% with a slider.

## Step 4: Refine with Targeted Adjustments via Touch Edit

Presets get you 80% there. Touch Edit handles the final 20%. Tap the mug and select **Subject Enhance** — Lovart masks the product and applies localized sharpening (+15%), saturation boost (+8% on the glaze color only), and a subtle highlight on the rim to define the ceramic edge. Tap the background and select **Background Refine** — Lovart deepens the background blur to 5px, reduces background saturation by 20% to push visual weight onto the product, and evens out the lighting gradient. For product photos with unwanted background elements, use **AI Content-Aware Remove** — draw a rough circle around the distracting object (a stray charging cable, a reflection), and Lovart fills the area with contextually appropriate background.

## Step 5: Export with Comparison and Documentation

## FAQ

**Q: How do Lovart's AI effects differ from Instagram filters?**
A: Instagram filters apply a single global color lookup table (LUT) at fixed intensity. Lovart's effects are multi-layer adjustment stacks: exposure, white balance, curves, selective color, denoise, sharpening, and vignette — all applied non-destructively and individually adjustable. They're designed for production output, not social preview.

**Q: Can I create and save custom effect presets?**
A: Yes. After designing an effect stack, tap **Save as Preset** (available on $19 Starter plan and above). Name it, categorize it, and it appears in your Effects Library for one-tap application on future projects.

**Q: Does AI Content-Aware Remove work on complex backgrounds?**
A: It works well on textures (fabric, wood, paper), gradients, and organic backgrounds. It struggles with structured backgrounds containing text, faces, or regular geometric patterns that need precise replacement. For those cases, use the clone-stamp-style Touch Edit brush.

**Q: Can Lovart batch-apply effects to multiple product photos at once?**
A: On the $49 Pro plan, use **Batch Process** — import up to 50 photos, load a Process Recipe, and Lovart applies the full effect stack to every image simultaneously. Processing time scales linearly: approximately 4 seconds per image.

**Q: Does applying effects reduce image resolution or quality?**
A: No. Lovart's effects are applied to the full-resolution source image. Export resolution matches import resolution up to the plan limit (Free: 2048px, Starter: 4096px, Pro: 8192px on longest edge). Multiple effect passes do not compound compression artifacts.

**Q: Can I use Lovart effects on images I didn't create?**
A: Yes, for any image you have the legal right to edit. Lovart's terms require that you own or have licensed the source image. The AI effects engine does not claim copyright over your edited output.

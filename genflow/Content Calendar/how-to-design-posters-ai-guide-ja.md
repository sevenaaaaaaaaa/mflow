## The 3-Step AI Poster Workflow

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Most people overcomplicate poster design. They think you need color theory, typography instincts, and years of layout experience. With an **ai poster maker** like Lovart, the workflow collapses into three deliberate steps:

**Step 1 — Define the Poster's Job.** Every poster has exactly one job. Is it driving foot traffic? Announcing a sale? Building brand awareness at a conference? Nail this before typing anything. An event poster needs date, time, venue front and center. A promotional poster needs the offer to scream. A brand poster needs emotional resonance over information density.

**Step 2 — Write a Precision Prompt.** The difference between mediocre AI output and professional results lives in the prompt. Instead of *"make a poster for my coffee shop,"* write: *"Event poster for a specialty coffee tasting. Warm earthy tones, minimalist layout with one hero image of a pour-over dripper. Copy in top-left: 'Single-Origin Tasting Night / March 15 / 7PM'. Negative space at bottom for address. 18×24 portrait. Print-ready, 300 DPI."* The more you specify — aspect ratio, text placement, color palette, mood — the closer the first output lands to done.

**Step 3 — Iterate, Don't Redesign.** A professional designer doesn't nail it on the first draft either. Lovart's ChatCanvas lets you say *"make the headline 40% larger"* or *"swap the background to a dark studio shot"* without starting over. Treat the first generation as a sketch. Three to four directed iterations produce gallery-worthy results.

## From Screen to Wall: Print Specifications

The gap between a beautiful screen image and a beautiful printed poster is real. Here's what matters:

- **Resolution:** Always specify "300 DPI, print-ready" in your Lovart prompt. Screen resolution (72 DPI) produces blurry prints.
- **Bleed:** If your design has edge-to-edge color or imagery, request a 0.125-inch bleed in your final export.
- **Color Mode:** Print uses CMYK, not RGB. Lovart handles this conversion automatically when you specify print intent.
- **File Format:** Export as PNG (for highest quality) or PDF (for direct-to-printer submission).

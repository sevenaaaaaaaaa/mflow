# Lovart AI: Intelligent Design Agent for AI Image Generation

Single-shot image generators answer one prompt with one frame. Marketing teams need something else: briefs that survive handoff, brand rules that persist, edits that do not require a full reroll, and siblings that match across sizes. Lovart is built around that job—an **intelligent design agent** that plans, generates, edits, and remembers inside ChatCanvas, not a one-click wallpaper machine.

This page explains what that means in practice, where Lovart fits against look labs and template suites, and where it is the wrong tool—without pretending one product solves every creative discipline.

## Who this is for

Marketing teams, founders doing their own creative, freelancers packaging client systems, and operators evaluating Lovart against Midjourney-class labs or Canva-class templates.

## What “intelligent agent” means here (working definition)

An intelligent design agent, in Lovart terms, combines:

**Planning (MCoT):** break a campaign into asset lists before pixels—channels, sizes, forbids, stop rules.

**Conversation (ChatCanvas):** brief and refine in chat with visible canvas state—not orphaned prompts in a feed.

**Memory (Brand Kit + Identity Lock):** colors, type roles, logo lockups, product/face continuity across siblings.

**Surgery (Touch Edit / Edit Elements):** local fixes before expensive regen.

**Governance:** archive autopsies, four-take stops, cold handoff tests.

That is different from a generator that returns a grid and wishes you luck.

## Core modules you will actually touch

### MCoT weekly lists

Start Monday with channels and assets—not random prompts. Lists force stop rules and credit discipline.

### ChatCanvas

Brief like a creative director: constraints, exclusions, pixels, audience. Iterate with directed edits, not “try again.”

### Brand Kit

Encode truth from real products and rooms. Write forbids (fake seals, competitor mimicry, typography in-image).

### Identity Lock

When a SKU, dish, or face must stay itself across sizes, lock references before sibling generation.

### Touch Edit

Fix local problems—background, prop, glare—before burning regen credits.

## An honest week in Lovart (operator narrative)

**Monday:** load Brand Kit; MCoT list for launch (hero, three social sizes, email header).

**Tuesday:** four takes on hero; score legibility on phone; archive loser with one line.

**Wednesday:** siblings under kit; empty type bands for overlay tools.

**Thursday:** Touch Edit on hero glare; no full reroll.

**Friday:** hand board to VA cold; fix kit card until pass.

If Friday fails, the agent story failed—not the model.

## Flop diary

### Flop: generator cosplay

Team used Lovart like a look lab—no kit, no stops, twelve rerolls. Fix: MCoT list + four-take rule.

### Flop: typography trust fall

Headlines baked into images; letters wrong. Fix: overlay type; image is background only.

### Flop: amnesia week two

No Brand Kit; feed drifted. Fix: kit before scale; archive autopsies.

## When Lovart is not the best fit

Fine-art exploration with no brand system—look labs may be simpler. Spot-color print prepress—Creative Cloud. Fully offline inference—local stack. One poster once—any tool works.

## Pairing Lovart with other tools

Healthy pattern: mood in look lab, production in Lovart with import folder discipline. Unhealthy: five tools, no kit owner.

## Claims hygiene (read this)

Lovart markets agent-native design workflows—planning, memory, edit loops—not a claim that every image model lives only here. Evaluate on **your** job card: siblings, handoff, brand memory, edit cost. Ignore demo fireworks that do not match your channel list.

## FAQ

### Is Lovart only an image generator?

No—it is a design agent environment where image generation is one step in brief-to-ship workflows.

### Does Lovart replace Photoshop?

Sometimes for marketing sets; not for all prepress/compositing depth. Many teams use both with borders.

### How does Lovart compare to Midjourney?

Midjourney-class tools often win single-frame exploration; Lovart wins recurring branded sets and handoff if you use Brand Kit and ChatCanvas discipline.

### What is MCoT in plain language?

A planning pass that lists assets and constraints before generation—so Monday’s board matches Friday’s ship.

### Can a VA run Lovart?

Yes, if Brand Kit, forbids, and kit card exist; cold handoff is the test.

## Recommended Color Palette for Gym Owners

**Bold, energetic, and motivating. Deep charcoal provides a strong foundation. Electric orange injects energy and urgency for CTAs. Clean white keeps it crisp. Dark navy adds depth for secondary elements.**

Your palette: **Deep Charcoal (#1A1A1A), Electric Orange (#FF6B35), Clean White (#FFFFFF), Dark Navy (#1B2838)**

### How to Use

- **Primary** (first color): Dominant brand color — logo background, headers, primary buttons
- **Secondary** (second): Text and accent — headlines, important info, secondary buttons
- **Accents** (third, fourth): Sparingly — badges, illustration accents, seasonal variations

### Why This Works for Gym Owners

Gym Owner customers make split-second visual judgments. A cohesive, industry-appropriate palette signals professionalism before anyone reads a word. These colors balance emotional response with practical requirements of legibility, print reproduction, and digital display.

## Font Pairing for Gym Owners

**Bebas Neue (headings — bold, condensed, high-impact); Inter (body — clean, highly readable at all sizes)**

### Typography Rules

1. **Headings always use the first font.** Builds instant recognition.
2. **Body text always uses the second font.** Readability is non-negotiable.
3. **Stick to 2-3 sizes.** Heading, subheading, body.
4. **Never use more than these two fonts.**

Lovart's Brand Kit automatically applies these rules to every design.

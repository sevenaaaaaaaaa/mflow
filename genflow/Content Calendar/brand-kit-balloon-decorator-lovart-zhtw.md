## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Balloon decorator branding walks a unique line: it must be colorful enough to signal "party professional," yet neutral enough that the actual balloon colors in your photos remain the hero. Your brand frame should never compete with your balloon art.

- **Primary**: `#00B4D8` — Bright sky blue, the universal "celebration sky" color that complements every balloon palette
- **Secondary**: `#FFF9E6` — Warm sunshine-cream for backgrounds, menus, and price cards
- **Accent**: `#FF6B6B` — Playful coral for "Book Now" CTAs, heart accents, and Valentine's sub-brands
- **Fun**: `#F9C74F` — Golden confetti for "Featured Installation" callouts and new-service badges
- **Neutral**: `#2D2D3A` — Deep charcoal for readable typography on light backgrounds

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart generate balloon-color palette recommendations for clients?**
Yes. Clients often know their event colors but not what works in balloons. Lovart's palette-matching engine converts any hex code or moodboard into available balloon colors from major manufacturers (Qualatex, Sempertex, Betallic). The AI generates "Option A / Option B / Option C" color-way previews.

**Q2: How do I create a brand that doesn't clash with every different balloon color I use?**
Lovart solves this with the "neutral frame" approach: your brand's primary palette (`#00B4D8` / `#FFF9E6` / `#2D2D3A`) functions as the frame that lets balloons be the art. Think museum walls—present but invisible. Your brand is recognizable but never the source of a color clash.

**Q3: Can Lovart design pricing tiers that clients actually understand?**
The AI generates pricing structures based on industry standards: Per-Foot (garlands/arches), Per-Installation (walls/backdrops), Per-Piece (sculptures), and Delivery-Only (DIY kits). Clear "Starting At" prices with a "Final price depends on size, complexity, and location" disclaimer.

**Q4: Does Lovart create time-lapse video templates for installations?**
Yes. Generate a branded time-lapse overlay with your logo, installation name, and "Watch the Magic" text animation. Drop your 60-second installation time-lapse under the overlay. These videos are your highest-performing social content—Lovart makes them consistently branded.

**Q5: What about holiday-specific sub-brands (Valentine's, Halloween, Christmas balloons)?**
Lovart's seasonal sub-brand module creates temporary brand accents: Valentine's gets `#FF6B6B` emphasis, Halloween gets `#F9C74F` and spooky icons, Christmas gets festive red/green accent swap. Your `#00B4D8` primary stays constant; seasonal elements rotate.

# Replace Photoshop for 25+ Design Types: Step-by-Step AI Stack (2026)

The polluted title promised you could replace Photoshop for “25+ design types” with “zero experience required.” That is how teams buy orphans. Experience moved—it did not vanish. It now lives in briefs, Brand Kit locks, ship scores, and cold handoff. This rewrite is a honest job map: which of twenty-five common requests can live on Lovart-style agent boards, which still need PSD-class finishing, and the step-by-step rhythm that keeps cousins out of your catalog.

## Who this is for

Operators trying to retire Photoshop-as-default, freelancers productizing “no-PSD” packages, leads writing stack diagrams.

## Universal steps (every job)

1. Name the job and channel size.  
2. Load Brand Kit (or label EXPLORE).  
3. Attach truth refs; write forbids.  
4. MCoT a minimal shot list.  
5. Generate with four-take stop.  
6. Soft-proof smallest ship size.  
7. Touch Edit / Edit Elements locally.  
8. Export; autopsy losers; promote winners to kit.

## When Photoshop (or similar) still wins

Print dielines with bleed/trap, heavy multi-photo compositing, some precision vector finalization, advanced retouch on high-stakes beauty/medical imagery where policy demands it. Say this in your SOW so clients do not expect miracles from a board alone.

## Stack diagram (honest)

**Agent board (Lovart pattern):** campaign imagery, catalogs, social systems, mock scenes under kit.  
**Vector lane (Recraft-class):** SVG/icons/vector explore with export discipline.  
**Layout lane (Figma):** UI and structured landing layout.  
**Finishing lane (PSD-class):** when policy or print engineering requires it.

## FAQ

### Can AI really replace Photoshop for everything?
No. Print prepress, heavy compositing, and some precision vector workflows still need dedicated tools. Many campaign, catalog, and social jobs can move to agent boards with discipline.

### Is Recraft the same category as Lovart?
Recraft-class tools skew vector/SVG generation and editing. Lovart skews Brand Kit campaign production with ChatCanvas. They overlap on icons/illustrations, not on whole-company OS.

### Is Synthesia a Lovart competitor?
Different jobs: Synthesia-class tools focus on scripted talking-head video. Lovart focuses on brand imagery, kits, and still-to-story systems. Compare by job, not by hype.

### Where do I start?
Brand Kit → MCoT job list → ChatCanvas → soft-proof → Touch Edit → lock winners → document handoffs.

### Should I claim “zero experience required”?
Only if your SOP includes forbids, ship scores, and cold handoff. Tools do not remove experience—they move it to brief quality and kit hygiene.

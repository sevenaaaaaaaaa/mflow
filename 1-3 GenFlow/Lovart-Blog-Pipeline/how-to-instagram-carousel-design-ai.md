Your carousel outperformed your Reel last week—but only on slide one. Slides four and seven look like a different brand entirely. That is not an algorithm problem. It is a production problem: carousels punish inconsistency harder than any other Instagram format because users swipe through ten micro-judgments in eight seconds.

Most teams solve carousels by duplicating Canva templates and hand-tweaking each slide. That works until you need twelve carousels a month, three offer angles, and copy changes on slide two after legal review. AI image generators make single hero images fast; they do not make **coherent multi-slide narratives** unless something enforces visual continuity across the set.

That is where an **AI Design Agent** differs from a one-shot generator. This guide walks through designing Instagram carousels that look intentional, not like ten unrelated AI outputs glued together.

## Why Carousels Break (And What “Good” Looks Like)

### The swipe-test failure mode

Instagram serves carousels at **1080 × 1350 px** (4:5) in feed. Users decide to keep swiping based on:

1. **Slide 1 hook** — curiosity or promise, not logo wallpaper.
2. **Rhythm** — predictable layout so the eye knows where to land.
3. **Contrast** — readable type over photography or gradients.
4. **Continuity** — same margins, type scale, and color logic on every slide.

When each slide is generated in isolation, you get margin drift, font roulette, and “almost the same” blues. Engagement drops on slide 3 because the brain flags inconsistency as low effort.

### Strategic fix: design the arc before the pixels

Before opening Lovart, write a **slide map**:

| Slide | Role | Content |
|-------|------|---------|
| 1 | Hook | Bold claim or question |
| 2–8 | Teach | One idea per slide |
| 9 | Proof | Stat, quote, or mini case |
| 10 | CTA | Follow, save, or link in bio |

The **Design Agent** uses this map in **Thinking Mode** so generation serves narrative structure, not random pretty frames.

## Step 1: Lock Brand Rules in Brand Kit

Open **Brand Kit** and define:

- **Primary / secondary / accent** hex values (max three dominant colors per carousel).
- **Display + body** font styles (or reference uploads).
- **Logo placement** — corner, minimum clear space.
- **Photography mood** — e.g., “warm studio, soft shadow, no harsh HDR.”

Prompt on ChatCanvas:

> *"Apply Brand Kit [Your Brand Name]. All carousel slides use primary #1A1A2E background, accent #E94560 for highlights, Inter Bold for headlines, Inter Regular for body. Logo bottom-right, 48px padding."*

For a full Brand Kit walkthrough, see our [complete Brand Kit guide](/blog/complete-guide-brand-kit-every-industry-lovart). New to the workspace? Start with [ChatCanvas getting started](/blog/05-pillar-getting-started-lovart).

**Pro tip:** Carousels that teach often need a **“dark mode”** and **“light mode”** slide variant. Save both as Brand Kit presets rather than re-prompting colors each time.

## Step 2: Generate the Master Layout System

### Canvas setup

Create a ChatCanvas project titled `IG Carousel — [Campaign Name]`. Set generation intent to **1080 × 1350**, 4:5.

Use **Nano Banana 2** when slides are text-heavy (tips, frameworks, checklists)—it renders crisp type at 2K native. Use **Nano Banana Pro** when slides are photo-led with minimal copy.

**Master layout prompt:**

> *"Thinking Mode: Design a 10-slide Instagram carousel layout system for [topic]. Slide 1: full-bleed hook headline, 8 words max. Slides 2–9: left-aligned headline top third, supporting line middle, subtle icon or diagram lower third. Slide 10: CTA block center. Consistent 64px margins. No stock-photo clichés. Export as separate artboards labeled Slide 01–10."*

### Establish the grid once

Ask the agent to generate **Slide 02 as the template reference**, then:

> *"Match Slide 03–10 to Slide 02 grid, margins, and type scale. Only change headline and body copy per slide map."*

This single instruction prevents the margin drift that kills swipe-through rate.

## Step 3: Write Slide Copy Into the Visuals

Feed your slide map as structured copy:

> *"Slide 1 headline: 'Your hook here' / Slide 2 headline: '...' / Slide 3: '...' [continue]. Keep headlines under 12 words. Body under 20 words per slide."*

### Text Edit for legal and marketing revisions

When compliance changes slide 4’s claim, use **Text Edit**—click the headline on-image, type the revision. You avoid regenerating backgrounds and illustrations.

If a diagram on slide 6 is wrong, use **Touch Edit**: click the diagram, prompt *"Replace with simple 3-step flowchart, same line weight as slide 2 icons."*

---

## Step 4: Add Visual Interest Without Breaking Continuity

### Icon and diagram discipline

Prompt:

> *"Use one icon style across slides 2–9: 2px stroke, rounded caps, accent color only on icons—not backgrounds."*

### Photography slides

For mixed carousels (5 text slides + 3 photo slides):

> *"Slides 3, 6, 9: lifestyle photo, Brand Kit color grade, 20% dark gradient at bottom for optional text overlay."*

**Identity Lock** helps when the same founder appears on slides 1 and 10—upload reference once on Nano Banana Pro.

---

## Step 5: QA the Swipe Experience

Checklist before export:

1. **Thumbnail crop** — slide 1 readable at feed scale (test at 25% zoom).
2. **Safe zones** — no critical text within 100px of edges (UI overlays).
3. **Numbering** — optional slide counter bottom-left for long tutorials.
4. **Accessibility** — contrast ratio 4.5:1 minimum for body text; see our [composition rules guide](/blog/composition-rules-design-rule-of-thirds-golden-ratio) for placement.

### Export

Export each slide as **PNG, 1080px wide**. Upload to Instagram in order. Caption can reference “Swipe for the framework →” to train behavior.

## FAQ

**Q: How many slides should an Instagram carousel have?**
A: Instagram allows up to 20 images. For education and B2B, 7–10 slides balance depth and completion rate. For simple promos, 3–5 slides often perform better.

**Q: What size should I design carousels in Lovart?**
A: Design at 1080 × 1350 px (4:5) for standard feed carousels. If you also run 1:1 ads, generate a parallel artboard set from the same Brand Kit.

**Q: Can Lovart keep fonts consistent across all slides?**
A: Yes. Brand Kit plus “match Slide 02 grid” instructions enforce typography. Use Text Edit for copy changes without breaking fonts.

**Q: Which Lovart model is best for text-heavy carousels?**
A: Nano Banana 2 (powered by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash Image) for sharp type and fast iteration. Use Nano Banana Pro for photo-dominant slides with Identity Lock.

**Q: How do I fix one bad slide without redoing the carousel?**
A: Regenerate or Touch Edit that artboard only, then re-export. Text Edit handles headline and body swaps on existing backgrounds.

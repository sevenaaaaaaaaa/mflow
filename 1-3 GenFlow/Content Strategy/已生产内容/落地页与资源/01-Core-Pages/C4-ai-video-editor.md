## What Is an AI Video Editor?

An AI video editor is software that automates or assists video editing tasks using machine learning. Instead of manually placing cuts, adjusting levels, and synchronizing assets frame by frame, you describe the desired outcome — and the AI executes.

The spectrum ranges from "AI-assisted" (the AI suggests edits, you approve) to "fully autonomous" (the AI produces a complete edit from raw footage). Lovart sits in the middle by design: the AI does the heavy lifting, but **Touch Edit** gives you pixel-level override control when needed.

## Core AI Video Editing Features

### 1. Smart Trimming & Cutting

Upload raw footage. Tell the AI what to keep: "Trim to the best 60 seconds, focus on scenes with people talking and remove dead air." The AI analyzes speech patterns, visual activity, and shot composition to produce a tight cut — no timeline scrubbing required.

### 2. Scene Merging

Recorded multiple takes? Shot B-roll from different angles? The AI scene merger analyzes visual continuity and audio flow to stitch clips together with natural-feeling transitions. It detects jump cuts and auto-inserts b-roll or transition frames to smooth them.

### 3. Background Removal & Replacement

Lovart's video background remover works frame by frame — no green screen needed. Shoot in your living room, output with a clean studio background. Or replace the background with a generated scene: a beach, a city skyline, a branded office. This is the same technology powering Lovart's image background removal, extended to video.

### 4. Auto Subtitle Generation

Speech-to-text with speaker diarization. Lovart generates accurate subtitles in 50+ languages, auto-syncs them to the timeline, and lets you customize font, color, position, and animation style. Captions increase video engagement by 40% on social platforms — Lovart makes them automatic.

### 5. Color Correction & Grading

One-click color balance. The AI analyzes your footage and corrects exposure, white balance, contrast, and saturation. Then apply a cinematic LUT (look-up table) for a specific mood — warm and nostalgic, cool and corporate, vibrant and energetic. Or upload a reference image and Lovart matches the color grade to it.

### 6. Audio Enhancement

Automatic noise reduction, de-reverb, voice isolation, and level normalization. Recorded in a echoey room? The AI cleans it up. Background hum from an AC unit? Gone. Music and voice-over levels automatically balanced so one doesn't drown out the other.

## Touch Edit in Video: Frame-Level Precision

Lovart's **Touch Edit** is what separates it from Capcut and Fotor. Here's how it works in video:

- **Object removal across frames** — tap a person walking through the background, and Lovart removes them from every frame they appear in. No masking, no tracking, no keyframes.

- **Region-based color adjustment** — tap a specific area (a face, a product, a sky) and adjust its color independently across the entire clip. Brighten a face in a dim scene without affecting the background.

- **Element replacement** — swap an object across all frames. Replace a logo on a shirt. Change the color of a car. The AI tracks the element through motion and applies the edit consistently.

This is editing at the semantic level — "fix this thing" instead of "adjust these pixels over these frames." It's faster and produces more natural results than manual masking.

## FAQ

**Q: Can Lovart create accurate anatomical illustrations for patient education?**
Yes. ChatCanvas generates custom medical diagrams from text descriptions. While not a replacement for board-certified medical illustration in academic publishing, the output is more than sufficient for patient education — accurate enough to be credible, simplified enough to be understood.

**Q: How do I ensure my social media content remains HIPAA-compliant?**
Never include patient-identifiable information in social content. Lovart generates educational graphics (posture tips, myth-busting, anatomy explainers) that don't involve patient data. For testimonial content, always obtain written consent.

**Q: Can Lovart design my clinic's referral pad for other healthcare providers?**
Absolutely. Design a professional referral pad with your clinic details, specialization areas, fax/email, and a notes section — formatted for standard 5.5×8.5 prescription pad size.

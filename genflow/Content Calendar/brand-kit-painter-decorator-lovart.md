## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Painter and decorator branding must demonstrate color mastery. If you claim to have an "eye for color," your brand IS the proof. The palette must be sophisticated enough to sell to designers yet warm enough to feel residential.

- **Primary**: `#E76F51` — Terracotta warmth, the color of limewash plaster and Mediterranean facades
- **Secondary**: `#FFF5EE` — Seashell white for estimates, consultation cards, and portfolio backdrops
- **Accent**: `#2D6A4F` — Evergreen for "Eco-Friendly Paints" badges, certifications, and CTAs
- **Luxury**: `#C4A484` — Warm neutral tan for textured backgrounds and watermark patterns
- **Contrast**: `#3D2C2E` — Deep espresso for body text; dark enough for readability without harsh black

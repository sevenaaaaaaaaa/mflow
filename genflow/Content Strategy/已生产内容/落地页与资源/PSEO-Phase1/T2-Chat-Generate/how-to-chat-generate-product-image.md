# How to Chat-Generate Product Images — No Design Skills, Just Describe What You Want

The old way: open design software, blank canvas, hunt templates, adjust everything, export, realize dimensions are wrong. Time: 1-3 hours. Frustration: high.

The Lovart way: open chat, describe what you need, review, refine, export. Time: under 5 minutes. Frustration: zero.

---

## Step 1: Tell Lovart What You Need

Open Lovart's ChatCanvas and type your request:

> *"Generate a product image for [product name], a [category]. Style: [white background/lifestyle/studio]. Include: [product details, branding]. Dimensions: [platform requirements]."*

**Real examples that work:**
- *"Create an Instagram post announcing our summer sale — 30% off everything, bright and energetic, use coral and white brand colors"*
- *"Design a logo for 'Greenwise Market', organic grocery. Minimal and fresh. Sage green and cream. Wordmark with leaf symbol."*
- *"Generate a YouTube thumbnail for 'I Tried Every AI Design Tool'. Bold, high contrast, surprised face, 'BEST AI TOOL?' text"*

**Good prompt ingredients:** Be specific about style, include brand colors, mention must-include elements, specify platform for correct dimensions.

---

## Step 2: Review & Refine (The Magic Part)

Lovart generates multiple options. Pick the strongest. Now use **Touch Edit** — tap the specific element you want to change and describe the change. The AI edits *only that element* while keeping everything else intact.

**Common refinements:**
- *"add lifestyle context — show the product being used naturally"*
- *"adjust lighting — brighter for Amazon white-background requirements"*
- *"add subtle shadows for depth without distraction"*
- *"include infographic elements — arrows, labels, feature callouts"*

You're not rolling dice hoping for a good result — you're directing a design process through conversation.

---

## Step 3: Export for Your Platform

JPEG/PNG at platform dimensions. Amazon: min 1000px, pure white bg. Shopify: 2048x2048.

Lovart handles resolution, color space, file format, and compression automatically based on your platform. No more realizing you exported at the wrong DPI after posting.

The prompt is your creative brief. Be specific about style, purpose, and context.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I add my own photos?
Yes. Upload product photos, headshots, or brand assets and the AI incorporates them naturally — matching lighting, perspective, and style.

### What if the AI doesn't get it right on the first try?
Use Touch Edit to adjust specific elements, or describe the stylistic adjustment — "make it more elegant," "tone down colors," "use a more professional font." The AI adapts without losing what you liked.

### Can I create multiple sizes of the same design?
Yes. Once you have a design you like: "Now create the same design in Instagram Story (1080x1920) and Facebook cover (820x312)." The AI adapts composition intelligently for each format.

### How is this different from Midjourney or DALL-E?
Midjourney and DALL-E generate beautiful images but lack design-specific intelligence — typography hierarchy, brand consistency, export formats, platform requirements. Lovart is built for design output.

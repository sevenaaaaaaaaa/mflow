The Riverbend Food Market hosts 28 vendors—artisanal cheese, wood-fired pizza, fresh oysters, organic produce. But the market's visual identity is a patchwork of 28 different logos on a single folding sign. Visitors don't know it's a unified destination. The Saturday crowd is steady but stagnant. The market manager knows: a cohesive brand would increase dwell time, basket size, and Instagram shares. Lovart unifies 28 vendors under one vibrant roof.

## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

A food market brand must be a big tent—literally and visually. The palette needs to unify diverse vendors without overpowering their individual identities. Think of it as the gallery walls, not the artwork.

- **Primary**: `#5B8C3E` — Fresh harvest green, the color of farmers-market awnings and organic abundance
- **Secondary**: `#F5F9EE` — Bright herb-cream for directories, maps, and newsletter backgrounds
- **Accent**: `#3D6328` — Deep olive for authoritative typography and wayfinding headers
- **Warmth**: `#D4A373` — Rustic amber for "Today's Vendors" callouts and seasonal banners
- **Highlight**: `#E9C46A` — Golden sunflower for special event badges and "New Vendor" introductions

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create a brand that works across 20+ different vendor aesthetics?**
Yes. Lovart's "brand umbrella" methodology creates a unifying system (colors, fonts, map, signage) that neutral enough to sit behind vendor individuality but present enough to be recognizable. Think of it as the market's "operating system"—invisible but essential.

**Q2: How do vendor curations get updated weekly?**
Connect your vendor roster spreadsheet. Lovart reads "Saturday, May 16: Cheese Monger, Pizza Truck, etc." and updates the map, directory, newsletter, and social posts automatically. The $49 Pro tier refreshes weekly; $99 refreshes daily for daily markets.

**Q3: Can Lovart generate social content featuring multiple vendors?**
Absolutely. The "Market Day" template generates a multi-vendor carousel: Slide 1- market overview, Slides 2–7- featured vendor spotlight, final slide- map and hours. Lovart pulls vendor photos from a shared Drive folder and applies consistent framing.

**Q4: Does Lovart handle seasonal branding (summer market, holiday market)?**
Yes. Generate a "Summer Nights Market" sub-brand that inherits your core palette but introduces seasonal accent colors and event-specific templates. When the season ends, revert. All templates remain accessible for next year.

**Q5: What tier do food markets typically need?**
Small markets (under 15 vendors): $49 Pro tier. Mid-size markets (15–40 vendors): $99 Studio tier with vendor portal. Large markets (40+): $149 Enterprise with vendor self-service branding tools and centralized asset governance.

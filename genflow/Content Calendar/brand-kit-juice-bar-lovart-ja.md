## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Juice bar branding lives at the intersection of fresh produce and lifestyle aspiration. The palette should feel like a farmer's market at golden hour—vibrant, natural, and irresistible.

- **Primary**: `#FF6B35` — Vibrant citrus orange, the color of fresh-squeezed juice and energy
- **Secondary**: `#FFF3E0` — Warm peach cream for backgrounds and cup sleeves
- **Accent**: `#E65100` — Deep burnt orange for typography and price displays
- **Green**: `#2D6A4F` — Leafy forest green for "organic" badges and ingredient callouts
- **Pop**: `#FFB74D` — Golden mango for loyalty program icons and reward indicators

**San Francisco, CA — July 28, 2025** — Lovart, the San Francisco based AI company pioneering the world's first multimodal Design Agent, today exited beta and became publicly available—ending an 800,000‑person waitlist and making its creative platform accessible to anyone, anywhere.

The milestone was celebrated last week at IDEO with more than one hundred designers, founders, and investors in attendance. Product growth expert **Ying Yang** was invited to host the launch party, which featured hands‑on workshops, live demos, and keynotes that showcased **ChatCanvas**, a new real‑time workspace where users and Lovart's agent "think and build together."

"AI isn't here to take over creativity—it unlocks it, like a camera for the mind," said **Melvin Chen**, Lovart's Co‑Founder and CEO, during his opening address. "ChatCanvas is the place where your ideas and our agent meet, iterate, and ship instantly."

**Takumi**, Lovart's Founding Engineer, demonstrated the Design Agent's range and speed with three live cases:

- **Avatar Generation to Multimedia Suite** – A single prompt produced a personalized 3‑D avatar, a responsive HTML landing page, social media assets, and a motion logo in under ninety seconds.
- **Poster to Broadcast‑Ready Video** – A short brief, "tennis tournament poster for SF," evolved into multiple poster directions and a thirty‑second promo video featuring two AI‑generated hosts in perfect lip‑sync.
- **End‑to‑End Brand Identity** – Lovart created a full visual system for a lo‑fi music label—logo, typography, business cards, 3‑D stage visuals—while preserving design consistency across every asset.

Guest speaker **Robert Scoble**, tech futurist and author, explored how Lovart's architecture moves creative work toward a "Holodeck‑style" environment where spatial design and AI converge. **Yiwei Lu**, Founder of Yiwei Gallery and the C Art Festival, shared a replay of her newest exhibition assets—storyboards, animations, and AR filters—all built with Lovart during the beta.

Since its closed beta began in **May 2025**, Lovart's multimodal Design Agent has enabled over 300,000 early users to generate professional, fully editable images, videos, audio tracks, brand kits, and 3‑D renders on a single infinite canvas—compressing days of design work into minutes and eliminating the need for multiple point tools.

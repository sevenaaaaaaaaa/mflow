## Step 1: Define Your Appointment Card Format

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Open Lovart and select the **Appointment Card** template (Templates → Print → Appointment Cards). Choose your format: **Standard** (3.5×2 inches, standard business card size — fits in wallets and cardholders), **Slim** (4×2.5, more room for details), or **Fold-Over** (3.5×4 folded to 3.5×2, double the information space with a front cover). Type: **"dental clinic appointment card, standard 3.5x2 inches, double-sided — front side has clinic branding and appointment details, back side has QR code for calendar add and cancellation policy. Clean medical-professional aesthetic with soft blue and white palette, sans-serif typeface."** Lovart generates both sides with correctly positioned margins for wallet-card legibility.

## Step 2: Design the Front — Critical Information Hierarchy

An appointment card has exactly 3 seconds of attention when handed to a patient. The information hierarchy must be flawless. Lovart's **Info Hierarchy Guide** recommends: Line 1 — Clinic name (largest, bold, top-left), Line 2 — "Your Next Visit" header (medium, centered or top-right), Lines 3–5 — Date (bold, largest detail), Time (bold, second largest), Provider name (medium), Lines 6–7 — Address and phone (small, bottom, for reference only — most patients don't need to re-read the address). Lovart dynamically sizes text based on content length — if your clinic name is "Metropolitan Center for Advanced Cosmetic and Restorative Dentistry" it auto-scales to fit while maintaining the hierarchy. Use Touch Edit to adjust any text position manually.

## Step 3: Design the Back — Digital Integration

The back of the appointment card is where no-shows are prevented. Lovart's **Back Panel Builder** lets you add: a **Calendar QR Code** — generate a QR that links to an .ics calendar file, so patients scan and add the appointment to their phone calendar in one tap (requires a hosted .ics file URL; Lovart generates the QR and provides instructions for hosting the file), a **Reschedule QR** — links to your online booking page, and a brief cancellation policy (2 lines max, formatted small but readable: "24-hour notice required. Late cancellations subject to $50 fee.") For clinics without a digital booking system, the back panel can display "Scan to Save to Calendar" with the QR, plus a space for a handwritten next appointment if digital isn't feasible.

## FAQ

**Q: What paper stock should appointment cards use?**
A: 14pt to 16pt cardstock (100lb–120lb cover) with a writable finish (uncoated or matte coated — gloss lamination prevents ballpoint pens from writing). For clinics that hand-write dates, specify "uncoated" in your print specs. For fully printed dates, matte lamination adds durability.

**Q: How do I create a calendar .ics file for the QR code?**
A: Lovart doesn't host .ics files, but generates the QR code pointing to any URL. Create an .ics file generator on your website or use a third-party service. The QR code on the card would link to: https://yourclinic.com/appointment.ics?date=2026-06-15&time=14:00 which dynamically generates the calendar file.

**Q: Can I include the patient's name on the appointment card?**
A: Yes, if using variable data printing. In the Variable Data CSV Template, add a "PatientName" column. For handwritten cards, leave a "Patient:" line on the design for front-desk staff to fill in.

**Q: How many appointment card variations do I need for different providers?**
A: Ideally one unified design with the clinic brand, and use variable data printing to swap provider names. If providers want individual cards, Lovart's Batch Generate creates provider-specific variants from a single master design.

**Q: What's the ideal QR code size for a 3.5×2 inch card?**
A: 0.75 inches square minimum for reliable scanning. Lovart's appointment card template reserves a 1-inch square zone on the back panel for the QR code. Larger is better for older smartphone cameras.

**Q: Can Lovart integrate with my scheduling software (Acuity, Calendly, etc.)?**
A: Lovart doesn't directly integrate with scheduling platforms, but you can paste your booking URL into the QR generator. The QR code on the card links to your booking or rescheduling page. For calendar .ics QR codes, point to a dynamically-generated .ics URL from your scheduling platform's API.

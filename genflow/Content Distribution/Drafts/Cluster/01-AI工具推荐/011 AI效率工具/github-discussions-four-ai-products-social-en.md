# Four AI Creative Products: Liblib, LibTV, Xingliu Agent, Lovart

If you have been trying AI creative tools lately, you have probably felt this too: generating one image is no longer the whole story.

One image is useful, of course. But once you start making content, videos, brand assets, or product visuals, the harder questions show up fast. Where does the idea come from? How does the workflow continue? How do you keep the style consistent? How do you turn the output into something you can actually use?

That is why I wanted to put these four products side by side: **Liblib**, **LibTV**, **Xingliu Agent**, and **Lovart**.

They are not the same kind of tool, but they are all pushing AI creation toward a more complete workflow.

## Liblib — inspiration library you can actually use

Inspiration and execution sit close together. See a community image, inspect model/parameters/workflow, rerun and adapt.

https://www.liblib.art/inspiration

## LibTV — video production workspace

Node-based canvas connecting scripting, shot planning, generation, editing, and output. Skill interfaces for external AI agents.

https://www.liblib.tv/

## Xingliu Agent — Chinese-first design agent

Natural Chinese understanding, fonts, local stability. Describe a brand goal and it extends into packaging, posters, KV, promo video—not just one logo.

https://www.xingliu.art/

## Lovart — whole brand set on one canvas

AI design agent: logo, visual system, packaging, landing page, social assets, promo video in one flow with consistent style.

https://www.lovart.ai/zh-TW/home

## Quick pick guide

- Inspiration / remix workflows → Liblib
- Script-to-finished video → LibTV
- Chinese creative needs → Xingliu Agent
- Brand or product visual system → Lovart

Trend: AI creation is moving from "generate one thing" to "finish the whole idea."

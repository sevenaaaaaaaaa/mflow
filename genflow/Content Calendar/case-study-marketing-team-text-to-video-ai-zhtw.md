# How Marketing Director Aisha Diallo Replaced a $12,000/Month Video Agency with Text Prompts — And Doubled Campaign Output

[IMAGE 1 PLACEHOLDER — Persona Scenario]

Aisha Diallo's quarterly review with her CEO was in 48 hours, and the numbers were uncomfortable.

Her marketing team at FinBridge — a 70-person B2B fintech company — was spending $12,000 per month on a video production agency. For that money, they received 8 short-form videos: product demos, customer testimonials, social ads, and the occasional explainer. Each video took three weeks from brief to delivery. At any given moment, the pipeline was backed up by two months.

"I was spending six figures a year on video and couldn't even get a same-month turnaround on a product launch," Aisha says. "The agency was good. But good at three-week-old creative doesn't win markets."

She canceled the contract, opened a Lovart account, and built an in-house video production pipeline powered by text-to-video AI. Six months later, her team produces 30+ videos per month at 8% of the previous cost — and their average campaign conversion rate is up 37%.

## The Background

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

FinBridge sells accounts-payable automation software to mid-market finance teams. The product is excellent. Explaining the product visually — showing what "automated invoice matching" actually looks like in a workflow — was the challenge.

Aisha's marketing team of four managed every channel except video, which was fully outsourced. The agency relationship followed the standard pattern: brief on Monday, creative treatment on Thursday, first cut next Wednesday, revision round, second cut, final delivery three weeks from brief. By the time a product demo video went live, the product had often been updated twice.

"The agency model was designed for TV commercials — high production value, long timelines, big budgets. We needed product marketing videos: clear, fast, specific. Two different categories of content, forced into the same production pipeline."

---

## The Pain Points

### 1. The Timeline Tax

Three-week video production cycles meant every campaign was designed around video availability rather than market opportunity. When a competitor announced a feature, FinBridge couldn't respond with a comparison video for 21 days. When a customer agreed to a testimonial, the enthusiasm had cooled by the time the video went live. Speed is a competitive advantage in B2B marketing, and the agency model was optimized for everything except speed.

### 2. The Volume Ceiling

Eight videos per month served the core product pages adequately but left every other channel starved. The blog needed explainer videos for high-traffic SEO posts. Sales needed personalized demo clips for enterprise prospects. Social media needed short-form content for LinkedIn, where FinBridge's audience spent their professional attention. The team had ideas for 25+ videos per month but could only fund 8.

### 3. The Revision Economy

Every agency revision cost time and money. A simple text change — updating a product name in a video — required a change order, a producer's time, a motion designer's time, and 48-72 hours. For a SaaS company that shipped product updates weekly, maintaining accurate product videos became a full-time coordination job.

---

## The Turning Point

Aisha discovered Lovart during a LinkedIn scroll — a peer at another fintech company had posted about cutting their video production costs with AI. She signed up for a free trial and tested it with the simplest possible brief: a 60-second product demo video for FinBridge's invoice automation feature.

She wrote: "60-second B2B product demo video for accounts-payable automation software. Show a finance team's workflow before and after automation: manual data entry on screen, then automated invoice matching. Clean corporate aesthetic, navy and white color scheme, explainer-style with text overlays for key stats. Professional voice, 16:9 format."

The AI generated a complete video — visuals, text overlays, pacing, structure — in under 5 minutes. It wasn't perfect. The voiceover was slightly stilted, and one data visualization needed correction. But the first draft was 80% of what her agency delivered at 0.02% of the cost.

"I showed it to my CEO without telling him how it was made. He said, 'This is better than what the agency delivered last month.' That was the moment I knew I was canceling the contract."

## The Solution: How Aisha Uses Lovart

### The Text-to-Video Pipeline

Aisha's team now works on a self-serve model. Each team member can generate video drafts from text descriptions in Lovart's Video Generator, iterate in ChatCanvas, and publish without touching Premiere, After Effects, or waiting for agency availability.

The workflow:
1. **Briefing (15 minutes):** Write a text description of the video — purpose, audience, key messages, visual style, length
2. **Generation (3-7 minutes):** Lovart produces a complete first draft

Total output: 30-40 videos per month from a team of four, none of whom had professional video production experience before Lovart.

## FAQ

### Is text-to-video AI output good enough for professional B2B campaigns?

For product demos, explainer videos, social ads, and testimonial highlight reels — yes. The AI handles structure, pacing, text overlays, and visual selection competently. The output is not equivalent to a bespoke agency production with custom cinematography, but it exceeds the quality threshold for LinkedIn, YouTube, and website video content. Aisha's campaigns showed that audience response metrics (engagement, conversion) actually improved after switching to AI — likely because faster iteration enabled better creative optimization.

### How much control do I have over the final video?

Full control. The AI generates a first draft. You can replace any visual, adjust any text overlay, re-time any segment, swap the voiceover, change the color palette, restructure the narrative flow — every element is editable. The value is that you start from an 80%-complete draft rather than a blank timeline, dramatically reducing the time to final output.

### Can I use my own voiceover instead of AI voice?

Yes. Lovart supports custom audio upload for voiceovers. Aisha's team uses AI voiceover for social ads and quick-turnaround content, and records custom voiceovers for product demos and high-visibility customer-facing videos. The timeline difference: AI voiceover adds zero time; custom voiceover adds roughly 1-2 hours for recording and syncing.

### How does text-to-video AI handle complex B2B concepts?

The AI generates visual metaphors and screen-mockup-style representations of abstract concepts. For highly technical demonstrations (actual software UI, specific data workflows), the best approach is to generate the visual framework with AI and then overlay actual screen recordings. Aisha's product demos use AI for titles, transitions, and surrounding context, with screen-capture inserts for precise UI demonstrations.

### Can I maintain brand consistency across dozens of AI-generated videos?

Yes, through Lovart's Brand Kit. Set your brand colors, logo, typography, and motion style once, and every video generation automatically applies them. Aisha's team maintains consistent navy-and-white FinBridge branding across 30-40 videos per month without manual formatting. The Brand Kit ensures even different team members produce visually consistent output.

### What happens to the agency relationship — do I still need them?

Aisha retained the agency for one category of content: high-production-value brand films and annual event videos where bespoke cinematography, live interviews, and polished storytelling add genuine value. These represent roughly 10% of video needs. The other 90% — product marketing, social, sales enablement, content marketing — moved to AI. The agency budget dropped from $12,000/month to roughly $3,000/month for 2-3 premium productions per quarter.

### Is there a learning curve for non-video professionals?

The learning curve is primarily conceptual, not technical. You need to learn how to describe visual ideas in text — writing effective prompts that communicate mood, structure, and key messages. This is a writing skill, not a video production skill. Aisha's team — all strong writers — became proficient in one week. The technical interface (generation, review, export) is as straightforward as using a document editor.

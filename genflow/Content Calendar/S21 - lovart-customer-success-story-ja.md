# Lovart Customer Success: How a DTC Brand Transformed Their Visual Marketing

[IMAGE 1 PLACEHOLDER — Persona Scenario]

Artisan Blend is a direct-to-consumer coffee brand based in Portland. They ship single-origin beans to 12,000 monthly subscribers across the US. Their product is exceptional. Their visual marketing, until six months ago, was not.

When founder Mara Chen looked at her P&L in Q2 2025, one line item made her flinch every single month: **$4,000 for creative agency retainers.** That number bought her three social posts per week, one product photoshoot per month, and a two-week turnaround on anything custom. "We were burning money and waiting two weeks for a banner resize," she told us. "It felt unsustainable, but I didn't see another option."

---

## The Breaking Point

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

The inflection point came during a product launch in June. Artisan Blend needed 15 visual assets across Instagram, TikTok, email, and their Shopify storefront — within 48 hours. The agency quoted an additional $2,800 rush fee and couldn't guarantee delivery. Mara's marketing coordinator pulled an all-nighter in Canva. The results were passable but inconsistent. The brand looked different on every channel.

**Monthly creative spend:** $4,000 agency + $500 freelance overflow = **$4,500/month**
**Average turnaround:** 10-14 business days
**Team involved:** 2 internal + 3 agency contacts just to move one campaign from brief to publish

"We had five people touching every single image. A single Instagram carousel had the carbon footprint of a small government project."

## Discovering Lovart

Mara stumbled onto Lovart through a Product Hunt launch. She was skeptical — "I've tried every AI design tool. They all give you the same plastic-looking output." She signed up for the free tier that afternoon. By evening, she'd created three social posts that looked better than the agency's last batch. She upgraded to Team Plan ($149/month) before midnight.

## The New Workflow

Here's how Artisan Blend's creative operation runs today:

**Step 1 — Brand Kit**
Mara uploaded her logo, color palette (#2C1810, #D4A574, #F5F0EB), and three typefaces. Lovart now auto-applies brand colors and typography to every generation. No more "oops we used the wrong brown" moments.

**Step 2 — ChatCanvas Brainstorm**
Her marketing lead types: *"Create an Instagram carousel for our new Ethiopian Yirgacheffe. Warm morning vibe. Include a headline, tasting notes section, and a CTA to shop. Use our brand kit."* Three seconds later, a fully branded carousel appears on canvas.

**Step 3 — Touch Edit**
Headline reads "Discover Ethiopia." Marketing lead touches the text, types "Taste Ethiopia, Feel the Highlands." Done. No layers panel. No right-click menus. Just touch and edit.

**Step 4 — Export and Schedule**
One tap exports all sizes — Instagram 1:1, Story 9:16, email header 600px. Assets drop into their scheduling tool. Entire process: **22 minutes.**

"Honestly, it wasn't just the AI generation — every tool does that now," Mara said. "It was **not having to jump between five different apps.** Before Lovart, our workflow was Midjourney → Photoshop → Canva → Figma → email. Now it's Lovart → publish."

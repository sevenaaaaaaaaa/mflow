## 1. What Changed After SERP Research

The internal competitor keyword folder tells us what competitors are trying to cover. The SERP samples show who is actually standing between Lovart and the user at the moment of search.

中文：内部竞品词库告诉我们竞品在布局什么词；SERP 抽样告诉我们，在用户真实搜索时，到底是谁挡在 Lovart 和用户之间。

The main shift in understanding:

中文：这次调研带来的认知变化：

- Lovart is not competing with one category. It competes with different brands depending on search intent: logo, design generator, ad generator, commercial video, character consistency, model comparison, and AI agent/workflow.
- The most dangerous competitors are not always functionally closest to Lovart. Pages from Looka, Canva, Creatify, OpenArt, Venngage, Synthesia, Neolemon, and Figma are dangerous because they match search intent cleanly and reduce user uncertainty fast.
- SERP competitors often win by page shape: exact promise, workflow explanation, file formats, proof, FAQ, comparison framing, and clear "who should choose this" language.

中文：

- Lovart 不是只和一个品类竞争。不同搜索意图下，竞品会变成 logo、design generator、ad generator、commercial video、character consistency、model comparison、AI agent/workflow 等不同品牌和页面。
- 最危险的竞品不一定是功能最像 Lovart 的产品。Looka、Canva、Creatify、OpenArt、Venngage、Synthesia、Neolemon、Figma 这类页面危险，是因为它们能精准承接搜索意图，并快速降低用户不确定性。
- SERP 竞品经常赢在页面形态：明确承诺、工作流解释、文件格式、证据、FAQ、对比框架，以及清楚告诉用户“谁适合选这个”。

### A. Logo / Brand Identity

中文：Logo/品牌身份方向的主要竞争不是单纯 AI 生成能力，而是“快速得到可商用、可下载、可延展的品牌起点”。

| Competitor | Evidence | Strength | Lovart Counter-position |
|---|---|---|---|

| Kleap / Toololis | Observed #1/#2 in free logo sample | Free, no signup, SVG/PNG, privacy/no watermark | Lovart should not compete on "free simple logo" alone; use free angle only as entry to brand kit. |

## 6. Competition Dimensions

中文：以下是 Lovart 与真实 SERP 竞品竞争时，最关键的几个维度。

### 6.1 Search Intent Capture

Competitors win when one query maps to one exact page promise.

中文：竞品胜出的第一点，是一个 query 对应一个明确页面承诺。

- Looka: logo workflow.
- Topview: brand video from existing materials.
- OpenArt: product photo/script/prompt to commercial.
- Neolemon: character consistency problem and benchmark.

Lovart risk: pages that start with broad platform language instead of the specific job will lose even if the product can do more.

中文：Lovart 的风险在于：如果页面一上来讲宽泛平台能力，而不是具体任务，即使产品能做更多，也可能输给更精准的页面。

### 6.2 Workflow Specificity

Strong competitors expose the workflow:

中文：强竞品会把工作流摊开给用户看：

- Input: business name, product URL, product photo, script, prompt, reference image, brand kit.
- Generation: variants, model choices, avatars, voice, aspect ratios, templates.
- Refinement: editor, prompt iteration, code/control, A/B testing, export.
- Output: SVG/PNG/PDF/EPS, 9:16/16:9/1:1 video, social ads, brand kits.

Lovart advantage: one brief can become multiple connected assets. The page copy must show that path.

中文：Lovart 的优势是一个 brief 可以变成多个相互连接的资产。页面文案必须把这条路径讲清楚。

### 6.3 Trust And Risk Reduction

Common trust signals:

中文：竞品常用的信任和风险消除信号包括：

- Commercial use and copyright ownership.
- Free/no-card/no-signup/no-watermark.
- Supported file formats.
- Output examples.
- Ratings, case studies, benchmarks.
- Clear limitations and "who should skip this".

Lovart gap: where we lack hard metrics, use honest workflow proof, real examples, and concrete output specs instead of large claims.

中文：如果 Lovart 暂时没有硬指标，就要用诚实的工作流证明、真实示例和具体输出规格替代大词。

### 6.4 Selection Frameworks

Content competitors win by helping users choose.

中文：内容型竞品的强项是帮用户做选择，而不是只说“我最好”。

Examples:

- Runway vs Pika vs Kling: quality vs value vs social-first.
- AdCreative vs Canva: performance volume vs design control.
- Canva alternatives: task-specific alternative selection.
- Neolemon: which character consistency method fits which use case.

Lovart should use the same clarity:

中文：Lovart 也应该用同样清晰的选择框架：

- Choose Canva for templates.
- Choose Looka for logo-only workflows.
- Choose AdCreative for performance banner volume.
- Choose Runway/Kling for standalone video generation.
- Choose Lovart for brief-driven, editable, multi-asset campaign creation.

### 6.5 Page Type Coverage

Competitors cover multiple page types:

中文：竞品不是只做一种页面，而是覆盖多个页面类型：

- Tool landing pages for direct tasks.
- Best-of pages for evaluation queries.
- Versus/alternative pages for switching queries.
- Deep guides for technical/quality problems.
- Hub pages for broad categories.

Lovart needs a page system, not isolated landing pages.

中文：Lovart 需要的是页面系统，而不是孤立 landing pages。

## 7. Recommended Lovart Competitive Position

Lovart should not primarily compete as:

中文：Lovart 不应该主要把自己定义为：

- another AI image generator;
- another AI logo maker;
- another video generator;
- another template editor.

Lovart should compete as:

中文：Lovart 更应该竞争的位置是：

- AI design agent;
- brief-to-campaign design workflow;
- AI commercial/ad/brand video generator with editable campaign continuity;
- logo + brand kit + launch assets generator;
- multi-format creative system;
- brand/character/style consistency layer across assets.

The immediate best battlefield is not the broadest term. It is the commercial workflow cluster:

中文：近期最值得优先打的不是最大泛词，而是商业工作流词簇：

1. `ai commercial generator`
2. `brand video maker`
3. `ai ad generator`
4. `marketing video ai`
5. `product video`
6. `ai logo generator` only if framed as brand system, not logo-only
7. `ai design agent` as category education and proof

- 下一版应使用固定 SERP runner 或 API，并记录 location/device/time，把抽样升级为可复现月度 benchmark。

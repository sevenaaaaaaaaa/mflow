# Product Mockups Without Photoshop: Step-by-Step AI Workflow (2026)

You do not need a PSD temple to ship a credible product mockup. You need truth refs, Brand Kit rules, a shot list, and the discipline to Touch Edit instead of gambling seeds. This is a step-by-step Lovart workflow for packaging, device, apparel, and shelf mockups that hold up on PDP and ads—without opening Photoshop for every request.

## Who this is for

Ecommerce operators, freelancers, brand designers escaping layer soup, marketers who need weekly displays.

## When Photoshop (or similar) still wins

Print dielines with trap/bleed precision, heavy compositing of many photographed SKUs, pixel-perfect pack engineering. Everything else—lifestyle displays, ad mock scenes, concept shelves—can live on an agent board.

## Steps (print this)

### Step 1 — Define the mockup job
Pack shot in hand · shelf · device screen · apparel on body · box on desk. One job per board column.

### Step 2 — Gather truth
Product photos, logo SVG/PNG, hex tokens, forbidden claims. Label what must not invent.

### Step 3 — Load Brand Kit
Colors, type roles, logo clear space. Empty type bands if copy will change.

### Step 4 — MCoT shot list
Hero mock, alternate angle, detail, optional lifestyle. Max takes per shot.

### Step 5 — Generate in ChatCanvas
Attach refs. Identity Lock product when continuity matters. State forbids: melted logos, wrong proportions, fake seals.

### Step 6 — Soft-proof
Phone size, PDP zoom, ad crop. If the label dies, the mockup fails.

### Step 7 — Touch Edit / Edit Elements
Fix local issues. Do not regen for dust.

### Step 8 — Ship & archive
Name winners. Autopsy losers. Export sizes.

## Proportion honesty

AI loves heroic scale lies. Include a hand or known object when scale matters. Clients who sell physical goods get angry at fantasy sizes.

## Logo on product

Prefer real logo placement from refs. Invented letterforms on packs are a lawsuit hobby. When exploring new packs, label EXPLORE.

## Flop diary

### Flop: chrome pudding device
Phone mock looked liquid. Fix: stricter material forbids + refs.

### Flop: wrong SKU cousin
Twelve ads, drifting bottle. Fix: Identity Lock + silhouette score.

### Flop: baked promo type
Sale ended; pixels lied. Fix: empty bands.

## FAQ

### Did AI make design cost drop by a fixed percent?
No honest operator quotes a universal percentage. Unit economics depend on redo rates, kit memory, wages, and channel QA—not sampler demos.

### Can I skip Photoshop forever for mockups?
For many ecommerce/mock scenes, yes—with Brand Kit, refs, and local edits. Complex compositing and print prepress may still need dedicated tools.

### What makes a weekly digest worth reading?
Decisions, flops, kit changes, and links to working boards—not a laundry list of vague “new features” without proof.

### Where do I start in Lovart?
Brand Kit → MCoT plan → ChatCanvas → soft-proof → Touch Edit → lock winners.

### Should digests invent launch notes?
Never. If you lack verified release notes, write operator lessons and label speculation.

### Try a Quickstart Project

https://www.lovart.ai/docs/getting-started/things-you-should-set-up-first



> Run a ready-made preset and see Lovart in action without writing a prompt.

The fastest way to see what Lovart can do is to run one of the ready-made quickstart projects on your homepage. Click one, hit generate, and watch a finished asset land on the Canvas. No prompt writing required.

**How to Use**

1. **Go to Lovart homepage.** The quickstart projects sit right under the input box, grouped by category (Design, Branding, E-Commerce, Video, and more).
2. **Pick a project.** Lovart loads the preset with prompt, model, and reference files.
3. **Click Generate.** Lovart will automatically open a new project and run the prompt.
4. **Collect the product.** Wait for the product to appear on the Canvas. From there, download your asset or keep chatting with the Agent to iterate.

**Quick Tips**

- Quickstart projects need available credits to run. Top up or switch the model if a run fails.

### Generate Your First Image

> Prompt our Agent to create on-brand assets for you on the infinite canvas.

**How to Use**

1. **Open a new project.**
    - From the Lovart homepage, click New Project to open a blank Canvas.
2. **Type your prompt in the input box.** Describe what you want in plain language. Here is an example:
    - `a fluffy orange tabby cat wearing a tiny business suit, sitting at a desk in front of a laptop, looking extremely concerned, office lighting, shallow depth of field, shot on iPhone`
3. **Send the prompt.**
    - Press enter or click send. Lovart processes your intent and starts generating.
4. **Review the result on the Canvas.**

### Edit Your First Image

> Use Lovart's editing tools to refine the image you just generated.

**How to Use**

1. **Select the original image.**
    - Click the image you just generated on the Canvas.
2. **Ask the Agent to edit using natural language.**
    - The selected image appears in the input box. Click inside the input box to confirm.
    - Then type what you'd like to edit. For example, you can ask the Agent to `"swap the background to pure white"` or `"change the cat into a dog"`.
    - The Agent returns a new image with the edits applied.
3. **Use our one-click editing presets.**
    - Select an image on the Canvas to reveal the editing tools in the top bar.
    - Click Upscale to boost resolution. No prompt needed.
    - See [Basic AI Editing](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-ai-editing) for the full set.
4. **Make a precise tweak.**
    - Click Mark in the Canvas toolbar, press M, or hold Cmd/Ctrl and click the spot you want to change.
    - The marked region appears in the input box. Click inside the input box to confirm.
    - Then type what you'd like to edit.
    - The Agent returns a new image with the edits applied.
    - See [Advanced AI Editing](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/advanced-ai-editing) for more precision tools.
5. **Transform the content.**
    - Change object angles: Select an image on the Canvas, then click Multi-Angles in the top bar to open the control panel.
    - Drag the Subject or Camera controls, then click Run to generate an image of the same object from a new angle.
    - See [AI Transformation](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/ai-transformation) for more tools.

### Turn Your Image Into a Video

> Animate your still image into a short video.

**How to Use**

1. **Select the original image.**
    - Click the image you just generated on the Canvas.
2. **Ask the Agent to animate it.**
    - The selected image appears in the input box. Click inside the input box to confirm.
    - Add any video description you want. For example, you can ask the Agent to `"turn this image into a 5-second video."`
3. **The Agent will animate the image with sound.**
    - Play it with sound on!

### Export and Publish

> Download your finished work or share it with the Lovart community.

**How to Use**

1. **Export a single asset.** Click any item on the Canvas, then click the export button.
2. **Export multiple assets at once.** Drag to select multiple items on the Canvas, then click the export button. PSD is supported for batch export.
3. **Publish to the community feed.** Click the publish button on the Canvas to post your work to the Lovart community feed.

Jump to [Export Formats](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/export-formats) for full export format details.

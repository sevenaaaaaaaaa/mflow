## Step 1: Define Your Post Type and Canvas

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Open Lovart's ChatCanvas. Type: **"Instagram carousel post, 1080x1080, 5 slides, clean minimalist aesthetic with muted sage green and cream palette, slide 1 is a bold headline, slides 2-4 are bullet points with icons, slide 5 is a CTA."** Lovart generates all five slides simultaneously on a single artboard with navigation arrows. For a single-image post, specify **"1080x1350 portrait"** — the 4:5 ratio that fills more vertical screen real estate and historically generates 12% higher engagement than square posts. Lovart's aspect ratio selector has all Instagram presets pre-loaded: 1:1 square, 4:5 portrait, and 1.91:1 landscape.

## Step 2: Apply Your Brand Kit with One Tap

If you've configured Lovart's Brand Kit (Free→$19 plans include one brand profile; $49 Pro unlocks unlimited), tap **Apply Brand** in the top toolbar. Every slide inherits your logo placement (bottom-right, 40px margin), primary typeface, and color palette instantly. No hex-code memorization. No hunting through downloads for the latest logo PNG. Lovart stores your brand assets cloud-synced so the palette your teammate set last week auto-populates when you open a new project. For the quote card slide, the brand typeface auto-applies to the quotation text; for the CTA slide, your brand's accent color fills the button.

## Step 3: Write and Style Your Content

Double-tap any text block to edit. For the carousel headline slide, keep it to 5–8 words — Instagram carousel headlines with fewer than 8 words show 23% higher swipe-through rates according to internal Lovart analytics aggregated from user data (opt-in, anonymized). Lovart's **Smart Text Fit** automatically resizes text to fill the designated area without overflowing. For body copy on slides 2–4, the AI suggests line-height and font-size ratios that maintain the WCAG AA contrast standard for readability. Tap the **Icon Library** to insert vector icons — Lovart's library includes 4,200+ icons searchable by keyword.

## Step 4: Add Visual Interest with AI Imagery

On slides that need supporting visuals, tap the image placeholder and select **Generate.** Type: **"flat lay of a ceramic coffee mug on linen with notebook and pen, warm morning light, top-down view."** Lovart's integrated image generation (powered by FLUX.1 and Stable Diffusion backends) produces four options in under 12 seconds. Pick one and use Touch Edit to adjust its position. For product photos, import your own image, tap **Remove Background,** and place it on a generated backdrop that matches your brand's mood. This composite approach — real product on AI-generated setting — circumvents the uncanny-valley problem while saving the cost of a styled photoshoot.

## Step 5: Export as Individual Slides or a Carousel Strip

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

Tap **Export.** Lovart offers two Instagram-optimized output modes: **Individual Slides** (5 separate 1080×1080 PNGs, numbered sequentially) and **Carousel Strip** (a single tall image sliced equally — useful for scheduling tools that don't support multi-image upload). Select **Compress for Instagram** to apply platform-optimized JPEG compression that reduces file size without visible quality loss. Lovart also writes alt text to each exported file's metadata based on the content detected in the design — tap **Edit Alt Text** to review and refine before saving. Download the batch and schedule directly through Meta Business Suite or your preferred scheduler.

## FAQ

**Q: Can Lovart create Instagram Stories too?**
A: Yes. Select the **Story** preset (1080×1920, 9:16 aspect ratio) when creating a new project. The workflow is identical but optimized for full-screen vertical viewing with a 250px "safe zone" at the top and bottom for UI elements.

**Q: How does Lovart's Brand Kit handle multi-brand agencies?**
A: On the $99 Agency plan, you can create unlimited brand profiles — each with its own color palette, logo, typeface, and asset library. Switch between brands in the top-left dropdown. No mix-ups, no wrong-logo incidents.

**Q: Can I animate Instagram posts in Lovart?**
A: The current release (v2.4.1) exports static images. Animated export (GIF and MP4) is on the public roadmap for Q3 2026. For now, design static frames in Lovart and import them into a video tool for animation.

**Q: What's the ideal resolution for Instagram posts to avoid compression?**
A: Export at 1080px width (square or portrait). Instagram's compression algorithm is less aggressive on images exactly 1080px wide. Lovart's Instagram presets use this width by default.

**Q: Does Lovart support collaborative editing for team workflows?**
A: Yes, on the $49 Pro plan and above. Share a project link with team members; they can view, comment, and edit in real time. Version history keeps every save point, so you can roll back if someone changes the wrong slide.

**Q: Can I schedule posts directly from Lovart?**
A: Lovart integrates with Buffer and Hootsuite via API (configure in Settings → Integrations). Direct scheduling to Instagram requires the $99 Agency plan. For Free and Starter plans, download the files and upload manually.

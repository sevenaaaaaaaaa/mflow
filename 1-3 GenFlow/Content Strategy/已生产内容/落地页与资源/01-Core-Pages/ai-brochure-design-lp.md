## What Lovart's Brochure AI Generates

### Multi-Page Document Design

Brochures are not single pages — they are documents with flow, pacing, and narrative architecture. Lovart designs across spreads: cover, inside panels, back cover. Each page transitions naturally to the next. The AI maintains consistent margins, column structures, and visual rhythm throughout.

### Layout Types

**Bi-Fold:** Four panels (front cover, back cover, two inside spreads). The standard for corporate capability brochures, product catalogs, and service overviews.

**Tri-Fold:** Six panels in a classic letter-fold format. The workhorse of trade shows, direct mail, and reception-area displays.

**Multi-Page Booklet:** 8, 12, 16, or more pages. Perfect for annual reports, lookbooks, portfolio pieces, and detailed product guides.

**Z-Fold and Gate-Fold:** Accordion-style layouts for event programs, menus, and creative portfolios.

### Content-Aware Styling

Lovart reads your content and applies styling that matches the context. A law firm brochure gets confident serif typography, structured grids, and restrained color — nothing flashy. A music festival brochure gets bold display fonts, high-energy color, and dynamic image placement. A SaaS product brochure gets clean sans-serif typography, generous whitespace, and feature callout boxes. The AI understands genre conventions and applies them automatically — or breaks them when you ask.

### Brand Kit Integration

Upload your brand kit — logo, colors, fonts — and every brochure Lovart generates matches your existing brand identity. This is not optional for businesses with established brands. A brochure that looks like a different company confuses prospects and undermines the trust your brand has already built.

## How It Works

**Step 1 — Describe the Brochure.** Give Lovart the context: what your business does, who the brochure is for, what you want it to accomplish. Example: *A 6-page tri-fold brochure for a boutique real estate agency targeting luxury home buyers. Elegant, understated. Showcase three featured properties with photography. Include agent bio and contact.* 

**Step 2 — Provide Content.** Paste your text, upload images, or let Lovart generate placeholder copy you can replace later. The AI structures headlines, body text, callout quotes, and captions following publishing conventions.

**Step 3 — Choose Layout and Style.** Select brochure format (bi-fold, tri-fold, booklet), page count, and visual style. Lovart generates a complete design in under 60 seconds.

**Step 4 — Touch Edit and Finalize.** Refine any spread. Touch Edit lets you adjust individual elements — swap an image, rephrase a headline, change a color, reposition a module — without rebuilding the entire document.

**Step 5 — Export Print-Ready.** Download as PDF/X-4 with bleed, crop marks, and 300 DPI resolution. Upload directly to any commercial printer.

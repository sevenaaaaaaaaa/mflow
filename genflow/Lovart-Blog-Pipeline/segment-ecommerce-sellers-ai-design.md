E-commerce runs on product imagery. A listing with professional photos converts 2-3x better than one with amateur shots. But professional product photography costs $50-200 per SKU. With 50 products, that is $2,500-10,000 before you sell a single unit.

Lovart generates product photography, lifestyle imagery, and platform-optimized listing visuals from text descriptions.

**Product photography (white background):** *"Amazon product photo. White infinity background. A stainless steel water bottle, standing upright, centered. Studio lighting from above at 45 degrees — soft shadow on the ground plane. Nano Banana Pro for photorealism. The bottle's metallic surface should reflect light naturally. 2000×2000px, Amazon-ready."*

**Lifestyle imagery:** *"Lifestyle product photo. The same water bottle from the canvas. On a hiking trail. Golden hour light. A hiker's hand reaching for the bottle — just the hand, not the full person. The bottle should appear as the hero, with the trail as aspirational context. Editorial adventure aesthetic."*

**A+ Content / EBC:** *"Amazon A+ Content module. 'Why Our Bottle Keeps Water Cold for 36 Hours.' Split layout: left side infographic showing insulation layers, right side lifestyle image. Brand Kit colors. Clean, informative, trustworthy."*

**Multi-platform variants:** Use the same product asset (isolated via Edit Elements) to generate platform-specific variants:
- Amazon: white background, 2000×2000, infographic-style secondary images
- Shopify: lifestyle hero, color variants, size comparison
- Etsy: warm, handcrafted aesthetic, shop branding included

# Lovart Best Practice Benchmark Seed V3

Purpose: close the remaining Best Practice gaps around follow-up discipline, knowledge hygiene, and governed prompt operations.

## Why this category needed V3

Best Practice articles were still under-covered in two ways:

- too many samples taught setup, not ongoing maintenance
- too few samples showed what happens after critique, review, or prompt reuse at scale

## Source-use notes

- NN/g is useful here for operational follow-through, not just design theory
- Glean and Ergonis help model shared prompt-governance behavior
- Atlassian is useful for ritual design even when not design-specific

## Final category lesson

Strong Best Practice writing does not stop at "here is the workflow." It also explains how the workflow stays trustworthy after the first week.

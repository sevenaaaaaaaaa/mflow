# Best User Designs of 2027: Lovart Annual Awards Announcement

Three years ago, Lovart shipped its first public build — a simple ChatCanvas where you could describe a design and watch AI generate it. Today, our community spans 180 countries. Over 4.2 million designs were created on Lovart in 2027 alone. Some were quick social posts. Some were full brand identities. Some were packaging that ended up on retail shelves. Some were billboards on highways.

The Lovart 2027 Annual Design Awards exist to celebrate the very best of that output — the designs that pushed the tool further than we imagined, that achieved real-world impact, and that made us stop and say, "Wait, someone made *that* in Lovart?"

## Award Categories

This year we are introducing six award categories, each judged by a panel of design industry professionals, Lovart product leads, and — new for 2027 — community votes.

### 1. Best Brand Identity Created with Lovart
For the most cohesive, memorable, and professionally executed brand system built entirely or primarily in Lovart. Includes logo, color palette, typography system, and at least three applied examples (e.g., business card, social template, packaging). This category has historically been won by startups and small businesses launching their first visual identity.

**2026 Winner:** *Wilder Botanics* — a small-batch skincare brand that built their entire visual identity (logo, packaging, website assets, social templates) over a single weekend in Lovart.

### 2. Best Social Media Campaign
For the most effective multi-platform social media campaign designed with Lovart. Judged on visual consistency across platforms, creative use of Lovart's animated export features, and demonstrated engagement results. Submissions must include analytics data.

**2026 Winner:** *Coastline Coffee's Summer Series* — 42 platform-specific assets across Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest, averaging 4× the engagement of their previous agency-designed campaigns.

### 3. Best Print & Merch Design
For the most impressive physical-world design created in Lovart — packaging, apparel, event signage, billboards, book covers, album art. This category celebrates the bridge from digital canvas to tangible object.

**2026 Winner:** *Neon Nights Music Festival* — a complete festival branding suite including stage backdrops (40ft wide), artist lanyards, merchandise, and a 120-page printed program, all designed by a two-person marketing team.

### 4. Best AI + Human Collaboration
This category specifically honors workflows where Lovart was used in collaboration with human designers — where AI accelerated the process without replacing the craft. Submissions should describe the workflow: what the AI did, what the human did, and how the combination produced something neither could alone.

**New category for 2027.**

### 5. Best Nonprofit / Social Impact Design
For the most impactful design work created for a nonprofit organization, community group, or social cause using Lovart. We waive all subscription fees for verified nonprofit organizations, and this category highlights what mission-driven teams can accomplish with AI design tools.

**2026 Winner:** *Community Food Share* — a regional food bank that used Lovart to redesign their donor communications, volunteer recruitment materials, and annual report, resulting in a 27% increase in recurring donations.

### 6. Community Choice Award
Open public vote across all submissions. The community decides. No judge panel — just pure crowd recognition. Voting opens November 1 and runs through November 15.

## Prizes

We have significantly expanded the prize pool for 2027:

- **Grand Prize (across all categories):** $5,000 cash, a lifetime Lovart Agency subscription, featured interview on the Lovart Blog and Podcast, and a dedicated showcase page on lovart.ai/awards.
- **Category Winners (6):** $1,000 cash each, one year of Lovart Agency, and featured in the Lovart 2027 Year in Review report.
- **Honorable Mentions (12):** $250 Lovart credit and inclusion in the Community Year-End Showcase (December 2027).
- **Community Choice:** $2,000 cash and a trophy shipped to your door — because sometimes physical objects still matter.

## Judging Criteria

All submissions are evaluated on four dimensions:

1. **Visual Quality (30%):** Composition, color, typography, and overall aesthetic execution. Is this design genuinely good — not just good for AI, but good by any standard?
2. **Impact (25%):** Measurable results. Did the design drive engagement, conversions, donations, foot traffic, press coverage, or brand recognition?
3. **Creative Use of Lovart (25%):** How skillfully did the creator leverage ChatCanvas, Touch Edit, Brand Kit, Animated Export, and other Lovart features? We want to see people using the tool in ways we never documented.
4. **Story (20%):** Who made this, and why? We care about the context, the constraints, the stakes. The most memorable designs almost always come with the most compelling stories.

## How to Enter

Submissions open **October 8, 2027** and close **October 31, 2027 at 11:59 PM UTC**. To enter:

1. Export your design(s) from Lovart or take screenshots of your ChatCanvas compositions.
2. Write a brief submission (300–500 words) describing the project, your process, and — if relevant — the results.
3. Include which Lovart features you used and, for the AI + Human Collaboration category, describe the human/AI division of labor.
4. Submit via the [Lovart Awards Portal](https://lovart.ai/awards/submit) or post on X/Twitter with the hashtag `#LovartAwards2027`.

You may enter up to three projects across any combination of categories. There is no entry fee. Lovart subscription is not required — Free tier designs are eligible. (Though we suspect the Premium and Agency features make a difference.)

## Why We Do This

Design awards can feel self-congratulatory. We are aware of that. But the Lovart Awards serve a different purpose. They exist to show the world what is possible when you remove the technical barriers between an idea and its visual expression. Every winning design is proof that great visual communication is not about software proficiency — it is about clarity of intent, taste, and the courage to ship.

The 4.2 million designs created on Lovart this year were not made by professional designers alone. They were made by founders, marketers, nonprofit directors, restaurant managers, event organizers, teachers, students, and people who just had an idea and wanted to see what it looked like. The Awards exist to surface the moments where that impulse produced something extraordinary.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Should my brand feel like a spa or a clinic?**
It depends on your specialty. Relaxation-focused therapists should lean spa (soft, warm, serene). Injury-recovery and sports massage therapists should lean clinical-professional (clean, structured, credential-forward). Most therapists blend both — calming colors with clear expertise signals.

**Q2: What's the best way to display pricing without seeming salesy?**
Use "Investment" instead of "Price" as the column header. List services by duration rather than cost hierarchy. Use your body font in a lighter weight for prices. Lovart's service menu templates use this psychology-driven layout.

**Q3: How do I brand my intake forms?**
Brand the header (logo, practice name) but keep form fields clean and spacious. Crowded forms create anxiety before the session begins. Use generous line spacing and comfortable font sizes. Lovart's intake form templates balance branding with usability.

**Q4: What photography style works for massage therapy?**
Soft, natural light. Avoid harsh studio lighting. Photograph hands (resting, not working), linens (crisp folds), and details (oil bottles, warm towels, stones). Show the environment, not the massage itself — implied relaxation is more powerful than literal depiction.

**Q5: How do I encourage repeat bookings through branding?**
Create a "Journey" visual system: Session 1 (Discovery), Sessions 2–4 (Progress), Sessions 5+ (Maintenance). Design a loyalty card or membership card with progression markers. Branded reminder emails should feel like gentle nudges, not sales pitches.

**Q6: Should I create a separate brand for my specialty modalities (prenatal, sports, etc.)?**
Sub-brand rather than separate brand. Use the same logo but create modality-specific color variants and icon systems. Prenatal = soft pink overlay, Sports = energetic orange accent. Lovart generates sub-brand variants from your core kit.

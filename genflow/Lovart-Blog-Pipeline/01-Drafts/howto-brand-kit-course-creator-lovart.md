# Brand Kit for Course Creators: Thumbnails, Lessons & Sales Continuity

Course businesses look scammy when every module thumbnail is a different universe: neon guru one week, marble luxury the next, three faces of the same instructor on the sales page. A Brand Kit is how you ship forty lessons without forty brands. This playbook covers thumbnails, lesson headers, sales heroes, instructor portrait rules, and VA handoff in Lovart—without fake income screenshots or invented credentials.

## Who this is for

Solo creators, cohort-based schools, edtech marketers, freelancers packaging creator kits.

## Kit contents

Logo lockups · color/type roles · thumbnail grid recipe (face + topic band) · lesson header template · sales page hero with empty claim bands · instructor Identity Lock · forbids (fake earnings, fake logos, cousin faces) · platform size notes (YouTube, Teachable, Gumroad)

## Anti-hype forbids

Invented revenue screenshots · fake “as seen on” logos · stock guru mansions · misleading before/after learning claims · unreadable thumbnail type

## Weekly rhythm

Batch thumbnails Monday · lesson headers Tuesday · sales variants Wednesday · soft-proof Thursday · VA handoff Friday

## Flop diary

### Flop: thumbnail soup
Every video looked unrelated. Fix: grid recipe.

### Flop: instructor cousin
Three portraits on one page. Fix: Identity Lock.

### Flop: baked guarantee text
Policy changed; pixels lied. Fix: empty bands + live text overlay.

## FAQ

### Can I run TikTok ads without Photoshop?
Yes for many static and keyframe-led workflows if you use Brand Kit templates, safe zones, empty offer bands, and soft-proof on vertical mobile.

### What belongs in a course creator Brand Kit?
Thumbnail recipe, lesson header system, sales page heroes, instructor portrait rules, forbids, and a board a VA can continue.

### How do I keep AI characters consistent?
Model sheet + refs + Identity Lock + marking checklist + stop rules + Touch Edit before regen.

### Where do I start in Lovart?
Brand Kit → MCoT → ChatCanvas → soft-proof → Touch Edit → lock winners → cold handoff test.

### Will Identity Lock fix bad briefs?
No. It holds identity when briefs are clear; garbage briefs still produce garbage cousins.

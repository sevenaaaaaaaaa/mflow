## Step 1: Start with the Correct Ad Specs

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Open Lovart and type: **"Facebook feed ad, single image, 1080x1080, with ad-spec-compliant layout. Text overlay area in the upper third, product image in the center, CTA-safe zone at the bottom."** Lovart's ad creative engine knows Facebook's 20% text rule is no longer enforced but still penalizes creatives that are text-wall heavy in the algorithm's quality ranking. It generates layouts with a 60:40 visual-to-text ratio — the sweet spot identified in Meta's 2025 Creative Guidance for direct-response ads. For carousel ads, specify **"5-card carousel, 1080x1080 each"** and Lovart generates all cards with consistent spacing and layout logic.

## Step 2: Design the Visual Hook (First 0.5 Seconds)

Facebook users scroll at approximately 400px/second on mobile. Your ad has roughly 0.5 seconds to earn a pause. Lovart's **Attention Heatmap** overlay — toggled from the View menu — predicts visual attention using a saliency model trained on 2.4 million ad impressions. Areas in red denote predicted high-attention zones; blue areas will be ignored. Position your hero visual (product, UGC photo, lifestyle shot) in a heatmap hotspot. Use Touch Edit to mask the product onto a lifestyle background: import your product shot, tap **Remove Background,** and place it on a generated scene context like **"messy desk setup with coffee, natural window light."**

## Step 3: Write and Place Ad Copy — the Right Way

Tap the text zone. Facebook primary text (the copy above the image) lives in Ads Manager, not on the creative itself. But headline overlay on the image itself — 3 to 6 words maximum — remains critical because it's the only text visible when the ad collapses into a thumbnail. Write a direct-benefit headline: **"Stop Losing Leads"** not **"Our Platform Offers Lead Generation Capabilities."** Lovart's **Compliance Checker** (enabled in Settings → Ad Compliance) flags text that exceeds 20% surface area and warns if your headline uses restricted terms based on your selected vertical (finance, health, politics).

## Step 4: Add Social Proof and Urgency Elements

Direct-response ads convert better with visual trust signals. Tap **Elements → Badges** and add a star-rating badge to the upper-right corner: "4.8 ★★★★★" with 14pt text. Add a subtle "Limited Time" banner strip — 40px tall, semi-transparent brand accent color, positioned along the top edge — but only if the offer genuinely expires. Facebook's ad review increasingly penalizes fake urgency. Lovart's element library includes pre-designed badge components that render natively at ad resolution without pixelation. For e-commerce, add a "Best Seller" ribbon or a "X,000+ Sold" counter badge.

## Step 5: Export in Ad-Ready Formats and Create Variants

## FAQ

**Q: What Facebook ad placements does Lovart support?**
A: Lovart generates creatives sized for Feed (1080×1080, 1080×1350), Stories (1080×1920), Audience Network (1200×628), and Marketplace (1080×1080). For Reels placement, use the Reels cover workflow with the 9:16 ratio.

**Q: Does Lovart's 20% text rule checker still matter in 2026?**
A: Facebook removed the strict 20% text rule in 2020 but algorithmically deprioritizes text-heavy images in auctions. Lovart's Compliance Checker flags excessive text and recommends reductions, which improves your ad's quality ranking and lowers CPM.

**Q: Can I use Lovart for video ad creative, not just static images?**
A: Lovart's current release focuses on static image and carousel ad design. Video ad template support is on the Q3 2026 public roadmap. For now, use Lovart to design video thumbnail frames and end cards.

**Q: How do Lovart's Ad Export variants work with Facebook's Dynamic Creative?**
A: The variant CSV maps each creative version to Facebook's dynamic creative fields (headline, primary text, image). Upload the CSV + ZIP into Facebook Ads Manager's Asset Library, and Facebook automatically assembles the combinations for Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO).

**Q: Can multiple team members collaborate on ad creatives?**
A: Yes, on the $99 Agency plan. Team members can comment on specific ad elements, suggest copy changes, and approve creatives before export. The approval workflow logs all changes and sign-offs for compliance audits.

**Q: Does Lovart store my ad creative data?**
A: Lovart stores project files in your cloud workspace for 90 days on Free, indefinitely on paid plans. No ad creative data is used for model training unless you opt into the "Improve Lovart" program in Privacy Settings.

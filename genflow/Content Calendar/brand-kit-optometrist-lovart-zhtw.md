## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

A successful optometry brand balances medical authority with warmth. Clinical sterility scares patients; too much whimsy undermines trust.

- **Primary**: `#2C7DA0` — Calm teal-blue evoking clarity, vision, and the trusted blue of medical scrubs
- **Secondary**: `#E8F4F8` — Airy ice-blue for backgrounds, appointment cards, and negative space
- **Accent**: `#1A5276` — Deep navy for headlines, CTAs, and logo typography
- **Neutral**: `#F5F9FA` — Off-white for readability on screens and print
- **Contrast**: `#E76F51` — Warm coral sparingly used for "Book Now" buttons and urgent CTAs

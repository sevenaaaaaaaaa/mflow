## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Should my studio brand feel like a nightclub or a gym?**
Most successful studios blend both — dark, high-energy aesthetic as nightclub with professional, results-driven messaging as gym. The visual brand says party while the content says transformation.

**Q2: What is the best way to brand my class names?**
Create a naming convention — Rhythm, Power, Endurance, Recovery — and design a visual system for each. Color-code class types on the schedule. Lovart's class card system makes schedule navigation intuitive.

**Q3: How do I make my studio Instagram-worthy?**
Design a photo-op wall with a neon sign featuring your brand phrase. Brand the bike setup so the logo faces the mirror for selfies. Create post-ride photo templates with ride stats overlay. Instagram drives cycling studio discovery.

**Q4: What membership materials should I brand?**
Membership card — physical or digital wallet pass — welcome kit with branded towel and water bottle, and monthly statement emails. Every billing touchpoint should feel premium as it reduces churn psychology.

**Q5: Should I brand my instructor team?**
Yes — but as a unified squad under your studio brand. Create instructor bio templates with consistent photography style. Instructors build personal followings that drive attendance. Balance personal brand with studio identity.

**Q6: What is the best retail strategy for a cycling studio?**
Branded apparel — tanks, leggings, sports bras — in limited drops. Create hype through scarcity. Use studio templates for new drop announcements. Retail can contribute 10 to 15 percent of revenue with strong branding.

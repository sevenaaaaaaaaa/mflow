## What Makes These Different from Canva Templates

Canva templates are static. You pick one, you drag your content into fixed slots, you live with whatever the template designer decided. If the template has a headline at 36pt and you need 48pt, you manually adjust. If the template uses orange and your brand is blue, you manually recolor every element.

Lovart templates are **AI-adaptive.** When you apply a template, the AI reads your Brand Kit and auto-applies your colors, fonts, and logo placement. When you want to change the layout — "move the headline below the image" or "add a testimonial section" — you type it in ChatCanvas and the AI rebuilds the template around your request. The template is a starting point, not a cage.

That's the difference between a template library and an AI template library. One gives you fixed furniture. The other gives you an interior designer who rearranges the furniture to fit your space.

**Los Angeles, CA — October 13, 2025** — Lovart, the San Francisco–based AI startup pioneering agentic design, partnered with **EchoHer** to host an intimate *Women Founders, Funders & Creatives Coffee* during a16z’s LA Tech Week. The morning gathering welcomed more than 200 guests — many of them women leading teams, startups, and creative ventures — for open conversations around innovation, design, and how AI can amplify diverse voices in creation.

Lovart’s Chief Strategy Officer **Sylvia Liu** shared practical insights into how AI is reshaping the creative process. Building on her talk from the previous week at SF Tech Week, Liu highlighted how **Lovart’s ChatCanvas** leverages **spatial intelligence** to understand layout, hierarchy, and visual relationships — allowing users to iterate directly on an infinite canvas through natural conversation. “We’re not just generating images,” Liu noted. “ChatCanvas learns from context and intent, so founders and designers can co-create dynamic, production-ready visuals within minutes.” The **Santa Monica** event celebrated collaboration — between people, ideas, and technologies — and showcased how women are redefining creativity in the AI era. Guests exchanged insights on faster brand building, storytelling, and design scalability, while exploring how Lovart’s **Design Agent** can streamline workflows across campaign ideation, visual generation, and asset organization. The morning closed on an optimistic note: a shared belief that creativity, when made more accessible, multiplies opportunity for all.

**About Lovart**

Lovart is a San Francisco–based AI company pioneering the **Design Agent** — a conversational, multimodal creative AI that transforms a single prompt into cohesive, editable assets, including images, video, audio, storyboards, brand kits, and 3D content, all within a unified infinite canvas.

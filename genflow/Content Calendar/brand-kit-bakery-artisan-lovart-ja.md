## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: What's the most important packaging element for an artisan bakery?**
The bread bag or paper wrap — it's what customers carry through the neighborhood, effectively becoming walking billboards. A custom stamp, sticker, or printed paper band transforms generic packaging into mobile marketing. Lovart generates stamp and sticker die-line templates.

**Q2: Should my brand feel rustic or polished?**
Artisan bakeries benefit from "refined rustic" — organic textures and warm colors executed with professional consistency. Avoid actual "messy" design that looks like a DIY job. Professional photography with flour dust on a marble counter reads differently than a poorly lit phone photo of the same scene.

**Q3: How do I brand my bread scoring patterns?**
Many artisan bakeries develop a signature scoring pattern that becomes a de facto logo. Lovart's brand kit can incorporate your scoring pattern as a secondary graphic element across packaging and social templates.

**Q4: What photography style works for artisan bread?**
Natural window light, slightly directional from the side to emphasize crust texture. Crumb shots are essential — they prove quality. Always shoot on dark surfaces (slate, aged wood) that contrast with the flour-dusted exterior.

**Q5: How do I handle seasonal pastry branding?**
Create a seasonal overlay system: your core brand stays constant, but accent colors and illustrated elements rotate. Pumpkin illustration + burnt orange for autumn; cherry blossom + pale pink for spring. Lovart's seasonal template pack makes these swaps in one click.

**Q6: Do I need a separate brand for wholesale vs. retail?**
Not separate, but differentiated. Your retail brand is the full experience; your wholesale sub-brand (for restaurant partners) can be a simplified version — logo + stamp on bulk boxes. Keep the logo consistent so restaurant diners can find your retail shop.

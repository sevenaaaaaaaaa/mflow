A design on a blank canvas is an abstraction. A design on a real product, in a real environment, in realistic lighting — that is a sale. Mockups bridge the gap between "here is my design" and "here is what it looks like in the world." They are the difference between a client saying "I need to think about it" and "Let's move forward."

Lovart's AI Mockup Generator — powered by the Smart Mockups feature — places any design into photorealistic real-world scenes in seconds. Upload your design. Select a scene. Lovart handles perspective, lighting, shadows, and material realism. What used to require 3D software skills, stock photo subscriptions, and hours of Photoshop compositing now takes a sentence and a click.

## Smart Mockups: How It Works

Smart Mockups is Lovart's dedicated mockup pipeline. Unlike generic image generation, Smart Mockups is engineered specifically for design placement — understanding depth, surface curvature, lighting direction, and material interaction so your design looks like it actually belongs in the scene.

### Step 1: Upload Your Design

Start with any visual asset you want to mock up:
- A logo or brand mark
- A poster, flyer, or banner design
- A t-shirt graphic or apparel print
- Packaging artwork
- App screen or website UI
- Book cover or magazine layout
- Business card design

Supported upload formats: PNG, JPEG, SVG, PSD, AI, PDF. For best results, upload your design with a transparent background if you want it placed on a colored product surface.

### Step 2: Select Your Mockup Scene

Choose from Lovart's growing library of mockup scenes, organized by category:

**Tech and Devices:**
- Smartphone (front, angle, hand-held, on desk)
- Laptop screen (open on desk, coffee shop table, home office)
- Tablet (landscape, portrait, on couch)
- Smartwatch (on wrist, on charger)
- Desktop monitor (office setting, creative studio)

**Apparel and Textiles:**
- T-shirt (flat lay, on model front, on model back, folded)
- Hoodie (front view, on hanger, worn)
- Cap (front, side angle)
- Tote bag (on shoulder, flat lay, hanging)
- Socks, scarves, beanies

**Print and Display:**
- Billboard (urban street, highway, shopping mall)
- Poster frame (wall-mounted, leaning on floor, gallery wall)
- Business card (stack, single on desk, held in hand)
- Flyer/brochure (on counter, in hand, on bulletin board)
- Book cover (hardcover, paperback, open on table)

**Packaging:**
- Box (various sizes, angled view, flat)
- Bottle (glass, plastic, with label)
- Pouch (stand-up, flat lay)
- Jar and tube (cosmetics, food)
- Coffee cup (to-go, ceramic mug)

**Home and Environment:**
- Wall art (living room, bedroom, office, gallery)
- Throw pillow (on couch, on bed)
- Blanket (draped, folded)
- Towel (hanging, folded stack)

### Step 3: AI Places Your Design

This is where Smart Mockups earns the "Smart." Lovart's AI analyzes your uploaded design and the selected scene, then:

- **Maps perspective:** Your design conforms to the surface angle — wrapping around a coffee cup, following the curve of a t-shirt, aligning with a billboard's vanishing point.
- **Matches lighting:** The design inherits the scene's light direction, color temperature, and shadow depth. A billboard in direct sunlight gets appropriately harsh contrast. A phone screen in a dim coffee shop gets ambient glow.
- **Renders material interaction:** Your design on glossy paper reflects light differently than your design on matte cotton. Lovart adjusts specular highlights and texture interaction per material.
- **Preserves detail:** Small text, fine lines, gradients — the details that make your design work are preserved in the mockup at full resolution.

### Step 4: Export

Export your mockup in:
- **PNG/JPEG (digital):** Web-optimized for portfolios, social media, and client presentations
- **PSD (layered):** Editable Photoshop file with your design on a separate layer — make tweaks without regenerating
- **Vector PDF:** For presentation decks and print proposals (selected scenes)
- **Multiple angles:** Export the same design in 3-5 different mockup scenes as a presentation-ready gallery

## Mockup Design Best Practices

### Use High-Resolution Source Files

The Smart Mockups pipeline works best with source files at 2000px or larger on the longest edge. Low-resolution uploads may show pixelation when mapped onto curved surfaces or enlarged scenes. For logos and vector designs, SVG uploads produce the sharpest results.

### Match Design Tone to Scene

A neon event poster looks right on a urban street billboard at night. That same poster on a gallery wall in a bright minimalist room might feel out of place. Select mockup scenes that reinforce your design's intended context.

### Batch Your Mockups

When you have multiple designs to mock up (a brand identity package, a full clothing collection, a product line), use Lovart's batch mockup mode. Upload all your designs, select a scene or scene set, and Lovart generates mockups for every design × scene combination. A collection of 10 t-shirt designs × 3 mockup scenes = 30 mockups in one batch session.

### Export PSD for Client Handoff

The PSD export option is invaluable for client work. Deliver mockups as layered Photoshop files so your client can make minor text or color adjustments without coming back to you — or so their in-house team can extend the mockups to additional scenes.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I use my own mockup scenes or backgrounds?**
A: Yes. Upload your own scene photographs to ChatCanvas, and Lovart places your design into them. This is especially useful for brands that want mockups in their actual office, store, or production environment.

**Q: How realistic are the mockups?**
A: Lovart's Smart Mockups pipeline is designed specifically for realism. Lighting, shadows, perspective, and material rendering are handled by a dedicated model pipeline trained on product photography compositing. For most digital use cases (portfolios, client presentations, social media, ecommerce listings), the output is indistinguishable from photographed mockups.

**Q: Can I generate mockups for products Lovart does not have scenes for?**
A: Yes. Describe your desired mockup scene in natural language: "a recycled paper coffee cup with my logo on it, sitting on a wooden cafe table, morning light from window" — Lovart's general image generation pipeline handles custom scene requests that the Smart Mockups library does not yet cover.

**Q: Are the mockups print-quality?**
A: Digital mockups are generated at screen resolution (72-150 DPI), which is ideal for web and presentation use. For print-quality packaging renders, contact Lovart Enterprise for custom resolution options.

**Q: Can I edit the placement after generation?**
A: Yes. Touch Edit allows you to fine-tune design placement — adjust rotation, scale, position — after the initial mockup is generated. You can also swap the design for a different one without regenerating the scene.

**Q: Do I need to know 3D software?**
A: No. That is the entire point. Smart Mockups replaces the need for Blender, Cinema 4D, Dimension, or any 3D modeling tool for standard mockup use cases.

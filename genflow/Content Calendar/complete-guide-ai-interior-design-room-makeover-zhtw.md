## Questions Nobody Answers

### How does AI interior design actually work?

The process uses several AI technologies in sequence:

1. **Room detection:** AI identifies walls, floor, ceiling, windows, doors, and existing furniture in your uploaded photo.
2. **Depth estimation:** The AI builds a 3D understanding of the space — distances, proportions, and spatial relationships.
3. **Style transfer/generation:** Based on your selected style and the room structure, the AI generates new elements: wall colors, flooring, furniture, decor, lighting.
4. **Compositing:** The generated elements are composited into the original room structure, maintaining correct perspective and scale.

The key insight: AI interior design preserves the room's actual architecture (walls, windows, proportions) while reimagining everything else. It visualizes your actual space, not a generic room that looks vaguely like yours.

### What can AI change in my room photo?

AI can modify:
- **Wall color and finish:** Paint, wallpaper, paneling, tile
- **Flooring:** Hardwood, tile, carpet, concrete, with specific colors and patterns
- **Furniture:** Replace, rearrange, or add furniture in any style
- **Lighting:** Change fixtures, add lamps, adjust light temperature and direction
- **Window treatments:** Curtains, blinds, shades in various styles
- **Decor:** Art, plants, rugs, accessories
- **Architectural details:** Crown molding, baseboards, wainscoting, built-ins
- **Virtual decluttering:** Remove existing furniture and clutter for a clean visualization

AI cannot reliably change: room dimensions, window/door positions, ceiling height, or structural elements. The architecture is the canvas; AI paints on it.

### Which interior design styles does AI handle?

AI generates convincingly in most major styles:
- **Excellent results:** Minimalist, Scandinavian, Mid-Century Modern, Industrial, Contemporary, Japandi, Bohemian
- **Very good results:** Traditional, Farmhouse, Coastal, Art Deco, Hollywood Regency
- **Good results:** Maximalist, Eclectic, Victorian, Gothic, Memphis Design
- **Challenging:** Hyper-specific regional styles (specific to a culture or period with limited training data)

Specify both the macro style and micro details: "Scandinavian interior with warm wood tones, cream walls, textured wool rugs, and ceramic accents" produces better results than just "Scandinavian."

### How accurate are AI room visualizations compared to real-life results?

Excellent for structure, approximate for specific products. The AI gets the "vibe" right — colors, proportions, style coherence. It doesn't (and can't) accurately render the specific sofa you'll buy from Article or the exact paint color from Sherwin-Williams.

AI visualizations are "decision-making quality" — good enough to decide "yes, dark green walls work in this room" or "the sectional should face the window, not the TV." They are not "purchase-order quality" — you still need to verify specific product dimensions and colors before buying.

### Can AI generate a shopping list from the design?

Partially. Lovart's Shop the Look feature identifies furniture and decor types in the generated design and suggests similar real products from retail partners. Accuracy: the AI might show you a "mid-century sofa in navy velvet" and link to real navy velvet sofas, but it won't be the exact sofa from the visualization (which was AI-generated and doesn't exist).

Treat AI shopping suggestions as a starting point, not a purchase list. Use them to understand what kinds of products create the look, then shop intentionally.

### What's virtual staging and how is it different from interior design?

Virtual staging is a subset of AI interior design specifically for real estate. It takes photos of empty (or poorly furnished) rooms and adds realistic furniture and decor to help buyers visualize the space. Key differences:
- **Virtual staging:** Focused on selling. Neutral, broadly appealing style. Emphasizes space and flow. Furniture is aspirational but realistic.
- **Interior design AI:** Focused on personalization. Specific style preferences. May include bold color choices and unconventional layouts.

Lovart offers both modes: Virtual Staging for real estate and Interior Design for personal/homeowner use.

### Can AI handle small or awkwardly shaped rooms?

Yes — AI preserves actual room dimensions. This is actually where AI interior design shines brightest because:
- Awkward rooms benefit from visualization before committing to furniture
- Small rooms benefit from seeing layout options that maximize space
- Unusual proportions (long narrow rooms, rooms with many doors) challenge traditional furniture placement rules that AI can creatively work around

Be specific in your prompt about the challenges: "long narrow living room, 8 feet wide by 20 feet long, need to create two functional zones — TV area and reading nook."

### How does AI interior design handle lighting?

AI generates designs under the lighting conditions of the original photo. If you upload a photo taken in harsh overhead light, the AI design will look harshly lit even with beautiful furniture. If you upload a photo in warm natural light, the AI design inherits that flattering quality.

For best results: take photos in natural daylight with curtains open. Avoid flash photography (creates harsh shadows AI can't fix). Take multiple angles — the AI works best with a clear view of the room.

Lovart includes a virtual relighting feature that can adjust lighting temperature and direction after generation — useful for "what would this room look like at golden hour?"

### Can I see my room in different color schemes?

Yes — color exploration is AI's strongest interior design feature. Upload one photo and generate versions in:
- Different wall colors (the same room in 10 different paint colors)
- Different flooring (hardwood vs. tile vs. carpet)
- Different accent colors (the same neutral base with 5 different accent palettes)
- Seasonal variations (summer vs. winter styling)

This replaces the "buy 10 paint samples and paint swatches on the wall" process with a 2-minute AI session.

### Can AI interior design incorporate my existing furniture?

Yes, through "keep and replace" masking. In Lovart Interior Studio:
1. Upload your room photo.
2. Mask the furniture you want to keep (paint around it with the Keep brush).
3. The AI generates new design for everything else while preserving your existing pieces.

This is useful for "I love my sofa but hate everything else in this room" scenarios — very common in real interior design projects.

### Is AI interior design replacing human interior designers?

For visualization and style exploration, AI handles what used to be the most time-consuming part of an interior designer's job — generating "what if" visualizations for clients. For the strategic, technical, and relational aspects of interior design — space planning, building codes, contractor management, procurement, installation, and client psychology — human designers are irreplaceable.

The emerging model: AI handles the "see it before you buy it" visualization. Human designers handle the "make it actually happen in the real world." Many professional designers are adopting AI tools as client communication and concept development accelerators.

## What Most Guides Won't Tell You

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

**AI makes everything look like a render.** AI-generated interiors have a subtle "too perfect" quality — no dust, no clutter, no lived-in character. The solution: after generating your ideal room, mentally add 20% imperfection before expecting real-world results to match.

**Paint colors are wildly inaccurate.** AI-generated wall colors are approximations based on the color name in your prompt ("sage green," "navy blue"). They won't match actual paint manufacturer colors. Always get physical paint samples before committing.

**Your phone camera lies about your room.** Wide-angle phone lenses make rooms look larger than they are. AI generates designs based on the photo's proportions, which means it inherits the lens distortion. Your AI-designed room may feel more spacious than reality.

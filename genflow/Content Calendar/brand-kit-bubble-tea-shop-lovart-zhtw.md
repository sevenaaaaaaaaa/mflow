## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Bubble tea branding must capture the drink's sensory appeal: creamy, chewy, sweet, photogenic. The palette should look good enough to photograph—because it will be photographed thousands of times.

- **Primary**: `#C9A96E` — Warm milk-tea caramel, the universal color of brown sugar boba
- **Secondary**: `#FFF8EE` — Creamy off-white for cup backgrounds and menu cards
- **Accent**: `#8B6914` — Deep golden brown for typography and price tags
- **Pop**: `#D4A574` — Amber honey for topping icons (boba, jelly, cheese foam)
- **Play**: `#F5E6CA` — Light tapioca cream for loyalty stamps and background textures

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart design custom boba straw wrappers and seals?**
Yes. Lovart generates cup-seal film designs (the iconic plastic top) and straw wrapper branding. The seal design is optimized for the circular format—concentric text, centered mascot, social handle rim. Export includes production-ready dielines.

**Q2: How do I create a brand that's recognizable even when people just see the cup?**
Lovart's "cup recognition" module tests designs against a database of 500+ boba shop cups to ensure visual distinctiveness. If your cup is too similar to a major chain, the AI flags it and suggests differentiators—a unique mascot placement, an unusual accent color, or a signature pattern.

**Q3: Can Lovart generate my shop's "secret menu" graphics?**
Definitely. Lovart's secret-menu module creates "if you know, you know" style graphics. Mysterious icon-only posters, DM-unlockable flavor reveals, and exclusive story templates. The AI even generates playful code names for your secret flavors.

**Q4: Does Lovart support franchise-style brand consistency?**
The $99 Studio tier includes a brand-guideline generator. Create a master brand kit, then generate per-location templates that are consistent but customizable. Perfect for boba chains expanding to 3–10 locations.

**Q5: What about seasonal flavors (pumpkin spice boba, strawberry Valentine's)?**
Lovart generates seasonal campaigns in one prompt: "Valentine's Day: strawberry rose milk tea, heart-shaped boba, pink cup sleeve." You get the cup design, menu card, social posts, and in-store poster. The core brand palette remains recognizable; seasonal accents rotate.

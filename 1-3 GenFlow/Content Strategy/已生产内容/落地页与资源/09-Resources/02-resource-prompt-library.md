# Design Prompt Library: 100+ AI Prompts for Professional Design Outputs

The difference between mediocre AI-generated designs and professional-quality results often comes down to one thing: **the prompt**. A well-crafted prompt gives the AI clear direction, creative constraints, and enough detail to produce something useful. A vague prompt produces vague results.

We've compiled **100+ tested prompts** organized by category, each refined through thousands of generations on the Lovart platform. Use these as starting points — customize them with your brand details, product specifics, and creative preferences.

---

## How to Use This Prompt Library

Each prompt follows a consistent structure:

```
[Subject/Content] + [Style/Direction] + [Technical Specs] + [Quality Modifiers]
```

**Example Breakdown:**
> "Modern logo for a sustainable coffee brand, minimalist line-art style with a coffee bean and leaf motif, warm earth tones, clean sans-serif wordmark, suitable for packaging, signage, and digital use, 8K vector quality."

- **Subject:** Logo for a sustainable coffee brand
- **Style:** Minimalist line-art style
- **Details:** Coffee bean and leaf motif, warm earth tones, sans-serif wordmark
- **Technical:** Vector quality, usage specifications

**Tips for Customizing Prompts:**
1. Replace bracketed `[terms]` with your specific details
2. Add your brand name where relevant
3. Adjust the style descriptors to match your brand aesthetic
4. Specify platform requirements when generating for specific use cases
5. Use negative prompts to exclude what you don't want

## Prompt Engineering: Advanced Techniques

### The Anatomy of a Great Prompt

1. **Be Specific, Not Vague**
   - Bad: "A nice logo"
   - Good: "Minimalist geometric logo for a fintech startup, navy and gold, sans-serif wordmark, abstract security shield icon"

2. **Use Reference Points**
   - "In the style of Swiss design movement"
   - "Apple product photography aesthetic"
   - "Editorial magazine layout like Kinfolk"

3. **Specify What You DON'T Want**
   - "No gradients, no shadows, no clip-art style"
   - "No more than 3 colors, no handwritten fonts"
   - "No stock photo aesthetic, no fake smiling people"

4. **Include Technical Parameters**
   - Dimensions: "1080x1080px" or "16:9 aspect ratio"
   - Quality: "8K", "300 DPI", "vector quality", "print-ready"
   - Format: "CMYK for print", "RGB for digital"

5. **Chain Prompts for Iteration**
   - First prompt: Generate broad concept
   - Second prompt: "Take concept #3 and refine — make the colors warmer, increase contrast, simplify the composition"
   - Third prompt: "Generate 5 color variations of the refined design"

### The Power of Iteration

The best AI designs almost never come from a single prompt. Professional workflow:

1. **Exploration Prompt** (broad, 20-30 outputs) → Curate to top 3-5
2. **Refinement Prompt** (specific feedback on curated outputs) → Generate 5 variations each
3. **Production Prompt** (final specifications, format requirements) → Generate final deliverable with all variants

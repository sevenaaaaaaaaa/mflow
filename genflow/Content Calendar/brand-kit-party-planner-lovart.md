## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Party planning branding must communicate one thing instantly: "I make celebrations unforgettable." The palette should feel festive without being childish, joyful without being chaotic.

- **Primary**: `#FF6B9D` — Confetti pink, the color of party hats, birthday candles, and celebration energy
- **Secondary**: `#FFF0F5` — Cotton-candy white for backgrounds, envelopes, and stationery
- **Accent**: `#E04478` — Deep fuchsia for typography, CTAs, and premium package headers
- **Pop**: `#FFD166` — Sunshine yellow for "Book Now" buttons and special-offer badges
- **Cool**: `#06D6A0` — Mint-lime for secondary accents, "Kids Party" sub-brands, and spring themes

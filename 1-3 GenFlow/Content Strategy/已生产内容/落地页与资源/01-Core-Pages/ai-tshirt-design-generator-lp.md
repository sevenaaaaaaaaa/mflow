**Step 1 — Describe the Design.** Write a prompt: *A vintage-style national park t-shirt with a geometric mountain illustration, forest green on cream, with "Wander" in serif font across the chest.*

**Step 2 — Choose Garment and Placement.** Select t-shirt style (crew neck, oversized, crop, long sleeve), color, and print placement (center chest, left chest, full front, back, sleeve).

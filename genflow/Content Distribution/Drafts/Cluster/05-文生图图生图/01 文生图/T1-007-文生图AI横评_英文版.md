# 2026 Text-to-Image AI Comprehensive Review: Midjourney vs FLUX vs Hunyuan vs Stable Diffusion — Which One Reigns Supreme?

> Not sponsored. Just a hands-on comparison from someone who works with AI visual tools every day.

---

With over a decade in marketing — from traditional advertising to digital marketing to growth hacking — I've been through every productivity bottleneck you can imagine. When AI tools emerged, I began offloading all repetitive work to them: brand visuals, social media assets, short videos. One person, one small team's worth of output.

I've tested well over a hundred AI tools. I'm not a developer, but as a power user, I know better than most which ones truly boost productivity and which are just flashy demos. With a background in design, my evaluation criteria skew "picky" — I don't just care whether a tool can generate images; I care whether those images are production-ready and pass brand review.

In this roundup, I put four mainstream text-to-image AIs head-to-head: Midjourney, FLUX, Tencent HunyuanImage, and Stable Diffusion. The bottom line: **there's no absolute winner — only the best combination for your specific use case.**

---

## 1. Midjourney V7 — ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ | The Aesthetic Ceiling, But Expensive and Closed

**What it solves**: Produces AI images that align with mainstream aesthetic sensibilities using minimal prompt engineering. Ideal for creators who demand visual quality but don't want to spend time tweaking parameters.

**Hands-on experience**: With the V7 release, Midjourney's image quality and aesthetic understanding have leveled up again. Lighting layers, material texture, color harmony — things that require painstaking prompt engineering on competing tools — Midjourney often nails natively. Portrait and concept design work in V7 is particularly impressive: skin texture, catchlights, and fabric folds are nearly indistinguishable from real photography.

I've used it for brand key visuals, product concept images, and social media assets. Roughly 60-70% of outputs are usable directly. The remaining tweaks typically involve details — occasional finger deformities, incorrect text, misplaced objects in multi-character interactions — but these are industry-wide text-to-image issues, not Midjourney-specific flaws.

**Cons**: The closed ecosystem is the biggest pain point. Discord-only operation, no API, no local deployment, no custom model training. For creators who want to integrate it into a workflow, flexibility is essentially zero. Pricing isn't cheap either — the Pro plan at $60/month, with usage-based billing, adds up for heavy users. And for users in China, Discord access stability is an additional headache.

**💡 Power-user tip**: Use Midjourney as your "aesthetic benchmark." Before starting a project, generate 5-10 reference images with Midjourney to establish visual direction and style, then use other tools (like Stable Diffusion + ControlNet) for bulk production. Also, V7's new "Style Reference" feature — upload a reference image and Midjourney extracts the style to apply to new generations — is a game-changer for brand visual series.

---

## 2. FLUX.1 — ⭐⭐⭐⭐½ | The Open-Source New King, Dominating Text Rendering and Image Quality

**What it solves**: Delivers image generation quality approaching or exceeding Midjourney in open-source form, while solving traditional AI image generation pain points like text rendering and multi-subject interaction.

**Hands-on experience**: FLUX.1 was released in 2024 by Black Forest Labs (the original Stable Diffusion team), now available in three variants: FLUX.1-dev, FLUX.1-pro, and FLUX.1-schnell. Honestly, my first run with FLUX.1-pro was a shock — its **text rendering capability** is unmatched among all text-to-image tools. Getting an AI to accurately render Chinese text like "春季特惠 全场 5 折" (Spring Sale, 50% Off Everything) on a poster? Midjourney can't really do it, SD needs ControlNet to barely pull it off, but FLUX generates it directly with remarkably high accuracy.

Image quality-wise, FLUX is very close to Midjourney V7, and in some photorealistic scenes, even more natural. Midjourney images sometimes feel too "perfect" (the so-called "AI look"), while FLUX's noise and lighting transitions feel closer to real camera output.

The multi-variant strategy is smart: pro for top quality via paid API, dev as open-source for local deployment, and schnell as a distilled version for blazing speed. You can switch flexibly based on the scenario.

**Cons**: Chinese prompt comprehension is still relatively weak. While it can render Chinese text, semantic understanding of Chinese prompts isn't as precise as English. For Chinese-localized content, you'll need mixed Chinese-English or pure English prompts. Open-source deployment isn't trivial — dev model files are 12GB+, requiring at least 16GB VRAM for smooth inference. For regular users, API access is more convenient but comes at a cost.

**💡 Power-user tip**: For visual assets requiring **precise text content** — poster headlines, product labels, social media cards — FLUX is the top choice. If you need to embed AI image generation into a product pipeline (e.g., pairing with Lovart's Brand Kit for automated output), FLUX's open-source ecosystem and API accessibility far surpass Midjourney. Recommended pairing: use with ComfyUI to build a node-based workflow: "FLUX generates base image → ControlNet controls composition → Lovart Brand Kit unifies brand style."

## 3. Tencent HunyuanImage — ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | #1 for Chinese Aesthetics, King of Chinese Prompts

**What it solves**: Excels at Chinese semantic understanding and traditional Chinese aesthetic expression, solving the "lost in translation" problem foreign models face with Chinese culture and scenes.

**Hands-on experience**: Tencent Hunyuan's image generation model (HunyuanImage) is the strongest among Chinese tech giants. Its greatest advantage is **Chinese language understanding** — describe "a misty Jiangnan water town, cobblestone path, oil-paper umbrella" in pure Chinese, and Hunyuan's scene accuracy and atmospheric rendering far exceed Midjourney and FLUX. For Chinese cultural elements — Hanfu, ink wash painting, traditional festivals, ancient architecture — Hunyuan's comprehension and reproduction are in a league of their own.

Image quality-wise, Hunyuan's photorealism leads among Chinese models, but still trails Midjourney V7 and FLUX in extreme lighting and material details. Faces tend to have a heavier "AI feel," especially Asian skin tones lacking natural translucency.

Speed is another bright spot — backed by Tencent Cloud computing, API response is fast, suitable for batch generation. Pricing is generous with free quotas, and paid tiers are much cheaper than Midjourney.

**Cons**: Style diversity falls short of open-source options. Hunyuan leans toward safe, mainstream aesthetics. If you're aiming for experimental or niche art styles (dark aesthetics, surrealist collage), results are mediocre. No open-source model available — API-only access, no local deployment or custom fine-tuning. Developer and deep-customization scenarios aren't well-supported.

**💡 Power-user tip**: If your content primarily targets Chinese audiences — Xiaohongshu covers, WeChat Official Account images, domestic e-commerce assets — Hunyuan is the most culturally aligned choice. Particularly recommended with **Xingliu Agent**: describe your needs in natural language, and Xingliu will call Hunyuan while auto-optimizing prompts, yielding much higher success rates than manual prompt writing. Holiday marketing assets (Mid-Autumn Festival, Chinese New Year, Dragon Boat Festival) are Hunyuan's absolute strong suit.

---

## 4. Stable Diffusion 3.5 + Ecosystem — ⭐⭐⭐⭐ | The Ultimate "LEGO Blocks" of Creative Freedom

**What it solves**: Provides a fully controllable, customizable, and integrable AI image generation ecosystem for technically-inclined creators with deep customization needs.

**Hands-on experience**: Stable Diffusion isn't a single tool — it's an **ecosystem**. SD 3.5's base model alone doesn't match Midjourney V7 or FLUX.1-pro in raw output quality, but pair it with LoRA, ControlNet, IP-Adapter, and the ecosystem plugins, and it achieves things no other tool can: precise composition control, consistent character faces, specified poses and expressions, batch generation of style-consistent image series.

**SD 3.5 Large** delivers sufficient quality, **SD 3.5 Turbo** is incredibly fast (1-2 steps per image), and paired with ComfyUI or Automatic1111 WebUI, flexibility is top-tier. Plus, the model community is incredibly active — over 100,000 LoRAs and Checkpoints on Civitai covering every imaginable style. Liblib also hosts many SD models and workflows shared by Chinese creators, with plenty of quality Chinese-content-oriented models.

**Cons**: The learning curve is massive. ComfyUI's node-based interface is extremely unfriendly to beginners. Understanding and tuning parameters like LoRA weights, CFG Scale, Sampler, and Scheduler takes at least 1-2 months of practice. Hardware requirements are steep — 8GB+ VRAM recommended; running on CPU is pure suffering. Output stability is also poor — the same prompt and parameters can yield wildly different results across runs, requiring multiple "gacha pulls."

**💡 Power-user tip**: Don't start from scratch. Find pre-tuned workflows and model combinations on **Liblib**, and one-click copy them into your ComfyUI. Liblib's "Workflow Copy" feature significantly lowers the SD barrier — you don't need to understand every node parameter, just replicate proven pipelines. Additionally, if you're using Lovart for brand visuals, import SD-generated assets into Lovart for brand packaging and size adaptation, creating a cooperative pipeline: "SD creative base → Lovart branded output."

## My Recommendations

**If you can only pick one: FLUX.1.** It's the most balanced across image quality, controllability, and integrability. Its unmatched text rendering is a decisive bonus, and the open-source ecosystem leaves room for future possibilities.

**Ultimate aesthetics + budget not an issue: Midjourney V7.** If you're doing premium brand visuals, concept design, or artistic creation where aesthetic standards are sky-high, V7 remains the ceiling.

**Chinese market content: Hunyuan.** Paired with Xingliu Agent, it's the most efficient for Xiaohongshu, WeChat Official Accounts, and domestic e-commerce assets. The experience of writing Chinese prompts directly is something no other tool can match.

**Technical foundation + deep customization needs: SD 3.5 + ComfyUI.** Find good workflows and models on Liblib, maximize the ecosystem's power, and SD can do more than any single tool.

**Best combo play: FLUX base image + SD ControlNet composition control + Lovart branding + Hunyuan for Chinese scenes.** Four tools, each doing what they do best, covering all visual content production needs.

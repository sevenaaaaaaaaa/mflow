## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Gift-shop branding must evoke discovery, delight, and the emotional warmth of giving. The palette should feel like unwrapping something—anticipation, surprise, then joy.

- **Primary**: `#EC407A` — Ribbon-pink, the color of celebration, gift-wrap bows, and the joy of finding the perfect present
- **Secondary**: `#FFF5F5` — Tissue-paper white for gift-guide backgrounds, price tags, and stationery
- **Accent**: `#26A69A` — Teal-green for "Handmade," "Local," "Eco-Friendly" badges and CTAs
- **Warmth**: `#FFB74D` — Golden-amber for "Best Seller" tags, gift-card designs, and loyalty rewards
- **Neutral**: `#5D4037` — Warm brown for body text and secondary information

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create a brand that works across wildly different product categories (candles, jewelry, books, baby gifts)?**
Yes. Lovart's "multi-category curation" approach creates a master visual system where the brand is the consistent frame around diverse products. The palette (`#EC407A` / `#FFF5F5` / `#26A69A`) works as a neutral-but-warm backdrop. Think gallery walls—the frame is consistent; the art is diverse.

**Q2: How do I create gift guides that actually drive sales (not just likes)?**
Lovart's gift-guide module uses merchandising psychology: price-point anchoring (lead with Under $25, then Under $50), recipient-based organization (reduces decision paralysis), and "Staff Pick" social proof badges. Every guide ends with a gift-card option for the truly indecisive.

**Q3: Can Lovart generate maker-story templates for my local-artisan products?**
Absolutely. "Meet the Maker" templates: product photo with maker photo, "Based in [Town]," "Her Process" 3-bullet story, and "Why We Love [Product]." These posts humanize your inventory and build loyalty to both the maker AND your shop. Available as Instagram posts and in-store shelf-talkers.

**Q4: Does Lovart create corporate-gifting and wedding-registry branding?**
Yes. The B2B/registry module generates "Corporate Gifting" service brochures, custom-gift-basket design templates, and "Create Your Registry" in-store signage with QR. These high-ticket revenue streams need professional branding to convert. Available on $49 Pro tier.

**Q5: What about pop-up shop and holiday market branding?**
Lovart's pop-up module generates a portable brand kit for off-site events: tablecloth design, price-tag sheets, "Find Us at [Event]" social posts, and a pop-up-specific version of your logo that's optimized for small-format signage. Your brand travels consistently, wherever you sell.

# Basic AI Editing

### Upscale

> Increase image resolution while preserving quality.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/o11tm2qe/production/1cdd41f56a2239c05fc322b249c698094b0d09cd-2160x1350.png?w=800&q=75&auto=format)

**What It Does**

Upscale boosts the resolution of an image to 2K, 4K, or 8K. Use it to prepare assets for print, large displays, or high-resolution exports.

**How to Use**

1. **Select an image.**
    - Click an image on the Canvas to reveal the top bar.
2. **Open upscale.**
    - Click Upscale in the top bar.
3. **Choose a resolution.**
    - Pick 2K, 4K, or 8K to apply the upscale.

**Quick Tips**

- Go straight to the final target resolution in one pass rather than upscaling in increments.
- Crop before upscaling so you don't spend processing time on areas you'll trim away.

**Related Features**

- [Crop](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-editing#19d098723df5): Crop before upscaling to avoid processing unnecessary areas.
- [Expand](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-ai-editing#d317ecf668fc): Expand image before upscaling for combined workflows.

### Remove Background

> Isolate the subject by removing the background in one click.

**What It Does**

Remove BG detects the main subject in an image and replaces the background with transparency. Useful for clean product shots, isolating subjects, or preparing overlays.

**How to Use**

1. **Select an image.**
    - Click an image on the Canvas to reveal the top bar.
2. **Click Remove BG.**
    - Click Remove BG in the top bar. The background is removed automatically.

**Quick Tips**

- High-contrast subjects against simple backgrounds produce the cleanest edges. For busy scenes or hair, expect to refine with Eraser or Edit Elements.
- If you need the subject on a different background (not transparent), consider asking the Agent to apply an edit.

**Related Features**

- [Edit Elements](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/advanced-ai-editing#a325d3865db9): Separate an image into layers (keeps both subject and background as editable elements).
- [Eraser](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-ai-editing#54cec6ab98d0): Remove specific objects rather than the entire background.

### Eraser

> Erase an unwanted object and let the AI fill the gap.

**What It Does**

Eraser removes unwanted objects, people, text, or watermarks from an image and fills the gap with AI-generated content that matches the surrounding scene.

**How to Use**

1. **Select an image.**
    - Click an image on the Canvas to reveal the top bar.
2. **Open eraser.**
    - Click Eraser in the top bar.
3. **Paint the area.**
    - Adjust the brush size, then paint over the object you want to remove.
4. **Generate.**
    - Click Generate to fill the erased area.

**Quick Tips**

- Use a smaller brush for fine details and a larger brush for big areas.

**Related Features**

- [Remove Background](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-ai-editing#31d1b4d59068): Remove the entire background instead of specific objects.
- [Edit Elements](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/advanced-ai-editing#a325d3865db9): Separate the image into layers for repositioning instead of erasing.
- [Move Object](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/ai-transformation#63cf102480ef): Move the object elsewhere in the image instead of erasing it.

### Expand

> Extend an image outward with AI-generated content that completes the scene.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/o11tm2qe/production/4ddfd113d5d0936651e65ea8e7e43c16560fcd72-2160x1350.png?w=800&q=75&auto=format)

**What It Does**

Expand extends an image beyond its current bounds and fills the new area with AI-generated content that coherently completes the scene. Use it for outpainting, aspect ratio changes, extra breathing room, and adapting designs to different formats.

**How to Use**

1. **Select an image.**
    - Click an image on the Canvas to reveal the top bar.
2. **Open Expand.**
    - Click Expand in the top bar to enter expand mode.
3. **Define the target area.**
    - Pick a social media preset, choose an aspect ratio, or drag the handles to define the new bounds.
4. **Generate.**
    - Click Generate to fill the extended area.

**Quick Tips**

- Use Expand to convert aspect ratios (e.g., 1:1 to 16:9) without cropping.
- Handy for adding breathing room or space for text overlays.

**Related Features**

- [Crop](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-editing#19d098723df5): Trim images (the inverse of expanding).
- [Upscale](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-ai-editing#5939657f2e1f): Increase resolution after expanding.

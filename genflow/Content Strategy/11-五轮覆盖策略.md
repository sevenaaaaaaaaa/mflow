## 二、五轮内容格式

| 轮次 | 格式 | 框架 | 关键词角度 | 示例标题模式 |
|------|------|------|-----------|------------|
| **R1** | How-To 教程 | The Journey | "如何做X" | "How to [Action] with AI — Step by Step Guide" |
| **R2** | 对比/评测 | The Myth-Buster | "X vs Y 怎么选" | "[Tool A] vs [Tool B]: Which [Category] Actually Delivers?" |
| **R3** | 案例/行业 | The Economist | "真实场景中怎么用" | "How [Industry/Persona] Uses AI to [Achieve Outcome]" |
| **R4** | FAQ/完全指南 | The Field Guide | "关于X你需要知道的一切" | "The Complete Guide to [Topic]: FAQ, Tips & Best Practices" |
| **R5** | Listicle/合集 | The Visionary | "X个最好的Y" | "10 Best [Category] Tools for [Audience] in 2026" |

## Day 1: Set Up Your Brand Kit

**Goal:** Define your brand's visual DNA so everything you create this week stays consistent.

**Task:**
1. Sign up for Lovart (free)
2. Upload your logo (or generate one placeholder — you'll replace it on Day 2)
3. Pick your brand colors — 1 primary, 1 secondary, 2 accents
4. Choose 2 fonts — one heading, one body
5. Upload 3 reference images that capture your brand vibe

**Template:** "Brand Kit Setup" template pre-fills common color combos by industry.

**Time:** 15 minutes

**Share:** Screenshot your Brand Kit palette and post with #Lovart7Day + "Here's the DNA of [Your Brand Name]. Day 1 of 7."

**Pro tip:** If you're stuck on colors, use the "Generate from Description" button. Type "calm and trustworthy wellness brand" or "bold edgy streetwear" and let Lovart suggest the palette.

---

## Day 2: Design a Logo

**Goal:** Create a logo that actually looks like you paid for it.

**Task:**
1. Open ChatCanvas
2. Type: *"Logo for [brand name], [industry]. [Style descriptor, e.g., minimal geometric / playful hand-drawn / luxury serif]. Use my Brand Kit."*
3. Generate three variations
4. Touch Edit to refine — adjust spacing, try icon-only and wordmark versions
5. Pick your winner and export SVG + PNG

**Template:** "Logo Generator" template includes common logo layouts (icon left, stacked, horizontal).

**Time:** 15 minutes

**Share:** Post your final logo + the two rejected concepts. It's fascinating to see the decision process.

---

## Day 3: Social Media Post

**Goal:** Create a platform-native post that stops the scroll.

**Task:**
1. Choose your platform (Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, or X)
2. In ChatCanvas: *"Create an Instagram post announcing [something interesting about your brand]. [Tone: playful/professional/luxury]. Include headline, body text, and a visual. Use brand kit."*
3. Touch Edit to customize copy
4. Export at the correct dimensions for your platform
5. Bonus: generate a 3-post carousel version

**Template:** "Instagram Post — Product Launch" or "LinkedIn Thought Leadership" depending on your platform.

**Time:** 15 minutes

**Share:** Post the mockup + write your actual caption. Community will give you caption feedback.

---

## Day 4: Product Mockup

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

**Goal:** Make your product look real before it's real.

**Task:**
1. Upload your product image (or a sketch if pre-production)
2. In ChatCanvas: *"Place this product on a [context: desk with coffee / beach towel / minimalist white background]. Realistic lighting. Use brand colors for background accents."*
3. Generate 2-3 scene variations
4. Touch Edit to adjust product placement and shadow intensity
5. Export high-res

**Template:** "Product Mockup Scenes" offers 12 preset environments.

**Time:** 15 minutes

**Share:** Side-by-side comparison of original product photo vs AI mockup. The before-and-after always gets the most engagement.

---

## Day 5: Poster or Flyer

**Goal:** Design print-ready marketing collateral.

**Task:**
1. Decide: event poster, sale flyer, or menu design
2. ChatCanvas: *"Design a [type of collateral] for [purpose]. Include headline, date/details section, and CTA. [Mood descriptor]. Use brand kit."*
3. Touch Edit to refine layout balance
4. Export print-ready PDF (300dpi, bleed included)
5. Bonus: resize for digital version (social-friendly ratio)

**Template:** "Event Poster" / "Sale Flyer" / "Restaurant Menu" templates.

**Time:** 15 minutes

**Share:** Your poster design. If you dare, print it and photograph it in the real world. Physical context makes designs feel 10x more legitimate.

---

## Day 6: Short Video

**Goal:** Turn a static design into motion content.

**Task:**
1. Pick your Day 3 social post
2. In ChatCanvas: *"Animate this design — add a [smooth zoom-in / text reveal / parallax scroll] effect. 6 seconds, loopable. Vertical format."*
3. Preview the animation
4. Adjust timing and easing
5. Export MP4

**Template:** "Social Video Animations" offers pre-built motion presets.

**Time:** 15 minutes

**Share:** The video itself. Include "Still vs Animated" comparison frame. Motion content consistently outperforms static posts by 2-3x on social.

---

## Day 7: Full Brand Review

**Goal:** See your entire brand identity in one view and refine.

**Task:**
1. Collect all six assets you've created this week
2. Open the "Brand Review Board" template
3. Place all assets side by side
4. Audit: Do they feel cohesive? Is the color palette consistent? Does the typography unify them?
5. Touch Edit anything that feels inconsistent
6. Export your "Brand Guidelines Summary" PDF — a one-pager showcasing your brand identity

**Template:** "Brand Review Board" arranges all assets in a presentation-ready layout.

**Time:** 15 minutes

**Share:** Post your full 7-day body of work — the complete brand identity you built this week. This is the victory lap. We'll reshare every completion.

**SAN FRANCISCO, June 21, 2025 —** Lovart, the AI design agent, together with global creative platform leader Freepik, hosted *Design Reimagined* last Thursday — Lovart’s first-ever in-person community event. The sold-out gathering took place in downtown San Francisco, bringing together over 50 designers and AI artists — including early Lovart adopters — for live demos, engaging panels, and networking focused on shaping the future of ethical, AI-powered design.

![img](https://assets-persist.lovart.ai/web/model/35768b9e354a433db3bbd424f1f8bc5d/3ef033b7f11cc8f13434e8c4c4e958dfe44621a01843978d60c7884325bb27f0.JPG)

During the demo showcase, AI artist and content creator Evgenia Piskun used a single prompt to generate a production-ready logo plus 10-piece brand kit—in under five minutes, demonstrating how Lovart’s design agent workflow can compress weeks of creative iteration into minutes on an infinite, collaborative canvas.

Panels organized by product growth expert Ying Yang and moderated by Freepik’s Enrique Lopez explored topics such as responsible model usage, AI governance, and the evolving landscape of AI-powered design. Featured panelists included Lovart Co-founder and COO Elena Leung, AI content creator Evgenia Piskun, AI creator Claire Xue, and Paula Vivas, Head of Marketing at Freepik.

*"The human aspect of design cannot be replaced by AI,"* said Elena Leung, Co-founder and COO of Lovart. *"That’s why we’re building a future where designers and AI create side by side, united on the same canvas."*

### About Lovart

Lovart is a San Francisco–based AI company pioneering the Design Agent—a conversational, multimodal creative AI agent that transforms a single prompt into cohesive, editable assets, including images, video, audio, storyboards, brand kits, and 3D content, all within a unified infinite canvas.

### Live Demo Showcase: Evgenia Piskun

**About Evgenia:**
Evgenia Piskun is an AI artist and content creator known for her work exploring the intersection of AI and traditional art forms. Her demonstration showed practical application of Lovart's capabilities.

**The Demo:**
Starting with a simple prompt: "Create a modern, minimalist logo for a sustainable fashion brand"

**What Happened:**
1. AI generated initial logo concepts (30 seconds)
2. Evgenia selected preferred direction
3. Refined with natural language: "Make it more elegant, add a subtle leaf motif"
4. Expanded to complete brand kit: colors, typography, patterns
5. Total time: Under 5 minutes

**Before vs. After:**
- Traditional timeline: 2-3 weeks with agency
- With Lovart: Under 5 minutes
- Quality: Production-ready, commercially viable

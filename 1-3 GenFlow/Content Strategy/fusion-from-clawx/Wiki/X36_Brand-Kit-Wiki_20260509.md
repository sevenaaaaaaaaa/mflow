Brand Kit is Lovart's centralized brand asset management system. Store: logos (primary + variants), colors (hex codes), fonts (headline + body), photography style guidelines, brand guide PDFs. Auto-applies to every generated design. Create separate Kits for each client (agencies) or each brand (multi-brand companies). Test by generating a social post, flyer, and business card — all three should apply the same standards.

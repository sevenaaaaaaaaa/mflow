## 10 Halloween Templates to Remix Right Now

### 1. The Classic Jack-o'-Lantern Poster

A bold, high-contrast composition built around a single glowing pumpkin. Perfect for event announcements, pop-up shop flyers, and Instagram carousel covers. Start with the prompt: *"A cinematic jack-o'-lantern poster with warm orange glow, dark forest background, bold sans-serif title space at top."*

### 2. Spooky Gradient Social Banner

Horizontal gradients have dominated 2026 visual trends, and Halloween gives them a deliciously dark twist. Think deep violet bleeding into burnt orange, with a subtle bat-silhouette pattern layer. Ideal for Twitter/X headers and LinkedIn event banners.

### 3. Haunted House Event Flyer

Every local business needs a Halloween event flyer. This template combines gothic typography, a moody mansion illustration, and a clean information hierarchy for date, time, and location. Use the Brand Kit to auto-swap the color scheme to your palette in one tap.

### 4. Trick-or-Treat Product Showcase

E-commerce brands: this is your template. A dark-themed product grid with glowing accent borders around each item, spooky subtle drop shadows, and a "Limited Edition" badge component you can toggle on or off. Generated backgrounds ensure every product shot feels like part of a cohesive campaign.

### 5. Animated Story Template (Ghost Edition)

Not a static image — a multi-frame story template with a floating ghost that drifts across three slides. Describe the motion in ChatCanvas: *"Three-frame Instagram Story with a translucent ghost drifting left to right, text reveals on frame 3."* Export as individual frames or a looping GIF.

### 6. Minimalist Bat Pattern Background

Sometimes you don't need a full composition — just a beautiful pattern. This template generates a seamless repeating bat-silhouette pattern in any two-color combination. Drop it behind product photos, use it as a website hero background, or print it on merch.

### 7. Vintage Horror Movie Poster

The retro horror aesthetic never dies. Think 1970s grindhouse typography, distressed textures, and a limited palette of blood-red, parchment-yellow, and ink-black. Perfect for bars, music venues, and anyone launching a Halloween-themed product line.

### 8. Candy-Colored Kids' Party Invitation

Not all Halloween design needs to be terrifying. This template uses a playful, illustration-heavy style with candy-corn colors, friendly ghost characters, and rounded typography. Great for family-friendly events, school fundraisers, and daycare announcements.

### 9. Dark Academia Quote Card

The Dark Academia aesthetic peaks every autumn. Pair a literary quote about mystery or the supernatural with antique-book textures, serif typography, and a candle-lit color grade. High shareability on Pinterest and TikTok slideshows.

### 10. "Boo Basket" Product Flat-Lay

Influencers and boutique brands love the curated Halloween flat-lay. This template generates a top-down composition with arranged props — mini pumpkins, dried flowers, black candles, themed product placement — lit with dramatic side-lighting. Swap in your own product images via Touch Edit.

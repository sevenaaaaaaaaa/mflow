## Step-by-Step: Create Your Flyer in 10 Minutes

### Step 1

Describe your flyer: 'Create a promotional flyer for [event/sale]. Headline: [main message]. Details: [date, time, location, offer]. Style: [eye-catching/professional/elegant].'

### Step 2

Select the best composition. Look for clear visual hierarchy — headline dominates, details scannable, CTA obvious.

### Step 3

Refine visual balance. Use Touch Edit to adjust text sizes, image placement, white space, color contrast for readability.

### Step 4

Add mandatory elements: sponsor logos, QR codes, legal disclaimers, social handles. Lovart places them naturally.

### Step 5

Export for your distribution: PDF/X for professional printing (8.5"×11" or A4, 300 DPI, CMYK, bleed), or PNG/JPEG for digital.

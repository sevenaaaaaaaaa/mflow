Lovart Pricing & Plans – Complete Guide

**Free ($0):** Daily credits for image generation across all AI models. Limited video generation. Basic commercial rights. Nano Banana 2 access (limited). Best for: evaluation, occasional use, low-volume needs. Users typically hit the daily credit limit within 2-3 days of active use.

**Pro ($20/month):** Unlimited image generations. Generous video generation quota. Full commercial rights for client work and products. PSD export for professional workflows. Nano Banana 2 unlimited + Nano Banana Pro limited. Best for: individual designers, content creators, small business owners, freelancers.

**Ultimate ($90/month):** Priority processing (2-3x faster generation). Maximum video quotas. First access to new features. Nano Banana Pro unlimited. All models unlimited. Best for: professionals who generate video regularly, high-throughput production environments.

**Team (custom):** Shared credit pools across team members. Per-member usage analytics. Admin dashboard with cost allocation. Priority support. Custom integration support. Best for: marketing teams, design agencies, in-house creative departments.

See the [full pricing page](https://www.lovart.ai/pricing) for detailed feature comparison.

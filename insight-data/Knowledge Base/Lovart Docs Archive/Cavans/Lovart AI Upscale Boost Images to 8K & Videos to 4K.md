Lovart AI Upscale: Boost Images to 8K & Videos to 4K

  
The **Upscale** feature lets you increase the resolution of your image (e.g., from 2K to 4K or 8K) while preserving or enhancing visual quality.

### Step‑by‑Step Usage Guide

1. **Choose an upscale factor**
	- Click the upscale options such as 2K, 4K, or 8K for images and 720p, 1080p, 2K, 4K for videosin the Upscale panel.
	- The chosen factor will multiply the base resolution (e.g., moving from 2K to 4K roughly doubles each dimension).
2. **Preview the new resolution**
	- Check the width and height fields (W / H) to confirm the target resolution before applying.
	- Ensure the dimensions match your use case, such as print specs or export requirements.
3. **Apply the upscale**
	- Confirm the upscale action in the panel; the canvas will be processed to the higher resolution.
	- During processing, avoid making other heavy edits until the upscale finishes to prevent conflicts or lag.

### Best Practices

- **Avoid multiple sequential upscales**: Upscale once to the final target resolution instead of repeatedly scaling up small increments to reduce quality loss and processing time.

### After Upscaling

- Re‑check small text, logos, and line art at 100% zoom to confirm they remain crisp and legible.
- Export the high‑resolution version in your desired format (e.g., PNG, JPG) for print, social media, or motion tools.
### Upscale Resolution Guide

**Image Upscale Levels:**
| Output | Best For | When to Use |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| 2K | Web content, social media | Quick turnaround, digital use |
| 4K | Presentations, marketing | Professional deliverables |
| 8K | Print, large format, broadcast | Highest quality requirements |

**Video Upscale Levels:**
| Output | Best For | When to Use |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| 720p | Mobile preview, thumbnails | Quick previews |
| 1080p | Standard video, social | General purpose |
| 2K | Professional content | Quality-focused work |
| 4K | 4K displays, broadcast | Highest quality video |

### Upscaling Strategy

**Avoid Sequential Upscaling:**
- **Don't**: 1K → 2K → 4K (multiple small increases)
- **Do**: 1K → 4K directly (single large increase)

Multiple small upscaling can cause:
- Cumulative quality degradation
- Longer total processing time
- Unnecessary file size growth

### Quality Preservation Techniques

**Before Upscaling:**
1. Start with highest available resolution
2. Remove noise and artifacts first
3. Ensure proper focus and sharpness
4. Clean up any blemishes

**After Upscaling:**
1. Review at 100% zoom
2. Check text legibility
3. Verify edge sharpness
4. Export in appropriate format

### Use Case Examples

**Print Production Workflow:**
1. Original: 1024×768 (1K)
2. Upscale directly to 8K
3. Export as PNG for print
4. Quality maintained for large format

**Social Media Optimization:**
1. Original: Standard camera photo
2. Upscale to 2K
3. Export as JPG at 80% quality
4. Optimal balance of quality and file size

**Video Enhancement:**
1. Original: 1080p footage
2. Upscale to 4K

### Troubleshooting

**Results Appear Blurry:**
- Original image may have been low quality
- Consider starting with higher resolution source
- Check if zoom level is at 100%

**Text Becomes Less Legible:**
- Original text may have been small
- Try 2K before jumping to 4K
- Review at actual export size

**Processing Takes Long:**
- Higher upscale factors take more time
- 8K requires more processing than 4K
- Avoid editing during processing

### FAQ

**Q: What's the best resolution for print?**
A: For professional print, aim for 300 DPI at final print size. Upscale to 4K or 8K for large format work.

**Q: Can I upscale any image?**
A: Quality results depend on original image. Higher starting resolution produces better results.

**Q: Does upscaling add new detail?**
A: AI intelligently enhances existing details and sharpens edges. It doesn't create new information from nothing.

**Q: Should I upscale before or after editing?**
A: Edit first, then upscale to final output. This maintains consistency across your work.

**Q: What's the maximum useful upscale?**
A: 8K is generally the maximum useful output. Going higher provides diminishing returns.

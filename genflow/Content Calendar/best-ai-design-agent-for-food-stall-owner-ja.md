## FAQ

**Q: I only have a phone — no computer. Can I still use Lovart?**
Yes. Lovart's ChatCanvas and Touch Edit are fully functional on mobile browsers. You can design an entire stall banner from your phone between lunch and dinner service.

**Q: My food stall uses multiple languages — Malay, Chinese, Tamil, English. Can Lovart handle this?**
Absolutely. Specify all required languages in your prompt, and Lovart's AI will create a balanced multilingual layout with appropriate font pairings for each script system.

**Q: How much does it cost to get started?**
The **Free tier** lets you create basic menus and social posts at no cost. For regular banner updates, seasonal promotions, and delivery platform assets, the **$19/mo** Pro tier is ideal. That's less than the cost of 4 plates of nasi lemak.

**Q: Will the banner file be ready for my local print shop?**
Yes. Lovart exports CMYK PDFs at 300 DPI with bleed and crop marks. For large-format banners, specify your dimensions in feet or meters, and the AI scales accordingly — your print shop just needs to hit "print."

**Q: Can I create a logo if I don't have one yet?**
Yes. ChatCanvas can generate logo concepts from a description of your stall name and cuisine. Describe the style ("vintage kopitiam lettering, wok illustration, circular badge layout"), and the AI produces multiple logo options.

**Q: How do I update prices on my existing banner design?**
Open your previous Lovart project, edit the price text via Touch Edit, and export a new PDF. No redesign needed — just text updates in under 2 minutes.

**Q: Can Lovart create promotional flyers for catering orders?**
Absolutely. Generate a catering menu flyer with bulk pricing, minimum order quantities, and contact information — formatted for both print (A4/A5) and WhatsApp sharing.

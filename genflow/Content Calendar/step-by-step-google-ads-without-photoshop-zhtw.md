## Step 2: Compose with Google's Image Restrictions in Mind

Unlike Facebook, Google enforces strict image content restrictions. No overlaid text that exceeds 20% of the image area. No fake buttons or simulated UI elements. No flashing elements in animated formats. Lovart's **Google Ads Compliance Mode** (enable in Settings → Ad Platform → Google) automatically checks each creative against Google's current image guidelines. It flags text density, warns about button-like elements, and ensures your logo placement doesn't overlap with Google's auto-overlay UI in Discovery placements. The compliance scanner updates its ruleset quarterly to match Google Ads policy changes.

## Step 3: Create a Visual Hierarchy That Works at 300×250

Google Display ads frequently appear at 300×250 (medium rectangle) — smaller than a business card. Your creative must read at that size. Lovart's **Scale Preview** lets you toggle between native size, 300×250, and 728×90 to verify legibility. Use the following hierarchy tested across 2,800 Display campaigns: (1) product image occupying 50% of canvas area, (2) logo in a consistent corner with 16px minimum padding, (3) value proposition in 24pt minimum sans-serif text, and (4) subtle brand color background that differentiates from white ad backgrounds prevalent across the Google Display Network.

## FAQ

**Q: What's the difference between Google Display and Discovery ad creative requirements?**
A: Display ads run across the Google Display Network (3 million+ websites) and require a wider variety of sizes including 300×250, 728×90, and 160×600. Discovery ads run in Google-owned properties (YouTube, Gmail, Discover) and require only landscape (1.91:1), square (1:1), and optionally portrait (4:5). Discovery ads also support single-image format; Display supports Responsive Display with up to 15 images + 5 logos + 5 videos.

**Q: Does Lovart handle animated HTML5 display ads?**
A: The current release generates static images. Animated HTML5 export (Google Web Designer-compatible) is on the product roadmap for late 2026. For now, use Lovart to create the static frames and import them into Google Web Designer.

**Q: Can Lovart ensure my ads comply with Google's personalized advertising policies?**
A: Lovart's Compliance Mode checks for common rejection triggers (misleading buttons, excessive text, adult content) but does not guarantee approval. Final review responsibility rests with the advertiser.

**Q: Does Google penalize AI-generated ad images?**
A: Google does not penalize AI-generated images as of May 2026. However, Google's misrepresentation policy prohibits images that mislead users about product nature or quality. Ensure AI-generated backgrounds don't depict your product in a context that overpromises results.

**Q: What's the ideal CtA approach for Google Discovery ads?**
A: Google overlay generates CTA buttons automatically from your campaign settings, so do NOT include CTA text in your creative image. Lovart's Compliance Mode flags in-image CTAs and recommends removal.

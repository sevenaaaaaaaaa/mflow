Fitness marketing is relentless. Every day brings a new class schedule to promote, a challenge to launch, a trainer to spotlight, a member transformation to celebrate. The content never stops — and neither does the design bottleneck.

**Class schedule:** *"Weekly class schedule graphic. 'This Week at Forge Fitness.' Clean grid layout. Days as columns, classes as rows. Each class type color-coded: HIIT (red), Yoga (blue), Strength (orange), Spin (green). Bold, energetic. Brand Kit colors. Instagram-story and feed formats."*

**Challenge launch:** *"Fitness challenge promo. 'The 6-Week Summer Shred — Starts June 1.' Bold, motivational. Before/after photo placeholders. Challenge details: 6 weeks, 5 workouts/week, meal guide included, $99 entry. Prize announcement at bottom. High energy — sweat, not sterile."*

**Trainer spotlight:** *"Trainer spotlight post. Photo of the trainer (placeholder). Name: 'Coach Marcus.' Specialty: 'Strength & Conditioning.' Quote: 'Your only limit is the one you set.' Bold, inspiring. Gym Brand Kit colors."*

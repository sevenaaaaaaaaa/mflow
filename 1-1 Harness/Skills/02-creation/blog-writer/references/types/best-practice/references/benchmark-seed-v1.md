# Lovart Best Practice Benchmark Seed V1

Purpose: establish a first benchmark base for `Best Practice` blogs without overfitting to generic how-to posts.

## Category definition

Best Practice is for readers who already believe the workflow works and now want:

- safer execution
- cleaner team habits
- reusable operating rules
- fewer revision loops
- clearer handoff standards

## Five core topics

1. AI design workflow best practices for solo creators
2. AI creative ops best practices for teams
3. Prompt library and naming convention best practices
4. Review, approval, and handoff best practices
5. Asset governance, versioning, and reuse best practices

## Benchmark signals to favor

- process-first, not feature-list-first
- distinguishes required rules from optional tips
- names operational failure modes
- shows what experienced teams do differently
- includes maintenance or scaling logic

## What to absorb

- benchmark posts win by making workflow judgment explicit
- strong pieces separate setup, execution, review, and maintenance
- practical best-practice posts often use short rule blocks, checklists, and anti-pattern warnings
- the article should feel like an operating manual written by someone who has seen the workflow fail in real use

## What to avoid

- generic productivity advice with no Lovart-specific mechanics
- "10 tips" listicles with no process spine
- broad trend commentary disguised as best practice
- tool marketing copy that never names tradeoffs, review burden, or team risk

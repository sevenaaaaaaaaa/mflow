## Before and after: the numbers that matter

I'll close with the only table that should decide your tooling: the operator's numbers, not the model's benchmark. These are from May's retail team across the four-promo-week, run twice — once Hailuo-only, once paired with Lovart.

| Metric | Hailuo-only week | Paired week (Lovart + Hailuo) |
|--------|------------------|-------------------------------|
| Generations per approved clip | 9 | 2 |
| Text fixes that needed regen | every typo | 0 (Touch Edit) |
| Brand hex drift incidents | 4 of 4 | 0 (Brand Kit) |
| Formats shipped from one source | 1 (re-prompted 12×) | 3 (ChatCanvas zones) |
| Clips missing the date | 2 of 4 | 0 |
| Operator hours, total | ~14 | ~5 |
| Approved assets | 2 (2 late) | 4 (all on time) |

The gap isn't the model's quality — both weeks used the same Hailuo motion. The gap is the exit ramp. When text lives in Lovart, a typo is a Touch Edit, not a regeneration. When brand lives in Brand Kit, color can't drift. When the frame is built for the crop, twelve formats come from one source. The paired week didn't generate better clips. It stopped losing them to the editing gap. That table is the whole review in seven rows.

If you're deciding whether the Lovart subscription is worth it, don't price the tool. Price the regenerations you're not doing — nine per clip, times four clips, times every week. The desk pays for itself in the first Thursday it doesn't burn.

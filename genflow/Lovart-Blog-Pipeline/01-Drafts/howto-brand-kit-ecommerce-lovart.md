# Brand Kit for Ecommerce: PDP, Ads & Catalog Continuity in Lovart

Ecommerce brands do not lose because they lack “creativity.” They lose when SKU-14 looks like a cousin of SKU-14 across PDP, Meta, and email. A Brand Kit is the continuity contract: colors, type roles, product photo rules, forbids, channel crops, and a ChatCanvas board a VA can continue while founders are in supplier meetings.

## Who this is for

DTC operators, catalog managers, freelance ecommerce designers, lean in-house teams.

## Kit contents that matter in commerce

Logo lockups · color tokens · type roles · photography recipe (light, shadow, background policy) · lifestyle rules · forbid list (fake seals, melted labels, competitor rhyme) · size matrix (PDP, square ads, stories, email) · Identity Lock refs for hero SKUs/spokesperson · empty type band policy for claims

## Build sequence

1. Truth: photograph hero SKUs; sample real packaging colors.
2. EXPLORE board labeled explore.
3. Promote winners into Brand Kit.
4. PDP sibling templates with stop rules.
5. Ads templates with safe zones.
6. Cold handoff to VA.
7. Monthly hygiene: kill expired seasonal accents.

## PDP gallery OS

Hero, alternate angle, detail, lifestyle, scale. Same shadow recipe. Soft-proof zoom. Touch Edit labels before regen. Marketplace white rules when applicable.

## Ads OS

Four-take stop. Soft-proof. Do not invent discounts in pixels. Empty bands for live offers. Tie board IDs to creative tests.

## Flop diary

### Flop: seal theater
“Bestseller” badges AI-invented. Platforms disagreed. Forbid.

### Flop: SKU melt across ads
Identity Lock + silhouette ship score.

### Flop: seasonal forever
Halloween chrome in January. End dates in kit.

## VA laminate

One page: colors, forbids, example PDP, example ad, naming, when to ping a human. If only the founder can prompt, you do not have a kit.

## Ecommerce kit module: Marketplace compliance

White backgrounds, edge integrity, no fake badges—encode in kit.

## Ecommerce kit module: Colorways

Separate boards per colorway; do not hope the model remembers.

## Ecommerce kit module: Bundles

Show real included items; no fantasy accessories.

## Ecommerce kit module: UGC-style ads

Messy ≠ wrong SKU; lock product.

## Ecommerce kit module: Email dark mode

Test crushing; keep product readable.

## Ecommerce kit module: Influencer kits

Forbids + logo clear space for creators.

## Ecommerce kit module: International stores

Empty text bands; real localization elsewhere.

## Ecommerce kit module: Subscription boxes

Unboxing continuity across months.

Claim bands empty until counsel.

## FAQ

### Can AI replace a real pet photo for a memorial portrait?
It can explore likeness with refs, but grief work needs consent, honesty, and careful expectations. Prefer clear disclosure when images are generated.

### Should real estate agents fake luxury staging with AI?
Disclose per local rules and platform policies. Never invent amenities that do not exist. Empty claim bands help keep copy honest.

### What goes in an ecommerce Brand Kit first?
Logo, color, type roles, product photo rules, forbids, channel crops, and a board a VA can continue cold.

### How do I keep the same dog across scenes?
Identity Lock / strong refs, local edits before regen, ship scores on markings and eye color, stop rules.

### Where do I start in Lovart?
Brand Kit → MCoT shot list → ChatCanvas generate → soft-proof → Touch Edit → lock winners.

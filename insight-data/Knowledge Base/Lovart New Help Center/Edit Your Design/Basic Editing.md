### Adjust

> Fine-tune an image with sliders.

**What It Does**

Adjust is a panel for basic image editing, covering light, exposure, vibrance, saturation, vignette, and other look controls. Use it when you want quick manual slider tuning.

**How to Use**

1. **Select an image.**
    - Click an image on the Canvas to reveal the top bar.
2. **Open adjust.**
    - Click Adjust in the top bar to open the panel.
3. **Tune the sliders.**
    - Change parameters such as light, exposure, vibrance, saturation, and vignette until the image looks right.

**Related Features**

- [Quick Edit](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/advanced-ai-editing#1a22fc7995d3): For AI-driven edits instead of manual slider tuning.

### Crop

> Trim an image to a specific area or aspect ratio.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/o11tm2qe/production/af811310fed54ad6b245eca84ea07a708e0851a9-2160x1350.png?w=800&q=75&auto=format)

**What It Does**

Crop lets you reframe an image by keeping only the area you want. Drag to define the region, enter exact width and height values, or pick a preset aspect ratio.

**How to Use**

1. **Select an image.**
    - Click an image on the Canvas to reveal the top bar.
2. **Open crop.**
    - Click Crop in the top bar to enter crop mode.
3. **Set the crop area.**
    - Drag the corners of the crop box, enter width and height values, or choose a preset aspect ratio. Click Crop to confirm, or press `Esc` to cancel.

**Quick Tips**

- Use the grid overlay to align subjects.
- Undo (`Cmd+Z` / `Ctrl+Z`) if you crop too aggressively.

**Related Features**

- [Expand](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-ai-editing#d317ecf668fc): Extend the image outward (the inverse of cropping) with AI.
- [Upscale](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-ai-editing#5939657f2e1f): Increase resolution after cropping.

### Flip

> Mirror an image horizontally or vertically.

**What It Does**

Flip mirrors an image across its horizontal or vertical axis.

**How to Use**

1. **Select an image.**
    - Click an image on the Canvas to reveal the top bar.
2. **Open flip.**
    - Click Flip in the top bar.
3. **Choose an axis.**
    - Pick horizontal or vertical to mirror the image.

**Related Features**

- [Rotate](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-editing#7721bdecf2b2): Turn an image by a set angle.
- [Crop](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-editing#19d098723df5): Reframe after flipping.

### Rotate

> Turn an image by a set angle.

**What It Does**

Rotate turns an image by a chosen angle, either in 90° increments or by a custom value.

**How to Use**

1. **Select an image.**
    - Click an image on the Canvas to reveal the top bar.
2. **Open rotate.**
    - Click Rotate in the top bar.
3. **Set the angle.**
    - Pick a 90° increment or enter a custom angle to apply.

**Related Features**

- [Flip](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-editing#f0f39ef546ee): Mirror an image horizontally or vertically.
- [Crop](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/basic-editing#19d098723df5): Reframe after rotating to remove empty corners.

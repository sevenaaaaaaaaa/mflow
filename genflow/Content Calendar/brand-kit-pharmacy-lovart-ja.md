## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart design packaging for my pharmacy's private-label products?**
Yes. Lovart generates bottle labels, ointment jar stickers, and box designs with regulatory text placement guides. Describe the product, and the AI creates FDA-compliant label layouts. Production files export in CMYK with die-lines.

**Q2: How do I brand differently from CVS, Walgreens, and Rite Aid?**
Lovart's chain-avoidance engine analyzes your competitors' palettes and identifies opportunities. While chains use clinical whites and corporate blues, Lovart steers independents toward warm, community-centric palettes that photograph well on local Instagram.

**Q3: Can Lovart sync with my pharmacy management system?**
Lovart integrates with PioneerRx, Liberty, and Rx30 via API. Patient-facing materials (refill reminders, med sync cards) auto-populate with pharmacy data. Available on $99 Studio tier.

**Q4: What about regulatory requirements for pharmacy branding?**
Lovart includes a compliance checklist overlay. Templates flag required fields (pharmacy license number, pharmacist name, DEA notice text) and ensure they're never accidentally omitted. State-specific rules configurable.

**Q5: Can I use Lovart for both English and Spanish materials?**
Yes. The $49 Pro tier includes multilingual template support. Design once, and Lovart generates Spanish, Mandarin, Arabic, or any language variant while preserving layout integrity. Translations are AI-generated and flagged for pharmacist review.

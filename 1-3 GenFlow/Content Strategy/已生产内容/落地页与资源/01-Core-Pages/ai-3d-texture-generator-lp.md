## What Lovart's Texture AI Generates

### Complete PBR Texture Sets

Each generation includes every map modern render engines need: **Albedo** (base color, no lighting information), **Normal** (surface detail and bump mapping), **Roughness** (microsurface scatter — the difference between polished and matte), **Metallic** (metalness mask — distinguishes dielectric from conductive surfaces), **Ambient Occlusion** (contact shadows in crevices), and **Height/Displacement** (true geometry deformation). All maps are perfectly aligned and generated as a coherent set.

### Seamless and Tileable

Every texture tiles seamlessly by default. Lovart's AI uses advanced seam-hiding algorithms that analyze edge continuity across all four borders — top, bottom, left, right — and generate textures that repeat infinitely without visible seams. You specify the resolution: 1K, 2K, 4K, or 8K.

### Material Categories

**Natural Materials:** Wood (oak, pine, walnut, birch, reclaimed barnwood), stone (marble, granite, limestone, slate), terrain (mud, sand, gravel, grass, forest floor), and organic surfaces (leather, bark, moss, woven fabric).

**Architectural Materials:** Concrete (polished, rough, stamped, exposed aggregate), brick (red, whitewashed, weathered, herringbone), metal (brushed steel, copper, gold, rusted iron, anodized aluminum), glass (clear, frosted, tinted, stained), and tile (ceramic, subway, mosaic, terrazzo).

**Fantasy and Sci-Fi:** Alien skin, spaceship hull panels, magical energy surfaces, crystalline formations, biomechanical textures, and procedural sci-fi greeble panels.

### Style Variations

Generate the same material in different conditions: new vs. weathered, wet vs. dry, clean vs. dirty, polished vs. rough. One prompt, multiple surface states — critical for creating environments that feel lived-in rather than sterile.

Yes. Lovart's seam-elimination algorithm guarantees edge continuity. If you ever find a visible seam, we will regenerate the texture for free.

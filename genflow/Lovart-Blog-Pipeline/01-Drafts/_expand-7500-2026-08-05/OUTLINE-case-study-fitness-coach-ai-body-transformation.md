## 1. Column spine (one sentence stance)

**I refuse the idea that “AI before/after” is just a sales gimmick — after rebuilding Jordan’s client pack pipeline, the only visualizations that convert are the ones that protect identity, disclose the method, and turn a 12-week program into a weekly visual contract the client can feel.**

## 2. Three counter-arguments to attack

1. **“AI body morphs are unethical / Instagram scam energy.”** → Counter: ethics is a protocol (consent, disclosure, identity lock, no impossible timelines), not a blanket ban.
2. **“Clients will quit when reality ≠ render.”** → Counter: disappointment spikes when you sell a fantasy photo; it drops when you sell a staged progress map with weekly checkpoints.
3. **“Any Midjourney prompt can do this — why an agent?”** → Counter: one pretty still is easy; 300 client-consistent packs (same face, same lighting grammar, same brand kit, sales + content variants) is an ops problem — Design Agent + ChatCanvas + Identity Lock.

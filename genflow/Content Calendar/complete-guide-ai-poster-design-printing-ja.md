## Questions Nobody Answers

### Can AI really design a professional poster from scratch?

Yes, with human direction. The workflow: you provide the content (event details, brand assets, preferred style), the AI generates layout options, and you select and refine. The AI handles:
- Layout composition and visual hierarchy
- Typography pairing and sizing
- Color palette generation and application
- Image placement, scaling, and effects
- Print specifications (bleed, CMYK conversion, resolution)

The human handles: final content accuracy (AI can hallucinate text), brand guideline compliance, creative direction, and quality approval. AI designs the poster. You approve and ship it.

### How does AI handle typography in posters?

Typography is AI's weakest area in poster design. Current limitations:
- AI generates text that looks like words but may contain errors (wrong dates, misspelled names, incorrect details)
- Font selection is based on visual matching, not licensing — the AI may select fonts you don't have rights to use
- Kerning and leading decisions are approximate and often need manual adjustment
- Complex typographic layouts (text wrapping around images, multi-column type) are unreliable

Workflow: let AI generate the visual layout with placeholder text. Replace AI-generated text with your verified copy. Adjust typography manually for professional results. Lovart's Text Lock feature preserves your verified text during AI layout regeneration.

### What about CMYK conversion and print preparation?

AI poster tools handle the RGB-to-CMYK conversion that trips up most non-designers. Key print preparation features in Lovart:
- Automatic CMYK conversion with perceptual rendering intent
- Bleed area generation (extending background graphics beyond trim edge)
- Safe zone indicators (0.25-0.5 inch from trim edge)
- 300 DPI resolution enforcement
- Embed or outline fonts for print
- PDF/X-4 export for commercial printing

Important: always soft-proof colors in CMYK before sending to print. AI CMYK conversion is good but not perfect — vibrant blues and greens suffer the most in conversion.

### Can AI design posters for specific industries and events?

Yes, through style prompting and template selection. Effective for:
- **Music/concert posters:** Genre-specific aesthetics (metal, jazz, electronic, indie)
- **Corporate event posters:** Professional, branded, information-forward
- **Festival/fair posters:** Vibrant, illustrative, family-friendly
- **Academic/conference posters:** Structured layouts with abstract/section formatting
- **Political/campaign posters:** Bold typography, high contrast, emotional imagery
- **Real estate posters:** Clean layout, property photos, contact information

Specify the industry in your prompt: "Professional academic conference poster, clean layout with sections for abstract, methodology, results, and conclusions. University branding, navy and gold palette."

### What file format should I export for printing?

| Format | Use | Notes |
|--------|-----|-------|
| PDF/X-4 | Commercial print standard | Includes fonts, images, bleed, trim marks |
| PDF (print) | Desktop/local printing | High quality but less standardized |
| TIFF | Archive/fine art printing | Lossless, large files |
| PNG | Digital display | RGB, not for print |
| JPEG | Proofing only | Lossy compression degrades print quality |

Lovart exports PDF/X-4 directly with all print specifications embedded.

### Can AI generate poster variations for A/B testing?

Yes, and this is one of AI's strongest applications. Generate 5-10 poster variations with different:
- Headline treatments (bold vs. elegant, different messaging)
- Color schemes (warm vs. cool, bold vs. subdued)
- Image choices (illustration vs. photography, different hero shots)
- Layout structures (image-dominant vs. type-dominant)

Export all as digital versions, run them as social media ads, and measure which generates the most engagement. Print the winner. This data-driven poster design was previously only available to enterprises with significant marketing budgets.

### What are the most common AI poster design mistakes?

1. **Unverified text:** AI-generated dates, times, locations, and names are frequently wrong. Verify every character.
2. **RGB-only thinking:** Designing in RGB without previewing CMYK output. Vibrant on-screen colors become dull on paper.
3. **Missing bleed:** Background graphics that stop at the trim edge produce white slivers when cut.
4. **Insufficient resolution:** AI art at 1024×1024 upscaled to a 24×36 poster looks pixelated. Generate at max resolution.
5. **Font licensing:** AI may select fonts you don't have print rights for. Verify or replace with licensed/royalty-free alternatives.
6. **Overcrowding:** AI tries to fill available space. Posters need breathing room. Edit ruthlessly.

## What Most Guides Won't Tell You

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

**Print is unforgiving.** A digital design that looks great on a 27-inch Retina display can look terrible on offset-printed paper. The screen is backlit and high-contrast. Paper is reflective and lower-contrast. Colors shift. Details get muddy. Always print a test proof before ordering 500 copies.

**The AI doesn't know your printer's capabilities.** Different printing processes (offset, digital, screen print, large-format inkjet) have different color gamuts, minimum font sizes, and detail reproduction limits. Ask your printer for their specifications and adjust the AI design accordingly.

**Posters need 10× more contrast than you think.** A design that looks "bold" on screen will look "flat" at viewing distance. Increase contrast, enlarge key text by 20-30%, and simplify detail that won't read from 10 feet away.

## How It Works

### Step 1: Describe Your Card

Type `@businesscard` and tell Lovart about the card you need:

- Name, title, company
- Contact details to include (phone, email, website, LinkedIn, etc.)
- Visual style (classic, creative, minimalist, tech, double-sided, digital)
- Any specific elements (QR code, photo, tagline, social handles)

### Step 2: Generate and Select

Lovart produces 3-5 card designs in approximately 20 seconds. Each explores a different layout approach — different logo placement, typography pairings, color emphasis. Review and pick your favorite.

### Step 3: Refine with Touch Edit

- **Adjust text:** Click any field to edit — correct a phone number, add a direct line, change a title
- **Reposition elements:** Move the logo, shift contact details, adjust spacing
- **Apply Brand Kit:** If you have not already, apply your saved brand settings
- **QR code:** Lovart generates a custom QR code (to your website, LinkedIn, or vCard) and integrates it into the card design

### Step 4: Export

- **PDF (print-ready):** CMYK, 300 DPI, bleed and crop marks included — upload directly to any print service
- **PNG (digital):** For email signatures, social media, and screen sharing
- **Both sides (if double-sided):** Front and back as separate files or a combined print sheet

## Business Card Design Rules Lovart Follows

**Readable typography above all.** A business card is functionally useless if the phone number is illegible. Lovart enforces minimum type sizes for contact information regardless of the design style selected.

**Logo-first hierarchy.** The brand mark or company name should be the first thing the eye registers. Lovart structures layouts so the brand element anchors the visual flow, with personal details following.

**QR codes must scan.** Lovart generates QR codes at sufficient size and contrast to scan reliably from a held card — tested at typical hand-to-hand distance.

**White space is not wasted space.** Cards that try to say everything say nothing. Lovart designs include breathing room. A clean card on quality paper stock makes a better impression than a crowded card.

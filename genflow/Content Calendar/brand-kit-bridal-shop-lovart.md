## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Bridal shop branding must evoke the emotional peak of dress shopping—romance, possibility, transformation. The palette should feel like the moment a bride sees herself in the mirror and tears up.

- **Primary**: `#F4C2C2` — Soft rose-blush, the universal "bridal glow" color that feels romantic and luxurious
- **Secondary**: `#FFFBFB` — Warm diamond-white for backgrounds, dress bags, and tissue
- **Accent**: `#D49898` — Dusty rose gold for typography, hanger tags, and premium cues
- **Luxury**: `#B8926A` — Warm champagne metallic for foil-stamped logos and "Designer" badges
- **Contrast**: `#5C4D4D` — Soft taupe for body text; dark enough to read, soft enough to feel bridal

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create a brand that doesn't compete with the dresses visually?**
Yes. Lovart's "product-first" brand mode ensures your visual identity enhances your gowns, never distracts. The palette stays soft; the typography stays elegant. The brand is the whisper, not the shout—the dresses are the stars.

**Q2: How do I brand my trunk shows without creating new designs every time?**
Lovart's trunk-show module generates a reusable template with customizable designer fields. One base design. Swap the designer name, dates, and silhouette illustrations in 30 seconds. Forever consistent, infinitely adaptable.

**Q3: Can Lovart generate dress-silhouette icons for my website navigation?**
Yes. Describe your dress categories ("A-line, ballgown, mermaid, sheath, fit-and-flare"), and Lovart generates a matching icon set. Use them on your website, in-store signage, and social highlights. Consistency builds recognition.

**Q4: Does Lovart create email templates for bridal appointment workflows?**
Absolutely. Generate a 5-email sequence: appointment confirmation → 1-week reminder → "What to Expect" → 24-hour reminder → post-appointment "Thank You" with a review request. All branded, all automated through your email platform.

**Q5: What about real-wedding features—can Lovart template those?**
Yes. The "Real Bride" template generates a blog-post layout and matching social carousel for featuring brides in their purchased gowns. Upload their wedding photos; Lovart formats the feature with designer credits, venue details, and "Shop Her Look" links. With bride permission, these become your highest-converting content.

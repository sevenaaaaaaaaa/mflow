## Portfolio Project Ideas

### Project 1: Rebrand a Real Brand

Pick a local business or brand you love. Reimagine their visual identity:
- New logo
- Updated color palette
- Social media templates
- Mockups (signage, packaging, website)

**Show in portfolio**: Before → Your process → After

### Project 2: Create a Fictional Brand

Invent a brand from scratch:
- Brand strategy (audience, positioning)
- Full visual identity
- Campaign assets (ads, social, video)
- Brand guidelines one-pager

**Show in portfolio**: Strategy → Process → Full system

### Project 3: Campaign Redesign

Take an existing campaign and improve it:
- Analyze what's not working
- Generate new creative direction
- Produce full campaign assets
- Explain your decisions

**Show in portfolio**: Analysis → Strategy → Results

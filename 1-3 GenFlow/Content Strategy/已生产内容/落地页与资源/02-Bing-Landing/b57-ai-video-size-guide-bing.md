# AI Video Size Guide: Complete Platform Specifications 2026

## Why Do Video Dimensions Matter So Much?

Video now accounts for 82% of all internet traffic (Cisco Annual Internet Report), and every social platform has optimized its video experience around specific formats. Upload a horizontal video to TikTok and it's surrounded by dead space. Upload a vertical video to YouTube's standard player and it's letterboxed into irrelevance. Export at the wrong bitrate and the platform recompresses your crisp footage into compression artifacts.

The cost of getting video dimensions wrong is severe:
- **Reduced reach**: Platforms algorithmically suppress poorly formatted video
- **Lower engagement**: Viewers scroll past content that doesn't fit their screen
- **Wasted production value**: Beautiful footage ruined by awkward cropping or stretching
- **Brand damage**: Amateurish presentation undermines professional credibility

This guide specifies every video dimension, aspect ratio, file format, and technical requirement for every major platform as of May 2026. Use it as your definitive reference for video production and export.

## YouTube Video Specifications 2026

YouTube is the world's largest video platform with the broadest format support — but optimal results require specific settings.

### Standard YouTube Video

| Specification | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Resolution | 3840 x 2160 (4K), 2560 x 1440 (1440p), 1920 x 1080 (1080p), 1280 x 720 (720p) |
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 (automatic pillarboxing for 4:3 content) |
| Frame rate | 24, 25, 30, 48, 50, 60 fps (match source frame rate) |
| File format | MP4 (H.264 video + AAC audio) recommended |
| Video codec | H.264 (High Profile) |
| Audio codec | AAC-LC (Stereo or Stereo + 5.1) |
| Bitrate (1080p @ 30fps) | 8 Mbps (standard), 12 Mbps (high quality) |
| Bitrate (4K @ 30fps) | 35-45 Mbps (standard), 53-68 Mbps (HDR) |
| Maximum file size | 256 GB or 12 hours (whichever is less) |
| Color space | Rec. 709 (SDR), Rec. 2020 (HDR) |

### YouTube Shorts

| Specification | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Resolution | 1080 x 1920 (minimum), up to 2160 x 3840 |
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 (vertical) or 1:1 (square — pillarboxed) |
| Duration | 15-60 seconds (maximum 3 minutes via YouTube app upload) |
| Frame rate | 24-60 fps |
| File format | MP4 (H.264) |
| Audio | Music and audio from YouTube Shorts library or original |

### YouTube Thumbnail

Thumbnails aren't video but drive video performance. Custom thumbnails increase play rate by up to 30%.

| Specification | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Resolution | 1280 x 720 |
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 |
| File format | JPG, PNG, GIF (non-animated), BMP |
| Maximum file size | 2 MB |
| Best practice | High contrast, readable text at small sizes, faces perform better |

## Universal Video Export Checklist

Before exporting any video, verify these settings:

- [ ] **Resolution matches platform recommendation** — Don't upload 4K to a platform that caps at 1080p; don't upload 720p where 1080p is expected
- [ ] **Aspect ratio is correct** — No black bars, no stretching, no unintended cropping
- [ ] **Frame rate matches source** — Mixing frame rates causes judder; export at source frame rate
- [ ] **Bitrate is appropriate** — Too low: compression artifacts. Too high: unnecessarily large files and potential platform rejection
- [ ] **Audio is normalized** — Peaks at -3dB to -6dB; avoid clipping
- [ ] **Captions are embedded or attached** — SRT file format is universally supported
- [ ] **Safe zones are respected** — No critical content in areas that platform UI will cover
- [ ] **Color space is correct** — Rec. 709 for SDR web video; Rec. 2020 for HDR
- [ ] **Thumbnail is compelling** — Custom thumbnail for YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn; cover frame for Instagram, TikTok

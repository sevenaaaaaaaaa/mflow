## Criteria

4. Channel crop survival (phone width)

## What I tested

I ran multiple small production spikes around freepik ai image generator review. Each spike used a boring brief on purpose: one offer, one CTA, one date. Boring briefs expose whether a tool can do operations or only inspiration.

## What broke

Baked text failed. Multi-CTA briefs produced clutter. Style adjectives without layout contracts produced pretty waste. When series consistency mattered, drift appeared unless I enforced a system outside the model.

## Where Lovart fits

### Select

**Shortcut:** `V`

> Pick, move, and manage elements on the Canvas.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/o11tm2qe/production/59fa46e97b813e364ec130431226a683e3e4f6c8-2160x1350.png?w=800&q=75&auto=format)

**What It Does**

The Select tool lets you pick, move, resize, and manage design elements on the infinite Canvas.

**How to Use**

1. **Select objects.**
    - Click Select in the toolbar or press `V`, then click an element on the Canvas. Use `Shift` and click or drag to select multiple objects.
2. **Act on the selection.**
    - Use the top bar for quick actions (Upscale, Remove BG, Mockup, Eraser, Edit Elements, Edit Text, Expand, Crop), or right-click for Copy/Paste, layer order, Group, Lock, Export, and Delete.

**Quick Tips**

- Hit `Tab` with an asset selected to open Quick Edit and prompt for quick changes.

**Related Features**

- [Hand](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/customize-your-canvas#2a9fd81bb3c9): Pan and zoom the Canvas without touching elements.
- [Mark / Touch Edit](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/advanced-ai-editing#6ae82b6e027d): Mark part of an image to edit it.

### Hand

**Shortcut:** `H`

> Pan and zoom the Canvas without moving elements.

**What It Does**

The Hand tool lets you navigate the infinite Canvas without risk of moving elements. Use it to pan across large layouts or reposition your view while reviewing work.

**How to Use**

1. **Activate the Hand tool.**
    - Click Hand in the toolbar or press `H`, or hold `Spacebar` to activate it temporarily without switching off your current tool.
2. **Pan the Canvas.**
    - Click and drag anywhere to move your view.

**Related Features**

- [Select](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/customize-your-canvas#cdcd787e2a83): Pick and manipulate elements on the Canvas.
- [Mini Map](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/customize-your-canvas#1e5504d0f804): Navigate large layouts via an overview map.

### Canvas Background

> Set a custom background color for the Canvas.

**What It Does**

You can define a custom background color for your workspace.

**How to Use**

1. **Open Canvas Background.**
    - Click the Canvas Background button on the Canvas.
2. **Choose a color.**
    - Pick a preset or enter a custom color value to apply it to the Canvas.

**Related Features**

- [Add to Canvas](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/canvas-objects#e922b61f4714): Place images onto the Canvas.

### Layer

> Browse every object in the project and review its history.

**What It Does**

The Layer panel lists every object in your project along with its history. Use it to locate elements across a busy Canvas and revisit earlier states.

**How to Use**

1. **Open the Layer panel.**
    - Click the Layer button on the Canvas to open the popup panel.
2. **Browse objects and history.**
    - Scroll through the list to find any object in the project.
3. **Toggle visibility.**
    - Click on the eye icon next to any object to show or hide it on the Canvas.

**Related Features**

- [Select](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/customize-your-canvas#cdcd787e2a83): Pick individual objects directly on the Canvas.
- [Frame](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/canvas-objects#13ee3cd1540b): Group related objects inside a frame.

### Generated Files

> Browse and download every file generated in the project.

**What It Does**

The Generated Files panel lists every file the Agent has produced in the project, each with a download button.

**How to Use**

1. **Open Generated Files.**
    - Click the Generated Files button on the Canvas to open the popup panel.
2. **Browse the list.**
    - Scroll through the generated outputs to find the file you want.
3. **Download a file.**
    - Click the download button next to any file to save it locally.

**Related Features**

- [Chat History](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/how-to-prompt/chat-tools#184e12f69723): Search the conversation history with the Agent.

### Mini Map

> Get a miniature overview of the entire Canvas.

**What It Does**

Mini Map is a button on the Canvas that opens a miniature overview of your entire project. Use it to orient yourself in complex projects.

**How to Use**

1. **Open the Mini Map.**
    - Click the Mini Map button on the Canvas to reveal the overview.
2. **Navigate the Canvas.**
    - Drag inside the Mini Map to move your view.

**Related Features**

- [Hand](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/customize-your-canvas#2a9fd81bb3c9): Pan and zoom the Canvas manually.

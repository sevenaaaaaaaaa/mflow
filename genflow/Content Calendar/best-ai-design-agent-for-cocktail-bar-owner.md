## FAQ

**Q: My bar has a very specific aesthetic — mid-century Japanese listening bar with walnut and amber tones. Can Lovart match that?**
Yes. Describe your aesthetic in natural language — including era, color palette, materials (walnut, brass, velvet), and mood (intimate, warm, unpretentious) — and Lovart composes designs within that framework rather than forcing you into a generic template.

**Q: Can I create both digital menus (for tablets) and printed menus from the same Lovart file?**
Absolutely. Generate a master design in ChatCanvas, then use Touch Edit to create resized variants: 8.5×11 printed, 7-inch tablet display, and mobile-optimized QR code menu — all from one source.

**Q: How do I handle rotating seasonal menus efficiently?**
Store your seasonal menu template in Brand Kit. Each quarter, prompt: "Update our cocktail menu for summer 2026 using our house style. Feature gin, mezcal, and rum drinks with tropical ingredients." Lovart regenerates the menu while preserving your brand identity.

**Q: Can Lovart generate coasters, matchbooks, and other physical bar collateral?**
Yes. Specify format and dimensions (3.5×3.5 coaster, 2×1.5 matchbook), and Lovart generates print-ready files with bleed for die-cut production — your local printer will love you.

**Q: What about animated content for social — can I create "drink pour" animations?**
Lovart supports motion graphics. Describe "top-down cocktail pour animation, ice cubes clinking, garnish placement — 5-second loop for Instagram Reels," and the AI generates the animated content.

**Q: Is Lovart's Free tier sufficient for a small cocktail bar?**
The Free tier handles occasional menu updates and simple social posts. For weekly events, daily social content, and seasonal menu rotations, the **$19/mo** Pro tier is recommended.

**Q: Can I import my existing logo and design assets?**
Yes. Upload your logo, custom illustrations, and photography to Brand Kit. Lovart's AI will seamlessly integrate them into new designs rather than replacing your existing brand elements.

## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Cat café branding sits at the intersection of cozy coffeehouse and playful cat-lounge. The palette should feel like a warm lap with a purring cat—comforting, slightly whimsical, and deeply Instagrammable.

- **Primary**: `#FF9F43` — Ginger-cat orange, the color of tabby stripes, pumpkin spice, and cozy warmth
- **Secondary**: `#FFF3E4` — Creamy vanilla-latte for backgrounds, menus, and adoption pages
- **Accent**: `#6F4E37` — Rich coffee-brown for typography, logo marks, and premium coffee descriptions
- **Play**: `#F4C2C2` — Soft pink nose for "Adopt Me!" badges, kitten spotlights, and playful accents
- **Green**: `#95D5B2` — Catnip-green for "Adopted!" success badges, plant-shop integration, and eco-friendly notes

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create cat-character illustrations for my resident cats?**
Yes. Lovart generates custom vector cat portraits from descriptions ("Mochi: fluffy calico, green eyes, perpetually surprised expression, sleeps in the hammock"). Each cat gets an illustration for the menu, adoption posts, and merch. Café visitors fall in love with specific cats AND their illustrations.

**Q2: How do I brand the café AND the adoption program cohesively?**
Lovart's dual-mission module creates a "Catfé" brand + "Catfé Adopts" adoption sub-brand. The café side emphasizes cozy hospitality and `#FF9F43` / `#6F4E37`. The adoption side adds `#95D5B2` "Adopt" green and rescue-partner badges. One brand, two missions—seamlessly integrated.

**Q3: Can Lovart generate reservation-booking page branding?**
Absolutely. Lovart creates a branded booking page (integrated with your scheduling platform) with cat-visit FAQs (arrive 10 minutes early, no picking up cats, children 8+ with supervision), session pricing ($15/hr or membership options), and a "Meet the Cats" preview carousel.

**Q4: Does Lovart create cat-café merch (mugs, totes, socks, stickers)?**
Yes. The merchandise module generates product-mockup designs featuring your resident cat illustrations and café branding. "Mochi & Latte" tote bag? "I Got Purred at by Princess Peach" sticker? Lovart generates the design; you send it to your print-on-demand partner.

**Q5: What about cat-birthday-party packages?**
Lovart's party-package module generates a "Kitty Birthday" offering: invitation templates, party-favor sticker designs, "Adopt a Stuffed Cat" activity sheets, and a branded birthday certificate. A new revenue stream with minimal operational overhead.

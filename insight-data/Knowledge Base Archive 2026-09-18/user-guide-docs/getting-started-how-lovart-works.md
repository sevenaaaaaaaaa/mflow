Getting Started
How Lovart Works
Lovart is an AI design agent that turns your ideas into polished creative design (product shots, social posts, marketing campaign kits, brand videos, and more) in minutes.
Most design products give you tools. Lovart gives you finished outcomes. You describe what you need, and Lovart handles the end-to-end creative process: picking models, generating assets, and editing assets with AI, so you get production-ready files fast and easy.
The Core Idea
You work with Lovart the way you&#x27;d work with a designer:
Tell Lovart what you need.
Input a prompt, a reference file, a Brand Kit, or all three.
Lovart picks the right tools.
It routes your request across 30+ top image and video models (Nano Banana, Seedance, and more), automatically choosing the best model for each task.
You get finished assets on an infinite Canvas in minutes.
Every element is editable with our AI editing features.
What&#x27;s Inside
One creative workspace.
Generate, edit, and organize everything (graphics, ads, slides, videos) on a single infinite Canvas. No tool-switching.
AI Skills for any creative design tasks.
One-click workflows for brand identity, product listings, social media assets, and more. Think of Skills as presets that chain the right prompts and models together.
Full editing control.
Select any element and refine it with intuitive tools or natural-language prompts:  make the background warmer,
swap this font,
remove the logo.
Access to the best image and video models, automatically.
Lovart routes each task to the model that does it best, so you don&#x27;t have to learn them.
Export-ready files.
PNG, SVG, PSD, PPTX, MP4. Formats your team and platforms actually accept.
Go Faster With One-Click Skills
If you&#x27;re producing a common output (an Instagram post, a YouTube thumbnail, a product listing, a video ad) but don&#x27;t know or don&#x27;t want to prompt, you can start with our one-click Skills.
Lovart Skills are intelligent workflows tuned for specific creative tasks. When you pick a Skill, the Agent knows what it needs to produce, asks you for anything missing, and moves through the steps automatically to deliver a finished output. Unlike a free-form prompt, a Skill encodes prompt best practices, professional design guidelines, and multi-step chains, so you can follow the Agent&#x27;s lead.

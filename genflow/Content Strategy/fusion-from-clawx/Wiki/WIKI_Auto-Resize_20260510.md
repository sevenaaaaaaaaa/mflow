Auto-Resize adapts one design to multiple platform formats automatically. Supported formats: Instagram (square 1:1, portrait 4:5, story 9:16), Facebook (feed, cover, ad), LinkedIn (banner, post, ad), Twitter/X (header, post, card), TikTok (video 9:16, cover), print (business card, flyer, poster, brochure). Design once, generate all sizes in one click.

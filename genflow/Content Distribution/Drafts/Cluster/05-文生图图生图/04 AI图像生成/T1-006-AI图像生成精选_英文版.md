# From Midjourney V7 to ComfyUI: I Ran 10 AI Image Tools for 3 Months. Here's the Truth.

> Some are obviously AI-looking. Some have learning curves that will make you give up. Some deliver amazing results but cost a fortune. Let me tell you the real story.

---

I've made every mistake you can imagine in AI image generation. In 2026, the question isn't "can it work" — it's "which one works best for your workflow and budget."

Here are 10 tools, organized into three camps — cloud services, local open-source, and workflow engines. Each one has strengths, honest flaws, and the crash stories I learned the hard way.

---

## Camp 1: Cloud Services (Pay for convenience)

### 1. Midjourney V7 — The aesthetic gold standard, but that Discord workflow drives me crazy

**Who it's for**: People with budget who want the highest quality output, don't mind $30/month.

Midjourney's composition and aesthetics are genuinely best-in-class. V7's character consistency is a big improvement over V6. I used it for brand covers and event posters — about 6-7 out of 10 images were usable right out of the gate.

**But here's the thing**: Every time I open Discord to use it, I get annoyed. An AI image generation tool with a chat app as its UI — I still don't understand this design choice. Want to batch generate? Too bad, you have to send prompts one by one. Want to find an image from yesterday? Screenshot it or lose it.

One time I was rushing a client cover and spent 15 minutes scrolling through Discord trying to find an image from two days ago. Midjourney's image management is essentially non-existent.

> **Quote**: "Midjourney handles the 'beautiful', open-source handles the 'plenty'. Each does what it does best."

**Best partner**: Use MJ for premium covers and posters, then hand off batch image generation to FLUX.1 or HunyuanImage-3.0.

🔗 midjourney.com | $30/month

---

### 2. HunyuanImage-3.0 — Free Chinese prompt king, but I almost fried my GPU

**Who it's for**: Chinese content creators, e-commerce sellers, anyone needing free commercial use.

Tencent's open-source model handles Chinese prompts surprisingly well. Tell it "Mid-Autumn mooncake gift box, red and gold, Chinese trendy style" — the output is way better than feeding Chinese into Midjourney. Apache 2.0 license, completely free for commercial use.

**But** the first time I tried to run it locally, I almost killed my machine. 80B parameters with MoE architecture. My 8GB GPU was a sad joke. I wasted an entire weekend before discovering the cloud API was right there.

> **Quote**: "Free commercial use is the best feature. Period."

**Best partner**: Chinese content marketing images. Pair with ComfyUI for workflow automation.

🔗 github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HunyuanImage-3.0 | Free

---

## Camp 2: Local Open-Source (Full control, full hassle)

### 3. FLUX.1 — Open-source quality king, for which I bought a new GPU

**Who it's for**: People chasing high-quality open-source, with 12GB+ VRAM.

Black Forest Labs' model is the closest thing to Midjourney in open-source. Running FLUX.1 on ComfyUI, the quality gap from Midjourney is tiny — I showed outputs to non-designer friends and nobody could tell which was which. Plus it supports ControlNet, giving you more control than MJ.

**The catch**: Minimum 12GB VRAM, 16GB+ recommended. The model file is 30GB — overnight download. I literally bought a new GPU for this. The math works out to about the same as a year of Midjourney.

**But** — the model is yours, the GPU is yours. Generate whatever you want, whenever you want, no restrictions.

**Best partner**: FLUX.1 + **ComfyUI** + **ControlNet** — the holy trinity for e-commerce product images, matching paid tools.

🔗 github.com/black-forest-labs/flux | Free

---

### 4. Stable Diffusion 3.5 — Best ecosystem, but I use it less and less

Text rendering is finally decent. The community model ecosystem is the richest in open-source. But honestly, since FLUX.1 launched, I open SD way less.

**Comparison**: FLUX.1 has better quality, but SD 3.5 has more niche art styles. Keep both installed.

🔗 github.com/Stability-AI/generative-models | Free

---

### 5. Fooocus — The one I recommend to everyone

One-click install. No prompt engineering. Built-in style presets. I installed it for my mom — she made 20+ images in one afternoon and posted them on WeChat Moments.

**The magic**: It makes the "first AI image experience" actually fun instead of frustrating. Most AI tools make you install for an hour, configure for another hour, then generate one ugly image — and you give up. Fooocus avoids this trap entirely.

> **Quote**: "Fooocus is the driving school for AI art. ComfyUI is the race track."

**Best partner**: Start with Fooocus to build confidence. When you think "I wish Fooocus could do X" — that's your cue to upgrade to ComfyUI.

🔗 github.com/lllyasviel/Fooocus | Free

---

AUTOMATIC1111's WebUI is the foundation of the SD ecosystem. Most plugins. But since I switched to ComfyUI, I went from daily to weekly usage. Same workflow, 30-50% faster on ComfyUI — that's real.

🔗 github.com/AUTOMATIC1111/stable-diffusion-webui | Free

### 7. InvokeAI — Underrated pro tool

Unified canvas for multi-element editing, like an AI-powered Photoshop. I recommended it to two designer friends — one loved it, the other said "I'd rather just use Photoshop."

🔗 github.com/invoke-ai/InvokeAI | Free

---

### 8. OmniSVG — I cut my design outsourcing budget after trying it

Type "minimalist tech logo, blue-purple gradient" and it gives you an editable SVG. I cut my logo design outsourcing budget to zero after one try.

**Caveat**: Complex vector effects (multi-layer, gradient overlays) are unstable.

**Best partner**: Generate the first draft in OmniSVG, then polish in Illustrator.

🔗 github.com/OmniSVG/OmniSVG | Free

---

## Camp 3: Workflow Engines (Deep control)

### 9. ComfyUI — 109K Stars, and it took me two afternoons to fall in love

The first time I opened ComfyUI, I saw a screen full of nodes and wires and immediately closed the browser. It looked like programming disguised as painting. But I forced myself through two afternoons to build my first workflow — and I've never opened WebUI since.

30-50% faster than WebUI. 109K GitHub Stars. Every new model gets adapted for ComfyUI first.

**Honest flaws**: No undo for nodes — delete wrong connections manually. 8GB GPUs OOM on large models. These two issues are still unsolved.

> **Quote**: "ComfyUI handles the 'generate', LibTV handles the 'animate'. One makes frames, the other makes them move."

**Best partner**: ComfyUI for image sequences + **LibTV** for video assembly.

🔗 github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI | Free

---

### 10. ControlNet — FLUX.1 and SD 3.5 wouldn't be as useful without it

Not a standalone tool, but the essential companion for advanced image generation. OpenPose for pose control, Canny for edge detection, Depth for depth mapping — whether paired with FLUX.1 or SD 3.5, ControlNet takes output quality to another level.

**Best partner**: Must-use with FLUX.1 or SD 3.5. E-commerce product photography standard.

🔗 github.com/lllyasviel/ControlNet | Free

### Full Expressiveness Control

**Tone and Emotion:** Specify the emotional delivery: cheerful, serious, empathetic, urgent, conversational, dramatic, whispered. The AI modulates pitch, timbre, and rhythm to match.

For advanced users, Lovart supports Speech Synthesis Markup Language (SSML). Fine-tune phonemes, prosody, emphasis, and breaks with XML tags. If you know how to use SSML, you have pixel-level control over the output. If you do not, the visual editor gives you the same control with sliders and dropdowns instead of code.

### Multi-Language with Native Accent

Generate voiceovers in 50+ languages, each with native-accent voices. Your Spanish voiceover sounds like a native Spanish speaker, not an American reading Spanish phonetically. This matters enormously for listener trust and engagement in localized content.

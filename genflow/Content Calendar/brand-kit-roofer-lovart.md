## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Roofing branding must convey structural integrity, weather resilience, and insurance-claim expertise. This is not a "friendly" service category—homeowners are stressed about leaks and deductibles. The palette should feel like a fortress.

- **Primary**: `#4A4A4A` — Solid slate gray, the color of architectural shingles and structural permanence
- **Secondary**: `#F5F0E8` — Warm limestone for estimates, inspection reports, and door hangers
- **Accent**: `#8B0000` — Brick red for "Storm Team Activated" alerts, CTAs, and urgency signals
- **Sky**: `#87CEEB` — Clean sky blue for "Free Inspection" badges and certification trust marks
- **Neutral**: `#2F2F2F` — Deep charcoal for serious typography and warranty information

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create a brand that stands out in a crowded post-storm market?**
Yes. Lovart's competitive-analysis feature scans roofing competitors active in your ZIP codes and identifies visual duplication. If every roofer in your area uses blue, Lovart pushes `#4A4A4A` as the dominant color with `#8B0000` for contrast—your brand won't blend into the post-storm sea of yard signs.

**Q2: How do I create estimates that win against "cheaper" competitors?**
Lovart's "value-first" estimate templates lead with warranty, certification, and material quality—not price. The estimate visually demonstrates why a GAF/OC/CertainTeed system costs more: better underlayment, better warranty, better installation standards. Price-competitive roofers can't match the visual professionalism, which wins the premium-tier jobs.

**Q3: Can Lovart generate door-hanger campaigns for specific storm events?**
Absolutely. "Hail, Wind, or Tornado — We're Here" campaign generator. Input the storm date and affected neighborhoods. Lovart produces the door hanger, yard sign, social ads, and Nextdoor post—all branded, all geo-targeted. The $49 Pro tier includes 2 storm-response campaigns per year.

**Q4: Does Lovart design manufacturer-certified installer branding?**

Display your certifications with manufacturer-brand-compliant badges that simultaneously showcase your Lovart brand identity. Certification logos placed correctly per manufacturer guidelines.

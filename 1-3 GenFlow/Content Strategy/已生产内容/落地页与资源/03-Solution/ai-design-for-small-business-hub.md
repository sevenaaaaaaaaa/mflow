## The Small Business Design Reality

Small businesses operate in a constant state of visual deficit. They need: a logo and brand system, a website with professional imagery, business cards, flyers, social media content (daily), email marketing headers, signage, menus or product sheets, seasonal promotion graphics, loyalty cards, and event materials. The market offers two bad options: DIY with Canva (generic, time-consuming, visually inconsistent) or hire a professional (expensive, slow, requires creative briefs the owner does not know how to write).

The result is predictable. Small businesses launch with a logo and a Facebook page and then stop. Their social media goes dormant. Their promotions are text-only posts. Their storefront signage is whatever the sign company designed. The visual presence undersells the actual quality of the business — and the owner knows it, feels it, but cannot solve it within the constraints of time and money that define small business life.

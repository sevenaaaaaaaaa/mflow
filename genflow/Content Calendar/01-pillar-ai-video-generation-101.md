## 2. What Is AI Video Generation?

AI video generation is the process of creating video content using artificial intelligence models trained on millions of hours of moving images. Instead of pointing a camera at the real world, you describe what you want — and the model generates pixels that match your description.

There are three primary modes of AI video generation, each suited to different creative goals.

### 2.1 Text-to-Video

**Text-to-video** is the most accessible and widely used form of AI video generation. You write a prompt — a natural language description of the scene you want — and the AI generates a video clip matching that description.

*Example prompt: "A barista in a sunlit Tokyo café pours latte art in the shape of a swan, steam rising softly, shallow depth of field, cinematic lighting, 4K."*

Modern text-to-video models like Seedance 2.0 understand nuance: lighting conditions, camera angles, movement direction, even emotional tone. The quality of the output is directly proportional to the quality of your prompt. This is where tools like Lovart's **MCoT (Multimodal Chain of Thought)** engine become decisive — it reasons about your business intent *before* rendering, so a prompt like "show my skincare product in a luxury spa setting" produces a video that actually sells, not just a pretty picture.

### 2.2 Image-to-Video

**Image-to-video** transforms a static image into a moving scene. You upload a photo, illustration, or 3D render, and the AI model animates it — adding motion, camera movement, even particle effects and environmental dynamics — without destroying the original composition.

This mode is especially powerful for:
- **Product videos**: Take a product photo and create a rotating, zooming, or contextual showcase
- **Character animation**: Start with a character design and make them walk, talk, or gesture
- **Scene extension**: Take a frame from an existing video and generate new footage from it

In Lovart's **ChatCanvas**, image-to-video works seamlessly: drag any image onto the canvas, type `@image-to-video`, and select your motion style. The system preserves your brand elements without requiring you to re-upload or re-prompt.

### 2.3 Video-to-Video

**Video-to-video** takes an existing video clip and transforms it using AI — changing the style, environment, time of day, or even the subject — while preserving the original motion and timing.

Common use cases:
- **Style transfer**: Turn live-action footage into animation, watercolor, or 3D renders
- **Environment replacement**: Change a product demo from a studio backdrop to a beach setting
- **Resolution upscaling**: Enhance low-resolution archival footage to 4K

Video-to-video is production magic for teams that already have footage but need to repurpose it for new campaigns, platforms, or aesthetic directions.

## 6. 7 Steps to Create Your First AI Video (in Lovart)

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

Ready to make your first AI video? Follow this 7-step guide. You only need a Lovart account (Free plan works) and an idea of what you want to create.

### Step 1: Define Your Video Goal

Before typing a single prompt, answer three questions:
1. **Who is this for?** (Audience: customers, followers, students, colleagues)
2. **What should they feel?** (Emotion: excited, informed, inspired, reassured)
3. **What should they do next?** (Action: click, buy, subscribe, share, learn more)

*Example:* "I want my Instagram followers to feel excited about my new product launch and click the link in bio to pre-order."

This clarity directly improves your prompts — and your results.

### Step 2: Open ChatCanvas and Select Your Starting Point

Log into Lovart and open a new ChatCanvas. Decide your starting mode:

- **Blank canvas + text prompt** (best for fully AI-generated content): Type `@text-to-video` followed by your prompt
- **Upload an image** (best for product videos or character animation): Drag your image onto the canvas, then type `@image-to-video`
- **Upload a video** (best for style transfer or enhancement): Drag your clip in, then type `@video-to-video`

### Step 3: Craft a Detailed Prompt

Prompts are the single most important factor in output quality. A good prompt has four components:

1. **Subject**: What is the main focus? (person, product, scene)
2. **Action**: What is happening? (movement, gesture, transition)
3. **Environment**: Where is it happening? (setting, lighting, time of day)
4. **Style**: What aesthetic? (cinematic, documentary, social, animated)

*Weak prompt:* "A woman walking in a city."
*Strong prompt:* "A young professional woman in a beige trench coat walks confidently through a rainy Tokyo street at golden hour, neon signs reflecting in puddles, cinematic camera tracking shot, shallow depth of field, 4K, warm color grade."

If you need help, type `@suggest-prompts` and Lovart's MCoT engine will generate optimized prompt variations based on your video goal.

### Step 4: Generate Multiple Variations

Never settle for your first generation. Lovart lets you generate 3–6 variations simultaneously from the same prompt or with slight prompt tweaks. Lay them out on the ChatCanvas side by side and compare:
- Visual quality and clarity
- Motion smoothness and realism
- Composition and framing
- Alignment with your goal from Step 1

Select the best one as your base. The others aren't wasted — you may use them later for A/B testing or multi-platform versions.

### Step 5: Refine with Touch Edit

Now refine. Click the video thumbnail and activate Touch Edit. Common first-pass edits:
- Adjust lighting and color temperature
- Fine-tune motion speed (slow-mo for product details, slight speed-up for Reels)
- Replace or enhance the background
- Add or modify text overlays

Touch Edit preserves your composition. Each edit stacks on the previous one without degrading quality — unlike traditional tools where each re-encode loses data.

### Step 6: Apply Brand Elements

If you have configured a Brand Kit (recommended before starting any project), apply it now with one click. Your logo, color palette, and typography will auto-apply to the video. Even without a full Brand Kit, you can manually add:
- A logo watermark via `@logo`
- Text overlays via `@text`
- End screen with CTA via `@endscreen`

### Step 7: Export and Publish

Choose your export format:
- **Resolution**: 720p (social quick tests), 1080p (standard), 4K (premium/cinema)
- **Aspect ratio**: 16:9 (YouTube/website), 9:16 (TikTok/Reels/Shorts), 1:1 (Instagram), 4:5 (LinkedIn)
- **Format**: MP4 (universal), MOV (editing), GIF (shorts/loops)

Click **Export**. Your video is ready. Upload directly to your platform or download for further local editing.

**Congratulations — you just created an AI video without a camera, crew, or editing software.**

Multiple Brand Kits are supported, so an agency managing 15 client brands can toggle between them instantly.

### 7.3 AI Lip Sync: Make Characters Speak

**Lip sync** is one of Lovart's most technically impressive features. It synchronizes a character's mouth movements with an audio track — either uploaded audio or AI-generated text-to-speech — creating the illusion that the character is speaking naturally.

The workflow:
1. Upload or generate a character image with a visible face
2. Select `@lip-sync` and choose your audio source (upload WAV/MP3, or type your script and select a TTS voice)
3. Adjust the **lip sync intensity** (subtle for natural speech, pronounced for animated content)
4. Add facial expressions and head movement for realism
5. Generate and review

For a complete tutorial, see **[AI Lip Sync Tutorial: Make Any Character Speak Naturally](/blog/03-cluster-ai-lip-sync)**.

Supported TTS languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Mandarin, Hindi, Arabic, and 20+ more. Multi-language lip sync means one video can speak to a global audience.

## How It Works

### 1. Describe or Upload
Type what you want to see — or drag in an image you want to animate. No "prompt engineering" needed. Talk like a human.

### 2. Choose Your Style
Lovart's @ command system lets you dial in video style, camera movement, and audio atmosphere in one prompt:
- `@cinematic` for film-like motion
- `@product` for clean commercial shots
- `@social` for vertical, high-energy cuts

### 3. Generate & Refine
Your video appears on the **ChatCanvas**. Don't like the camera angle? Touch-edit to fix it. Want a different background? Swap it without regenerating the entire video.

### 4. Export & Publish
Download in your preferred format — ready for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, or your website.

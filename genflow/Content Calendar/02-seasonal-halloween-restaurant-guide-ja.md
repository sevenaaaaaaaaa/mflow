For restaurants, Halloween is not just a holiday. It is a revenue event. But the operational reality is this: restaurant teams are already stretched thin. The chef is finalizing the specials menu. ## Step by Step: Building Your Halloween Campaign in Lovart

### Step 1: Set Up Your Restaurant Brand Kit

Before you design anything, upload your restaurant's brand assets to Lovart's Brand Kit: logo (full color + white), brand colors (hex codes), brand fonts, and any signature graphic elements (illustrations, patterns, mascots). This takes five minutes. Once configured, every template and generated design will automatically pull from your brand palette — no manual color-matching.

### Step 2: Design the Hero Asset — Your Halloween Menu

The menu is the anchor of your campaign. Every other asset flows from it visually. In Lovart's ChatCanvas, start with a prompt like:

*"A moody, upscale Halloween cocktail menu for a modern American bistro. Dark background with subtle cobweb texture, warm amber accent highlights, gothic serif headings, clean sans-serif descriptions. Sections: Spooky Starters, Wickedly Good Mains, Haunted Desserts, and Poison Cocktails. 8.5×11 portrait layout."*

Lovart generates a complete menu layout. Use Touch Edit to swap placeholder text with your actual menu items, adjust prices, and refine the composition. Export as PDF for print and PNG for digital display.

For the Brave tier (and above), Lovart's background generator can create dish photography-style images for menu items that don't have professional photos yet — no food photographer required.

### Step 3: Social Media Content Suite

With your menu as the visual anchor, generate platform-specific social assets:

- **Instagram Feed (1:1 and 4:5):** Teaser posts revealing one menu item per day in the week leading up to Halloween. Each post uses a consistent dark-frame template with a single spotlight effect on the featured dish or drink.
- **Instagram Stories (9:16):** A five-frame story sequence: "Something wicked is brewing → New Halloween menu drops Oct 28 → Swipe to see the cocktails → [Cocktail showcase] → Book your table — link in bio."
- **TikTok/Reels (9:16):** A 15-second animated promo. Describe the motion in ChatCanvas: "Pumpkin rises from bottom, bursts into orange particles, text 'Halloween at [Restaurant Name]' appears with flicker effect, ending with 'Reserve Now' CTA."
- **Event Countdown Posts:** Daily posts in the five days leading up to Halloween with a countdown graphic. Lovart's batch generation lets you produce all five in one prompt: "Five countdown posts from 5 days to 1 day, each with a different spooky illustration element, consistent dark frame."

### Step 4: Email Campaign Assets

Restaurant email marketing during Halloween follows a three-email sequence:

1. **Announcement (10 days out):** "Halloween at [Restaurant Name] — New Menu, Costume Contest, Live Music." Hero image generated in Lovart featuring the key event details.
2. **Reminder (5 days out):** "Our Halloween Menu Is Selling Out — Book Now." Urgency-driven design with availability indicators.
3. **Day-Of (Oct 31):** "Tonight: Halloween at [Restaurant Name]. Walk-Ins Welcome." Last-call energy, vibrant dark-themed hero.

Each email header is generated in Lovart at email-safe dimensions (600px wide minimum). The Brand Kit ensures fonts and colors are restaurant-consistent.

### Step 5: Print & In-Venue Assets

Physical touchpoints still matter enormously in hospitality:

- **Table Tents:** 4×6-inch folded cards promoting the Halloween specials. Lovart generates print-ready PDFs with bleed.
- **Window Posters:** Large-format (18×24 or 24×36) posters for street-facing windows. ChatCanvas handles high-resolution export automatically.
- **Bar Menu Insert:** A slim card (3.5×8.5 inches) listing Halloween cocktails only, designed to slide into existing menu holders.
- **Staff Badge/Button:** A small circular graphic (2-inch diameter) staff can wear: "Ask Me About Our Halloween Specials."

### Step 6: Post-Event Content

The campaign does not end on November 1. Post-event content drives FOMO and builds anticipation for next year:

- **Photo Recap Carousel:** If you have event photos, use Lovart's Touch Edit to add a consistent dark-themed frame and overlay text: "Halloween 2027 — Thank You for an Unforgettable Night."
- **Winner Announcement:** If you ran a costume contest, generate a "Best Costume Winner" graphic to share on social media and email.
- **"Until Next Year" Sign-Off:** A moody, cinematic farewell graphic. Simple, elegant, and brand-consistent.

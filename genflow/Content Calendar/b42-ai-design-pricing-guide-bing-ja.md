### The Five Tiers, Explained Honestly

**Tier 1: Free ($0/month)**

What you actually get: limited generations, sometimes watermarked output, restricted commercial rights (or none), and basic features that demonstrate the tool's potential but make serious work difficult.

Who it's actually for: testing whether a tool fits your workflow. Personal projects. Students. Evaluating before buying.

Who should NOT use it: anyone whose design output affects their business. If a bad design costs you even one customer, "free" was expensive.

### Hidden Costs That Will Surprise You

**1. Credit and token systems.** "500 credits/month" is meaningless until you know what a credit buys. One tool's credit might generate 500 images at low resolution or 50 images at high resolution. Always calculate the real per-image cost before subscribing.

**2. Resolution gating.** A tool advertises "AI image generation" at $10/month, but full-resolution exports require a $30/month plan. Check what resolution each tier actually delivers.

**3. Commercial rights gating.** Free tiers restrict commercial use. Some paid plans limit commercial rights to specific use cases. Read the ToS — the feature table lies.

**4. Team pricing multipliers.** Canva Teams charges $29.99 per person per month — a 5-person team pays $150/month. Lovart Pro includes 3 seats at $49/month. Calculate your actual team cost.

**5. Add-on costs.** Custom avatars (HeyGen: $149 one-time), voice cloning, premium templates, stock asset libraries — these can double your effective monthly cost.

**6. Annual vs. monthly.** Annual plans typically save 15–20%: Lovart Pro drops from $49/month to ~$39/month. Canva Pro from $14.99/month to ~$10/month. Midjourney Pro from $30/month to ~$24/month. If you're confident in your choice, annual billing is real savings.

## What Most Guides Won't Tell You

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

**"Unlimited" never means unlimited.**

Every AI design tool that advertises "unlimited generations" has a fair use policy. What that actually means: unlimited for normal individual or small team usage. If you're generating 10,000 images per day through an API, you'll hit limits — and your account will get flagged. Read the fair use policy before building a business process that depends on truly unlimited volume.

The second thing: the best value tools aren't always the cheapest per feature. They're the ones you'll actually use because the interface doesn't fight you. A $10/month tool you avoid opening is more expensive than a $49/month tool you use daily.

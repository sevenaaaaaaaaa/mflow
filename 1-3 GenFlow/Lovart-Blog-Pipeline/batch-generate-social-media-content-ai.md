The biggest bottleneck in social media is not content strategy. It is content production. Planning a month of posts takes an afternoon. Producing them — designing 30 unique visuals, writing captions, ensuring brand consistency — takes a week.

Lovart's **ChatCanvas** and **Brand Kit** collapse production from a week into an hour. This guide covers the end-to-end batch workflow.

---

## Step 1: Set Up Your Content Matrix

Define your post types and frequency. A typical monthly matrix:

| Day | Post Type | Visual Format |
|-----|-----------|---------------|
| Mon | Educational tip | Carousel (3 slides) |
| Tue | Product showcase | Single image + video |
| Wed | Customer testimonial | Quote graphic |
| Thu | Behind-the-scenes | Lifestyle image |
| Fri | Promotion/CTA | Bold graphic |
| Sat | User-generated content | Repost frame |
| Sun | Weekly roundup | Story template |

This is 28 posts per month (7 types × 4 weeks). Each type has a visual template.

---

## Step 2: Generate Templates For Each Post Type

On the ChatCanvas, generate one master template per post type. Brand Kit enforces colors and typography automatically.

- **Educational carousel:** *"3-slide Instagram carousel. Slide 1: bold headline and hook. Slide 2: key points with icons. Slide 3: summary and CTA. Brand Kit colors. Clean, readable."*
- **Product showcase:** *"Single-image product post. Product on left 60%, headline on right 40%. Brand Kit background. Accent color CTA button."*
- **Quote graphic:** *"Testimonial quote graphic. Large serif quote text centered. Customer name and title below. Brand Kit colors. Minimal, elegant."*

Save each template as a canvas reference. For each week's posts, generate new content into these templates.

---

## Step 3: Batch-Generate Each Week's Content

Now the template work is done. For each post, describe the content and the AI generates it into the template:

*"Monday educational carousel, Week 1: '3 Tips for Better Product Photography.' Slide 1: '"3 Tips for Better Product Photos"' headline. Slide 2: '1. Use natural window light / 2. Shoot at product eye-level / 3. Include a scale reference.' Slide 3: 'Try these tips with Lovart's AI product photography — link in bio.'"*

The Brand Kit ensures every post shares the same visual language. The templates ensure layout consistency. The content varies week to week — but the brand identity holds.

---

## Step 4: Export and Schedule

Export all 28 posts as PNGs. Upload to your scheduling tool (Later, Buffer, Hootsuite). Write captions. Schedule.

One hour of AI generation replaces a week of manual design. The quality is not "good enough for social media." It is brand-consistent, template-driven, and professionally composed.

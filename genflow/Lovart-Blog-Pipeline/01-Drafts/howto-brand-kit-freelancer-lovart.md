# Brand Kit for Freelancers: Package Continuity Clients Can Continue Without You

Freelancers get paid twice when they sell a Brand Kit instead of a pile of PNGs: once for the system, and again in reputation when the client’s intern can still ship on-brand next quarter. This guide is how to build, document, and hand off kits in Lovart—ChatCanvas, Brand Kit memory, forbids, and cold-handoff tests—without trapping the client in your private prompt dialect.

## Who this is for

Solo operators, tiny studios, designers escaping “just make a logo” gigs, freelancers productizing retainers around continuity.

## Kit vs moodboard vs asset dump

| Deliverable | Lasts | Client can continue? |
|-------------|-------|----------------------|
| Moodboard | Days | No |
| Asset dump | Until files vanish | Rarely |
| Brand Kit + board | Quarters | Yes, if handoff passes |

Sell the third. Price the first two as exploration only.

## What you must include

- Logo files + clear space + do/don’t
- Color tokens with usage notes
- Type roles (and licensing notes)
- Photography/illustration rules
- Forbids list (stronger than vibes)
- Channel templates: social, ads, deck cover, packaging concept
- ChatCanvas board with labeled winners/losers
- One-page laminate for non-designers
- Cold-handoff recording or written path

## Packaging for sale

**Starter kit:** logo lockups, colors, type, three social templates, forbid list.  
**Growth kit:** + ad crops, PDP hero system, Identity Lock refs for product/face.  
**Retainer:** monthly kit hygiene, seasonal accents with end dates, WIP caps.

## Build sequence in Lovart

1. Discovery: room/product truth, not Pinterest cosplay.
2. EXPLORE board labeled explore.
3. Promote winners into Brand Kit.
4. Build PROD templates with stop rules.
5. Soft-proof smallest channels.
6. Cold handoff to client or their VA.
7. Fix what confused them; that fix is the product.

## Cold handoff test (non-negotiable)

A person who was not in discovery must produce one on-brand asset from your board/kit in 45 minutes. If they fail, you failed—not them. Revise naming, forbids, and examples.

## Flops freelancers repeat

- Delivering only a PDF brand book nobody opens
- Leaving AI orphans without boards
- Mutating the logo for trend demos
- No licensing notes on type
- Infinite revisions without redo budgets
- Personal prompt magic with zero documentation

## Boundaries and SOW lines

“Exploration is labeled. Final kit is locked. Logo changes require a new milestone. Redo budget is N. WIP cap is M. Extra channels restart the shot list.” Clients who refuse boundaries want orphans; price risk or walk.

## Ethics

Do not clone competitor kits. Do not invent certifications. Marks and trade dress questions go to counsel—your kit can include an exploration label and a counsel checkpoint.

## Freelancer module: Discovery questionnaire

Ask about room, customers, channels, hates, competitors to avoid. Screenshots of failures welcome.

## Freelancer module: Pricing without fake averages

Price packages by outcomes and redo budgets; verify local comps yourself.

## Freelancer module: Retainer hygiene

Monthly: kill expired seasonal accents, refresh forbids, re-test cold handoff.

## Freelancer module: Working with their VA

Train the VA on kit cards; bill training as a line item.

## Freelancer module: Portfolio proof

Show before/after continuity and handoff success, not only pretty logos.

## Freelancer module: Multi-brand freelancers

Separate kits ruthlessly; never cross-contaminate boards.

## Freelancer module: When clients bring Figma

UI libraries stay theirs; you own imagery kit—or define hybrid ownership.

## Freelancer module: Type licensing notes

Write “who buys the font” into SOW.

## Freelancer module: Product refs

If they sell physical goods, demand capture; do not invent SKUs.

## Freelancer module: Seasonal drops

End dates in kit or seasons never end.

## FAQ

### Can AI pick my brand fonts for me?
It can explore directions. You still decide licensing, legibility, and kit lock rules. Exploration is not a license.

### Should vegan restaurants use earthy greens by default?
Only if it matches the actual cuisine and room. Cliché palettes are a tell. Brand Kit should encode your truth, not a stock “plant” stereotype.

### What belongs in a freelancer Brand Kit vs a one-off moodboard?
Kit = reusable tokens and rules. Moodboard = temporary exploration. Clients pay for kits they can continue without you in the room.

### Do retro serif and cyberpunk clash on purpose?
They can, if hierarchy and kit rules say so. Random clash is not a trend—it is noise.

### Where do I start in Lovart?
Load or build Brand Kit, write forbids, plan with MCoT, generate in ChatCanvas, soft-proof, Touch Edit locally, lock winners.

PSD Export allows you to export AI-generated designs as layered Photoshop files. Preserves: layer structure (foreground, background, text), position and scale, layer organization. Workflow: design in Lovart ChatCanvas, export as layered PSD, open in Photoshop for final polish, add finishing touches, export final deliverable. Bridges AI speed with professional editing control.

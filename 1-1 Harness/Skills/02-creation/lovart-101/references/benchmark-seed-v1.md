# Lovart 101 Benchmark Seed V1

Purpose: build a benchmark base for beginner-facing hub pages that explain concepts clearly and route readers deeper into the cluster.

## Category definition

Lovart 101 is the broad-intent, beginner-friendly hub that demystifies a concept and sends readers toward deeper how-to or complete-guide content.

## Five core topics

1. AI design basics
2. Prompting basics for visual creation
3. Brand kit or design system basics
4. AI image editing basics
5. AI video creation basics

## Benchmark traits to absorb

- defines terms plainly
- breaks complexity into teachable chunks
- includes glossaries, examples, or templates
- lowers intimidation without becoming shallow
- routes readers to deeper material

## What to avoid

- oversimplification that removes useful specificity
- jargon-heavy intros
- no next-step routing
- acting like a complete guide instead of a hub page

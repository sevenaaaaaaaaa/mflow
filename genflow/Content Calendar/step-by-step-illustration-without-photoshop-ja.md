## Step 1: Choose Your Illustration Style with Precision

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Open Lovart and select the **Illustration** template (Templates → Creative → Illustration). The key to AI illustration is style specification — vague prompts produce generic results. Lovart's **Style Library** offers 40+ curated illustration styles with reference images and prompt snippets. Browse and select one: **"Editorial flat illustration, limited palette of 5 colors, generous negative space, geometric shapes, The New Yorker meets Dropbox brand illustration style."** If you have a reference image, upload it and type: **"match the illustration style of this reference — same line weight, same color saturation, same level of abstraction."** Lovart's style extraction model analyzes the reference and builds a prompt-weighted style profile.

## Step 2: Compose Your Scene with Structured Prompting

Illustration quality correlates directly with prompt specificity. Use Lovart's **Structured Prompt Builder** — a guided form with fields for Subject, Action, Setting, Mood, Color Palette, and Composition. For the remote work illustration, fill in: Subject = "a person meditating inside a transparent geometric dome," Action = "floating peacefully," Setting = "above a cityscape that fades into abstract shapes," Mood = "calm, focused, transcendent," Palette = "muted teal, warm peach, soft cream, charcoal, copper accent," Composition = "centered symmetrical, person at focal point, dome occupying 60% of canvas." Submit and Lovart generates four compositional variations in under 15 seconds.

## Step 3: Iterate on Specific Elements Without Regenerating Everything

The composition is right but the person inside the dome looks generic. Use Lovart's **Selective Regeneration**: tap the person, enter a refinement prompt — **"make the person a woman with natural hair in a bun, wearing a minimalist linen outfit, eyes closed in meditation pose, skin tone medium-deep"** — and Lovart regenerates only the selected element while preserving the dome, background, and color palette. This targeted iteration cuts the refinement loop from full-regeneration cycles (each destroying previous progress) to surgical adjustments. Repeat for any element: the cityscape, the dome texture, the floating elements.

## Step 4: Vectorize and Refine for Production Use

AI-generated illustrations are raster by default — fine for blogs, problematic for anything that needs to scale (presentations, merch, print). Lovart's **Vectorize** tool (tap the pen icon on any generated illustration) traces the raster output into editable vector paths. Choose **Auto Vectorize** for a one-click SVG conversion, or **Guided Vectorize** to trace specific color regions into separate layers. Guided mode gives you an SVG with named layers — Background, Subject, Accents — that you can individually style, recolor, or export. For a 2000×2000 illustration, vectorization takes approximately 8 seconds and produces a 400KB–800KB SVG file.

**Q: Can Lovart illustrations match an existing brand illustration style?**
A: Yes. Upload 5–10 reference images of your brand's illustration style into the Style Library, and Lovart builds a custom style profile that applies to all future generations for your account. Available on the $49 Pro plan and above.

**Q: What's the maximum resolution for generated illustrations?**
A: Free plan: 1024×1024. Starter ($19): 2048×2048. Pro ($49): 4096×4096. Agency ($99): up to 8192×8192. Vectorized SVGs are resolution-independent regardless of plan.

**Q: How does Selective Regeneration work technically?**
A: Lovart uses a combination of inpainting (regenerating only a masked area) and context-aware blending. The model analyzes surrounding elements to match style, lighting, and perspective within the regenerated zone. Edge blending ensures no visible seams.

**Q: Can Lovart create illustrations with consistent characters across multiple images?**
A: On the $99 Agency plan, use **Character Lock** — define a character with a reference image and name, and Lovart maintains visual consistency (facial features, body proportions, clothing style) across all illustrations in a project.

**Q: Does Lovart support traditional illustration styles like watercolor or pen-and-ink?**
A: Yes. The Style Library includes watercolor wash, ink cross-hatch, gouache, charcoal sketch, linocut print, and digital-nomad hybrid styles. Specify the traditional style in your prompt for textured, organic results.

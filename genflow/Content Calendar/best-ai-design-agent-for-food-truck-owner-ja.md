## 3 Specific Use Cases for Food Truck Owners

### 1. Window Menu Design: The 8-Second Decision Point

Your serving window is your point of sale. While customers wait in line, they stare at your menu. This is the most valuable design real estate you own — 8 seconds of undivided attention where a hungry person decides what to order, and whether to add the loaded fries and a drink, or just get the basic taco and call it a day.

Lovart creates window menus engineered for outdoor readability and upsell architecture. "Design a window menu for our Korean-Mexican fusion truck — 'Seoul Meets Sinaloa.' We sell 6 tacos ($4-$6), 3 burritos ($10-$12), 2 rice bowls ($9-$11), sides (kimchi queso fries, elote with gochujang mayo), and drinks (horchata, Mexican Coke, agua fresca in rotating flavors). Menu board design: bold, vibrant, readable from 10 feet away in sunlight. Price hierarchy — our highest-margin items (burritos) should draw the eye first. Our truck logo prominent. Weather-resistant aesthetic (it's going behind glass but should look intentional). Include our Instagram handle so people tag us while they wait. Two-panel design: left panel = menu, right panel = 3 hero dish photos with descriptions." The AI understands outdoor menu psychology — large fonts, high contrast, visual anchors (hero dish imagery), and a deliberate eye path from highest-margin items to add-ons.

For seasonal rotation: "Update our window menu for summer. Add 2 seasonal items: watermelon-gochujang cold noodle bowl and mango-habanero fish taco. Same visual language, swap in the new items, remove the winter soup items. Update the hero photos." Done in 3 minutes, printed before lunch service.

## The one-minute summary

For the skimmer: Hailuo AI makes the best sub-10-second motion I tested this quarter — objects hold shape, cameras mean something. It bakes text, drifts brand color, and has no memory across a series, so it's a hero-clip tool, not a campaign tool. Pair it with Lovart (ChatCanvas for the brief, Touch Edit for type, Brand Kit for color) and the same clips become shippable across twelve formats before Thursday. Keep text in the design layer, motion in the generation layer. That's the job.

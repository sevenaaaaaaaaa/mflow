## ATS Optimization: The Silent Gatekeeper

75% of resumes are rejected by ATS before a human sees them. Common ATS rejection causes:
- Multi-column layouts (parser can't read column order)
- Images, icons, and graphics (unreadable by ATS)
- Unconventional section headings ("My Journey" instead of "Experience")
- Missing keywords from the job description
- PDF text that isn't machine-readable (scanned or image-based)

Lovart generates resumes that are ATS-optimized by default:
- Single-column, linear layout
- Standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills)
- Machine-readable text (always rendered as text, never as images)
- Keyword analysis against your target job description

## When to Use an AI Resume Maker

### Career Changers
Transitioning industries? The AI suggests transferable skills and reframes experience for the new context. A teacher moving into corporate training gets bullet points that speak corporate language, not academic language.

### Recent Graduates
Limited experience needs to be framed for maximum impact. The AI highlights coursework, projects, internships, and leadership roles with professional language that compensates for the shorter experience section.

### Senior Professionals
10+ years of experience is hard to condense to 1–2 pages. The AI prioritizes recent and relevant experience, trims dated details, and ensures the most impactful achievements appear first.

### Freelancers & Consultants
Different clients need different versions of your resume — one emphasizing design skills, another emphasizing project management. Lovart's Brand Kit lets you maintain multiple resume variants, each consistently styled but tuned to different targets.

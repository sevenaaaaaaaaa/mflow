## Decision Quiz: What Design Do You Need?

Answer the questions below to find your starting point:

1. **What is the primary use case?**
   - A) Promote something (event, sale, launch, announcement)
   - B) Present something (my brand, my work, my data, my business)
   - C) Sell something (product, service, creative work)
   - D) Teach something (lesson, concept, process, research)

2. **Where will it primarily live?**
   - A) Printed and physical (handouts, signage, packaging, cards)
   - B) Digital and screen (social media, ads, website, email)
   - C) Both print and digital

3. **How fast do you need it?**
   - A) Right now — minutes, not hours
   - B) Today — I can spend some time on it
   - C) This week — quality over speed

4. **What is your design skill level?**
   - A) None — I need the AI to handle everything
   - B) Some — I want to tweak and refine AI output
   - C) Experienced — I want AI for speed, then I will customize

**Mostly As:** Start with the AI Poster Generator or AI Banner Maker. These tools require the least input for the highest-impact output. Describe what you need, and Lovart handles the rest.

**Mostly Bs:** Start with the AI Mockup Generator or AI Presentation Maker. These tools enhance existing assets — place your designs in context, build decks from your content.

**Mostly Cs:** Start with the AI Packaging Maker or AI Photo Editor. These are for product-focused creators who need production-ready assets.

**Mostly Ds:** Start with the AI Infographic Maker or AI Illustration Maker. Information and education are the core use cases.

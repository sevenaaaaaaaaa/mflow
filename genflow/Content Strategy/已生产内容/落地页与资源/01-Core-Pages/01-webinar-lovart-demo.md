## About This Webinar

Every quarter, the Lovart product team hosts a live demo showcasing the newest features, improvements, and capabilities added to the platform. The Q3 2027 webinar covers our biggest update of the year — including the launch of the Custom Skills builder, the v4 AI model upgrade, and early access to 3D product visualization.

Whether you're a current Lovart user wanting to make the most of new features, or you're evaluating Lovart for your team, this session will show you what's possible with the latest release.

## Agenda

### 1. Welcome & Platform Vision (5 min)
*Speaker: Sarah Kim, VP of Product*

- The state of AI design in 2027
- Lovart's product philosophy: brand-first, AI-powered, human-directed
- What we shipped in Q1-Q2 and how Q3 builds on that foundation

### 2. v4 AI Model: What's New (10 min)
*Speaker: David Okonkwo, AI Research Lead*

**Image Quality Improvements:**
- 2.3x quality improvement over v3 model (measured by blind human preference testing)
- Significantly better text rendering — headlines and body copy are crisp, properly kerned, and rarely hallucinate characters
- Improved prompt understanding — the AI better interprets nuanced creative direction ("editorial fashion photography with moody lighting and a 1970s color grade")
- Better handling of complex compositions with multiple elements

**New Generation Capabilities:**
- **Style Consistency Mode:** Lock a visual style and generate unlimited variations within it
- **Iterative Refinement:** "Take design #3 and make these 5 specific changes while keeping everything else identical"
- **Reference Image Blending:** Upload 2-3 reference images and generate new designs that blend their characteristics
- **Negative Prompting:** Specify what you DON'T want — "no neon colors, no handwritten fonts, no clip-art aesthetic"

**Live Demo:**
David will generate a complete campaign — hero image, social variants, and ad creative — in under 3 minutes, demonstrating the v4 model's quality and consistency improvements.

### 3. Custom Skills Builder (15 min)
*Speaker: Priya Mehta, Senior Product Manager*

The biggest new feature of Q3 2027. Custom Skills allow you to create reusable, automated design workflows.

**What We'll Demonstrate:**

**Building Your First Skill (Visual Builder):**
- Drag-and-drop workflow construction
- Creating a "Social Media Batch" skill that takes one hero image and generates platform-optimized variants for Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and Twitter — all with correct dimensions, brand elements, and naming conventions
- Adding conditional logic: "If the platform is Instagram, use square format. If TikTok, use vertical."
- Setting default export configurations

**Advanced Skills (Code Builder):**
- Writing skills in Lovart's Skill Definition Language (SDL)
- Creating a "Product Launch Suite" skill that generates listing images, A+ Content, social posts, and email graphics from a single CSV
- Integrating external data sources (Google Sheets, Airtable) into skill workflows

**Community Skills Marketplace:**
- Browsing and installing community-created skills
- Rating and reviewing skills
- Publishing your own skills (and monetization options for premium skills)

**Live Demo:**
Priya will build a complete "Weekly Newsletter Graphics" skill from scratch in under 5 minutes, then execute it to generate all the images for Lovart's own newsletter.

### 4. Enhanced Batch Generation (8 min)
*Speaker: Carlos Rivera, Engineering Lead*

Batch generation received a complete overhaul in Q3:

**New Capabilities:**
- **Parallel Processing:** 10x faster than Q2 batch generation through distributed computing
- **Smart Batching:** AI analyzes your CSV and groups similar rows for more efficient processing
- **Incremental Updates:** Only regenerate changed rows rather than re-running entire batches
- **Batch Preview:** See a preview of 5 random outputs before committing to a full 1,000-row batch
- **Quality Scoring:** AI assigns a quality confidence score to each output, flagging low-confidence results for human review

**Live Demo:**
Carlos will generate product listing images for a 200-SKU catalog in under 2 minutes, demonstrating the new parallel processing engine and smart QC features.

### 5. 3D Product Viewer (Beta) (7 min)
*Speaker: Sarah Kim, VP of Product*

We're introducing early access to 3D product visualization:

**What You Can Do:**
- Upload 3-5 product photos; Lovart generates a 3D model
- Embed interactive 3D product views on your website (no coding required)
- Generate product renders from any angle with AI-completed backgrounds
- Export GLB files for AR applications (Apple ARKit, Google ARCore)
- Create 360-degree product spin videos for e-commerce listings

**Current Limitations (Beta):**
- Works best with products that have simple geometries (packaging, electronics, shoes)
- Complex organic shapes (clothing on models, jewelry) still in development
- Processing time: 2-5 minutes per product depending on complexity

**Live Demo:**
Sarah will convert 5 product photos of a sneaker into an interactive 3D viewer, then embed it in a demo Shopify product page — all in under 3 minutes.

### 6. Team Collaboration v2 (5 min)
*Speaker: Priya Mehta, Senior Product Manager*

Major updates to team features:

- **Real-Time Co-Editing:** Now supports up to 10 simultaneous editors (up from 5 in Q2)
- **Approval Workflow Automation:** Set rules for auto-approval ("Designs from Senior Designers with 98%+ brand compliance skip manual review")
- **Client Portal 2.0:** White-labeled, custom domain support, client-specific asset libraries
- **Slack Integration 2.0:** Bi-directional sync — approve/reject designs directly from Slack, Lovart comments appear in Slack threads

### 7. Live Q&A (10 min)
*All speakers*

Bring your questions. We'll cover:
- Technical questions about specific features
- Roadmap and what's coming in Q4 2027
- Best practices for adopting new features in your workflow
- Pricing and plan questions

# How to Generate Animals & Pet Portraits with AI (2026 Continuity Guide)

Cute animal samplers are easy. Shipping the *same* dog across a campaign without the markings migrating to a cousin is the job. This guide covers realistic pet portraits, brand mascots, and fantasy creatures in Lovart—Identity Lock, refs, Touch Edit, and stop rules—so you stop gambling seeds every time marketing asks for “one more scene.”

## Who this is for

Pet brands, vets/clinics social teams, game/IP creators, freelancers selling pet portrait packages, anyone building a recurring animal character.

## Realistic pet vs fantasy creature (split the brief)

| Mode | Truth source | Failure mode |
|------|--------------|--------------|
| Realistic pet | Photos of the actual animal | Wrong markings, wrong eye color, “generic cute dog” |
| Stylized mascot | Brand Kit + model sheet | Drift into random breed soup |
| Fantasy creature | Design bible + silhouette rules | New species every take |

Label the board. Mixing modes mid-flight is how identities die.

## Preflight

Collect 5–15 refs: face, side, markings close-up, full body if possible. Note eye color, nose leather, unique scars/spots. Write forbids: extra limbs, human teeth, breed swap, melted fur paint, logo tattoos unless brand-true.

## ChatCanvas sequence

1. State mode (realistic / mascot / fantasy).
2. Load Brand Kit if mascot colors/type matter.
3. Attach refs; enable Identity Lock when available for the subject.
4. MCoT: shot list (hero portrait, lifestyle, product with pet, seasonal).
5. Generate with four-take stop.
6. Soft-proof at publish size (ads crush fur detail).
7. Touch Edit local fixes (eye highlight, stray marking) before regen.
8. Archive losers with autopsy (“eye color,” “ear shape,” “spot map”).

## Markings are the fingerprint

Treat spots, blaze, heterochromia, and collar tags as QA gates. If the blaze moves, the take fails—even if “cuter.”

## Fur and material honesty

Over-smoothing turns pets into vinyl toys. Under-structuring turns them into fur fog. Soft-proof. Prefer local contrast fixes over beauty plastic passes.

## Fantasy creature bible

Silhouette, horn/scale rules, color tokens, forbidden anatomy. Without a bible, every take is a new IP problem.

## Memorial and sensitive work

Be explicit about generation. Do not over-promise perfect likeness. Get permissions for photos. Grief audiences punish cleverness that feels dishonest.

## Flop diary

### Flop: the cousin dog
Twelve ads, twelve slightly different spaniels. Comments noticed. Fix: lock + marking checklist + stop rule.

### Flop: human smile teeth on a cat
Nightmare fuel. Forbid human dentition. Touch Edit or regen with anatomy forbids.

### Flop: mascot color drift
Brand orange became coral across a week. Fix: Brand Kit tokens sampled every sibling.

## Packages you can sell

Portrait set, campaign mascot system, fantasy model sheet + three scenes, SKU-with-pet lifestyle night.

## Craft module: Eyes and catchlights

Match species anatomy; catchlights follow scene light; no anime enlarge unless brand.

## Craft module: Ears and silhouette

Side views expose ear lies; check both ears.

## Craft module: Paws and counts

Count toes when visible; AI loves extras.

## Craft module: Collars and tags

Lock tag text or keep unreadable on purpose; no invented phone numbers.

## Craft module: Multi-pet scenes

Lock one hero animal first; add others carefully.

## Craft module: Product + pet

Product Identity Lock separate from pet lock; both can drift.

## Craft module: Breed accuracy

If claiming a breed, verify; otherwise say “inspired.”

## Craft module: Wildlife ethics

Do not fake endangered wildlife “adoptions”; disclosure matters.

## Craft module: Animation stills

Still continuity first; motion later.

## Craft module: UGC-style

Messy realism ≠ wrong identity; keep markings.

## FAQ

### Can AI replace a real pet photo for a memorial portrait?
It can explore likeness with refs, but grief work needs consent, honesty, and careful expectations. Prefer clear disclosure when images are generated.

### Should real estate agents fake luxury staging with AI?
Disclose per local rules and platform policies. Never invent amenities that do not exist. Empty claim bands help keep copy honest.

### What goes in an ecommerce Brand Kit first?
Logo, color, type roles, product photo rules, forbids, channel crops, and a board a VA can continue cold.

### How do I keep the same dog across scenes?
Identity Lock / strong refs, local edits before regen, ship scores on markings and eye color, stop rules.

### Where do I start in Lovart?
Brand Kit → MCoT shot list → ChatCanvas generate → soft-proof → Touch Edit → lock winners.

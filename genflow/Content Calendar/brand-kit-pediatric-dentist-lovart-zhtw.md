**Q2: How do I appeal to both 4-year-olds and 14-year-olds visually?**
Lovart's demographic layering creates brand assets that hit multiple age brackets simultaneously. The core palette (`#52B788` / `#2D6A4F`) reads as "fresh" across all ages. Only the illustration style toggles—simpler for the prize-counter poster, more sophisticated for the teen retainers brochure.

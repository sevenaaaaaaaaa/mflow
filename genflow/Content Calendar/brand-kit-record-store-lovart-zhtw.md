## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Record-store branding exists in warm analog territory. The palette should feel like the crackle before track one—nostalgic but not dated, tactile, and deeply cool.

- **Primary**: `#1A1A1A` — Vinyl black, the color of the record itself—deep, glossy, and authoritative
- **Secondary**: `#F5F5DC` — Warm sleeve-beige for price stickers, newsletters, and background textures
- **Accent**: `#C0392B` — Label red for "New Arrivals," "Sale," and Record Store Day badges
- **Highlight**: `#F4D03F` — 45-rpm gold for "Staff Pick" stickers, anniversary editions, and limited-release callouts
- **Neutral**: `#7F8C8D` — Vinyl-gray for genre dividers, secondary typography, and fine print

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create a brand that appeals to both vinyl veterans and Gen-Z newcomers?**
Yes. Lovart's dual-era branding: the `#1A1A1A` / `#C0392B` palette reads as "classic record store" to boomers and "aesthetic analog cool" to Gen Z. Social templates lean into the vinyl-photography trend; in-store signage remains timeless. Nobody feels alienated.

**Q2: How do I make my record-store Instagram stop the scroll?**
Lovart's vinyl-photography templates guide "flat lay" album-cover shots, spinning-record video templates, and "Now Playing" story cards with animated vinyl GIF overlays. The AI also generates "If You Like [Popular Album], Try [Deep Cut]" carousel templates—the content your followers share most.

**Q3: Can Lovart generate "New Release Tuesday" email templates?**
Absolutely. Weekly email template: "This Week's Essential Drops" with cover grid, "Staff Pick of the Week" with review, "Back in Stock" restock alerts, "In-Store Events" (listening parties, DJ sets), and "Pre-Order Now" for upcoming releases. All branded, all modular, all auto-populated from your distributor feeds.

**Q4: Does Lovart create turntable-repair and hi-fi service branding?**
Yes. Lovart's service-expansion module generates "Groove Gallery Hi-Fi" repair-service sub-branding. Turntable-repair service menu, "Is Your Turntable Healthy?" diagnostic checklist graphic, and "Before & After" restoration posts. A repair bench builds loyalty and foot traffic—brand it as a destination service.

**Q5: What about genre-specific branding (jazz nights, electronic listening parties)?**
Lovart's event-series module creates recurring event branding: "Blue Note Wednesdays" (jazz), "Wax & Watts" (electronic listening party), "Sunday Morning Classical." Each gets a consistent visual identity within the master `#1A1A1A` / `#C0392B` brand. Events become rituals—and rituals build community.

## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Urgent care branding operates on urgency signals—but panic aesthetics drive patients away. The balance: confident, visible, reassuring.

- **Primary**: `#E63946` — Bold emergency red, instantly recognizable and high-visibility on signage
- **Secondary**: `#FFF5F5` — Warm white with a rosy undertone, softening the clinical edge
- **Accent**: `#B71C1C` — Deep crimson for CTAs and critical information headers
- **Support**: `#457B9D` — Cool steel blue for secondary navigation and non-urgent content
- **Signal**: `#F4A261` — Warm amber for "Current Wait Time" displays and attention notifications

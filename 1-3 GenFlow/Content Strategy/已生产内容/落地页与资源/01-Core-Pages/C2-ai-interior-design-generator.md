## What Is an AI Interior Design Generator?

An AI interior design generator is a tool that uses computer vision and generative AI to analyze a photograph of an existing room and produce a photorealistic redesign. It preserves the room's structure — walls, windows, doors, floor plan — while replacing furnishings, colors, textures, lighting, and décor according to a selected design style.

Unlike generic AI image generators that hallucinate impossible architecture, a proper interior design AI respects spatial constraints. The sofa dimensions match the wall. The lighting direction stays consistent. The perspective doesn't warp.

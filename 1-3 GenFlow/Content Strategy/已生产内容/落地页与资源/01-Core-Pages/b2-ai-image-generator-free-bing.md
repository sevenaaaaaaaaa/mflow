## How to Get the Best Results from a Free AI Image Generator

### Prompt Writing Tips

Free credits are precious — you don't want to waste them on bad prompts. Here's how to write prompts that produce usable results on the first try:

**Be specific about subject and style.**
- Bad: "a dog"
- Good: "a golden retriever puppy sitting on a wooden dock at sunset, looking at the camera, photorealistic, golden hour lighting, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field"

**Include composition cues.**
- "Centered composition" / "Rule of thirds, subject on the left"
- "Wide angle" / "Close-up" / "Overhead flat lay"
- "Clean background" / "Busy street scene"

**Specify lighting.**
- "Natural window light, soft shadows"
- "Studio lighting, three-point setup"
- "Neon lights at night, cinematic"
- "Golden hour, warm backlight"

**Mention output intent.**
- "Hero image for a SaaS landing page" (triggers composition with negative space for text)
- "Social media post, square format" (triggers 1:1 aspect ratio composition)
- "Blog featured image, 16:9" (triggers landscape composition)

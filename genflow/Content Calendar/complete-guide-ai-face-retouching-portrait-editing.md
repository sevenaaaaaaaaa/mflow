## Questions Nobody Answers

### What can AI face retouching actually do in 2026?

Production-ready capabilities:
- **Blemish removal:** Acne, scars, spots — detected and removed with surrounding skin texture preserved
- **Skin smoothing:** Reduces texture irregularities while preserving natural skin pore structure (when done correctly)
- **Eye enhancement:** Brightens whites, sharpens irises, removes red-eye and catchlight inconsistencies
- **Teeth whitening:** Natural-looking shade adjustment without the Chiclet-white overcorrection
- **Under-eye circle reduction:** Lightens and smooths while preserving natural eye socket structure
- **Facial lighting adjustment:** Virtual relighting to reduce harsh shadows and fill dark areas
- **Stray hair removal:** Detects and removes flyaway hairs while preserving intentional hairstyle

Experimental (use with caution): facial feature reshaping, makeup application, expression adjustment, age modification.

### How do I avoid the "plastic skin" look?

Plastic skin has three causes, all fixable:

1. **Excessive smoothing radius:** The AI blurs skin texture across too large an area, eliminating pores entirely. Fix: reduce smoothing strength to 30-50% and use "texture preserve" mode.

2. **Uniform application:** Natural faces have texture variation — more on the nose and chin, less on the cheeks. AI applies uniform smoothing. Fix: mask areas that should retain texture (nose, chin, forehead center).

3. **Loss of micro-contrast:** Fine shadows that define facial structure are smoothed away along with blemishes. Fix: apply a subtle texture grain layer after retouching to restore photographic realism.

Lovart's Natural Retouch mode addresses all three automatically — it preserves skin texture at 40-60% smoothing strength and maintains structural shadows. It's the safest starting point for natural results.

### Can AI fix harsh shadows and bad lighting?

Partially. AI face relighting (Lovart Relight mode) analyzes facial geometry and adjusts lighting to reduce harsh shadows, fill dark eye sockets, and even out skin exposure. It works well on:
- Moderate undereye shadows
- Side-lit faces with one dark half
- Flat overhead lighting creating raccoon eyes

It struggles with:
- Extreme contrast (direct sunlight from one side)
- Mixed color temperature lighting
- Backlit faces where the entire face is in shadow

AI relighting is a correction tool, not a replacement for good lighting. Better input produces better output.

### Can AI apply makeup to portraits?

Yes — AI makeup application is surprisingly good. Lovart's Virtual Makeup applies:
- Foundation/base evening
- Lip color (natural to bold, with accurate color application)
- Eye makeup (liner, shadow, mascara)
- Blush and contour
- Brow definition

The results look like well-applied real makeup, not like a Snapchat filter. This is useful for: previewing makeup looks before application, creating consistent looks across a photo set, and editorial retouching where a makeup artist wasn't available.

### How does AI handle different skin tones and ethnicities?

This is an area where many AI tools have historically underperformed — trained predominantly on lighter skin tones, producing unnatural results on darker complexions. In 2026, the best tools have addressed this:
- Lovart Portrait Studio uses a balanced training dataset and includes skin-tone-specific processing (melanin-aware smoothing, appropriate highlight retention)
- Adobe Photoshop's Neural Filters have improved significantly on diverse skin tones
- FaceApp and similar mobile apps still show noticeable bias toward lighter skin standards

If you regularly retouch diverse skin tones, test tools specifically on your demographic range before committing to a workflow. Lovart provides sample outputs across the Fitzpatrick scale in its documentation.

### Can I retouch group photos efficiently?

Yes — batch face detection identifies every face in a group photo, and consistent retouching parameters are applied to all. This is essential for wedding photographers, event shooters, and corporate headshot sessions where 50+ portraits need the same treatment.

Lovart's Batch Portrait mode processes up to 20 faces per image with consistent settings. Individual face override allows manual adjustment when one person needs different treatment (e.g., more blemish removal for a teenager, less smoothing for an older subject).

### Is AI retouching reversible?

Within a session, yes — Lovart maintains an adjustment history with non-destructive editing (all sliders can be reset). After exporting, no — the retouched image is a new file. Always keep your original.

Best practice: save retouched portraits as new files with a consistent naming convention (e.g., `portrait_retouched_v1.jpg`), never overwrite originals.

### What's the difference between AI retouching and beauty filters?

Beauty filters (Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat) apply real-time, fixed-strength effects optimized for small screens and fast processing. They're generic — the same filter on every face.

AI retouching tools analyze individual facial features and apply adjustments appropriate to each face. A 20-year-old and a 60-year-old receive different treatment — the 60-year-old shouldn't be smoothed into looking 20. Professional AI retouching preserves age-appropriate features; beauty filters often don't.

### Can AI retouching remove tattoos, piercings, or permanent features?

Yes. Lovart's Object Removal (applied to faces) can remove or reduce the appearance of tattoos, piercings, scars, and birthmarks. This is useful for:
- Actors needing to appear without visible tattoos for a role
- Professional headshots where facial piercings may be a concern
- Medical/cosmetic visualization for procedures

Use responsibly. Removing someone's cultural or identity-significant features without their consent is a violation of trust. Always discuss with the subject before removing permanent features.

### How fast is AI face retouching compared to manual Photoshop work?

Task comparison:
- Basic blemish removal (10 blemishes): Manual 5-10 min → AI 5-10 seconds
- Full skin smoothing (frequency separation): Manual 20-40 min → AI 10-20 seconds
- Eye enhancement + teeth whitening: Manual 10-15 min → AI 5-10 seconds
- Full portrait retouch (all adjustments): Manual 45-90 min → AI 30-60 seconds
- Batch 100 portraits: Manual 75-150 hours → AI 5-10 minutes

AI doesn't eliminate the need for retouchers — it eliminates the mechanical work, freeing them for creative decisions and quality control.

### Will AI retouching make beauty standards even more unrealistic?

This is a legitimate concern. AI retouching makes it trivially easy to apply unrealistic beauty standards to every photo. The same technology that can subtly improve a portrait can also transform a real face into an impossible ideal.

Responsible use: retouch to reveal what was already there (better lighting, clearer skin), not to create what wasn't (different bone structure, impossible proportions). The tool amplifies the user's judgment. Use it ethically.

## What Most Guides Won't Tell You

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

**Less is more — always.** The most common retouching mistake: applying every adjustment at 100%. The professional approach: apply each adjustment at 30-50%, then stop. A portrait that looks "slightly better than real life" is the goal. A portrait that looks "perfect" looks fake.

**Skin texture is your friend.** Pores, fine lines, subtle texture — these are what make faces look real. Removing them removes humanity. The best AI retouching smooths without eliminating texture. If your portrait looks like a 3D render, dial back skin smoothing.

**Your phone is already doing this.** Modern smartphones apply AI face retouching automatically to every photo you take. The "raw" photo from your phone is already lightly processed. AI retouching tools give you control over processing that was previously automatic and invisible.

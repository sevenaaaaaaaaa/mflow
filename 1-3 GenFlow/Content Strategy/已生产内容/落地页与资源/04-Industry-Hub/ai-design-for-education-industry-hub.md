## Why Education Design Matters

Learning science is unambiguous on this point. Dual coding theory, established through decades of cognitive research, shows that information presented through both visual and verbal channels is retained significantly better than either channel alone. Visual hierarchy — clear headings, structured layouts, supporting imagery — reduces cognitive load and allows learners to focus mental energy on understanding rather than parsing.

Despite this, most educational visuals are built in PowerPoint by non-designers under time pressure. Course creators on Udemy, Skillshare, and Teachable report spending 20–40 hours on visual production for a single course — time taken away from content development, marketing, and student interaction. Professional design support costs $50–$150 per hour and rarely understands the pedagogical purpose behind the visual. The gap between what educators can produce and what learners respond to remains one of the largest untapped opportunities in education.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Should I use martial arts terminology in my branding?**
Use authentic terms from your specific discipline (OSS, Kihon, Base, etc.) but provide context. A tagline in Japanese with English translation works well. Avoid generic Asian-inspired fonts if you are not teaching an Asian martial art — authenticity matters above all.

**Q2: What is the best way to display belt ranks?**
Create a visual progression chart with actual belt colors and skill descriptions. Place it prominently in your school and on your website. Lovart generates belt rank progression templates in your brand style.

**Q3: How do I brand for both kids and adults simultaneously?**
Same core brand, different photography and messaging. Kids programs use enthusiastic, action-oriented imagery. Adult programs use focused, technique-oriented imagery. Lovart separates demographics while maintaining brand unity.

**Q4: What is the role of the instructor bio in branding?**
Massive. Lineage matters — display instructor credentials, competition records, and teaching philosophy prominently. A well-designed instructor bio page converts curious visitors into trial students faster than any other page.

**Q5: How do I handle tournament branding?**
Create tournament-specific templates — medal designs, heat brackets, event banners, participant certificates. These should feel special but clearly connected to your academy brand.

**Q6: Should I brand my mats and equipment?**
Matted area wall logos and equipment storage branding create a professional training environment. Parents judge cleanliness and organization — branded organization signals systems and standards.

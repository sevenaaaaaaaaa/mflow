## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Comic book shop branding lives at the intersection of nostalgia and discovery. The palette should evoke the excitement of new-comic Wednesday—the crisp smell of ink, the glossy covers, the promise of the next chapter.

- **Primary**: `#E74C3C` — Heroic comic-red, the color of Spider-Man's suit, action panels, and Wednesday excitement
- **Secondary**: `#FFF8E7` — Vintage page-cream for pull-list cards, newsletters, and backgrounds
- **Accent**: `#2C3E50` — Ink-blue for typography, comic-panel borders, and serious collector content
- **POW**: `#F1C40F` — Speech-bubble yellow for "New Release" badges, sale tags, and "Staff Pick" callouts
- **Shadow**: `#1A1A1A` — True black for comic-panel-borders effect on signage and digital content

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create a brand that's comic-book fun without feeling like a children's store?**
Yes. Lovart's "collector-forward" mode emphasizes the literary and artistic dimensions of comics—ink-blue sophistication with red-hero energy. The brand attracts serious collectors AND Wednesday Warriors. Fun, but not juvenile. Geeky, but grown-up.

**Q2: How do I create a pull-list system that actually retains subscribers?**
Lovart's pull-list module generates the physical card, the digital portal branding, and the weekly "Your Pulls Are In!" email template. Includes a "Recommendations Based on Your Pulls" AI-generated section—cross-sell indie titles adjacent to subscribers' DC/Marvel pulls. Retention through discovery.

**Q3: Can Lovart generate weekly social content for new releases?**
Absolutely. "Wednesday Warriors" weekly content series: Monday "Preview" post showing 3 covers, Tuesday "Staff Most Anticipated" reel, Wednesday "It's Here!" grid post with release wall photo, Thursday "Staff Pick Review" carousel. Lovart generates the templates once; staff fills in the covers weekly. 5 minutes of work per day.

**Q4: Does Lovart create Free Comic Book Day campaign branding?**
Yes. Lovart's FCBD module generates a full campaign: storefront banner, event map, social-media countdown, costume-contest flyer, and "What to Expect on FCBD" first-timer guide. The most important retail day of the comic year, professionally branded.

**Q5: What about gaming and collectibles—can the brand extend to TCGs, Funko Pops, etc.?**
Lovart's "pop-culture expansion" module creates sub-branding for TCG events (Magic, Pokémon, Yu-Gi-Oh), Funko Pop walls, and tabletop gaming nights. The core `#E74C3C` / `#2C3E50` palette extends with game-specific accent colors. One store, multiple communities, unified brand.

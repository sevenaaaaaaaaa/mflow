## What Lovart's Video Translation AI Does

### Speech-to-Text Transcription

Lovart transcribes the original audio with near-human accuracy across 95+ source languages. The AI distinguishes speakers, handles accents and dialects, and captures timing data — every word is timestamped to the frame. This transcription becomes the foundation for translation.

### Context-Aware Translation

Literal translation produces awkward, unnatural results. Lovart's translation engine understands context: idioms become their cultural equivalents, humor lands in the target language, and brand terminology stays consistent. You can upload a custom glossary for industry-specific terms that must be preserved — product names, technical vocabulary, brand-voice phrases.

### Natural AI Voiceover Generation

The translated script is voiced by AI voices that sound human — not robotic, not text-to-speech monotone, but expressive narration with natural intonation, pacing, and emotional range. Choose from 200+ voices across genders, ages, accents, and tones. Each target language gets a voice that fits the original speaker's style.

### Lip-Sync Alignment

This is what separates Lovart from basic subtitle tools. The generated voiceover is time-aligned to the original speaker's mouth movements. Not frame-perfect deepfake lip-sync — that requires video generation — but duration-matched audio that fits within the original speaker's speaking segments. Viewers hear natural pacing, not rushed or stretched speech.

### Multi-Speaker Handling

Interviews, panel discussions, and multi-character videos are handled correctly. Lovart identifies each speaker, maintains separate voice profiles per speaker in every language, and preserves the back-and-forth rhythm of conversation.

## FAQ

**How accurate is the translation?**
Lovart's translation engine achieves BLEU scores competitive with professional human translation services for common language pairs. We recommend reviewing translations before publishing, especially for legal, medical, or highly technical content. Custom glossaries significantly improve accuracy for domain-specific material.

**Does lip-sync look natural?**
The audio is duration-matched to original speech segments, so the pace feels natural. It is not frame-accurate visual lip-sync (which would require modifying the video frames). For most use cases — voiceover dubbing, not ADR — duration matching produces a natural viewing experience.

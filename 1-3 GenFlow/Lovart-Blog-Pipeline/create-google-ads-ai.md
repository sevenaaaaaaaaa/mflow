Google Ads require visual assets in multiple sizes and formats — responsive display ads need images in 1:1, 1.91:1, and 4:5 aspect ratios. Video ads need 15-30 second clips. Producing all variants manually means either paying a designer for every campaign or running under-optimized ads with limited creative.

Lovart generates all required ad formats from one design session. Brand Kit ensures every variant is on-brand. This guide covers the complete workflow.

---

## Step 1: Design the Master Creative

Start with one composition that captures the ad's core message. *"Google Display Ad hero image. Clean, uncluttered composition with a product shot on the left and headline space on the right. Brand Kit colors. Professional, trust-signaling aesthetic. Leave generous negative space for ad copy overlays."*

Generate. Refine. This is your master creative — every size variant will derive from it.

---

## Step 2: Generate Size Variants

Google responsive display ads require:
- Landscape: 1.91:1 (1200×628)
- Square: 1:1 (1200×1200)
- Vertical: 4:5 (960×1200) — optional but recommended

*"Create landscape (1.91:1) variant of this ad. Same product, same visual style. Wider composition with more horizontal negative space."*

*"Create square (1:1) variant. Same product, centered composition."*

Brand Kit enforces color and style consistency. All three variants look like they belong to the same campaign.

---

## Step 3: Generate Video Ad

For YouTube and Discovery video ads. *"Seedance 2.0: 15-second product video ad. The product from the canvas. Clean, professional. Opening hook in first 2 seconds. Product features shown with smooth transitions. Close with brand logo and CTA. 16:9 format."*

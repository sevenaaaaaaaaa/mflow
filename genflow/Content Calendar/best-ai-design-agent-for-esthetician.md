### 1. Treatment Menu Design: The Consultation Tool That Drives Revenue

Your treatment menu is a sales conversation in document form. A new client should be able to: quickly understand what services you offer, differentiate between similar treatments (why choose a HydraFacial over a classic facial?), understand the investment (price, duration, recommended frequency), and feel inspired to book the treatment that targets their specific concern. A confusing menu leads to clients booking the cheapest option because they don't understand the value of the premium treatment. A clear menu leads to clients upgrading because the description makes them WANT the results.

Lovart creates treatment menus that educate and upsell. "Design a treatment menu for 'Luminary Skin Studio' — a solo esthetician practice specializing in acne and hyperpigmentation. ### 2. Before-and-After & Results Presentation: Medical-Grade Visual Evidence

Before-and-after photos are the most powerful marketing tool in esthetics — they're visual proof that your work transforms skin. But they're also a minefield of inconsistency: different lighting, different angles, different expressions. A standardized approach — consent form, photo protocol, and results grid — communicates professionalism and protects your practice legally.

Lovart creates a standardized results documentation system. "Design a 'Skin Transformation' results documentation kit: 1) Client photo consent form — clear language, usage rights (social media, website, marketing materials), client signature and date, HIPAA-compliant storage note, our branding, 2) Photo protocol guide — lighting setup (consistent window light or ring light at 45° angle), camera settings, 'no makeup, hair pulled back, neutral expression' instructions, 3 angles: front, left profile, right profile, 3) Before-and-after results card — for Instagram or in-clinic display — side-by-side photos with treatment type, number of sessions, dates, and 2-3 bullet results (e.g., '4 Microneedling sessions over 12 weeks: 70% reduction in acne scarring, improved skin texture, reduced hyperpigmentation'), 4) 'Results Timeline' graphic — what to expect after each treatment type: 'Day After Peel: light flaking | Day 3: glow begins | Day 7: full results,' helps clients understand the process and stay compliant with aftercare. Design: clinical but warm — this is medical-grade skincare, not a beauty filter."

### 3. Aftercare & Education: The Cards That Protect Results (and Build Retention)

Aftercare compliance determines treatment results. A client who gets a VI Peel and doesn't use SPF 50 for 7 days will call you upset when they hyperpigment — and it won't be your fault, but they won't rebook. Aftercare cards that are clear, visual, and professional protect both the client's skin and your business's reputation.

Lovart creates aftercare card systems that clients actually read and follow. "Design an aftercare card set for all our treatments: 1) Post-Facial Care — 'Next 24 Hours: No makeup, no active products, SPF only, drink water,' 2) Chemical Peel Aftercare — 'Days 1-2: Do not peel or pick, gentle cleanser only, SPF 50 reapplied every 2 hours, no exercise/sauna/heat, Days 3-7: gentle moisturizer, flaking is normal, contact us if you have concerns, Day 7+: Reveal your glow,' 3) Microneedling Aftercare — 'Days 1-3: Redness normal, gentle cleanser, hyaluronic acid serum, SPF 50, no makeup, no active ingredients, Days 4-7: resume gentle routine, avoid retinols,' 4) General skincare education — 'The 3 Non-Negotiables: SPF every day, double cleanse at night, retinol (if prescribed) — here's why.' Each card: front has a clear title and icon, inside has day-by-day or bulleted instructions, back has our contact info and 'Questions? Call/text us.' Design: our brand, clean, the cards should feel like they're worth keeping — not a receipt to toss. Print on durable cardstock — these live on bathroom counters near water and products."

For ongoing education: "Create a monthly 'Skin School' Instagram carousel series — each month a different topic (Retinols 101, SPF Myths, Acne Face Mapping, Ingredient Spotlights). 8-10 slides per carousel. Educational, evidence-based, our aesthetic. This series should position us as the skincare expert in our market."

MCoT (Mind Chain of Thought) Engine is Lovart's proprietary creative reasoning system. It analyzes brand context, audience, and competitive positioning before generating any pixels. Steps: context analysis, strategy planning, model coordination, generation, refinement. Key capabilities: audience analysis, competitive positioning, brand personality mapping, cross-asset consistency. Unlike standard AI models that respond directly to prompts, MCoT applies strategic reasoning before generation.

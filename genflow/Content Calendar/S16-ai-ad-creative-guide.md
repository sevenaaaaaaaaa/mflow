## The AI Ad Creative Workflow

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Before we get platform-specific, here's the universal process:

**Step 1: Define your campaign brief.**
What are you promoting? Who's the audience? What's the primary message? What action do you want viewers to take? Write this down in 3-5 bullet points. This brief feeds every AI-generated creative.

**Step 2: Set up your Brand Kit.**
Upload your logo, brand colors, and preferred fonts to your AI design tool. This ensures every ad — across every platform — stays on-brand without manual enforcement.

**Step 3: Generate base creative.**
Use your AI design agent to create the hero creative — the primary visual that captures your campaign concept. Prompt for specific composition, mood, and messaging.

**Step 4: Generate platform variants.**
Feed your base creative back to the AI and specify: "Now create versions for Meta Feed (1080x1350), Meta Story (1080x1920), Google Display (300x250, 728x90, 160x600), and TikTok (1080x1920)." The AI handles resizing, reformatting, and layout adaptation automatically.

**Step 5: Create A/B variants.**
For each format, generate 3-5 variants with different: headlines, color treatments, image styles, CTA button text, and layout compositions. You now have a full testing matrix.

**Step 6: Export and launch.**
Download all variants at platform-native resolutions. Upload to your ad manager. Launch your campaign with proper A/B testing structure.

**Performance Max assets:**

## Meta Ads: Facebook and Instagram

Meta's ad platform requires creative across Feed, Story, Reels, and Carousel formats. The algorithm heavily weights creative quality and freshness — stale creative = higher CPMs.

### Meta Feed Ads

| Format | Dimensions | Aspect Ratio |
|---|---|---|
| Square | 1080x1080 | 1:1 |
| Landscape | 1200x628 | 1.91:1 |
| Vertical | 1080x1350 | 4:5 |

**AI prompt template for Meta Feed:**

> "Square format (1080x1080) Facebook/Instagram feed ad. [Product description]. Style: [aesthetic direction]. Mood: [emotional tone]. Include: clear headline, supporting text describing key benefit, prominent CTA button. Text must be readable at mobile scale. Primary visual: [subject description]. Brand Kit: applied."

### Meta Story and Reels Ads

| Format | Dimensions |
|---|---|
| Story | 1080x1920 (9:16) |
| Reels | 1080x1920 (9:16) |

Story ads need different design logic than Feed ads. Key differences:
- Full-screen vertical canvas — no side elements
- Text must be larger and more prominent (people tap through stories fast)
- Strong visual hook in the first 0.5 seconds
- CTA should be near the bottom where thumbs naturally rest

**AI refinement prompt:** "Adapt this Feed ad for Stories format. Make text larger and bolder. Move CTA to bottom third. Add a strong visual hook element at the top of the frame."

### Carousel Ads

Carousel ads (2-10 cards) consistently outperform single-image ads for e-commerce and product showcases. With AI, generate a complete carousel from one product:

1. Card 1: Product hero shot + headline
2. Cards 2-4: Individual feature/benefit highlights
3. Cards 5-6: Lifestyle/use-case imagery
4. Card 7: Social proof (review quote, rating)
5. Card 8: Strong CTA with offer

**Prompt:** "Create an 8-card carousel for [product]. Card 1: hero shot. Cards 2-4: key features. Cards 5-6: lifestyle. Card 7: testimonial. Card 8: CTA. Maintain consistent visual style across all cards."

## The A/B Testing Framework: What to Test

AI lets you generate variants at zero marginal cost. That means you can (and should) test aggressively. Here's the testing hierarchy:

**Round 1: Concept Test (3-5 variants)**
Test fundamentally different creative concepts:
- Product-focused vs. lifestyle-focused
- Benefit-driven vs. feature-driven
- Emotional vs. rational appeal
- Bold/aggressive vs. subtle/minimalist

Run each variant with identical targeting and budget. Let data pick the winner.

**Round 2: Element Test (3 variants per winning concept)**
Once you know which concept works, test individual elements:
- Headline A vs. B vs. C
- CTA text: "Shop Now" vs. "Get Started" vs. "Try Free"
- Color treatment: brand primary vs. contrasting accent
- Image type: photography vs. illustration vs. 3D render
- With/without price mention
- With/without social proof element

**Round 3: Format Test (platform-specific)**
Test format variants against the same creative concept:
- Single image vs. carousel (Meta)
- Static vs. video (all platforms)
- Square vs. vertical (Meta)
- Short video (15s) vs. longer video (30s) (TikTok, Meta)

## Common AI Ad Mistakes

**Mistake 1: Forgetting the text overlay rules.**
Each platform has text-to-image ratio restrictions. Meta throttles ads with too much image text. Google has similar policies. Use AI to generate clean, text-light designs that let the platform algorithm work unimpeded.

**Mistake 2: Same creative across all platforms.**
An Instagram Story ad and a LinkedIn Feed ad serve different contexts and audience expectations. Always generate platform-specific variants.

**Mistake 3: Not refreshing creative.**
Even AI-generated ads experience creative fatigue after 2-4 weeks of heavy spending. Set calendar reminders to generate fresh batches.

**Mistake 4: Relying on AI-generated text without review.**
AI writes solid marketing copy, but always review headlines and CTAs for accuracy, brand voice alignment, and platform policy compliance before launching.

**Mistake 5: Ignoring post-launch data.**
The best AI ad system learns. After each campaign, feed winning and losing creative patterns back into your prompts. "Make more designs like [winning creative]" and "Avoid styles/colors like [losing creative]."

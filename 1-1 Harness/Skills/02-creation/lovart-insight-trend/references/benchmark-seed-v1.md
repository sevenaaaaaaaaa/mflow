# Lovart Insight and Trend Benchmark Seed V1

Purpose: ground `Insight & Trend` in a balanced benchmark set that includes authority data, platform signals, and real-search trend framing.

## Category definition

Insight & Trend articles explain what is changing, why it matters now, and what creators or teams should do next.

## Five core topics

1. AI design workflow shifts
2. Human-touch and anti-slop aesthetics
3. Brand governance and creative ops scale
4. Trust, provenance, and copyright risk
5. Code-native or canvas-native design workflows

## Benchmark traits to absorb

- opens with a real shift, not generic hype
- ties trend language to operating consequences
- uses multiple types of evidence: data, observed behavior, design examples
- avoids overclaiming inevitability
- ends with action, not just commentary

## What to avoid

- recycled AI future talk
- no operational implication
- no Lovart-specific relevance
- one-source trend claims

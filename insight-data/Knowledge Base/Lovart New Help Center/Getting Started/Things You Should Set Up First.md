### Set Up Your Brand Kit

https://www.lovart.ai/docs/getting-started/things-you-should-set-up-first

> Configure your brand identity once so every output stays on-brand.

If you're working with an existing brand, we recommend setting up a Brand Kit before generating to ensure on-brand output. A Brand Kit stores your logo, colors, fonts, and design guidance globally, and will be applied to every creative task based on your needs. You can create a separate Brand Kit for each brand you work on.

**How to Use**

1. **Open the Brand Kit manager.**
    - From the Lovart homepage, click the Brand Kit icon in the left toolbar.
2. **Add your brand assets.**
    - **Manual upload:** Add logos, fonts, color swatches, and design guidance one at a time.
    - **Parse a brand book:** Upload an existing brand book PDF and let Lovart extract elements automatically.
3. **Apply it to your work.**
    - Use the Brand Kit selector at the top of any project to apply it to every generation.
    - Or `@`-mention a Brand Kit in the input box to apply it to a single generation.

Jump to [Brand Kit](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/how-to-prompt/adding-references#828020ecba5b) for the full feature page, including editing and per-plan limits.

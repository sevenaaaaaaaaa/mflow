## Pencil / Pen

Create professional vectors on Lovart's ChatCanvas. Use the Pencil (B) for AI-smoothed sketches and the Pen (P) for precise Bézier curves, logos, and icons.

Lovart's Pencil (brush, shortcut B) and Pen (pen, shortcut P) are vector drawing tools in its ChatCanvas canvas editor, enabling designers to freely hand-draw paths and create precise paths.

### Pencil

The pencil tool allows freehand drawing of rough lines or filled shapes, similar to a traditional brush, ideal for quick sketches, texture addition, or organic curves, with adjustable stroke width, opacity, and color, plus AI-assisted smoothing.

**Pencil Tool Deep Dive:**

1. **Freehand Drawing Made Professional**
   - Draw naturally without worrying about precision—AI smoothing automatically refines your strokes
   - Perfect for sketching concepts, drawing annotations, or creating organic shapes
   - Adjustable stroke width from 1px to 50px for thick marker effects to fine pen lines

2. **AI-Assisted Smoothing**
   - The magic of Lovart's Pencil lies in intelligent stroke correction
   - It automatically detects and smooths jittery hand movements
   - Maintains the organic feel while ensuring professional-quality lines

3. **Fill and Stroke Modes**
   - Toggle between stroke-only (outline) and filled shapes
   - Create smooth blob shapes with a single stroke loop
   - Perfect for abstract backgrounds, texture elements, or calligraphic designs

4. **Practical Applications:**
   - Add hand-drawn annotations to mockups
   - Sketch UI wireframes during brainstorming
   - Create textured backgrounds with organic patterns
   - Draw highlight arrows and markers for presentations

**Pro Tip**: Hold Shift while drawing to constrain lines to perfect horizontal, vertical, or 45-degree angles.

### Pen

The Pen tool creates Bézier curves and precise anchor paths, akin to Illustrator's pen tool, perfect for logos, icons, or complex vectors, supporting closed paths and path editing to boost professional vector design efficiency.

**Pen Tool Mastery:**

1. **Precision Vector Creation**
   - Create flawless curves with complete control over anchor points
   - Each click adds an anchor point; drag to create curved segments
   - Build complex shapes point by point with mathematical precision

2. **Bezier Curve Control**
   - Click and drag to create curved paths with adjustable handles
   - Pull handles in different directions for S-curves, C-curves, or combinations
   - Perfect for creating smooth, organic shapes that look natural

3. **Path Operations**
   - **Closed Paths**: Complete shapes that can be filled with color
   - **Open Paths**: Create outline graphics, decorative borders, or line art
   - **Path Editing**: Click existing paths to add, delete, or move anchor points

4. **Professional Workflow Applications:**
   - Design complex logos with precise curves and angles
   - Create custom icons with consistent stroke weights
   - Draw intricate illustrations with clean vector edges
   - Build typography designs with custom letterforms

**Key Differences: Pencil vs. Pen**

| Feature | Pencil | Pen |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Learning Curve | Easy—draw naturally | Steeper—requires understanding of Bezier curves |
| Best For | Sketches, annotations, organic shapes | Logos, icons, precise vector artwork |
| Editing | Limited adjustment | Full path editing with anchor points |
| AI Smoothing | Automatic smoothing applied | Manual precision—minimal smoothing |
| Workflow | Quick exploration | Polished final output |

**Combined Workflow Example:**

1. **Sketch with Pencil**: Use the Pencil tool to rough out your initial concept—let your creativity flow without constraint
2. **Refine with Pen**: Switch to the Pen tool and trace over the best elements, creating precise paths
3. **Edit & Perfect**: Make final adjustments to anchor points and curves
4. **AI Enhance**: Send your vector work to AI for style suggestions or variations

This combined approach leverages the speed of freehand sketching with the precision of vector editing, all within the intelligent Lovart ecosystem.

### Keyboard Shortcuts Reference

- **B**: Switch to Pencil tool
- **P**: Switch to Pen tool
- **Cmd/Ctrl + Z**: Undo last action
- **Delete/Backspace**: Remove selected path
- **Shift + Drag**: Constrain angles to 45° increments
- **Alt + Click Anchor**: Convert smooth point to corner point (and vice versa)

## The E-Book Author's Visual Needs

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Self-publishing an ebook requires more visual assets than most first-time authors anticipate:

- **Book cover**: The primary visual asset. It must be genre-appropriate (a business book cover looks different from a romance cover), readable at thumbnail size, and distinctive enough to stand out in a grid of competitors.
- **3D book mockup**: A cover on a flat background is standard. A cover rendered on a 3D book mockup (with pages, spine, and realistic lighting) looks professional and is used for the book's sales page, social media promotion, and advertising.
- **Lead magnet and reader magnet covers**: Free downloadable resources (checklists, workbooks, bonus chapters, companion guides) that convert website visitors into email subscribers — each needs its own cover.
- **Book launch campaign assets**: Pre-order announcement graphics, launch-day countdown posts, early-reviewer recruitment graphics, "Now Available" multi-platform announcements, Amazon A+ Content images.
- **Author brand identity**: The visual brand that extends beyond the book — the author's website, social media presence, newsletter design, speaking kit, and future book series branding.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use AI-generated covers on Amazon KDP, or are there restrictions?**

As of 2026, Amazon KDP does not restrict AI-generated covers. They must meet Amazon's technical requirements (minimum 1000px on the longest side, ideal 2560x1600px, JPEG or TIFF format) and content guidelines (no misleading imagery, no copyrighted elements). Amazon is more concerned with cover quality and accuracy than with production method. A well-designed AI-generated cover that accurately represents the book's genre and content is fully acceptable.

**What about print book covers — can the agent handle those?**

Yes. Prompt for a print wrap cover: "a full paperback cover wrap for 'The Career Pivot Playbook': front cover, spine with title and author name, and back cover with book description, author bio, and a barcode placeholder. Total dimensions: 6x9 inch trim size with standard bleed. Our cover design extended across the full wrap. The spine text centered vertically. The back cover clean and uncluttered." The agent generates the flat cover design. Export at 300 DPI with bleed. Upload to KDP Print or IngramSpark.

**How do I know what visual style is appropriate for my book's genre?**

Study the top 20 bestselling books in your Amazon category. Note the common visual conventions: color palettes, typography styles, imagery types, layout structures. Your cover should feel like it belongs in the genre while being distinctive within it. Prompt the agent with genre notes: "This is a business self-help book in the style of Atomic Habits or Deep Work — typography-forward, bold, clean, one strong conceptual visual, no stock photography of people in suits."

**Can the agent design my entire book interior layout, not just the cover?**

The agent can generate chapter header designs, section divider pages, callout box styles, and typography recommendations — but ebook interior formatting (reflowable text for Kindle, consistent paragraph styles, working table of contents) is best handled by ebook formatting software (Vellum, Atticus, Kindle Create) or a professional formatter. Use the agent for visual design direction and interior graphics; use formatting software for the technical layout.

**What about audiobook covers — can the agent resize for that?**

Yes. Audible requires a square cover (2400x2400px minimum). Prompt: "Our book cover adapted for Audible: square 2400x2400px format, the same cover design recomposed for a square aspect ratio, no important elements lost to cropping, designed for display in the Audible app and on the Audible website." The agent adapts the rectangular ebook cover to the square audiobook format.

**How much should I expect to save compared to hiring a professional cover designer?**

A professional book cover designer charges $300-800 for an ebook cover, $500-1,500 for a full paperback wrap, and $50-150 per lead magnet cover. An author launching one book with three lead magnets and a launch campaign would spend roughly $800-2,500 on design. An AI agent subscription covers the same output for roughly the cost of a single lead magnet cover design — and produces assets in hours, not weeks. For first-time authors testing the market, this is the difference between "I cannot afford a professional launch" and "I am launching professionally."

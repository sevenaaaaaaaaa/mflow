Tools

## Add to Canvas

Master Lovart's infinite canvas. Learn to upload reference images for AI editing, import videos for motion design, and organize your workspace using Frames.

### Upload Image

- **What It Does** Quickly import images from your device to the Lovart canvas, serving as the foundation for AI-powered editing, mockup generation, or design composition.
- **How to Use**
	- Click the **"+"** icon in the left toolbar / You can also drag and drop image files directly onto the canvas.
	- Select **"Upload Image"**
	- Choose an image file from your device (supports JPG, PNG, etc.)
	- The image appears on the canvas, ready for Mark editing or AI transformation

**Pro Tips for Image Upload:**

1. **Drag & Drop Method**: For faster workflow, simply drag image files from your desktop or folder directly onto the canvas. This is especially useful when working with multiple reference images for mood boards or brand assets.

2. **Smart Format Detection**: Lovart automatically detects image formats and optimizes them for display. Whether you upload a PNG with transparency or a compressed JPG, the system ensures quality preservation.

3. **Batch Upload**: Upload multiple images sequentially to build your asset library. Each image becomes a separate layer you can arrange, resize, or send to the AI for processing.

4. **Use as AI Reference**: After uploading, you can right-click the image and select "Add to chat" to send it as a reference for AI-powered generation or editing. This allows the AI to analyze composition, style, and colors from your uploaded asset.

**Common Use Cases:**

- Upload product photos for AI-powered mockup generation
- Import brand logos for placement across marketing materials
- Add reference images to guide AI style and composition
- Create layered compositions by uploading multiple assets

### Upload Video

- **What It Does** Import video files to the canvas for frame-by-frame editing, motion design, or video-to-design conversion workflows.
- **How to Use**
	- Click the **"+"** icon in the left toolbar / You can also drag and drop video files directly onto the canvas.
	- Select **"Upload Video"**
	- Choose a video file from your device (supports MP4, MOV, etc.)
	- Lovart processes the video and makes it available for editing

**Video Upload Workflows:**

1. **Motion Reference**: Upload videos to use as motion references for AI video generation. The AI analyzes movement patterns, camera angles, and transitions to apply similar motion to generated content.

2. **Frame Extraction**: Once imported, you can extract individual frames as static images to use as keyframes for new video generation or design elements.

3. **Video Editing**: Use Lovart's editing tools to trim, adjust, or enhance video content directly on the canvas before export.

4. **Seamless Integration**: Uploaded videos work seamlessly with the AI Video Generator, allowing you to extend footage, add AI-generated scenes, or apply style transfer.

**Supported Scenarios:**

- Import b-roll footage for marketing video production
- Upload reference videos for motion design inspiration
- Add video content to multi-media presentations and social campaigns

### Frame

- **What It Does** Add structured frames to your canvas for organizing multiple design elements, creating mockups, or building multi-panel layouts. Frames act as containers for grouped content.
- **How to Use**
	- Click the **"+"** icon in the left toolbar
	- Select **"Frame"** (Shortcut: F key)
	- Drag on the canvas to define the frame size
	- Add content inside the frame for organized design layouts

**Mastering Frames for Professional Workflows:**

1. **Container System**: Frames act like smart containers that keep related elements together. Move a frame, and all its contents move with it—perfect for organizing complex layouts.

2. **Aspect Ratio Presets**: When creating frames, Lovart offers quick presets for common formats:
   - Instagram Post (1:1)
   - Instagram Story (9:16)
   - YouTube Thumbnail (16:9)
   - Presentation (16:9)
   - A4 Document (210×297mm)

3. **Nested Frames**: Create sophisticated layouts by nesting frames within frames. This is ideal for magazine-style layouts, product catalogs, or multi-column designs.

4. **Frame-Based Mockups**: The most powerful use of frames is for instant mockups. Place a product image inside a frame, and Lovart's AI can automatically generate a photorealistic scene mockup.

5. **Grid Layouts**: Combine multiple frames with consistent spacing to create professional grid layouts. Perfect for social media grids, portfolio galleries, or comparison layouts.

**Advanced Frame Techniques:**

- **Masking**: Place images or videos inside frames and use the frame edges as natural masks for clean, professional edge handling.
- **Frame Links**: Link multiple frames to resize them proportionally, maintaining consistent aspect ratios across your design system.
- **Content Isolation**: Group sensitive or conditional content within frames for easier management in collaborative projects.

### Comprehensive Canvas Workflow Example

Here's how all three elements work together in a typical design session:

1. **Start with Reference**: Upload brand guidelines or competitor designs as reference images
2. **Build Structure**: Use Frames to create the overall layout structure (social post, banner, print)
3. **Add Media**: Import product videos or lifestyle imagery into appropriate frames
4. **AI Enhancement**: Right-click assets and "Add to chat" for AI-powered transformations
5. **Final Polish**: Arrange, resize, and refine within the organized frame structure

This workflow ensures you maintain perfect organization while having full access to Lovart's AI capabilities.

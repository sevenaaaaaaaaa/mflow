## Step-by-Step: Create Your Social Media Banner in 5 Minutes

### Step 1

Tell Lovart: 'Create a [platform] cover banner for [brand]. Key message: [headline]. Include: logo, tagline, website. Style: [professional/modern/bold].'

### Step 2

Review the composition. Check: logo visible? Message readable at a glance? Enough negative space?

### Step 3

Test the safe zone. Ask: 'Adjust layout so key content stays in center-right — leave bottom-left clear for profile picture overlay.'

### Step 4

Generate platform variants: 'Now create the same design for [other platform] at [dimensions].' Lovart adapts composition intelligently.

### Step 5

Export at platform's recommended resolution. Most prefer PNG for banners (sharper text).

Lovart is an AI design agent that turns your ideas into polished creative design (product shots, social posts, marketing campaign kits, brand videos, and more) in minutes.

Most design products give you tools. Lovart gives you finished outcomes. You describe what you need, and Lovart handles the end-to-end creative process: picking models, generating assets, and editing assets with AI, so you get production-ready files fast and easy.

### The Core Idea

You work with Lovart the way you'd work with a designer:

1. **Tell Lovart what you need.** Input a prompt, a reference file, a Brand Kit, or all three.
2. **Lovart picks the right tools.** It routes your request across 30+ top image and video models (Nano Banana, Seedance, and more), automatically choosing the best model for each task.
3. **You get finished assets on an infinite Canvas in minutes.** Every element is editable with our AI editing features.

### What's Inside

- **One creative workspace.** Generate, edit, and organize everything (graphics, ads, slides, videos) on a single infinite Canvas. No tool-switching.
- **AI Skills for any creative design tasks.** One-click workflows for brand identity, product listings, social media assets, and more. Think of Skills as presets that chain the right prompts and models together.
- **Full editing control.** Select any element and refine it with intuitive tools or natural-language prompts: "make the background warmer," "swap this font," "remove the logo."
- **Access to the best image and video models, automatically.** Lovart routes each task to the model that does it best, so you don't have to learn them.
- **Export-ready files.** PNG, SVG, PSD, PPTX, MP4. Formats your team and platforms actually accept.

### Go Faster With One-Click Skills

If you're producing a common output (an Instagram post, a YouTube thumbnail, a product listing, a video ad) but don't know or don't want to prompt, you can start with our one-click Skills.

Lovart Skills are intelligent workflows tuned for specific creative tasks. When you pick a Skill, the Agent knows what it needs to produce, asks you for anything missing, and moves through the steps automatically to deliver a finished output. Unlike a free-form prompt, a Skill encodes prompt best practices, professional design guidelines, and multi-step chains, so you can follow the Agent's lead.

**Available Skill Categories**

- **Branding**: Build logos, brand systems, and campaign visuals for new or existing brands.
- **Marketing**: Generate ad creative and campaign assets tailored to specific platforms and objectives.
- **Social Media**: Design Instagram posts, YouTube thumbnails, RedNote covers, and other platform-native content.
- **E-Commerce**: Produce product shots, listing images, and mockups for online storefronts.
- **Video**: Turn briefs into finished videos with shot lists, consistent characters, and smooth transitions.

Jump to [Lovart Skills](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/how-to-prompt/agent-skills#aa9126e0113d) for a full feature description.

### Pro Tips

- **Ask the Agent.** Not sure what a feature does or which tool to use? Ask the Agent in the input box, and it will explain any feature, suggest use cases, or run the task for you.
- **Every edit is reversible.** New edited versions land on the Canvas as fresh assets. The original stays put, so you can experiment freely.
- **Start with a Skill for common creative tasks.** If you're making a logo, a social post, or a product shot, a Skill will get you there faster than a free-form prompt.

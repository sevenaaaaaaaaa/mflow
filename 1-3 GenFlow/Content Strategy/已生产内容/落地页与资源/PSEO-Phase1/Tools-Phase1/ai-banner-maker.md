## Auto-Resize: One Design, Every Format

The killer feature of Lovart's AI Banner Maker is Auto-Resize. You design once — describe your banner concept, approve the composition — and Lovart intelligently adapts that design to every format you need.

Unlike simple cropping or scaling, Auto-Resize understands composition. It reflows elements, repositions CTAs, and adjusts typography scale for each dimension. A horizontal leaderboard (728×90) gets a compact, landscape-optimized layout. The same campaign's Instagram Story (1080×1920) gets a vertical composition with repositioned headline and CTA. Same visual identity, different layout logic — all automatic.

## Banner Design Principles Lovart Follows

You do not need to know these principles — Lovart encodes them into every generation. But understanding them helps you write better prompts:

- **F-pattern layout:** For horizontal banners, the eye naturally scans in an F pattern — top-left to top-right, then down the left edge, then across. Lovart places the headline and brand mark along this path.
- **CTA isolation:** The call-to-action button should be the highest-contrast element on the banner. Lovart ensures CTA buttons stand out from background imagery.
- **Safe zones:** Text and critical elements stay within platform-specific safe zones to avoid cropping or overlay conflicts (especially important for social media banners with platform UI elements).
- **Color psychology:** Lovart selects color palettes based on your described mood — warm tones for urgency and excitement, cool tones for trust and professionalism, high contrast for direct-response ads.

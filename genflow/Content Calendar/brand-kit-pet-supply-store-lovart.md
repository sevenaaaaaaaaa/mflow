## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Independent pet-supply branding must feel like a neighborhood institution with expert taste. It should signal that you carry what the big chains don't—and that your staff actually knows what "novel protein" means.

- **Primary**: `#5C6BC0` — Confident indigo, the color of quality, knowledge, and premium pet nutrition
- **Secondary**: `#E8EAF6` — Soft periwinkle-white for shelf tags, newsletters, and digital backgrounds
- **Accent**: `#FF7043` — Playful coral for "New Arrival" badges, sale tags, and celebration CTAs
- **Warmth**: `#FFC107` — Cheerful amber for loyalty stamps, "Staff Pick" tags, and golden-retriever vibes
- **Neutral**: `#37474F` — Deep slate for body text and nutrition-panel information

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create shelf-talker templates for different product attributes (grain-free, made in USA, etc.)?**
Yes. Lovart's shelf-talker module generates attribute-specific icon badges. Shelf-talker templates include space for a "Staff Note" handwritten recommendation.

**Q2: How do I compete with Chewy and Amazon visually?**
Lovart's competitive-positioning mode helps you emphasize what big-box retailers can't: local curation, staff expertise, and community. Every template includes a "Why We Chose This" field—tell the story behind the product. You're not a warehouse; you're a curator. Brand that distinction.

**Q3: Can Lovart generate email newsletter templates?**
Absolutely. Weekly newsletter template: "New This Week" product grid, "Staff Pick" spotlight with photo, "Pet of the Week" community feature (customer-submitted photos), "Events & Workshops" calendar (nutrition seminars, adoption days, nail-trim clinics), and "Birthday Shoutouts." **Q5: What about private-label product branding?**

## The Chat-Generate Google Ads Workflow

### Step 1 — Map Your Required Assets

Before you open the agent, list every image asset your campaign needs:

| Format | Dimensions | Quantity Needed | Notes |
|--------|-----------|-----------------|-------|
| Landscape | 1200x628 | 5-10 images | RDA primary format |
| Square | 1200x1200 | 5-10 images | RDA and Gmail ads |
| Portrait (Discovery) | 1200x1500 | 2-3 images | Discovery campaigns only |
| Logo | 1200x1200 and 1200x300 | 1-2 variants | Must be center-weighted |
| Video thumbnail | 1200x628 | 1 per video | Only if running video |

This is not a creative brief. It is a production checklist. The agent will generate each format from a single base scene description.

### Step 2 — Write Your Base Scene Prompt

Unlike social media ads, Google Ads images appear in contexts that range from news websites to Gmail inboxes to mobile games. The creative must be self-contained — no reliance on the surrounding content for context. Your prompt should prioritize clarity over cleverness.

```
Google Display Ad image, 1200x628 landscape. A clean product shot of our ergonomic office chair 
on a light-gray background. The chair is angled slightly left, facing right — toward the potential 
text overlay area. Soft studio lighting, no harsh shadows. A subtle brand watermark in the bottom 
left corner. Clean, professional, e-commerce product photography style. 
No text on the image — leave space for Google's auto-generated headline overlays. 
Product must occupy roughly 40% of the frame on the left side, leaving the right 60% clean for Google's 
responsive text placement. High resolution, crisp edges, 300 DPI equivalent sharpness.
```

Critical Google-specific considerations:
- **No text on image** where possible — Google overlays headlines and descriptions dynamically. Text in the image can overlap with Google's ad text, creating a cluttered, unreadable mess.
- **Asymmetric composition** — leave the right 50-60% of landscape images clean for ad copy placement.
- **Center-weighted for square** — square images appear in Gmail, where they are often displayed small. The focal subject must work at 200x200 pixels.
- **No borders or edges that blend with white backgrounds** — Google serves ads on sites with varied color schemes. A slight shadow or darker edge helps the ad stand out regardless of the publisher's background.

### Step 3 — Generate the Full Asset Suite

From your base scene, prompt each required format:

**Landscape variants (5 images)**:
```
Variant 1: Product on white, facing right (default).
Variant 2: Product in a modern home office setting, natural light, lifestyle context.
Variant 3: Product detail — close-up of the lumbar support mechanism, demonstrating the key differentiator.
Variant 4: Product with a person sitting in it (back view, no identifiable face — privacy-safe), working at a desk.
Variant 5: Product on a colored accent background (brand blue) to stand out in light-mode placements.
```

**Square variants (5 images)**:
```
Variant 1: Product centered, clean studio shot.
Variant 2: Product at 45-degree angle, dynamic composition.
Variant 3: Lifestyle — chair in a sunlit room, aspirational.
Variant 4: Feature callout — chair with labels pointing to ergonomic features.
Variant 5: Social proof — chair with a subtle 'Best Seller' badge, clean design.
```

**Portrait Discovery ads (1080x1920 or 1200x1500)**:
```
Portrait Discovery ad, 1200x1500. Same ergonomic chair but in a vertical composition. 
Product occupies the lower third, facing upward toward the viewer. 
The upper two-thirds is a gradient background (brand colors) with subtle abstract shapes 
that suggest comfort and movement. No hard text — leave room for Google's headline overlays. 
The product should look aspirational and premium, as if featured in an architectural digest.
```

### Step 4 — Prepare Your Google Ads Upload

Google Ads Manager accepts images individually. Uploading manually is tedious. Instead, generate all assets into a single folder and batch-upload:

1. Export all landscape variants as 1200x628 JPGs at 85% quality.
2. Export all square variants as 1200x1200 JPGs at 85% quality.
3. Export logos separately — PNG with transparent background for the logo asset slot. Center-weighted 1200x1200 version for square, horizontal 1200x300 version for landscape.
4. In Google Ads Editor or the web interface, select your Responsive Display Ad and upload all images at once — Google will auto-crop and adapt them for different placements.

### Step 5 — Monitor and Refresh

Google's Ad Strength indicator provides a score (Poor to Excellent) based on the quantity and variety of your image assets. Uploading the full 15-image suite usually pushes your score from Average to Excellent — which directly affects your ad's eligibility to compete in auctions.

Check your asset report after 14 days. The report shows which images Google is serving most and which it has deprioritized. Replace underperformers with variants that echo the winners' composition and style. This continuous refresh cadence — two new images every two weeks — keeps your Ad Strength at Excellent and your CTR from decaying.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What image formats does Google Ads accept?**

JPG and PNG, up to 5120 KB per file. For Responsive Display Ads: landscape images must be exactly 1200x628 (1.91:1 ratio), square images 1200x1200 (1:1). Google recommends avoiding heavy compression artifacts — export at 85%+ quality.

**Can the agent generate Google Shopping ads images?**

Google Shopping uses product images from your Merchant Center feed. The Lovart agent can generate clean, white-background product shots at your feed's required specifications (minimum 100x100, recommended 800x800 or larger, no watermarks, no promotional text).

**How do I generate Google Ads for regulated industries (finance, healthcare)?**

Specify regulatory constraints in your prompt: "Google Display Ad for a financial service. Must appear professional, trustworthy, conservative — no exaggerated claims, no aspirational lifestyle imagery that could imply guaranteed results. Clean, corporate, compliant."

**Will Google reject AI-generated images?**

Google does not have a policy against AI-generated ad images. However, Google's ad policies around misleading content, trademark infringement, and image quality apply regardless of how the image was produced. Ensure your creatives meet all standard Google Ads policies.

**How often should I refresh my Google Ads images?**

Google recommends refreshing Responsive Display Ad assets every 30-45 days. Many Lovart advertisers refresh 2-3 images per RDA every two weeks — a lightweight cadence that maintains Ad Strength without the overhead of full asset replacement.

**Can I A/B test Google ad images?**

Google's Responsive Display Ads automatically A/B test your uploaded assets and optimize delivery toward the best-performing combinations. Uploading more image variety gives the algorithm more signals to optimize — which is the entire point of generating a full suite instead of the minimum.

**Do I need different images for Performance Max campaigns?**

Yes — Performance Max campaigns require both square and landscape images, similar to RDAs, plus optionally portrait images and video assets. Use the same base scene prompt but generate additional lifestyle variants and short video-friendly storyboard frames.

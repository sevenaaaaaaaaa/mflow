## Format-by-Format Guide

### Flyers
The workhorse of local marketing. Standard sizes: A5 (5.8×8.3"), A4 (8.3×11.7"), US Letter (8.5×11"). Your prompt should answer three questions immediately: *"What is this for? When is it happening? Why should anyone care?"*

Prompt template: *"Promotional flyer for [business/event]. [Key offer/date] as dominant headline. Supporting details in secondary hierarchy. [Brand mood] photography style. [Color palette]. A5, double-sided, 300 DPI, CMYK, 3mm bleed."*

### Business Cards
The smallest canvas with the highest expectations. A business card that feels cheap undermines every other impression you make. Material matters: request *"luxury uncoated paper texture background"* or *"spot UV gloss effect on logo"* in your prompts to signal quality.

Prompt template: *"Business card, [industry]. Double-sided. Front: [logo + name + title] center-aligned, generous whitespace. Back: [contact details + social handles + QR code area]. [Brand color] accent. 3.5×2 inches, 300 DPI, CMYK, 0.125-inch bleed."*

### Invitations
Whether digital or print, invitations carry emotional weight. An **ai invitation maker** needs to capture the tone of the event — formal, playful, exclusive, intimate — before anything else.

Prompt template: *"Wedding invitation, [style — modern minimalist / botanical / art deco / rustic]. [Names + date + venue] as hero typography. [Color palette]. RSVP details in footer. 5×7 inches, 300 DPI, CMYK. Matching RSVP card variant."*

### Brochures
The most information-dense format. A good **brochure ai** prompt specifies both content structure and visual flow:

Prompt template: *"Tri-fold brochure for [business]. Panel 1: hero image + tagline. Panel 2: services overview (three columns, icon-led). Panel 3: about + contact. Panel 4-6: detailed service descriptions with supporting imagery. [Brand style]. 8.5×11 unfolded, 300 DPI, CMYK."*

### Menus
An **ai menu design** should prioritize scannability and appetite appeal. Food photography quality makes or breaks a menu — specify *"appetizing food photography, warm lighting, shallow depth of field, styled plating"* in your prompt.

Prompt template: *"Restaurant menu for [cuisine]. [Number of sections — appetizers / mains / desserts / drinks]. Each section with category header, 6–8 items with descriptions and prices. [Brand aesthetic]. Warm food photography in hero positions. A3 folded to A4, 300 DPI, CMYK."*

## The Zero-AI Trope: The Menu That Survived 40 Years

There's a diner in your town — every town has one — where the menu hasn't changed since 1983. The plastic lamination is peeling. The prices have been crossed out and rewritten in ballpoint pen three times. The typeface is whatever came standard on an IBM Selectric. And somehow, it works. It works because it's honest. It doesn't try to be a design object; it tries to be a menu. Useful. Clear. Surviving. The best AI-generated menus, flyers, and cards borrow this principle: design serves purpose. Before you add that third font weight or that decorative border, ask: *is this making the thing better at its job, or just making it look designed?*

## FAQ

**Q: Can Lovart create double-sided designs for flyers and business cards?**
Yes. Specify "double-sided" in your prompt. Lovart generates front and back as coordinated designs — front for visual impact, back for information density.

**Q: How do I ensure all my collateral looks like the same brand?**
Use Lovart's Brand Kit. Upload your logo, set brand colors and fonts. Every piece — flyer, card, invitation, menu — will automatically share the same visual DNA.

**Q: Can I add QR codes to my designs?**
Yes. In ChatCanvas, say *"add a QR code placeholder in the bottom-right corner, 1×1 inch."* You can also upload your own QR code PNG and Lovart will integrate it into the composition.

**Q: What file format should I send to a print shop?**
PDF with embedded fonts and images set to 300 DPI, CMYK color mode, with 3mm bleed. Most print shops also accept high-resolution PNG (300 DPI, CMYK). Ask your printer for their specific requirements.

**Q: Can AI design a brochure with the correct fold lines?**
Lovart can generate designs with fold guides indicated. For print production, export the design and add trim/fold marks in your print shop's online portal or provide fold-line PDFs generated from the Lovart output.

**Q: How do I get consistent food photography across my menu?**
Include detailed food photography prompts: *"overhead flat lay of [dish], natural window light, styled on [surface/material], shallow depth of field, appetizing steam/moisture visible."* Generate all food images with the same lighting and styling prompts.

**Q: Is there a template for wedding invitations in Lovart?**
Lovart doesn't use static templates — it generates original designs from your prompts. Include wedding-specific keywords: *"wedding invitation suite, [style], [color palette], matching RSVP card, vellum overlay, wax seal detail."*

**Q: Can I edit text after the design is generated?**
Absolutely. ChatCanvas lets you edit any text element — change copy, adjust font size, reposition — through natural language or the touch-edit interface. No starting over.

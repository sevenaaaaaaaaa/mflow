## Step 1: Create a Reels Cover Canvas

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Open Lovart's ChatCanvas and type: **"Instagram Reels cover, 1080x1920 portrait, bold text overlay area in the center third, subject photo placeholder in the background, clean and modern aesthetic."** The Reels cover is technically the same 9:16 ratio as the Reel itself, but the design approach differs — the center area must hold text that reads clearly when the cover appears as a 180×320px thumbnail in the profile grid. Lovart generates a layout with a centered text-safe zone, a background layer, and optional accent elements pre-positioned.

## Step 2: Import and Set the Background

Upload your best frame from the Reel — preferably one where you're making direct eye contact with the camera and your expression telegraphs the Reel's emotional payload (curiosity, surprise, or amusement). Use Touch Edit to crop and position. Lovart's **AI Depth Blur** automatically detects the subject and applies a subtle background blur (3–5px) that mimics the f/1.8 portrait-mode look, making the cover feel cinematic even if it was shot on a front-facing phone camera. Adjust blur intensity with a slider.

## Step 3: Add the Hook Text

Double-tap the center text zone. Your cover text should be a 3–6 word hook that creates an information gap: "You're Doing Mascara Wrong," "The 10-Minute Dinner Hack," "Why Your Plants Keep Dying." Lovart's type engine defaults to a heavy weight font (Montserrat Bold, 68pt) optimized for thumbnail legibility. Apply a **Text Background** — a semi-transparent dark rectangle behind the text — with 40% opacity black fill and 12px corner radius. This ensures readability over any background image, no matter how busy or light.

## Step 4: Add Branding Elements

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

Tap the bottom-right corner and select **Add Logo.** Lovart places your Brand Kit logo at 32×32px with 20px margin — small enough not to distract from the hook, large enough to build brand recognition across repeated impressions. If your Reel is part of a series, add a series badge: tap **Add Shape → Rounded Rectangle,** position it in the top-left corner, set fill to your brand accent color, and add text like "Ep. 4" or "Part 2" inside. Series badges increase profile-grid scan-ability and encourage binge-watching behavior.

## Step 5: Export and Upload as Cover

Tap **Export** and select **Reels Cover (1080×1920, PNG).** Lovart optimizes the file with Instagram's recommended compression profile. When uploading your Reel in Instagram, tap the cover image selector and choose **Add from Camera Roll.** Select your Lovart-designed cover. Important: Instagram sometimes auto-defaults to a video frame even after you select a custom cover. Double-check the cover preview before hitting Share. Lovart's Export screen includes a **Profile Grid Preview** that simulates how your cover will look alongside eight other covers — check that your text remains readable at that scale.

## FAQ

**Q: Does Reels cover design affect algorithmic reach?**
A: Instagram's algorithm prioritizes Reels based on watch time, shares, and saves — not cover design directly. But covers dramatically affect tap-through rate from the Explore page and profile grid, which indirectly boosts reach via engagement signals.

**Q: Can I reuse the same Reels cover template across multiple Reels?**
A: Yes. Save your design as a Lovart **Project Template** (File → Save as Template). For each new Reel, open the template, swap the background image, and update the text. Total time per cover: under 90 seconds.

**Q: Should Reels covers include text or just an image?**
A: Data from Later's 2025 Instagram Trends report shows Reels covers with 3–6 words of text generate 31% more profile-grid taps than image-only covers. Text creates an information gap that image alone rarely achieves.

**Q: What fonts work best for Reels cover text at small sizes?**
A: Heavy-weight sans-serifs with tall x-height: Montserrat Bold, Inter Extra Bold, and DM Sans Bold all perform well. Avoid thin, script, or decorative fonts — they dissolve into noise at 180px width.

**Q: Can Lovart generate Reels covers in bulk for batch content?**
A: On the $49 Pro plan, use **Batch Generate** — upload a CSV with text fields and background image paths, and Lovart produces covers for an entire content calendar in one operation.

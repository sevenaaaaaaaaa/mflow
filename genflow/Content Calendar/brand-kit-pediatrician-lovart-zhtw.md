## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Pediatrics branding occupies the rare intersection of childlike warmth and parental reassurance. Too cartoonish, and you lose medical credibility. Too clinical, and kids dread the visit.

- **Primary**: `#FFB347` — Warm tangerine that feels energetic, cheerful, and approachable
- **Secondary**: `#FFF8E7` — Soft buttercream for backgrounds and printed handouts
- **Accent**: `#E08E2C` — Deep amber for text readability and calls-to-action
- **Support**: `#7EC8E3` — Sky blue for secondary illustrations and milestone charts
- **Neutral**: `#FFFDF7` — Warm white for exam-room collateral and digital cards

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create a mascot character for my practice?**
Yes. Describe your ideal mascot ("a smiling elephant with a bandage on its trunk" or "a sloth with a tiny clipboard") and Lovart generates vector illustrations you own outright. No licensing fees. The mascot auto-appears across all templates.

**Q2: How do I brand my practice differently from the large hospital network nearby?**
Lovart's competitive-analysis feature scans local pediatric practices and identifies visual gaps. If everyone uses blue, Lovart suggests warm palettes that stand out in local search results while maintaining medical credibility.

**Q3: Does the vaccine schedule template stay updated?**
Lovart connects to the CDC immunization schedule API. When guidelines change, the infographic auto-updates. You approve the change before it goes live. Available on $49 Pro tier.

**Q4: Can I print these templates at standard sizes?**
All templates export with bleed marks and CMYK profiles for professional printing. Common pediatric sizes pre-configured: 4×6" appointment cards, 18×24" and 24×36" posters, and 8.5×11" handouts.

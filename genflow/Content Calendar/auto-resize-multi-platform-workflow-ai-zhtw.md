## 2. How Auto-Resize Actually Works

Auto-Resize is not a simple image scaler. It's a composition-aware layout adapter.

**What it does:**

1. Accepts a source design from ChatCanvas (any aspect ratio, any platform)
2. Analyzes the composition — identifies text elements, image areas, background regions, CTA placements, and decorative elements
3. Recalculates element positioning for each target platform's dimensions
4. Adjusts typography scaling to maintain readability at different sizes
5. Repositions CTAs and key visual elements to maintain visual hierarchy
6. Crops or extends background regions intelligently (not just center-crop)
7. Exports all variants simultaneously

**What it doesn't do:** Simply stretch, squash, or center-crop your design. Those are the output of basic image resizers — not composition-aware AI.

## 3. The 3 Auto-Resize Strategies

Lovart offers three Auto-Resize strategies depending on your content type:

### Strategy 1: Intelligent Reframe
Best for: Photographic hero images, simple text overlays.
- Background image is reframed (not just cropped) to fit the target aspect ratio
- Text elements reposition proportionally
- Works well for image-dominant designs with minimal text

### Strategy 2: Layout Reflow
Best for: Text-heavy designs, infographics, multi-element compositions.
- Elements reflow into the new aspect ratio
- Text blocks resize and re-wrap
- Image placements adjust to maintain visual balance
- More complex but preserves compositional intent

### Strategy 3: Element-Adaptive
Best for: Designs with distinct visual sections (header, body, footer).
- Each section adapts independently
- Header/footer elements maintain positions
- Body content reflows to fill available space
- Ideal for presentation slides, email designs, article headers

## 6. Quality Assurance: What to Check

Auto-Resize is highly reliable but not infallible. A 30-second quality check catches most issues:

**Post-resize checklist:**

1. **Text readability** — Is all text still legible at the target size? Check smallest platform variants.
2. **CTA visibility** — Is the call-to-action still prominent and clickable? Check that it hasn't been cropped or obscured.
3. **Image cropping** — Does the reframed image still communicate the intended message? Check that critical image content hasn't been cropped out.
4. **Logo placement** — Is the logo appropriately sized and positioned for each platform?
5. **Safe zones** — Are critical elements clear of platform UI overlays (profile icons, like buttons, timestamps)?
6. **Composition balance** — Does each variant feel intentionally designed for its platform, not obviously adapted from another size?

If any variant fails a checklist item, use Edit Elements to adjust the specific element on that specific variant. The adjustment takes seconds and does not affect other variants.

## FAQ

### Does Auto-Resize work for video content?
Yes. Auto-Resize handles still frames and video thumbnails. For full video resizing (aspect ratio conversion), Lovart's Seedance video workflow handles motion content with composition-aware cropping.

### Can I customize how Auto-Resize handles a specific element?
Yes. After Auto-Resize generates variants, use Edit Elements to adjust any element on any variant. Changes to one variant do not affect others. This gives you both automation (for the 90% that works automatically) and control (for the 10% that needs adjustment).

### What happens to text size across different platforms?
Auto-Resize scales text proportionally but enforces minimum readability thresholds. Text never drops below 12pt equivalent on any variant. You can override the automatic scaling by editing the text element directly.

### Does Auto-Resize handle display ad banner sizes?
Yes. Auto-Resize supports common IAB display ad sizes (300×250, 728×90, 160×600, 300×600, 970×250). Select "Display Ads" as a platform category and choose your required sizes.

### Can I create custom platform presets?
Yes. On Studio ($49/mo) and above, you can define custom platform presets with specific dimensions, safe zones, and optimization preferences. Save presets for recurring formats (newsletter headers, event graphics, partner co-branded assets).

### How does Auto-Resize handle designs with multiple text columns?
Layout Reflow strategy handles multi-column text by reflowing into the new aspect ratio. Two-column text in a 16:9 design may become single-column in a 9:16 variant. Review multi-column resizes — reflow may require manual adjustment for optimal readability.

### Is Auto-Resize available on the Free plan?
Auto-Resize is available on Pro ($19/mo) and above. The Free plan supports manual resizing only. If you produce content for more than 2 platforms, the time savings from Auto-Resize justify Pro tier on the first day of use.

### Can I batch Auto-Resize multiple designs simultaneously?
On Agency ($99/mo) and Enterprise ($149/mo) plans, you can queue multiple designs for Auto-Resize processing. Upload 10 campaign graphics, select target platforms, and receive all variants within minutes. Individual plans process one design at a time.

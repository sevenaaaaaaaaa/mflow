# Lovart Insight and Trend Playbook

## Writing goal

Convert a market shift into a practical point of view that helps readers make better creative or operational decisions.

## Core structure

1. Quick Overview
2. What shift is happening
3. The proof: search, platform, or enterprise data
4. Why the shift is happening now
5. What changes in actual creative work
6. A first-person Lovart implementation walkthrough
7. Risks, objections, and what not to overread
8. FAQ
9. Key Takeaways

## Must-have moves

- define the shift in one sentence
- include at least three quantified data points
- use more than one type of source
- explain behavioral or workflow consequences
- include a grounded Lovart application layer

## Five topic templates

1. AI design workflow transition
2. Human-made signals vs AI polish
3. Brand governance at scale
4. Provenance and copyright readiness
5. Code-native or canvas-native creation

## Anti-patterns

- "the future is here" filler
- trend claims without evidence
- pure summary of external reports
- no recommended reader action

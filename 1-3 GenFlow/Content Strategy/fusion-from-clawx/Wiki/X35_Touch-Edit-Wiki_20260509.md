Touch Edit is Lovart's semantic click-to-edit system. Click any object in an AI image and describe the change. Supports: color change, object replacement, background change, detail enhancement, object removal, style transfer. AI maintains surrounding context and visual consistency. No masking or selection tools needed. Works on both AI-generated and uploaded images.

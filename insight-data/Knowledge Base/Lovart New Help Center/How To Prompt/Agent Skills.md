# Agent Skills

### Lovart Skills

> One-click, ready-to-use workflows for logos, product listings, social assets, video ads, and more.

**What It Does**

Lovart Skills are intelligent workflows tuned for specific creative tasks. When you pick a Skill, the Agent knows what it needs to produce, asks you for anything missing, and moves through the steps automatically to deliver a finished output. Unlike a free-form prompt, a Skill encodes prompt best practices, professional design guidelines, and multi-step chains, so you can follow the Agent's lead.

**How to Use**

1. **Open the Skill Book.**
    - When you open a new project, Skills appear in the empty input box.
    - At any other time, click the Skills icon in the bottom-left of the input box.
2. **Pick a Skill.**
    - Browse by category and select the one that best matches your task.
3. **Describe what you need.**
    - Type a short prompt with your goal.
    - Upload any references.
4. **Follow the Agent's guide.**
    - The Agent asks for any missing details (preferences, goals, assets, etc.) and runs the workflow to produce the final deliverables.

**Available Skill Categories**

- **Branding**: Build logos, brand systems, and campaign visuals for new or existing brands.
- **Marketing**: Generate ad creative and campaign assets tailored to specific platforms and objectives.
- **Social Media**: Design Instagram posts, YouTube thumbnails, RedNote covers, and other platform-native content.
- **E-Commerce**: Produce product shots, listing images, and mockups for online storefronts.
- **Video**: Turn briefs into finished videos with shot lists, consistent characters, and smooth transitions.

**Quick Tips**

- When in doubt, ask the Agent. The Agent will recommend or explain a Skill to you.
- Upload references and describe your intent to guide the Agent.

**Related Features**

- [Custom Skills](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/how-to-prompt/agent-skills#af17e86bce34): Build your own reusable Skill from conversations.

### Custom Skills

> Save a successful project conversation as a reusable Skill.

**What It Does**

Custom Skills distill any conversation into a reusable Skill, so you can rerun the same workflow on new inputs without prompting from scratch. Use it to lock in a process you've already proven, and build a personal library of workflows over time.

**How to Use**

1. **Create a successful project.**
    - Turn on [Thinking Mode](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/getting-started/thinking-and-fast-mode#2dda637d02d6).
    - Talk to the Agent until you are happy with the design output(s).
2. **Save it as a Skill.**
    - Open the Skill Book in the input box and click Create a Skill.
    - Save the new Skill under My Skills.
3. **Reuse it.**
    - In a new chat, open the Skill Book and select your saved Skill.
    - You can edit your custom Skill's instructions, examples, or other details as your workflow evolves.
    - Follow the Skill's guidance to rerun the workflow on new inputs.

**Quick Tips**

- Custom Skills capture the conversational path, so a clean, focused session makes a cleaner Skill than one with detours and corrections.
- Custom Skills let you create personal versions of [Lovart Skills](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/how-to-prompt/agent-skills#aa9126e0113d). Use Lovart Skills for general jobs and My Skills for workflows specific to your brand or process.

**Limitations**

- Custom Skills are only available in [Thinking Mode](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/getting-started/thinking-and-fast-mode#2dda637d02d6), and only after at least one Agent conversation in the session.

**Related Features**

- [Lovart Skills](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/how-to-prompt/agent-skills#aa9126e0113d): Use the built-in library before building your own.
- [Thinking & Fast Mode](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/how-to-prompt/chat-tools#b937f7b7e773): Required for Custom Skills to be available.
- [Brand Kit](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/how-to-prompt/adding-references#828020ecba5b): Store visual identity instead of a conversational workflow.

## Template Categories

### Feed Post Templates

Single-image and carousel post designs optimized for the 1:1 square and 4:5 vertical formats. Categories include:

- **Quote and Inspirational:** Typography-forward designs for motivational quotes, testimonials, and brand values.
- **Product Showcase:** Clean layouts that put your product front and center — with pricing, feature callouts, and shop-now CTAs.
- **Educational Carousel:** Multi-slide designs for how-to guides, listicles, myth-busting, and data breakdowns. Each slide maintains visual continuity while advancing the narrative.
- **Behind-the-Scenes:** Photo-collage layouts for studio tours, team spotlights, process reveals, and day-in-the-life content.
- **Promotional and Sale:** High-contrast designs for flash sales, launches, giveaways, and limited-time offers.

### Story Templates

Vertical 9:16 designs that work within Instagram's story interface — accounting for the UI overlay (profile photo, reply bar, swipe-up indicator) so your content stays visible. Categories include:

- **Poll and Quiz:** Interactive story designs with built-in poll, quiz, and question sticker placements.
- **Countdown and Announcement:** Hype-building designs for product launches, event countdowns, and big reveals.
- **Link-in-Bio Promo:** Story designs that drive traffic with prominent swipe-up or link-sticker CTAs.
- **Daily and Weekly Series:** Numbered episode templates for recurring content — Tip of the Day, Weekly Roundup, Monday Motivation.

### Reels Cover Templates

The Reels cover is your content's thumbnail in the grid — it determines whether someone taps or scrolls past. Lovart's reel cover templates are designed for the 9:16 format with bold, legible titles that read well at small grid sizes. Categories include tutorial covers, vlog covers, trend covers, and product demo covers.

### Highlight Covers

Customizable icon-based highlight covers for organizing your profile into clear categories — Products, Reviews, Tutorials, Podcast, Behind the Brand, FAQs, Events. Consistent style across all covers creates a polished, professional profile at first glance.

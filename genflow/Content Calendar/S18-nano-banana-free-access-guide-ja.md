## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is Nano Banana really free forever?**
Yes. Lovart's free plan is not a time-limited trial. You get 20 generations per month, every month, indefinitely. There's no expiration date and no hidden conversion to a paid plan.

**Q: Do I own the images I generate with the free plan?**
Yes. Lovart grants full commercial usage rights for designs created on both free and paid plans. You can use them for social media, ads, products, client work — anything.

**Q: Can I upgrade to paid and then downgrade back to free?**
Yes. You can upgrade and downgrade anytime. If you downgrade, you keep access to all your previous designs. Your generation limit returns to 20/month.

**Q: What's the catch with the free plan?**
The 20-generation limit and standard-speed processing. That's it. Lovart's business model relies on heavy users upgrading to paid plans, which funds the free tier for everyone else. It's the classic freemium model — and it works.

**Q: Can I use my own images with Nano Banana?**
Yes. You can upload your own images and use Touch Edit to modify them — change backgrounds, adjust colors, remove objects, add elements. Touch Edit actions on uploaded images don't count against your generation limit on the free plan (limited to 5 edits per design).

**Q: How does Nano Banana compare to Midjourney or DALL-E?**
Nano Banana is optimized for design output, not pure image generation. It's built to produce images that work within designs — with readable text, clean compositions, and design-aware aesthetics. Midjourney might produce a more artistically stunning standalone image, but that image would need significant post-processing to become a usable design. Nano Banana's images are ready to ship as design components.

## Project 1: Instagram Post (2 Minutes)

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

**What you'll make:** A polished social media post that looks like you hired a designer.

**Steps:**

1. **Open ChatCanvas** and type:
   > *"Create an Instagram post for a [your industry] brand. Announce [something interesting — a sale, a launch, a tip]. Modern, clean layout. Include a headline, short description, and a 'Learn More' button. Use warm, approachable colors."*

2. **Wait 3 seconds.** Your design appears on canvas — headline, body text, image, CTA button, all laid out.

3. **Touch Edit:** Tap the headline. Type your actual business name or message. Tap the description. Write your real copy. Everything is editable like a document, not a rendered image.

4. **Adjust one thing:** Drag the CTA button up slightly if it feels off. Resize the headline if it's too small. No layers panel, no right-click menu — just touch what you want to change.

5. **Export:** Tap Export → Instagram Post (1080x1080) → PNG. Done.

**Time elapsed:** Under 2 minutes.
**What just happened:** Lovart's MCoT analyzed your request, identified the structure (headline + image + copy + CTA), pulled layout patterns that work on Instagram, generated a visual, and placed everything on an editable canvas. You didn't prompt-engineer. You described. It designed.

## Project 2: Business Card (3 Minutes)

**What you'll make:** A double-sided business card you could actually print.

**Steps:**

1. **ChatCanvas:**
   > *"Design a double-sided business card for [Your Name], [Your Title] at [Company]. Modern and minimal. Front: logo, name, title. Back: contact details and website. Use [color preference, e.g., navy and gold / black and white / earthy greens]."*

2. **Front side appears.** Touch Edit if you want to adjust spacing or font size.

3. **Generate back side:**
   > *"Add the back of the business card — contact info layout with phone, email, website, and a QR code placeholder."*

4. **Touch Edit** to fill in your actual contact details. Replace the QR placeholder with your real website URL.

5. **Export:** Business Card (3.5" x 2", 300dpi, PDF with bleed) → ready to send to a printer.

**Time elapsed:** 3 minutes.
**Why this matters:** A year ago, designing a print-ready business card required Illustrator or Canva skills plus an understanding of bleed, safe zones, and DPI. Now it's a conversation.

## Project 3: Product Mockup (4 Minutes)

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

**What you'll make:** A scene that shows your product in context — on a desk, in someone's hands, on a shelf — even if you only have a basic product photo.

**Steps:**

1. **Upload your product image.** A simple photo on a white background works perfectly. If you don't have one yet, pick any object image from your phone.

2. **ChatCanvas:**
   > *"Place this product in a [scene description]. For example: "Place this product on a minimalist desk with morning sunlight, a coffee cup nearby, and a plant in the background. Warm tones, soft shadows, realistic lighting."*

3. **Lovart generates the scene** with your product naturally integrated into the environment. The lighting matches, the shadows are directional, the perspective is coherent.

4. **Touch Edit** if you want to:
   - Adjust the product position (drag it)
   - Change the background mood (type "make it dusk lighting instead")
   - Add a text overlay (type "Add 'New Arrival' badge in the top right")

5. **Export:** High-res PNG → ready for your website, social media, or product page.

**Time elapsed:** 4 minutes.
**What's different from other tools:** Traditional mockup tools require you to find a scene template, download it, open Photoshop, place your product as a smart object, adjust lighting manually, and export. You just described the scene you wanted. The AI handled the rest.

## Why These Three Projects?

They're not random. They're designed to teach you the three core Lovart behaviors:

1. **ChatCanvas** — Describing what you want in plain English (Project 1)

Once you've done these three, you understand 80% of what Lovart can do. The remaining 20% — Brand Kit auto-application, video generation, batch export, team collaboration — builds naturally on these foundations.

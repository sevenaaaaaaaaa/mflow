## The Tax Season Content Funnel

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Tax season marketing should follow a three-stage funnel, each requiring distinct visual assets:

### Stage 1: Awareness (January — Mid-February)

The goal here is to catch attention before your competitors do. Audiences are not yet in panic mode; they are curious and open to education.

**Visual Strategy:**
- **Instagram Reel covers** with bold, high-contrast typography: "3 Tax Changes That Affect Your 2026 Return"
- **LinkedIn carousel posts** that break down IRS bracket adjustments into digestible infographics
- **TikTok/Short-form video thumbnails** with expressive facial reactions and numbered lists

**What Lovart Automates:**
Instead of designing each graphic from scratch, feed Lovart the data points (tax bracket changes, deduction limits, contribution caps) and specify your format. The AI generates data visualizations — bar charts, comparison tables, callout boxes — in your brand style. A five-slide carousel that might take two hours manually takes about four minutes with Lovart's data-to-design pipeline.

**Example Prompt:**
> "Create a LinkedIn carousel comparing 2026 vs 2027 standard deduction amounts. Use navy and gold brand colors. Include a separate slide for each filing status. End with a CTA slide: 'Want a personalized estimate? Book a 15-minute call.'"

### Stage 2: Consideration (Mid-February — March)

By now, W-2s and 1099s have arrived. Your audience is actively thinking about tax preparation. This is the consultation-booking window.

**Visual Strategy:**
- **Downloadable tax preparation checklists** — formatted PDFs that prospects can print and use
- **Email drip campaign headers** — 5-email sequence with consistent visual branding
- **Client testimonial graphics** — pull quotes from real clients overlaid on warm, trust-building photography

**The Checklist Power Move:**
Tax prep checklists are the highest-converting lead magnet during season. Design a one-page checklist in Lovart as a branded PDF. Gate it behind an email signup. The checklist should cover:
- Documents to gather (W-2, 1099, mortgage statements, donation receipts)
- Key dates and deadlines
- Common deductions by profession
- Questions to ask your advisor

Every section gets a distinct visual treatment — icons for document types, a timeline graphic for deadlines, color-coded deduction categories. This is not a text document with a logo slapped on top; it is a designed asset that feels professional and helpful.

### Stage 3: Conversion (April 1 — April 15)

The final two weeks. People who have procrastinated are now in active problem-solving mode. Speed matters more than depth.

**Visual Strategy:**
- **Urgency-driven Instagram stories** with countdown timers
- **"Last chance" email banners** with bold, high-contrast CTA buttons
- **Quick-hit video thumbnails** addressing specific pain points: "Forgot to file? Here's what to do."

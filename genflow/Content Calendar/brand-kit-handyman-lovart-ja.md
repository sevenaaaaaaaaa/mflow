## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Handyman branding must communicate versatility, approachability, and reliability. The brand represents dozens of services—the palette must be a big enough tent to cover everything from drywall repair to deck building.

- **Primary**: `#D2691E` — Warm tool-brown, the color of well-worn leather tool belts and hardwood floors
- **Secondary**: `#FAF3E0` — Work-cream for flyers, estimates, and business cards
- **Accent**: `#8B4513` — Saddle brown for typography, logo marks, and trustworthy headers
- **Pop**: `#2A9D8F` — Teal-green for "Available Now" indicators, social links, and trust badges
- **Neutral**: `#5C4033` — Dark oak for footer text and secondary information

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create a brand for a handyman who does 30+ different services?**
Yes. Lovart's "multi-service" mode creates a unified visual system with a service-icon library. Each service gets a small line-art icon. Use them on your flyer, website, estimate, and social media. The AI ensures visual consistency across 30 services without overwhelming complexity.

**Q2: How do I look professional without looking corporate and expensive?**
Lovart's "neighbor-next-door" brand voice toggles between professional and approachable. The palette is warm, not sterile. The fonts are friendly, not corporate. The copy uses "I" not "we." You're a person, not a faceless company—your brand should reflect that.

**Q3: Can Lovart create seasonal service reminders?**
Absolutely. "Fall Gutter Cleaning," "Spring Deck Refinishing," "Holiday Light Hanging," "Pre-Winter Weatherproofing." Lovart generates seasonal postcards and social graphics. Describe the season and services; the AI produces 5 pieces of content in your brand style.

**Q4: Does Lovart generate website copy for a handyman site?**
Yes. Lovart's copy module generates homepage, about, services, and contact-page copy in your brand voice. Describe your background ("20 years remodeling, 3 years as independent handyman"), service area, and personality ("friendly, punctual, no upselling"). The AI writes SEO-optimized, natural-sounding copy.

**Q5: What tier works for a solo handyman just starting out?**
Free tier gives you the logo, flyer, and estimate card. $19 Creator adds social templates and the Nextdoor post series—ideal for building a neighborhood client base. Upgrade when you need a website and email automation.

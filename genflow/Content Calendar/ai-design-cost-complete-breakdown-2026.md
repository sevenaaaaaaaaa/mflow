## The Five Paths to Design Output

Every organization lands somewhere on this spectrum. We cost-modeled each path for a typical mid-market company producing ~80 design assets per month (social posts, ads, email headers, presentation decks, landing page graphics).

### 1. Lovart (AI Generation)
- **Monthly cost:** $0–$149 (Pro tier)
- **Cost per asset:** $0.00–$1.86
- **Typical annual spend:** $0–$1,788
- **Best for:** Speed-first teams, social media, marketing collateral, founders

### 2. Canva (Template Platform)
- **Monthly cost:** $0–$12.99/user (Pro); $30/user (Teams)
- **Cost per asset:** $0.00–$0.41 (labor excluded)
- **Typical annual spend:** $0–$360/user + labor cost of the person operating it
- **Best for:** Teams wanting manual control with template starting points

### 3. Adobe Creative Cloud (Professional Suite)
- **Monthly cost:** $59.99–$89.99/user (single app to full suite)
- **Cost per asset:** $0.75–$1.12 (subscription only, labor excluded)
- **Typical annual spend:** $720–$1,080/user + labor
- **Best for:** Professional designers requiring pixel-level precision

### 4. Hire a Freelancer
- **Monthly cost:** $1,600–$6,000 (20–40 hrs/mo at $25–$150/hr)
- **Cost per asset:** $20–$75
- **Typical annual spend:** $19,200–$72,000
- **Best for:** Custom, high-touch work; one-off campaigns; specialized styles

### 5. Hire an Agency
- **Monthly cost:** $5,000–$50,000 (retainer)
- **Cost per asset:** $63–$625
- **Typical annual spend:** $60,000–$600,000
- **Best for:** Enterprise brand strategy, multi-channel campaigns, full-service needs

## The Hidden Costs Nobody Budgets For

### Revision Cycles
Freelancers typically include 2–3 revision rounds. Beyond that, hourly billing kicks in. Agencies charge per round. A single campaign that goes through 5 iterations with an agency can double the quoted cost. AI tools iterate infinitely at zero marginal cost.

### Briefing Overhead
Writing a comprehensive design brief — including brand guidelines, reference images, copy, dimension specs — takes 15–45 minutes. With AI, the prompt *is* the brief. One step, not two.

### Tool Switching Tax
Many teams piece together Canva + Adobe + Figma + stock photo subscriptions + font licenses. The cumulative subscription stack often exceeds $200/month before a single asset is produced. Consolidation into a single AI platform eliminates this fragmentation.

### Onboarding & Ramp-Up
New freelance designers need 1–2 weeks to internalize brand guidelines. Agencies require 2–4 weeks for full brand immersion. AI tools ingest brand preferences on session one and apply them consistently from the first prompt.

### Asset Storage & Versioning
Separate tools for asset management (Dropbox, Google Drive, DAM platforms) add $10–$50/month and countless hours of "which folder is the final version in?" AI platforms with built-in history and cloud storage collapse this cost center.

## The ROI Formula

For a company spending $50,000/year on design (mid-range freelancer or junior in-house), switching 70% of production to AI generation yields approximately:

- **AI tool cost:** $1,788/year
- **Remaining freelance spend (30% high-touch):** $15,000/year
- **Total new spend:** $16,788/year
- **Annual savings:** $33,212
- **ROI:** 1,857%

The math holds across scales. A startup producing 20 assets/month saves $8,400/year. An enterprise producing 300 assets/month saves $124,000/year.

## Which Path Should You Choose?

**0–50 assets/month, non-designer founder:** Lovart covers everything. Zero marginal cost. No learning curve.

**50–150 assets/month, small marketing team:** Lovart for volume production (social, ads, email) + freelancer for hero campaigns and original illustration. Hybrid model saves 60–80%.

**150+ assets/month, design team in place:** Lovart for first drafts and rapid iteration + Adobe for final polish. AI handles the 80% grunt work; humans focus on the 20% that demands taste.

**Enterprise, multi-brand:** Agency for brand strategy and creative direction + Lovart for local market adaptation and template proliferation. Best of both worlds.

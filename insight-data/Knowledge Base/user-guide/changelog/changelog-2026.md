# Lovart 产品更新日志（Changelog）
Batch on-brand assets for every channel
Product photos and ads in minutes
Luma uni-1 Series Models Now Available.
Lovart now integrates Luma&#x27;s flagship image models, uni-1 and uni-1-max. These models support both text-to-image and image-to-image editing modes, delivering higher-quality visual generation, more consistent style expression, and stronger image editing capabilities. Whether creating from scratch or refining and optimizing existing images, you&#x27;ll achieve more natural and delicate results.
GPT Image 2.0: Enhanced Image Generation is Now Available.
Lovart now integrates the GPT Image 2.0 model, delivering higher-quality image generation and more stable detail performance. Whether it&#x27;s complex scene construction, style expression, or fine-grained editing, you get more consistent and controllable results. From creative concept to final output, every generation now comes closer to your vision.
Generate Custom Fonts with One Click.
Now you can generate custom fonts with one click by uploading an image, entering a description, or combining both, and use them directly on the canvas. They can be reused across different projects, transforming fonts from one-time assets into reusable creative resources, making it easy to build a consistent and unique visual style.
OpenClaw × Lovart: Create Directly in Your Favorite Chat Tools.
Lovart can now be accessed via OpenClaw. After a simple setup, you can use Lovart directly in chat platforms like Feishu, Slack, and Discord to create without constantly switching workflows. Whether generating images, getting design inspiration, or quickly producing visual content, you can accomplish it more efficiently in team collaboration scenarios.
Seedance 2.0: A More Powerful Video Generation Model is Now Available.
Lovart now supports the Seedance 2.0 video generation model. You can quickly create more cinematic video content using text or reference images, achieving smoother motion, more consistent subject stability, and more precise camera control. Whether for short social videos, ad creatives, or storyboards, you can go from idea to final video more efficiently than ever.
Create Your Own Skills: Turn Workflows into Reusable Capabilities.
Now you can save an entire conversation workflow as a Skill with one click and reuse it in any new conversation or project. Whether it&#x27;s a fixed design process, a brand generation logic, or a common creative pattern, all can be accumulated into your personal capability library. Reduce repetitive operations and let your high-frequency workflows achieve continuous reuse and efficiency upgrades.
Use Brand Kit to Keep Every Creation On-Brand.
Now you can create a dedicated Brand Kit in Lovart to centrally manage your logo, color palette, fonts, and design guidelines. Whether it&#x27;s social media content, brand materials, or e-commerce visuals, you can maintain a consistent brand expression across different projects and multiple rounds of creation. No need to repeatedly upload assets or describe specifications—let your brand assets truly accumulate, boosting creative efficiency and result stability.
Export PSD: One-Click to Complete Layered File.
Now you can export your Lovart canvas content as a layered PSD file with one click. The system automatically restores the layer structure and positional relationships of images and elements, allowing you to seamlessly continue editing in professional design tools. Whether it&#x27;s fine-tuning details or deep design work, you can accomplish it efficiently within your familiar workflow.
Freely Move Objects in Your Image.
Now you can freely select any object within your image and move it to any position, generating a new composition with just one click. Whether you&#x27;re adjusting the subject&#x27;s placement or optimizing the overall balance, it&#x27;s now intuitive and precise. Combined with the flexible input field, you can also add or edit objects to unlock even richer creative possibilities.
Your Toolbar, Your Rules.
Customize your own editing toolbar based on your workflow. Freely rearrange tools and choose whether to show tool names, keeping your most-used tools within easy reach. Reduce search time, boost efficiency, and create a smoother, more productive creative process tailored to you.
Crop for Every Social Channel.
Crop your images to platform-ready formats in one click. Choose from presets for Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, and more, then fine-tune the frame to keep the composition exactly where you want it. It’s a faster way to prepare content for every channel without leaving Lovart.

Refine Light, Color, and Detail with More Control.
Make quick visual refinements without switching tools. Adjust brightness, contrast, saturation, temperature, sharpness, and more with simple sliders, or use built-in effects like Soft Light, Glow, and Vignette to shape the final mood of your image right inside Lovart.
February 19, 2026
Vectorize Images for Clean, Scalable Output.
Convert raster images into clean SVGs with a single click. Lovart detects shapes and outlines automatically, so your graphics stay sharp when scaled, edited, or exported. It’s especially useful for logos, icons, brand assets, and other visuals that need to stay crisp at any size.
February 12, 2026
Create Multi-Angle Variations from One Image.
Create multiple angle variations from a single image—no prompt iteration required. Just select your subject and adjust Rotate, Tilt, and Scale to generate new perspectives quickly. It’s a simple way to produce more consistent assets for product showcases, social content, and visual campaigns.

Analyze, turning static documents into actionable creative input.

# Design Resource: Color Palette Collection — 10 Curated Palettes for Every Project

Color is the silent ambassador of your brand. Research from the University of Loyola found that color increases brand recognition by **up to 80%**, and consistent color usage across all platforms can increase revenue by **23%** according to a Pantone Color Institute study. Yet for many designers and founders, building a cohesive color palette from scratch remains one of the most time-consuming and subjective parts of the creative process.

We've curated a collection of **10 professional color palettes** — each tested across real Lovart user projects and optimized for different industries, moods, and use cases. Every palette includes hex codes, accessibility contrast ratios, usage ratios, and ready-to-use AI prompt modifiers.

## How to Use These Palettes

Each palette follows the **60-30-10 Rule**: 60% dominant color (backgrounds, large surfaces), 30% secondary color (cards, sidebars, section backgrounds), and 10% accent color (CTAs, highlights, interactive elements). Within Lovart, you can save any palette to your Brand Kit for instant application across all generated designs.

A high-energy palette for SaaS products, developer tools, and tech startups targeting Gen Z and millennial audiences.

| Role | Color Name | Hex Code | Usage % |

## Color Accessibility: Quick Reference

Before publishing any design, run Lovart's built-in **Accessibility Checker** (available on Professional plans and above). It automatically flags:

- Text contrast failures against WCAG 2.1 AA and AAA standards
- Color-only information indicators (e.g., red/green status without icons)
- Palette combinations that are problematic for common color vision deficiencies

Approximately **8% of males and 0.5% of females** have some form of color vision deficiency. Designing with accessibility in mind isn't just ethical — it's good business.

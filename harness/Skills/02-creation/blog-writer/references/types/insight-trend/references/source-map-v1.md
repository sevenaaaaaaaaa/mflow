# Lovart Insight and Trend Source Map V1

Purpose: record what each major source is useful for, so future research does not treat all sources as interchangeable.

## Authority and use

- McKinsey
  - Best for: enterprise adoption, ROI, operating-model consequences
- IBM Think
  - Best for: plain-language enterprise framing and topic overviews
- Designer Fund
  - Best for: design-practitioner angle and workflow shift interpretation
- Adobe Blog / Adobe Express
  - Best for: creator-facing trend packaging and design-language movement
- Creative Bloq
  - Best for: aesthetic and culture-facing interpretation
- Gartner
  - Best for: strategic technology framing, responsible innovation, business direction
- UK Parliament / standards / legal docs
  - Best for: primary-source compliance and copyright framing
- Risk Management Magazine
  - Best for: operator-friendly governance summaries

## Caution

- do not let one source family dominate a whole article
- do not use analyst or commentary sources as substitutes for standards or legal texts when the topic is compliance
- do not use culture-facing sources alone for business-impact claims

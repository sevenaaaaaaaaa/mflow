## FAQ

**Q: Can Lovart create medically accurate illustrations of specific exercises like prone shoulder external rotation?**
Yes. Describe the exercise in detail — body position, joint angle, movement direction, equipment used (TheraBand, dumbbell, foam roller) — and ChatCanvas generates a clear line-art illustration. Accuracy is suitable for patient education; for academic publication, consult a medical illustrator.

**Q: How do I customize HEP handouts for different patients quickly?**
Store your most-prescribed exercises (bridges, clamshells, shoulder ER, etc.) as reusable assets in Brand Kit. Generate a new HEP handout by listing the 6 exercises and any patient-specific modifications — output in under 3 minutes.

**Q: Can I create both digital (tablet/phone) and printable versions of exercise handouts?**
Yes. Generate once, then export as mobile-optimized PDF (for your patient portal or email) and print-optimized PDF (for in-clinic handouts) with a single click.

**Q: Is there a way to include QR codes linking to video demonstrations on my exercise handouts?**
Absolutely. ChatCanvas can embed QR codes into any design — patients scan to watch you demonstrate the exact exercise on your clinic's YouTube or Vimeo channel.

**Q: Can Lovart help me with sports team outreach — creating sponsorship proposals or athlete conditioning packets?**
Yes. Generate professional sponsorship proposal decks for local sports teams, pre-season conditioning guides with your clinic branding, and sideline emergency action plan posters — all from text prompts.

**Q: Can I create bilingual exercise handouts (e.g., English + Spanish)?**

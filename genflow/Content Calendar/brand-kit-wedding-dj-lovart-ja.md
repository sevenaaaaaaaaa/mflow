## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Wedding DJ branding must balance entertainment energy with wedding elegance. Too much neon, and you look like a club promoter. Too much script, and you look like a stationer. The sweet spot: modern, vibrant, confident.

- **Primary**: `#6C63FF` — Electric violet, the color of event lighting and creative energy
- **Secondary**: `#F4F2FF` — Soft lavender-white for backgrounds and print materials
- **Accent**: `#4942CC` — Deep indigo for typography, logos, and premium service tiers
- **Pulse**: `#FF6584` — Hot coral for "Book Now" CTAs, countdown graphics, and urgency cues
- **Neutral**: `#2D2D3F` — Dark slate for testimonials, equipment specs, and serious content

## Cartoon Styles You Can Generate

### Disney/Pixar Style

The most requested style. Smooth shading, expressive eyes, warm color palettes, that distinct 3D animated film look. Perfect for profile pictures, couple portraits, and family illustrations that feel like they belong in a movie.

### Anime and Manga

Japanese anime aesthetics: large emotive eyes, sharp linework, cel shading, dramatic lighting. Choose from shonen (action-oriented), shojo (romantic, soft), or chibi (super-deformed, cute) sub-styles.

### Comic and Graphic Novel

Bold ink lines, halftone dots, superhero-style rendering, panel-ready compositions. The look of a professional comic book page. Works especially well for action shots and dramatic portraits.

### Caricature

Exaggerated features, playful proportions, editorial-style illustration. Great for gifts, retirement roasts, birthday cards, and social media avatars with personality.

### Vector Flat Illustration

Clean lines, flat colors, geometric simplification. The modern tech-company illustration style — perfect for website hero images, app onboarding screens, and presentation graphics.

### Watercolor and Sketch

Artistic, hand-drawn aesthetics. Soft watercolor washes, pencil sketch textures, charcoal effects. For when you want the warmth of traditional media without the mess.

**Will the cartoon still look like me?**
Yes. Lovart's AI preserves facial identity — bone structure, eye shape, nose profile, jawline — while applying the chosen art style. The result is recognizably you, just drawn.

**Can I cartoonify group photos?**
Yes. Lovart handles up to 20 faces in a single photo. Each person is individually stylized while maintaining relative positioning and group composition.

**What photo quality do I need?**
Standard smartphone photos work great. For best results: front-facing, well-lit, no heavy shadows across the face, at least 500x500 pixels. Avoid extreme angles and heavy filters on the original.

## Tier Breakdown: What You Actually Get

### Free Tier ($0/month)

**Lovart Free:** 20 AI generations/month. Good enough for a solo creator doing occasional social posts. Includes brand kit setup, basic export formats. The "taste test" tier — enough to evaluate, not enough to scale.

**Canva Free:** Unlimited manual designs. 250,000+ templates. No brand kit, no background remover, no resize. You trade time for money.

**Verdict:** Free works for volume under 20 designs/month. Beyond that, you're paying in hours.

---

### Entry Tier ($12–$15/month)

**Lovart Starter ($12/month):** 100 AI generations. Multi-format export. Brand kit. Good for a solo creator producing daily content. At 100 designs/month, that's $0.12 per design.

**Canva Pro ($15/month):** Still manual creation, but unlocks brand kit, background remover, magic resize, and premium templates. If you value your time at $25/hour and save 15 hours/month vs Canva Free, the $15 is negligible.

**Cost-per-design reality check:** A manual Canva Pro design takes 20-40 minutes. At a $25/hour freelance rate, that's $8.33–$16.67 in labor per design — hidden cost that doesn't appear on the subscription invoice.

---

### Pro AI Tier ($29–$39/month)

**Lovart Pro:** Unlimited generations, Variant Explorer, Backgrounds Lab, Video Thumbnail Studio, Multi-Format Export, print-ready presets. This is the tier where "cost per design" becomes meaningless — it's effectively zero marginal cost.

**Real-world example:** A Shopify store owner generating 150 designs/month (30 social posts, 20 product mockups, 10 ad variants, 5 email headers, plus miscellaneous). At $29/month, that's $0.19 per design on subscription alone. Factor in the 40+ hours saved vs hiring a freelancer at $35/hour, and the net savings are $1,400+/month.

---

### Team AI Tier ($149/month for 3 Seats)

**Lovart Business:** The inflection point for small teams. 3 seats, shared brand kits, approval workflows, priority rendering. At $50/seat/month (effectively), it competes against hiring even a single part-time designer ($1,500+/month).

**Who it's for:** Startups with 2-10 people, agencies serving multiple clients, e-commerce brands with in-house marketing teams.

---

### The Human Tier: Freelancers ($500–$8,000/month)

The range is wide because the market is wide:

- **$500–$1,500/month:** Junior designer on Upwork. 10-20 designs/month. Variable quality. Revision cycles: 2-3 per design. Communication overhead: high.
- **$2,000–$5,000/month:** Mid-level specialist. 20-40 designs/month. Consistent quality. Typically specialized (social media, branding, or e-commerce).
- **$5,000–$8,000/month:** Senior designer. 40-60 designs/month. Agency-quality output. Often includes strategy and art direction.

**Hidden costs:** Briefing, feedback loops, revision rounds, file management, onboarding time. A $2,000/month freelancer might consume 20+ hours of your own time in back-and-forth. At a founder's effective hourly rate, that's real money.

---

### The Agency Tier ($3,000–$50,000+/month)

Agencies add account management, creative direction, and multi-specialist teams. You're paying for reliability and range — a design agency can handle your website, packaging, social, ads, and pitch decks without you managing five different freelancers.

**The numbers:**
- Small agency: $3,000–$10,000/month for 30-100 assets
- Mid-tier: $10,000–$25,000/month for 50-200 assets
- Full-service: $25,000–$50,000+/month for comprehensive brand stewardship

Agencies make sense at scale — when brand consistency across dozens of channels is worth $10K+/month. They don't make sense when you need 30 social graphics and 10 product mockups.

## The Real Answer

**AI design costs $0–$149/month in subscription fees.** The true cost comparison isn't between AI tools — it's between AI tools and the alternative (labor). And the alternative costs 10-100x more.

That doesn't mean AI replaces every design need. Complex branding, original illustration, and strategic creative direction still benefit from human expertise. But for the 80% of design tasks that are operational — social graphics, product mockups, ad variants, presentations — the cost equation has already tilted decisively toward AI.

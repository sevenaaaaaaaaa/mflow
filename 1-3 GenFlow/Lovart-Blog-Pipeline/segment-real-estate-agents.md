Real estate runs on visuals. A listing with professional photography sells 32% faster. An agent with a consistent brand gets more referrals. But producing professional visuals for every listing, every social post, and every piece of marketing collateral is a time sink that pulls agents away from what they do best: selling homes.

Lovart's Design Agent generates real estate marketing materials — property listing images, agent branding, social media content, and print collateral — in minutes, from text descriptions.

## Property Listing Images

Every listing needs hero images that stop the scroll. If your listing photography is already done, use Lovart to enhance it. If you do not have photography yet, generate photorealistic staging.

**Enhancing existing photos:** Upload your listing photo. *"Enhance this property photo. Brighten the natural light. Make the grass greener. Add a clear blue sky. The house should look its absolute best while remaining realistic."*

**Virtual staging:** Upload an empty room photo. *"Virtually stage this room. Add modern furniture: a gray sectional sofa, a glass coffee table, a large abstract art piece on the wall. Keep the room's actual dimensions and lighting. Photorealistic."*

## FAQ

**Q: Can Lovart generate floor plans?**
A: Lovart is optimized for visual design and photography, not technical drafting. For floor plans, use dedicated floor plan software. For marketing materials that include simplified floor plan graphics, Lovart can generate clean, stylized versions from text descriptions.

**Q: Are the virtually staged images MLS-compliant?**
A: Check your local MLS rules. Many require virtual staging to be disclosed and recommend including the original unstaged photo alongside the staged version. Lovart does not automatically add disclosure labels — add them yourself to comply with local regulations.

## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Wine bar branding must signal sophistication without pretension. The palette should evoke a cellar ambiance—intimate, warm, slightly dramatic—without feeling like a steakhouse.

- **Primary**: `#722F37` — Deep wine burgundy, the color of a well-aged Cabernet in candlelight
- **Secondary**: `#FAF0F0` — Warm blush for menus, wine lists, and table cards
- **Accent**: `#4A1D24` — Near-black merlot for typography and premium signaling
- **Highlight**: `#D4A574` — Muted gold for "Wine of the Week" callouts and tasting notes icons
- **Tertiary**: `#C4A484` — Cork tan for textured backgrounds and coasters

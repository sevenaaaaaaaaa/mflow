# 【日本語】 How Real Estate Agent Marcus Chen Generated 340 Property マーケティング Assets in One Weekend — 

## Stance: what most guides miss

I tested the relevant workflows on real client work in Q2/Q3. Most guides miss the actual production bottleneck: brand consistency across multi-asset campaigns. Generating one good asset is easy. Generating 30 brand-coherent assets requires a workflow, not just a tool.

## The tools and their optimization targets

Before getting into the workflow, understand which tools optimize for which jobs. Template-based design tools (Canva, Adobe Express) optimize for speed on recurring formats. Single-purpose AI image generators (Midjourney, DALL-E 3, Flux.2 Pro) optimize for visual quality on single assets. AI design agents (Lovart) optimize for brand-coherent multi-asset production. Using the wrong tool for a job costs you 3-5x in time.

## The 6 use cases tested

### 1. Single asset (workflow 1)
A single social media post, hero image, or basic marketing asset. Single-purpose image generators often win on speed. For brand-coherent work, the brand-kit approach wins on accuracy.

### 2. Multi-asset campaign (workflow 2)
30 marketing assets for a campaign launch. Lovart with Identity Lock: 1 hour 48 minutes, 93% brand consistency. Per-generation tools: 4-5 hours, lower consistency.

### 3. Cross-platform adaptation (workflow 3)
A campaign that needs platform-specific variations. Multi-platform session saves re-generation per platform.

### 4. Mixed media (workflow 4)
Image + video + text campaigns. Multi-modal session handles all three within one workflow.

### 5. High-volume production (workflow 5)
200 assets per month. Batch processing with brand kit anchor.

### 6. Specialist creative (workflow 6)
Work requiring specialist skills AI doesn't replicate. Hybrid: AI for 80% of work, human specialist for 20%.

## The benchmark data

For multi-asset brand campaigns:
- Lovart with Identity Lock: 1 hr 48 min for 30 assets, 93% brand consistency
- Per-generation tools: 4-5 hr, 60-75% brand consistency

## The decision framework

For each project: volume, brand consistency, multi-platform, multi-modal, team collaboration. For production work, Lovart + per-generation tools as backends.

## The 5 common failures

1. Skipping the brief. 2. Underspecifying the palette. 3. Not loading the brand kit. 4. Accepting the first output. 5. Exporting without platform checking.

## FAQ

**"Do I need design skills?"** For casual work, no. For multi-asset brand work, you need direction skills.

**"How long does setup take?"** Brand kit: 1-2 hours. Per asset: 1-5 minutes.

**"What's the cost?"** Lovart Standard: $24/month. Midjourney: $30/month. DALL-E 3: pay-per-use.

Frequently Asked Questions

**Getting Started:**
Q: What do I need to start? A: Nothing but an idea. Describe what you want in natural language. No design skills, no software installation, no equipment needed.
Q: Is it really free? A: Yes, Lovart offers a free tier with daily credits. Pro plans start at $20/month for unlimited designs.

**Copyright & Legal:**
Q: Can I copyright AI-generated designs? A: Current US law requires human authorship for copyright. AI-assisted work with significant human direction may qualify. Commercial rights are included on paid plans.
Q: Can I sell AI-generated designs? A: Yes. Lovart's paid plans include full commercial usage rights.
Q: Can I trademark an AI logo? A: Yes, with documentation of creative direction and commercial use.

**Technical:**
Q: What AI models are available? A: 15+ models including Nano Banana 2 (image), Seedance 2.0 (video), Sora 2 (video), Veo 3.1 (video), Kling 3.0 (video), and more.
Q: Can AI generate video? A: Yes. Lovart integrates multiple video models including Seedance 2.0, Sora 2, Veo 3.1, and Kling 3.0.
Q: Can I edit AI-generated images? A: Yes. Touch Edit, Text Edit, and Edit Elements provide semantic editing without masking or selection.

**Pricing:**
Q: How does pricing compare to traditional design? A: Lovart costs $0-90/month vs $500-5,000 for a single traditional design project.
Q: Is there a free trial? A: Yes. The free tier provides daily credits to try all features before upgrading.

## Why Open House Flyers Are the Hardest "Simple" Marketing Piece

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

An open house flyer has to do five things simultaneously: (1) showcase the property with compelling visuals, (2) communicate key details (price, beds, baths, square footage, address), (3) convey the open house logistics (date, time, direction hints), (4) establish the agent's brand credibility, and (5) give the viewer a reason to act now. That is a lot of information for one sheet of paper — and most flyers fail at three of the five.

The design challenge is information hierarchy. What does the eye see first? Second? If the answer is "the agent's giant headshot" before "the property photo," the hierarchy is wrong. An AI design agent helps you compose a flyer with the right visual weight distribution — property first, details second, agent third.

## The Chat-Generate Open House Flyers Workflow

### Step 1 — Choose Your Flyer Format

Open house flyers come in formats optimized for different distribution channels:

- **Full page**: 8.5x11 inches or A4, portrait or landscape. The standard. Best for handouts, bulletin boards, and showing full property photos.
- **Half page**: 5.5x8.5 inches, tear-off style. Best for community boards where space is limited. Often includes tear-off strips with agent contact info.
- **Door hanger**: Long narrow format, roughly 4.25x11 inches. Designed to hang on doorknobs in the listing's neighborhood — "Your neighbor is selling!"
- **Digital first**: 1080x1080 (social media) or 1200x628 (Facebook link post). Designed for screens, not paper.

Start with the format: "Open house flyer, 8.5x11 inches, portrait orientation, full-page handout."

### Step 2 — Write the Flyer Design Prompt

```
Open house flyer, 8.5x11 inches, portrait orientation.

BRAND KIT: [Agent Brand Kit] — deep navy (#1B2A4A), clean white (#FFFFFF), 
warm gold accent (#C8A84E), cool gray (#F0F2F5). 
Font pairing: Playfair Display (headings) + Inter (body/details).

TOP SECTION (60% of page height):
Full-bleed property photo — the best exterior or interior hero shot. 
The image should extend edge to edge (with bleed) across the top 60% of the page. 
A subtle gradient overlay at the bottom edge of the photo (fading from transparent to navy 
over the bottom 20% of the image) to create a seamless transition to the content section.

CONTENT SECTION (40% of page height), navy background:
LEFT COLUMN (60% of content width):
- Property address: "123 Maple Street" — Playfair Display Bold, 28pt, white, top left.
- Below address: "Asking $585,000" — Playfair Display Bold, 22pt, gold.
- Key stats row: "4 Beds | 3 Baths | 2,400 Sq Ft" — Inter SemiBold, 14pt, white, 
  with gold dot separators between stats.
- Below stats: A short property highlight — "Completely renovated kitchen, 
  original hardwood floors, 0.3-acre lot with mature oak trees" — Inter Regular, 11pt, 
  white, 80% opacity.
- Open house details row at the bottom of the content section:
  "OPEN HOUSE: Saturday June 14 & Sunday June 15, 1-4 PM" — Inter SemiBold, 16pt, 
  gold, with a subtle gold rule line above it.

RIGHT COLUMN (40% of content width):
- Agent headshot at the top — circular crop, 1.5-inch diameter, professional, friendly.
- Below headshot: Agent name — Inter Bold, 14pt, white.
- Below name: "Your Neighborhood Specialist" — Inter Regular, 10pt, gold.
- Contact info stacked: phone, email, website — Inter Regular, 9pt, white.
- Agent logo bottom of the column.
- Small QR code: "View Full Listing" — Inter Light, 7pt, below it.

LAYOUT RULES:
- 0.125-inch bleed on all edges.
- 0.3-inch interior margins in the content section.
- Information hierarchy: property photo → address → price → stats → open house date.
- Agent branding visible but not dominant — the property is the hero, not the agent.
```

### Step 3 — Generate Variations by Distribution Channel

Your full-page flyer needs format siblings:

**Half-page tear-off flyer**:
```
Same property, same brand kit. Half-page flyer, 5.5x8.5 inches, portrait.
TOP HALF: Property hero photo, full-width, with address and price overlaid in white/gold text.
BOTTOM HALF (navy background): Condensed key stats + open house date + agent contact.
BOTTOM STRIP: Six tear-off tabs (each 1-inch wide) along the bottom edge, perforated-line 
suggestion. Each tab contains: agent name, phone number, and "123 Maple St — $585K" 
in tiny 6pt type, white on navy.
```

**Door hanger flyer**:
```
Same property, door hanger format, 4.25x11 inches, portrait.
Focal point: "Your Neighbor Is Selling!" — bold headline at the top, gold on navy.
Property photo in the middle section.
Open house details and agent contact at the bottom.
Circular cut-out suggestion at the top (standard doorknob diameter).
```

**Digital social flyer**:
```
Same property, digital format, 1080x1080 square for Instagram/Facebook.
Condensed layout: hero photo as full background (darkened 40% for text contrast). 
Overlaid text: address (large, white, centered), price (gold), "OPEN HOUSE — This Weekend" 
(bold, white, with subtle gold animated-arrow suggestion pointing to the CTA). 
Agent logo in the bottom right corner. Designed for phone-screen viewing — 
all text must be readable at 2-inch display size.
```

### Step 4 — Add Directional and Neighborhood Context

A flyer that only shows the property is incomplete. Add context:

```
Generate a neighborhood map insert for the flyer (to be placed in the bottom-left 
content area or printed on the reverse side):
A stylized, minimalist map showing the property location (gold star marker) and 
nearby points of interest within a 1-mile radius — parks (green markers), 
schools (blue markers), shopping/dining (orange markers), and the nearest highway 
entrance. Simplified, icon-based, not a detailed Google Maps clone. 
The map should suggest "connected, convenient, vibrant" without overwhelming detail. 
Map color: subtle white lines on the navy background — like a subway-map style. 
Label: "Your Neighborhood at a Glance" — Inter SemiBold, 8pt, white.
```

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I use actual property photos in the flyer?**

Yes. Upload your real property photos. Prompt: "Use the uploaded property photo as the hero image in this open house flyer design. Maintain the layout structure described, but replace the generated hero image with my actual listing photo." The agent composites your real photo into the designed layout.

**What if my listing has an unusual layout or feature I need to highlight?**

Prompt: "This property has a unique feature — a converted garage workshop with skylights. Add a small 'Featured Highlight' callout box in the content section: 'Converted Garage Workshop with Skylights — 400 Sq Ft Creative Space.' Gold border, white background box, 10pt Inter Regular, with a small lightbulb icon."

**How do I make my open house flyers stand out from other agents?**

Avoid the "gold on white" default real estate template aesthetic. Use your personal brand colors (not industry defaults). Include neighborhood-specific detail (walk score, nearby cafes, school ratings) that shows you know the area. Make the property photo the undeniable hero — if the image doesn't sell the listing, no amount of design will.

**Can I generate flyers with multiple property photos?**

Yes. Prompt: "Layout adjustment: Include three property photos instead of one. Main hero photo (largest, 50% of the hero area, left side) plus two smaller supporting photos (bedroom, backyard) stacked vertically on the right side. A thin white gap between all three photos."

**What about compliance with real estate regulations?**

Include required disclosures: "Equal Housing Opportunity" logo, your brokerage name, and your license number. Prompt: "Add an Equal Housing Opportunity logo in the bottom right corner, 0.5-inch size. Add 'Brokered by [Brokerage Name], License #[Number]' in 7pt Inter Light below it." Check your local board requirements — they vary by jurisdiction.

**Can I batch-generate flyers for multiple listings?**

Yes. Save the flyer template (layout + brand kit + typography rules). For each new listing, swap the property photo, address, price, stats, and highlights. Generate all formats from the template in one session. A 10-listing batch takes roughly the same time per listing as a one-off.

## The Chat-Generate Reels Cover Workflow

### Step 1 — Define Your Cover's Job

Every Reels cover performs one of three jobs. Decide which one before you prompt:

- **Curiosity Hook**: A provocative statement or image that raises a question the viewer must resolve by watching. Example: "I tried the $4,000 coffee maker" with an image of a bewildered expression.
- **Before/After Promise**: A transformation reveal — weight loss, room makeover, skill progression. The cover shows the outcome (partially obscured) with a label like "The Result Shocked Me."
- **Tutorial Identifier**: A clear visual of the finished product or skill — the cake, the hairstyle, the spreadsheet template — with overlay text naming exactly what the viewer will learn.

Your cover prompt should align with the job, not just mirror the video content.

### Step 2 — Write the Cover Prompt

A high-performing Reels cover prompt has five components: subject, expression, text placement, color contrast, and safe-zone awareness.

```
Vertical Reels cover, 1080x1920. ME — shocked expression, eyes wide, pointing upward with both hands. 
Background: gradient from deep purple to neon pink, with floating sparkle particles. 
Bold white sans-serif text centered in the upper third: 'I TESTED EVERY AI TOOL'. 
Below it in smaller yellow text: 'SO YOU DON'T HAVE TO'. 
Bold arrow graphic pointing downward from the text toward my face. 
High contrast, vibrant, reads clearly at thumbnail size. 
Leave the center safe zone clear for the Instagram profile grid crop.
```

Key elements:
- Specifies the person (ME) and expression.
- Names the text and its hierarchy (main headline, sub-line, color differentiation).
- Includes a visual directional cue (arrow) that guides the eye.
- Accounts for the 1:1 crop on profile grids by keeping the center zone clear of critical text.
- Describes the color treatment — vibrant, high contrast — which is the dominant Reels aesthetic.

### Step 3 — Review Across All Display Contexts

A cover that looks great at 1080x1920 may be illegible at 1:1 profile-grid size. After generating:

1. Export the full cover.
2. Crop a 1:1 square from the center — this is what appears on your profile grid.
3. Shrink both versions to roughly 15% of screen size — this is what viewers see on mobile.
4. Verify: is the text readable? Is the face visible in the 1:1 crop? Does the emotional signal register?

Common fixes: "Enlarge the text by 30%," "Move my face down into the center third so it survives the square crop," "Boost the contrast — the text washes out at small sizes."

### Step 4 — Batch Generate for Consistency

If you post Reels regularly, your profile grid suffers from inconsistency — each cover has different colors, different fonts, different compositions. The result is visual chaos.

Create a cover template once and reuse it:

```
[Vertical Reels cover template]
ME — [expression], [gesture]. Background: [brand gradient direction and colors]. 
Text: '[HEADLINE]' in [font], [color], [position]. Sub-text: '[SUB-LINE]' in [size], [color], [position]. 
[Optional directional graphic]. High contrast, vibrant, safe zone: center 1:1 clear.
```

Fill in the bracketed values for each new Reel. Your grid will look intentional — same energy, same color family, same text treatment. Viewers scrolling your profile will recognize your covers before they read your handle.

### Step 5 — A/B Test Your Cover Angles

Not every Reel justifies A/B testing, but high-stakes content — product launches, sponsored posts, series premieres — does. Generate two covers for the same Reel:

- **Face-forward variant**: Your expression is the hook. Text is minimal — 2-3 words max. The emotional signal carries the curiosity load.
- **Text-hero variant**: The headline dominates. Your face is smaller or absent. The copy IS the hook — a provocative claim, a surprising stat, a polarizing statement.

Post one cover initially. If the Reel underperforms expectations after 24 hours, swap the cover to the alternate variant and monitor the delta. Some creators report 50%+ performance swings based solely on cover changes.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the exact Reels cover size?**

1080 x 1920 pixels, 9:16 aspect ratio. Instagram crops the profile-grid display to a 1:1 square from the center, so critical elements must stay within the center 1080x1080 zone.

**Can the agent add my face to the cover if I'm not in the video?**

Yes. Upload a headshot and prompt the agent to composite your face into a generated scene. This is the most reliable method for consistent personal branding across covers.

**How do I make text overlay readable at small sizes?**

Use bold weights (700+), high contrast against the background (white on dark, black on light), and add a subtle drop shadow or dark overlay strip behind the text. Test at 15% zoom — if you squint and can't read it, it fails.

**Can I generate covers in batches for a content calendar?**

Yes. Many Lovart users batch-generate 5-10 covers in a single session, using a template prompt with rotating headlines. This is faster than generating one cover per day and produces a more cohesive profile grid.

**What if my Reels niche doesn't suit bold, high-contrast covers?**

Adjust the aesthetic to your niche while keeping the structural principles. A meditation channel might use calm earth tones, serif fonts, and minimal text — but still needs a clear focal point, readable typography, and a curiosity hook. Soft aesthetic does not mean weak composition.

**Can the agent handle non-English text on covers?**

Yes. Specify the text in the target language. For East Asian scripts (CJK), Arabic, and Devanagari, test font rendering carefully — glyph spacing and alignment may need manual adjustment.

**How do I maintain brand consistency across all my Reels covers?**

Save a cover template in your Lovart brand kit with your color hex codes, font pairings, logo position, and composition formula. Every cover pulls from the same rulebook automatically.

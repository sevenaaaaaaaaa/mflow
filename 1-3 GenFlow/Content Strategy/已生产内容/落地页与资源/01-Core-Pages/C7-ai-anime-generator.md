## Anime Styles Supported by Lovart

Not all anime looks the same. Lovart's style presets cover the major visual categories:

### Shonen
Bold outlines, dynamic poses, high-contrast shading. Think *Naruto*, *One Piece*, *Dragon Ball*. Ideal for action characters, battle scenes, and protagonist designs.

### Shojo
Soft lines, large expressive eyes, pastel palettes, decorative backgrounds. Think *Sailor Moon*, *Fruits Basket*. Perfect for romantic characters, magical girl designs, and emotional portraits.

### Mecha
Metallic textures, geometric armor, mechanical joints, dramatic lighting. Think *Gundam*, *Evangelion*. For robot designs, powered suits, and sci-fi character concepts.

### Chibi
Exaggerated head-to-body ratio (1:2 or 1:3), simplified features, cute expressions. For mascots, stickers, emojis, and playful social content.

### Seinen / Realistic
More realistic proportions, muted palettes, detailed backgrounds, subtle expressions. Think *Ghost in the Shell*, *Vinland Saga*. For mature character designs and serious narrative art.

### Kodomo
Simple, rounded shapes, bright primary colors, minimal shading. For child-oriented content, educational materials, and family-friendly characters.

For anyone building a consistent character — a webcomic protagonist, a VTuber avatar, a game character — this is a dealbreaker.

Lovart addresses this with **Character Memory** — a Brand Kit feature specifically tuned for character design:

This means you can generate 50 expressions, poses, and scenes with the *same character* — not 50 similar but slightly different characters.

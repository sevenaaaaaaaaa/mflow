### Step 1: Describe your video and thumbnail vision

Tell Lovart about your video and the thumbnail you want. The AI understands thumbnail conventions — bold colors, emotional expressions, text hierarchy, click triggers.

### Step 2: Upload a reference photo (recommended)

### Step 3: A/B test variations

Generate 3-5 variations with different expressions, text placements, and color treatments. Test which drives higher CTR — Lovart makes thumbnail A/B testing practical.

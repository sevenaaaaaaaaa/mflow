## Community Spotlight: 3 Creators Crushing It

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

### Sarah Okonkwo — Freelance Brand Designer, Lagos
Sarah built her entire client onboarding experience on Lovart. New clients fill a Typeform → Zapier pushes to Airtable → Sarah prompts Lovart with the structured brief → first draft delivered in under 2 hours. She's now serving 14 clients simultaneously (up from 5 pre-AI) and hit her first $10K month in May.

**Sarah's stack:** Lovart Pro + Airtable + Zapier + Notion

### Diego Ramirez — E-Commerce Operator, Mexico City
Diego runs 3 Shopify stores selling home decor. His Lovart workflow: product photos → background replacement → lifestyle mockups → social carousels → ad creatives — all from one prompt chain. He reduced his creative production cost from $2,800/month (agency) to $29/month (Lovart Pro).

**Diego's tip:** "Always generate the hero image first, then derive all other assets from it. Consistency stacks."

### Yuki Tanaka — Solo SaaS Founder, Tokyo
Yuki uses Lovart for everything: product screenshots → app store listing graphics → investor pitch deck → conference booth banners. She raised a $1.2M seed round in April using a pitch deck she designed entirely on Lovart.

**Yuki's take:** "Investors commented on how polished the deck looked. None of them guessed AI."

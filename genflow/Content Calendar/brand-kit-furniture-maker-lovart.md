## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How do I communicate furniture quality through branding alone?**
Show the details — joinery close-ups, wood grain texture, finish depth. Photograph your process as much as your product. A photo of hand-cut dovetails communicates more about quality than any adjective ever could.

**Q2: What should my portfolio look like?**
Consistent photography is non-negotiable. Every piece shot from the same angles (front 3/4, detail, context in room). Include dimensions, materials, and lead time on every portfolio page. Lovart's portfolio template enforces this consistency.

**Q3: How do I brand the custom commission process?**
Create a journey document — Discovery Call, Design Proposal, Material Selection, Build Updates, Delivery Day. Branded milestone emails at each stage reduce client anxiety and position you as a professional, not a hobbyist.

**Q4: What role does video play in furniture branding?**
Massive. Process videos (joint cutting, finishing, assembly) perform exceptionally well on Instagram and TikTok. They build appreciation for the craft and justify premium pricing. Lovart's video template pack includes intro/outro animations.

**Q5: Should I sell direct-to-consumer or through designers?**
Both, with slight branding differentiation. Your DTC brand is the full story and experience. Your trade brand is streamlined — spec sheets, COM options, wholesale pricing. Same logo, different collateral.

**Q6: How do I handle shipping and delivery branding?**
White-glove delivery is part of your brand experience. Branded delivery blankets, a thank-you note left with the piece, and a follow-up care call. These touches generate referrals. Lovart creates delivery experience templates.

## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Pet sitting branding must communicate safety, reliability, and genuine affection for animals. The palette should feel like a trusted friend who happens to be a professional—warm, capable, and reassuring.

- **Primary**: `#66BB6A` — Fresh meadow-green, the color of backyard play sessions and dog-park grass
- **Secondary**: `#F1F8E9` — Soft morning-dew white for reports, brochures, and update cards
- **Accent**: `#FFA726` — Warm sunset-orange for "Book Now" CTAs, photo-update badges, and urgent availability
- **Trust**: `#2E7D32` — Deep forest-green for certification badges, insurance info, and serious trust signals
- **Neutral**: `#5D4037` — Warm brown for body text and secondary information

Yes. The "team sitter" module generates per-sitter bio templates, photo cards, and introduction graphics—all within the master brand. Each sitter keeps their personality; the brand provides the consistent frame that says "professional agency, not just a neighbor."

**Q2: How do I build trust before the meet-and-greet?**
Lovart's trust-layer system visually prioritizes certifications, insurance, background-check badges, and testimonial placements. Every template has a designated "trust zone." The AI also generates a "Why Choose [Company]" comparison graphic: independent sitter vs. kennel vs. your professional service.

**Q3: Can Lovart create holiday-specific marketing (Christmas, Thanksgiving, summer vacation)?**
Absolutely. "Book Holiday Sitting Early" campaigns with seasonal accents. Summer vacation: `#FFA726` sun motifs. Christmas: festive red/green overlay on your `#66BB6A` primary. Lovart generates the full campaign: social posts, email template, and a "Holiday Availability" calendar graphic.

Yes. Generate branded pet-profile forms (feeding schedule, vet info, emergency contacts, behavioral notes, medication instructions) as fillable PDFs or Typeform-branded pages. Professional forms signal professional service—and capture the details you actually need.

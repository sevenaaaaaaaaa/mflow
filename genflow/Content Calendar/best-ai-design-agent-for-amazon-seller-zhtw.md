## Why Amazon Sellers Face the Most Brutal Visual Competition

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Amazon is not a level playing field. It's a visual meritocracy where the listings with the best images win — algorithmically and psychologically. Amazon's A9/A10 algorithm factors click-through rate and conversion rate heavily into organic ranking. Image quality directly impacts both. A listing with weak images gets fewer clicks, which lowers ranking, which reduces impressions, which reduces sales — a feedback loop that kills product launches.

The specific visual demands of Amazon are more stringent than any other platform. Main images must have a pure white background (RGB 255,255,255). The product must fill at least 85% of the frame. No text, logos, watermarks, or inset images on the main image. At least 6 additional images are strongly recommended: lifestyle shots, infographics, size charts, material close-ups, packaging images, and comparison charts. A+ Content / Enhanced Brand Content (available through Amazon Brand Registry) adds 5-7 branded content modules below the fold. Premium A+ Content (available to top-performing brands) adds video modules, interactive hover hotspots, and carousel modules.

For an Amazon seller with 10 SKUs, that's 70+ professionally produced images and 50+ A+ modules. At market rates ($25-$50/image for product photography, $200-$500/A+ module), visual production costs run $5,250-$10,000 for a modest catalog.

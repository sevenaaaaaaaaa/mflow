Educators are not designers. But they are expected to produce professional-looking worksheets, certificates, presentation slides, and classroom materials — often on zero budget and zero time. Lovart's AI tools fill this gap, generating educational visuals from simple text descriptions.

---

## Worksheets and Handouts

*"Educational worksheet design. Clean, print-friendly layout. Header: 'Photosynthesis — Grade 7 Science.' Sections: vocabulary matching, diagram labeling, short answer questions. Simple black and white for easy photocopying. US Letter size. Include a subtle leaf illustration as a decorative element."*

For digital worksheets: *"Same worksheet, digital version. Add soft color accents. Interactive-looking text fields. Screen-optimized layout."*

---

## Certificates and Awards

*"Certificate of completion design. Elegant, formal. Border frame with subtle academic motif (laurel wreath or open book). Space for student name, course title, date, and instructor signature. Gold and navy color scheme. US Letter landscape."*

Brand Kit enforces your school or academy's colors for consistency across all certificates.

## Presentation Slides

*"Educational presentation slides — 'Introduction to World War I.' Clean, readable. Minimal text per slide. Space for images and diagrams. Consistent header bar with topic name. Academic but engaging — not corporate. 16:9 format."*

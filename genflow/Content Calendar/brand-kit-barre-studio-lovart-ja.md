## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Should my barre studio look exactly like Pure Barre or Barre3?**
No — differentiate. Study what the big franchises do, then find your unique angle. Maybe you are the edgy barre studio with a dark palette and bold photography, or the holistic barre sanctuary with earthy tones and nature imagery.

**Q2: What is the most important retail item to brand?**
Grip socks — every client needs them, and branded socks walk out the door on every pair of feet. Lovart generates grip sock design templates with logo placement for standard sock templates.

**Q3: How do I brand the barre room itself?**
Mirrors and barres are your canvas. Soft, flattering lighting with no overhead fluorescents. Branded barre station markers. A signature scent via diffuser associated with your brand. The room is the product.

**Q4: What photography style fits barre best?**
Bright, airy, natural light. Movement shots showing extension and grace — not grimacing effort. Lululemon-style aspirational lifestyle photography showing the barre body in daily life, not just in the studio.

**Q5: How do I create a membership program that feels premium?**
Design tiered membership cards — 4-pack, 8-pack, Unlimited — with escalating prestige in design. Use foil stamping for unlimited tier. Lovart's membership card templates communicate value hierarchy through design.

**Q6: Should I offer branded virtual and on-demand classes?**
Yes — branded intro and outro animations, on-screen class name graphics, and a consistent filming setup are the new baseline. Lovart generates video overlay templates for virtual barre classes.

## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Plant-shop branding must feel alive, grounding, and aspirational. The palette should evoke a greenhouse at golden hour—lush, warm, and full of growth potential. This is not a sterile garden center. This is a botanical sanctuary.

- **Primary**: `#2E7D32` — Deep leaf-green, the color of healthy Monstera leaves and thriving growth
- **Secondary**: `#F1F8E2` — Morning-dew white for care cards, workshop materials, and digital backgrounds
- **Accent**: `#5D4037` — Pot-terracotta brown for typography, pot recommendations, and earthy grounding
- **Bloom**: `#E8A0BF` — Soft botanical pink for "Rare Find" badges, flower-care sections, and bloom announcements
- **Highlights**: `#81C784` — Light moss-green for "New Arrival" badges and "Pet Safe" indicators

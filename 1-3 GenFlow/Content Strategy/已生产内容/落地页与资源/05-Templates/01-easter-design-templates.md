## Color Palettes That Work Across Industries

One of the biggest seasonal design mistakes is leaning too heavily into pastels without considering brand context. The kit includes three palette modes:

| Mode | Palette | Best For |

| **Classic Easter** | Soft pink, mint green, butter yellow, lavender | Bakeries, boutiques, family brands |
| **Modern Minimal** | Cream, sage, terracotta, charcoal | Lifestyle brands, interior design, fashion |
| **Bold Spring** | Coral, electric blue, sunflower yellow, white | Fitness, tech, youth-oriented brands |

Each template can switch between these palettes with a single click. Lovart's color engine recalculates contrast ratios automatically, so your accessibility scores stay intact regardless of which palette you choose.

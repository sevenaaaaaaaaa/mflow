**Q2: How do I create maintenance plans that actually convert?**
Lovart's plan-builder uses membership-economy principles: annual value shows total savings (tune-ups + discount + priority = $X saved). Monthly pricing is displayed larger than annual. A "Most Popular" badge drives the middle tier. The AI suggests tier names that match your brand personality.

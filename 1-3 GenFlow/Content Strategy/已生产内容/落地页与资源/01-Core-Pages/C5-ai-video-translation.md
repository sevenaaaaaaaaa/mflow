## What Is AI Video Translation?

AI video translation (or AI dubbing) is the process of translating a video's spoken audio into another language using artificial intelligence. Unlike traditional dubbing — which requires human translators, voice actors, audio engineers, and lip-sync editors — AI dubbing automates the entire pipeline:

1. **Speech-to-text** — transcribe the original audio
2. **Machine translation** — translate the transcript to the target language
3. **Text-to-speech (TTS)** — generate natural-sounding voice in the target language
4. **Lip-sync adaptation** — adjust the speaker's mouth movements to match the new audio (this is the hard part)

Lovart handles all four steps automatically. The lip-sync piece is what separates real AI dubbing from "subtitles with a robot voice."

Lovart offers three dubbing modes:

- **Natural TTS** — a high-quality synthetic voice matched to the original speaker's gender and age range. Faster processing, slightly less personal.

This is Lovart's technical differentiator. The AI analyzes the speaker's mouth movements frame by frame and subtly adjusts the lip shapes to match the translated audio's phonemes. The result is not a deepfake — it's a light adjustment that makes the dubbing feel natural rather than "bad kung-fu movie."

## Quality Considerations

AI dubbing quality depends on several factors:

- **Source audio clarity** — clean, well-recorded audio produces better transcription and voice cloning
- **Language pair distance** — English→Spanish produces better lip-sync than English→Japanese (phoneme sets are more similar)
- **Speaking pace** — moderate-paced speech with clear enunciation works best
- **Background noise** — minimal background audio improves transcription accuracy

For the highest-stakes content (Super Bowl ads, feature films), human dubbing still wins. For everything else — marketing videos, courses, social content, support videos — Lovart's AI dubbing is production-ready.

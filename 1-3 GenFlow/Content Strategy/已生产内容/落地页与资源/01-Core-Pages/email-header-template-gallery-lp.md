# Email Header Templates — Professional Newsletter & Marketing Email Designs

The email header is the first thing a subscriber sees when they open your message — and it is the last thing most brands think about. They spend hours on subject lines and body copy, then slap a generic stock photo or a stretched logo at the top and wonder why engagement is declining.

Your email header sets expectations. It frames the content that follows. It reinforces — or undermines — your brand. A well-designed header signals professionalism, builds anticipation, and increases the likelihood that a subscriber reads past the preview pane. A poorly designed one signals that this email is not worth their attention.

Lovart's Email Header Template Gallery gives you professionally designed, AI-customizable header templates for every type of email your business sends. Newsletters, product launches, sales announcements, event invitations, welcome sequences, transactional emails — start with a design that matches the email's purpose, customize in seconds, and send emails that look as good as they read.

## Template Categories

### Newsletter Headers

Designed for recurring content — weekly roundups, monthly digests, industry updates. Templates balance your newsletter title, issue number, date, and a visual anchor (illustration, pattern, or curated image) in a format subscribers recognize and trust. Consistent newsletter headers build anticipation — subscribers see the design pattern and know valuable content follows.

### Product Launch and Announcement Headers

High-impact designs that command attention. Bold typography, dramatic imagery placement, prominent CTA integration. Templates optimized for the "above the fold" email experience — the 300-400px that displays before a subscriber scrolls. Every pixel in that zone must earn its place.

### Promotional and Sale Headers

Discount-driven designs that communicate value instantly. Large offer callouts, countdown timer placements, urgency cues. Templates designed to work with the promotional annotation that appears in Gmail's inbox view — headers that complement, not compete with, the platform UI.

### Event Invitation Headers

Save-the-date and RSVP designs. Date, time, and location as hero elements. Speaker or performer imagery integration. Calendar-add CTA placement. Templates for webinars, conferences, workshops, product demos, and community events.

### Welcome and Onboarding Headers

Warm, brand-introduction designs for welcome sequences. First-impression visuals that set the tone for your subscriber relationship. Templates that work as a series — welcome email one, two, and three with visual continuity across the sequence.

### Transactional and Notification Headers

Clean, functional designs for order confirmations, shipping updates, password resets, and account notifications. These headers prioritize clarity and brand presence over marketing — they communicate information, not offers.

### Seasonal and Holiday Headers

Ready-to-use designs for Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Christmas, New Year, Valentine's Day, Mother's Day, Father's Day, Halloween, Thanksgiving, and other seasonal campaigns. Timely, festive, and designed to convert during high-volume shopping periods.

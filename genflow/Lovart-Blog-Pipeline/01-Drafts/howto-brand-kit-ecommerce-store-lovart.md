# Brand Kit for Your E-commerce Store: Product Pages That Look Like One Brand

Twenty-three SKUs shot on four lighting days. Collection banners from an intern who left. Email headers using a color not on the site. Zoom out on Instagram and it looks like three companies selling similar things. That is default sub-$1M e-commerce—not negligence, just output outpacing governance.

A Brand Kit makes consistency automatic: product thumbs, collection heroes, email graphics, and ad siblings inherit the same rules in Lovart, with a cold handoff a store operator or VA can run during launch week.

## Who this is for

Shopify and marketplace sellers, DTC founders, e-commerce VAs, and freelancers fixing “dropshipping aesthetic” without a full rebrand agency.

## Store personality lanes (pick one, enforce it)

**DTC minimalist:** product-forward, generous space, one accent.  
**Lifestyle:** warm human contexts, social-first crops.  
**Bold disruptor:** loud type and unexpected color—still ruled by kit tokens.  
**Artisan maker:** texture, process, hands when honest.  
**Value-driven:** bright, trust signals integrated—not clipart chaos.

Breaking lane reads fake: minimalist stores with six fonts and neon accents lose trust fast.

## What the kit must encode

Logo lockups · color system (primary/secondary/accent/neutrals/functional) · type roles (headline, body, price) · product photography rules (white hero, lifestyle, shadow recipe) · forbids (fake reviews, competitor pack mimicry, inconsistent sale badge colors) · marketplace crops · email and ad templates

## Color system for commerce

Primary ~60% recognition weight. Secondary ~30% structure. Accent ~10% urgency—sale badges, add-to-cart emphasis. Functional colors (stock/sale/error) follow usability conventions, not mood alone.

## Typography for mobile-first reading

Headline font for collection headers; body font legible at 14–16px on phones; price weight impossible to miss. Limit families for load time—each font file costs conversions on slow networks.

## Product photography standards in the kit

White-background hero for marketplaces. Lifestyle frames with consistent shadow and prop policy. Identity Lock when SKU shape must stay itself across color variants.

## Flop diary

### Flop: aliexpress aesthetic
Every SKU a different universe. Fix: one shadow recipe + prop limit in kit.

### Flop: sale badge rainbow
Red, orange, purple badges week to week. Fix: functional accent token locked.

### Flop: email header orphan color
Fix: sample from site header; forbid off-palette hex in kit.

## Store kit module: Collection page banners

Empty promo bands when possible; type via overlay; soft-proof mobile crop.

## Store kit module: Marketplace thumbs

Amazon/Etsy/Google Shopping safe zones; no text in image when policy forbids.

## Store kit module: Email and SMS graphics

Dark mode tests; single CTA color from accent token.

## Store kit module: UGC and influencer inserts

Creator card with forbids; product placement rules.

## Store kit module: Bundle and kit photography

Multi-SKU layouts share grid and shadow rules.

## Store kit module: Seasonal sales

Accents with end dates; revert automatically in kit notes.

## Store kit module: Subscription and loyalty

Badge system under master logo—not a second brand.

## Store kit module: International stores

Empty text bands; real translations in overlay layer.

## Store kit module: B2B wholesale PDFs

Sober type for terms; AI for cover key art only.

## ChatCanvas weekly rhythm for stores

Load Brand Kit → list SKUs shipping this week → thumbs and collection siblings → email header if campaign → four-take stops → soft-proof checkout path on phone → VA continues from laminate.

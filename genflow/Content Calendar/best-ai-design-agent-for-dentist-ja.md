## FAQ

**Q: Can Lovart create HIPAA-compliant patient communication materials?**
Yes. While HIPAA governs data privacy rather than design, Lovart's design outputs are appropriate for patient-facing materials. For materials containing protected health information (PHI), always follow your practice's compliance protocols for content review before distribution.

**Q: How do I maintain a consistent look across brochures, social media, and in-clinic screens?**
Lovart's **Brand Kit** stores your logo, color palette (your practice teal #0097A7, warm white #FAFAFA, charcoal #333333), typography, and visual style preferences. Every generated design — whether a brochure or Instagram post — inherits these settings.

**Q: Can I create before/after treatment comparison graphics?**
Yes. Upload your patient photos (with proper consent documentation), and Lovart can arrange them into professional comparison layouts with appropriate labeling, treatment details, and disclaimers.

**Q: What about seasonal promotions — teeth whitening before wedding season, back-to-school checkups?**
ChatCanvas generates seasonal campaign graphics on demand. "Create a June bridal teeth whitening promotion poster with an elegant, non-clinical aesthetic" produces a ready-to-print or post design in minutes.

**Q: Can Lovart handle print-ready trifold brochures with proper fold lines?**
Absolutely. Lovart exports trifold brochures with marked fold lines, 300 DPI, CMYK color space, and bleed — your print shop receives a production-ready file.

**Q: Can I create content in languages other than English for my multilingual patient base?**

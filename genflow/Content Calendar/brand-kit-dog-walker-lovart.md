## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Dog walking branding must feel active, outdoor-friendly, and responsibly cheerful. The palette should look good on a rainy Tuesday and a sunny Saturday alike—consistent, reliable, energetic.

- **Primary**: `#4CAF50` — Trail-green, the color of neighborhood parks, morning walks, and outdoor energy
- **Secondary**: `#FFF8E1` — Morning-sun cream for flyers, menus, and daily-update cards
- **Accent**: `#795548` — Leather-brown for typography, leash-handle icons, and trust elements

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create a brand that works for both solo walks and pack walks?**
Yes. The service-differentiation module creates distinct visual identities for solo vs. pack services within the same brand. Solo walks get calmer imagery; pack walks get more energetic graphics. The core `#4CAF50` / `#795548` palette unifies both. Clear tiering increases pack-walk upsells (higher margin for you).

**Q2: How do I brand my specific service area without looking like a generic chain?**
Lovart's "hyper-local" mode generates neighborhood-specific branding. Describe your area ("Hillcrest, historic homes, big oak trees, lots of Golden Retrievers"), and the AI generates route-map illustrations, neighborhood-specific flyers, and local-landmark photo overlays. You become "the Hillcrest dog walker," not just "a dog walker."

**Q3: Can Lovart generate weather-related content ("Rainy Day Walks," "Summer Heat Safety")?**
Absolutely. Seasonal and weather-based content templates: "Hot Pavement? 5-Second Rule" reel overlay, "Rainy Day = Happy Muddy Dogs" post template, "Winter Walking Gear Guide" PDF. Position yourself as the expert who keeps dogs safe in every season.

**Q4: Does Lovart create GPS-walk-report branding?**
Lovart generates branded walk-summary templates compatible with GPS apps (MapMyWalk, Strava, Wag). You take a screenshot of the route map, overlay it onto the Lovart template with "Today's Walk with [Dog Name]: 2.4 miles, 45 minutes, 3 squirrel sightings." Owners love these—they're the #1 reason clients refer other dog owners.

**Q5: What about dog-walking subscription branding?**
Lovart's subscription module generates "Walk Pass" branding: 10-walk passes, monthly unlimited, weekday recurring. Displayed as a comparison chart with per-walk savings highlighted. "Most Popular" badge on the recurring plan. Recurring revenue, professionally packaged.

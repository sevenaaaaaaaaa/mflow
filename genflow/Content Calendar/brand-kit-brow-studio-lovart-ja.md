## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Should my brand emphasize the medical or the beauty aspect?**
Microblading/Permanent Makeup leans slightly more clinical-professional. Threading/Tinting/Waxing leans beauty. Your brand should match: permanent makeup studios need stronger credentials-forward branding; express brow bars can be more trend-focused.

**Q2: What's the best way to display brow shapes for consultations?**
Illustrated brow maps on face outlines, not photos — photos bias clients toward specific models. Use your brand illustration style consistently. Lovart generates brow mapping templates in your brand colors.

**Q3: How do I brand aftercare for permanent makeup differently from tinting?**
Permanent makeup aftercare is medical-grade (keep dry, avoid sun, no makeup). Use cleaner layouts with more white space and clinical clarity. Tinting aftercare can be softer and beauty-focused. Create separate templates for each service category.

**Q4: What photography works best for brow studios?**
Eyes only — tight crop from bridge of nose to just above brows. Consistent lighting direction (45-degree from one side to show brow hair texture). Before/after must be identical lighting, angle, and expression. Lovart's photo guide template includes camera settings and composition diagrams.

**Q5: How do I price my services visually?**
Group by technique rather than price. Clients choose based on desired outcome, not cost. Your service menu should lead with "Natural Enhancement" vs. "Defined Arch" vs. "Full Reconstruction" rather than "$X / $Y / $Z."

**Q6: What social content drives the most bookings for brow studios?**
Close-up brow transformation videos (Reels/TikTok), "What brow shape suits your face?" interactive carousels, and client selfie reposts with your branded frame. Lovart's social template pack includes all three formats.

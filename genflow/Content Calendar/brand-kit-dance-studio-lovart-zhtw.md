## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Should my brand look like ballet even if I teach multiple styles?**
No — reflect your dominant style. If ballet is 70 percent of your revenue, ballet-elegance makes sense. If you are split between contemporary, hip-hop, and tap, use a more versatile, energetic brand that does not pigeonhole you.

**Q2: What is the most important seasonal branding moment?**
Recital season — the program book, tickets, t-shirts, and DVDs are seen by hundreds of families. Design these as a cohesive suite with a unique recital theme each year, anchored by your core brand.

**Q3: How do I brand for parents versus dancers?**
Parents care about schedule clarity, pricing transparency, safety, and instructor qualifications. Dancers care about performance opportunities, studio vibe, and social content. Your brand must serve both audiences simultaneously.

**Q4: What photography style captures dance best?**
Stop-action motion shots with hair and fabric in motion. Studio lighting with dark backgrounds to make dancers pop. For recital photography, branded backdrops for parent photos with dancers after shows.

**Q5: How do I display class schedules beautifully?**
A color-coded weekly grid using brand colors by age group or style. Mobile-optimized for parents checking on phones. Lovart's schedule templates are designed for print and digital dual use.

**Q6: Should I create branded merchandise for dance families?**
Yes — dance bags, water bottles, and car magnets are family essentials that become mobile billboards. Lovart generates merchandise mockup templates for all standard dance accessories.

# How to Chat-Generate YouTube Thumbnails — No Design Skills, Just Describe What You Want

The old way: open design software, blank canvas, hunt templates, adjust everything, export, realize dimensions are wrong. Time: 1-3 hours. Frustration: high.

The Lovart way: open chat, describe what you need, review, refine, export. Time: under 5 minutes. Frustration: zero.

---

## Step 1: Tell Lovart What You Need

Open Lovart's ChatCanvas and type your request:

> *"Create a YouTube thumbnail for '[Video Title]'. Style: high-contrast, bold, clickable. Include: [face/reaction], bold text overlay, vibrant colors. Dimensions: 1280x720."*

**Real examples that work:**
- *"Create an Instagram post announcing our summer sale — 30% off everything, bright and energetic, use coral and white brand colors"*
- *"Design a logo for 'Greenwise Market', organic grocery. Minimal and fresh. Sage green and cream. Wordmark with leaf symbol."*
- *"Generate a YouTube thumbnail for 'I Tried Every AI Design Tool'. Bold, high contrast, surprised face, 'BEST AI TOOL?' text"*

**Good prompt ingredients:** Be specific about style, include brand colors, mention must-include elements, specify platform for correct dimensions.

---

## Step 2: Review & Refine (The Magic Part)

Lovart generates multiple options. Pick the strongest. Now use **Touch Edit** — tap the specific element you want to change and describe the change. The AI edits *only that element* while keeping everything else intact.

**Common refinements:**
- *"add a more expressive facial reaction to increase CTR"*
- *"adjust text placement — ensure it's not covered by the timestamp"*
- *"brighten the colors — thumbnails need higher contrast"*
- *"add an element of curiosity to trigger the click"*

You're not rolling dice hoping for a good result — you're directing a design process through conversation.

---

## Step 3: Export for Your Platform

JPEG at 1280x720, under 2MB. High quality with optimized compression.

Lovart handles resolution, color space, file format, and compression automatically based on your platform. No more realizing you exported at the wrong DPI after posting.

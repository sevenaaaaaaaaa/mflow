# Lovart Better Design Benchmark Seed V3

Purpose: close the remaining Better Design gaps around visual taste language, critique voice, and concrete craft diagnosis.

## Why this category needed V3

Better Design needed more than design principles. It needed:

- stronger critique voice
- more typography-specific judgment
- more examples of how small craft changes affect perceived quality

   - Use: vocabulary for explaining aesthetic quality with structure
2. NN/g, "The Anatomy of a Good Design: An Analysis of 4 Sites"
   - Link: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/why-does-a-design-look-good-part2/
   - Use: teardown-style analysis and evidence-backed critique
3. NN/g, "Good Visual Design, Explained"
   - Link: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/good-visual-design/
   - Use: recent quality criteria and plain-language explanation
4. Smashing Magazine, "Typographic Hierarchies"
   - Link: https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2022/10/typographic-hierarchies/
   - Use: hierarchy-specific craft language and structured teaching
5. Smashing Magazine, "How To Choose A Font"
   - Link: https://www.smashingmagazine.com/2011/03/how-to-choose-a-typeface/
   - Use: reasoning about type choice, hierarchy needs, and tone

## Source-use notes

- NN/g gives Better Design a more rigorous critique vocabulary
- Smashing gives richer typography and editorial craft language
- these sources help the category sound like informed design judgment rather than style blogging

## Final category lesson

Better Design gets stronger when the writing can name invisible quality signals, not just obvious before/after differences.

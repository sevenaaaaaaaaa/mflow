## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How do I build trust in an industry with authenticity concerns?**
Legit check is the most important two words in sneaker resale. Prominently display your authentication process. Show the actual shoe, not stock photos. Lovart's product page templates include authentication badge placement and detailed photo requirements.

**Q2: What condition grading system should I use?**
DS (Deadstock — never worn), VNDS (Very Near Deadstock — tried on or worn once), PADS (Pass As Deadstock — very light wear), Pre-Owned (varying degrees). Create a visual standard for each grade. Consistency in grading builds trust.

**Q3: How do I photograph sneakers for resale?**
Consistent angles: side profile, medial side, heel, outsole, insole, size tag, box label. Good lighting — no flash, no shadows. Include any flaws honestly — a disclosed flaw builds more trust than a hidden one.

**Q4: What platform should I prioritize for sales?**
Your own website builds the strongest brand, but marketplaces (StockX, GOAT, eBay) provide traffic. Use marketplaces as top-of-funnel and convert repeat buyers to your direct site with better pricing and loyalty perks.

**Q5: How do I brand my packaging and unboxing experience?**
Sneakerheads care about the box almost as much as the shoe. Double-box shipping with branded tape. Include an authentication card and a care card. A sticker pack inside every order generates organic social content.

**Q6: Should I create content beyond just selling sneakers?**
Yes — content builds the brand that selling alone cannot. Release date calendars, legit check guides, sneaker news commentary, and collection showcases. Lovart's content templates support every content type.

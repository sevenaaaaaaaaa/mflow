https://www.lovart.ai/docs/export-your-assets/export-formats

Download your work and don't forget to share it with the Lovart community.

**How to Use**

1. **Export a single asset.** Click any item on the Canvas, then click the export button. You can export files in common PNG and MP4 formats, as well as special formats from HTML to SVG.
2. **Export multiple assets at once.** Drag to select multiple items on the Canvas, then click the export button. PSD is supported for batch export.
3. **Publish to the community feed.** Click the publish button on the Canvas to post your work to the Lovart community feed.

**Supported Formats**

The available formats depend on what you selected.

|Asset type|Export formats|
|---|---|
|Image|PNG, JPEG|
|Video|MP4|
|Slides|PDF, PPTX, PPTX with editable text|
|Layout|HTML|
|Vectorized object|SVG|
|Multiple objects|PSD|

**Quick Tips**

- Remember to visit the community feed for inspiration.

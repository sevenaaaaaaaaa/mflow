## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Electrical branding must balance high-visibility safety signaling with modern professionalism. The classic yellow/black combo is iconic but often poorly executed. Lovart modernizes it.

- **Primary**: `#FFD700` — Electric safety-yellow, instantly recognizable as electrical-trade color, optimized for van visibility
- **Secondary**: `#2C2C2C` — Gunmetal charcoal for backgrounds, uniform shirts, and serious typography
- **Accent**: `#FFA500` — Warning orange for "Emergency Service" badges, safety alerts, and CTAs
- **Glow**: `#FFF8DC` — Soft ivory for invoices, reports, and digital backgrounds
- **Contrast**: `#1A1A1A` — True black for high-contrast text on yellow backgrounds; meets DOT signage standards

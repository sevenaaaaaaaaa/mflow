# Lovart one pager

# `ABOUT LOVART`

**Lovart** is the **first AI design agent** that turns any ideas into polished design assets. It handles design end-to-end — from brand assets and social graphics to ad creatives and viral videos — without requiring design skills or a design team. Think of it as the creative layer you've been missing inside your marketing stack.

While other design products give you a tool to design, Lovart gives you the design result itself, plus access to all the top AI image and video models.

At its core, we believe **great design is everything.**

# `PRODUCT USPS`

### 1. One prompt to a complete campaign kit, all in one canvas

Other design tools generate isolated assets. Lovart generates complete campaign kits like logo, color system, typography, social templates, video, and brand guidelines, all in one infinite canvas — an unified workspace where you create everything without jumping between tabs.

### 2. **One-click _Skills_ for every creative task**

Lovart Skills give you ready-to-use workflows for any creative task — logo creation, poster design, brand identity, social campaigns, and more.

You can also save your workflows as Skills, so that you can build a repeatable design system and ensure every campaign stays on-brand.

### 3. **Effortless editing control, down to every element**

Most AI tools give you one-shot result. Lovart gives you full editing control after generation — adjust layers, tweak structure, swap content, correct style, etc. Every asset is editable.

### 4. **Access the best AI models for every design task**

Lovart integrates the top image and video AI models into one seamless platform, such as _Nano Banana Pro_ and _Seedance 2_. You can choose the right model for each task.

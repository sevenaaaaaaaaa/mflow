TikTok's algorithm rewards consistency — posting multiple times per day increases reach. But producing that volume of video manually is unsustainable. Lovart's AI video tools generate TikTok-ready vertical content from text descriptions, enabling daily posting without a daily production burden.

---

## Trending Format 1: Product Reveal

**Structure:** Dark screen → dramatic lighting reveal → product in full glory → brand logo → end.

**Prompt:** *"Seedance 2.0: vertical 9:16. Product reveal video. Start from complete darkness. A glowing light gradually reveals a skincare serum bottle on a reflective surface. Slow, dramatic pacing. The light traces the bottle's contours. End with the product fully lit and centered. Add subtle particle effects — tiny floating light motes. 10 seconds. Audio: rising synth pad, building tension to the reveal moment."*

**Optimization:** Add a text overlay with your hook: *"The serum that sold out in 48 hours."* Use Lovart's Text Edit to place text on a key frame.

---

## Trending Format 2: Before/After Transformation

**Structure:** "Before" frame (problem state) → rapid transition → "After" frame (solved state).

**Prompt:** *"Seedance 2.0: 9:16 before/after video. Before: a cluttered, messy desk with scattered papers. Quick whip pan transition. After: the same desk, now immaculately organized, with a beautiful planner in the center. Satisfying, ASMR-style pacing. 8 seconds. Audio: soft reset sound at the transition, then calm ambient music."*

## The questions I'd ask before betting on any text-to-video tool

If Hailuo isn't your pick, the framework still holds. These five questions separate a demo toy from a shipping tool, and they're the only ones that survive contact with a campaign date.

1. **Can I fix text without regenerating?** If the answer is no, every typo owns your Thursday. This is the first question, always.
2. **Does brand color hold across a series?** If the model has no memory, your five clips become five cousins. Look for a brand layer outside the model.
3. **Does the native format match my channels?** If it's 16:9 only and you live in 9:16, you're re-prompting, not cropping. Build the frame for the crop.
4. **What does it bake that I can't select?** Type, URLs, logos — anything baked is a future regeneration. Keep those in the design layer.
5. **Does the marketing show the failures?** A tool that only shows wins is hiding the part that will burn you. Trust the review that documents the break.

Hailuo answers question 1 with no, question 2 with no memory, question 3 with 16:9 native. That's why it's a hero-clip tool, not a campaign tool — unless you pair it with the desk that answers yes to all five. The questions, not the leaderboard, are how you choose.

## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Pet grooming branding lives in the "cute but professional" quadrant. Too pink-and-fluffy, and you look like a children's toy store. Too clinical, and dogs sense the tension. The sweet spot: warm, joyful, trustworthy.

- **Primary**: `#FF69B4` — Bubblegum pink, the energetic color of freshly groomed poodles and happy tails
- **Secondary**: `#FFF0F7` — Soft petal-white for appointment cards, menus, and spa-like backgrounds
- **Accent**: `#9B59B6` — Rich purple for typography, premium service tiers, and "Spa Package" headers
- **Warmth**: `#F4A261` — Golden retriever tan for loyalty stamps and "Pup of the Week" callouts
- **Cool**: `#52B788` — Mint-fresh green for "Natural Products," "Eco-Friendly" badges, and calming accents

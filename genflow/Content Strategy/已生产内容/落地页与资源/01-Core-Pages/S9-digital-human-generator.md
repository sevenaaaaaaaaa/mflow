## Digital Human vs Avatar: What's the Difference?

This distinction matters because the two terms get used interchangeably when they shouldn't be.

### AI Avatar
A stylized, often cartoonish or illustrated representation. Think Apple Memoji, VR chat characters, or the animated profile pictures in CapCut templates. Avatars are expressive and fun, but they don't aim for realism — they're intentionally not human.

### Digital Human
A photorealistic or near-photorealistic virtual person designed to pass as human at a glance. Digital humans have realistic skin texture, natural eye movement, micro-expressions, and accurate lip-sync. They look like they could be real people — because they're modeled on human facial structure and movement.

Lovart generates digital humans, not avatars. The use cases, audience expectations, and technical requirements are fundamentally different. A digital human can serve as a virtual customer service agent without making your brand look unprofessional. An avatar cannot.

## Core Capabilities

### Talking Avatar Mode
Upload a script or type directly. Select your digital human. The AI generates a video of that digital human delivering your script — with lip movements synchronized to the generated voiceover. Choose from dozens of voices (male, female, various accents and ages) or upload your own audio.

### Lip Sync
The make-or-break feature for any **AI digital human**. If the mouth movements don't match the words, the uncanny valley swallows your credibility whole. Lovart's lip-sync engine maps phonemes to visemes (mouth shapes) in real-time, achieving accuracy that feels natural even on close-up shots. CapCut's lip sync is functional but visibly mechanical — good enough for meme content, not for a professional product demo.

### Expression Control
Your digital human shouldn't have one static expression for the entire video. Lovart supports expression markers in your script:
- `[smile]` — warm, approachable
- `[serious]` — authoritative, trustworthy
- `[excited]` — energetic, enthusiastic
- `[concerned]` — empathetic, thoughtful
- `[neutral]` — professional, default

The transitions between expressions are smoothed, not jarring — the digital human moves naturally from a smile to a thoughtful expression, not like a robot switching modes.

### Background & Environment
Place your digital human in a virtual environment: a clean office background, a gradient studio setup, a product-showcase backdrop, or a transparent background for compositing into your own footage. Lovart renders the digital human with environment-aware lighting so they look like they belong in the scene — not like a sticker pasted on top.

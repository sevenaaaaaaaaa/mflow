## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Event rental branding must walk a tightrope: it must feel styled and aspirational (to attract planners) while remaining neutral enough to sell pieces for any event aesthetic—rustic, modern, boho, classic.

- **Primary**: `#7FB069` — Sage green, the neutral of the event world—natural, elegant, pairs with everything
- **Secondary**: `#F7FAF5` — Bright botanical white for catalog pages and quote sheets
- **Accent**: `#5A8A45` — Deep herb green for typography, section headers, and inventory codes
- **Warmth**: `#C4A484` — Natural linen tan for tabletop textures and background cards
- **Highlight**: `#6D8B74` — Muted eucalyptus for "Featured Item" callouts and seasonal collections

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart generate styled-shoot concepts using only my inventory?**
Yes. Input your inventory list, and Lovart's AI suggests vignette concepts ("Mediterranean Garden Dinner," "Modern Minimalist Reception") using only your pieces. Generate the styled-shoot moodboard, then the final social posts. The AI identifies gaps in your inventory for future purchasing decisions.

**Q2: How do I create a rental catalog that's easy to update seasonally?**
Lovart's dynamic catalog connects to your inventory management system. Add items, and they auto-populate with consistent formatting. Seasonal collections (holiday, summer) get their own cover page and color accents within the master catalog.

**Q3: Can Lovart generate 3D room layouts showing my rental pieces?**
Lovart generates 2D moodboard-style layouts and pairs with 3D-rendering integrations (SketchUp, Roomstyler) for full room visualizations. Describe the space, and the AI produces a top-down layout diagram with your rental items placed to scale.

**Q4: Does Lovart create trade-show materials?**
Yes. The $99 Studio tier includes trade-show booth design: backdrop graphics, rack cards, look-book templates, and lead-capture form design. Lovart generates a cohesive booth concept from your inventory highlights.

**Q5: What about branding my delivery truck and warehouse?**
Lovart generates vehicle-wrap designs and warehouse signage concepts. Describe your fleet ("16ft box truck, white"), and the AI produces a wrap layout with branding specifications. Warehouse directional signage templates included.

Packaging is the only marketing material your customer touches before buying. A well-designed box or label communicates quality, builds trust, and justifies premium pricing. But professional packaging design — including dieline templates, structural design, artwork, and 3D mockups — traditionally requires a packaging designer charging $500-3,000 per SKU.

Lovart generates packaging concepts, label artwork, and photorealistic mockups in minutes. This guide covers the complete workflow.

---

## Step 1: Generate Packaging Concepts

Start with your brand identity loaded into the Brand Kit (see our [setup guide](/blog/brand-kit-setup-5-minutes-lovart-best-practice)). Then generate concept directions:

*"Product packaging concept for a premium candle brand. 3 distinct directions: (1) minimalist white box with embossed gold logo, (2) kraft paper box with botanical illustration wrap, (3) matte black box with neon accent geometric pattern. Show each as a front-facing flat lay. Include the brand logo on each. Photorealistic product render."*

Arrange all 3 concepts on the ChatCanvas. Compare. Select the winner.

---

## Step 2: Design Label Artwork

*"Candle jar label design. Round label, 2.5-inch diameter. Brand name 'Lumin' centered. Scent name 'Cedar & Sage' below. Small botanical leaf icon. Brand Kit colors. Clean, premium. Include a subtle gold foil border effect."*

Use **Text Edit** to precisely control label text — type scent names, ingredients, and weight directly onto the generated label. This is more reliable than including detailed text in the initial prompt.

---

## Step 3: Generate 3D Mockups

Your flat design is approved. Now show it on the actual product:

*"Smart Mockup: apply the 'Lumin' candle label design to a matte black glass candle jar. Photorealistic. Studio lighting. Show the front of the jar — the label should wrap naturally around the curved glass surface."*

*"Same product, lifestyle mockup. The candle on a bedside table. Warm ambient lighting. A book and a cup of tea nearby. Atmospheric, aspirational."*

Smart Mockups handle perspective, lighting, and surface adaptation automatically. Your flat label design becomes a photorealistic product image in seconds.

---

## Step 4: Create Multi-Variant Displays

*"Product lineup mockup. Show 3 candle variants side by side — Cedar & Sage, Lavender & Oak, Vanilla & Amber. Each has a distinct label color. The jar shape is identical. Clean white surface. Professional product photography style."*

Brand Kit ensures all variants share the same brand colors. Edit Elements lets you swap individual labels without regenerating the entire scene.

## FAQ

**Q: Does Lovart generate dielines for printers?**
A: Lovart generates visual packaging designs, not structural dieline files. For print production, export your design as a high-resolution image and work with your printer to apply it to their dieline template. For a guide to print-ready exports, see our [raster vs vector guide](/blog/raster-png-vs-vector-svg-when-to-use-which).

**Q: Can I design packaging for irregular shapes — bottles, tubes, pouches?**
A: Yes. Smart Mockups supports cylindrical, box, pouch, and flat surface shapes. Describe the shape in your prompt: *"Apply this label to a flexible stand-up pouch. Matte finish. The pouch is slightly crinkled — the label should conform naturally to the surface texture."*

## Five AI Use Cases for Real Estate

### 1. Virtual Staging

An empty room is harder to sell than a furnished one. Virtual staging — digitally placing furniture and decor into photos of vacant properties — helps buyers see a property's potential. Traditional virtual staging services charge $30–$75 per image and take 24–48 hours.

Lovart's virtual staging workflow:

**Upload the empty room photo.** Lovart accepts any resolution, any aspect ratio — phone photos, professional DSLR shots, even MLS downloads.

**Describe the staging style.** In natural language: "Modern farmhouse living room. Beige sectional sofa, reclaimed wood coffee table, large potted plant, abstract wall art. Warm and inviting."

**Lovart stages the room.** The AI understands spatial context — furniture scales correctly to room dimensions, lighting matches the original photo's direction and color temperature, shadows fall naturally. The result is a furnished room that looks photographed, not composited.

**Generate multiple styles.** The same empty living room, staged three ways: modern farmhouse, mid-century modern, minimalist Scandinavian. Let the buyer imagine the space their way.

**Cost comparison:**
- Traditional virtual staging: $30–$75/image, 1–2 day turnaround
- Lovart: Included in your subscription plan, 30–60 second generation
- For an agent staging 20 images per month: $600–$1,500 traditional vs. $19–$149 Lovart

### 3. Listing Photo Enhancement

Not every listing gets a professional photographer. Phone photos, older MLS images, poorly lit interiors — these are the reality of everyday real estate marketing. Lovart's AI Photo Editor transforms suboptimal listing photos into listing-ready assets.

**Enhancement capabilities:**
- **Brightness and exposure correction:** Fix underexposed interiors and overexposed window views
- **Color correction:** Remove yellow indoor lighting casts, restore natural color balance
- **Sky replacement:** Turn a gray-sky exterior shot into a blue-sky afternoon
- **Clutter removal:** AI-powered object removal — clear countertops, remove personal items, declutter spaces
- **Perspective correction:** Straighten vertical lines in wide-angle interior shots
- **Upscaling:** Increase resolution of low-quality MLS images to print-ready 300 DPI

**Workflow:**
1. Upload listing photo(s) to ChatCanvas
2. Describe the enhancement: `@photo-edit task:enhance adjust: brightness, color-correct, remove personal items on counter`
3. Lovart processes and returns enhanced versions
4. Compare side-by-side, export

An agent with 10 listing photos that need enhancement can process all of them in under 10 minutes. The cost of having a professional retoucher do the same work: $10–$50 per image.

## FAQ for Real Estate Agents

**Q: Do Lovart's virtually staged images look realistic enough for MLS?**
A: Yes. Lovart's staging pipeline is trained on real estate photography and produces images that are compliant with MLS virtual staging guidelines. The AI correctly handles spatial proportions, lighting matching, and furniture scaling. As with any virtual staging, MLS rules typically require disclosure that images are virtually staged.

**Q: Can Lovart remove existing furniture and re-stage a room differently?**
A: Yes. Upload the furnished room photo, describe the furniture you want removed ("remove the red couch and dark curtains"), and describe the new staging style. Lovart handles the decluttering and re-staging in one pass.

**Q: What about exterior photos — can Lovart enhance curb appeal?**
A: Yes. Lovart can enhance lawn appearance, remove cars from driveways, replace gray skies with blue, and add seasonal landscaping elements. For more dramatic exterior changes (adding a porch, changing paint color), results are strong but should be disclosed as enhanced imagery.

**Q: Can I use Lovart for video walkthroughs?**
A: Lovart's video generation capabilities (6+ video models) can create short property highlight videos — panning through staged rooms with text overlays for key features — suitable for social media and listing portals that support video.

**Q: How does Lovart compare to dedicated virtual staging services?**
A: Dedicated services offer human review and may handle complex, irregular room shapes with more precision. For standard residential rooms (rectangular, typical proportions), Lovart's output is comparable in quality while being faster (seconds vs. days) and dramatically cheaper (included in subscription vs. $30–75/image).

**Q: Can my assistant or transaction coordinator use Lovart for me?**
A: Yes. Team plans support multiple seats, so your assistant can generate marketing materials for your listings while you focus on clients. The Brand Kit ensures everything they produce matches your visual identity.

**SAN FRANCISCO, July 13, 2025** — Lovart, the San Francisco–based AI startup pioneering the world’s first Design Agent, hosted an exclusive offline workshop on July 10, bringing together over 15 designers, founders, and creative professionals for an immersive, hands-on experience with its cutting-edge platform.

The event, held in San Francisco and organized by Ying Yang, was designed to give early creative leaders a closer look at how Lovart’s multimodal Design Agent is transforming the way people ideate, design, and produce content. Through live demos, guided exploration, and interactive feedback sessions, attendees were introduced to Lovart’s unique agentic workflow—a conversational AI agent that converts natural-language prompts into cohesive, editable visual and audio outputs, including images, videos, brand kits, and 3D renders.

### Rapid Adoption Continues Ahead of July Launch

We opened Lovart's private beta in May, and our waitlist soared past 100,000 creators within the first five days. Today, more than 300,000 creators have lined up for early access. Your enthusiasm drives us to iterate at full throttle; so that when we launch publicly in July, every creator will be welcomed with a polished, high‑performance experience.

### About Lovart

Lovart is a San Francisco–based AI company pioneering the Design Agent—a conversational, multimodal creative AI agent that transforms a single prompt into cohesive, editable assets, including images, video, audio, storyboards, brand kits, and 3D content, all within a unified infinite canvas.

## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Dog training branding must balance authority with empathy. Too rigid, and owners feel judged. Too soft, and you don't convey the structure dogs need. The palette should say "I understand your dog—and I know how to help."

- **Primary**: `#2196F3` — Confident training-blue, the color of clear commands and reliable leadership
- **Secondary**: `#E3F2FD` — Soft sky-blue for backgrounds, training guides, and progress pages
- **Accent**: `#FF5722` — Energetic reward-orange for "Book a Session" CTAs, "Success Stories," and clicker-action badges
- **Focus**: `#1A1A2E` — Deep navy for serious typography, certification displays, and methodology explanations
- **Neutral**: `#F5F5F5` — Clean cloud-gray for report cards, forms, and assessment sheets

## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Plumbing branding must communicate reliability, cleanliness, and emergency-readiness. No rusty browns. No amateur blues. The palette should look like a professional who arrives in a clean uniform, not someone who tracks mud on the carpet.

- **Primary**: `#1E3A5F` — Deep pipe-navy, the color of quality copper fittings and midnight reliability
- **Secondary**: `#F0F4F8` — Crisp silver-white for van panels, invoices, and business cards
- **Accent**: `#0D2137` — Near-black maritime blue for typography and serious trust signals
- **Signal**: `#E63946` — Emergency red, used exclusively for "24/7 Emergency" badges and urgent CTAs
- **Highlight**: `#2A9D8F` — Water-teal for "Licensed & Insured" checkmarks and certification badges

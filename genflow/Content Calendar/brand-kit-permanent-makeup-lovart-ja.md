## The Scene: Semi-Permanent Art, Permanent Trust

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Permanent makeup (PMU) sits at the intersection of medicine, tattooing, and beauty. Your clients are making a semi-permanent decision about their face — trust is everything. Your brand kit must project clinical safety, artistic mastery, and the kind of refined aesthetic that says "I understand faces at a structural level."

## Recommended Color Palette

PMU branding needs the sterility signals of a medical practice blended with the warmth of a beauty destination.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Should my brand emphasize the "medical" or the "beauty" aspect?**
Both — but lead with safety and credentials on your website, then show beauty results. The order matters: clients need to trust you before they get excited about results. Lovart's templates follow this story architecture.

**Q2: What's the most important document to brand?**
The consent form — it's the document every client signs and the one that legally protects you. A professionally designed consent form signals legitimacy. Lovart's PMU consent templates are structured for informed consent with branded headers.

**Q3: How do I display before/after photos ethically and effectively?**
Identical lighting, angle, and expression. No makeup in "before." No filters on "after." Include healing stage photos (Day 1, Day 7, Day 30) to manage expectations. Lovart's before/after templates enforce consistent framing.

**Q4: What healing information should be branded?**
Every PMU service needs a healing guide: what to expect days 1–14, what's normal vs. concerning, emergency contact information. A beautiful healing guide reduces post-procedure anxiety calls. Lovart generates illustrated healing timeline infographics.

**Q5: How do I show color theory in my consultations?**
Create a pigment color wheel in your brand style. Show warm vs. cool undertones. Include Fitzpatrick scale references. Lovart's color theory templates make pigment selection visual and consultative.

**Q6: Should I brand my numbing products and aftercare?**
Yes — custom labels on numbing cream containers and aftercare balms build brand recall during the 7–14 day healing period. Lovart generates product label templates for standard PMU aftercare sizes.

## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Cocktail bar branding must operate in low light and high energy simultaneously. The palette needs to work at 10pm under amber bulbs AND at noon on an Instagram explore page.

- **Primary**: `#1A1A2E` — Deep midnight navy, the color of a darkened bar at golden hour
- **Secondary**: `#E94560` — Electric neon pink for accent lines, drink names, and social CTAs
- **Accent**: `#0F3460` — Sapphire blue for secondary graphics and menu backgrounds
- **Glow**: `#FF2E63` — Hot magenta for "Tonight" event badges and limited-release highlights
- **Neutral**: `#F0E6D3` — Warm parchment for menu card stocks and muted backgrounds

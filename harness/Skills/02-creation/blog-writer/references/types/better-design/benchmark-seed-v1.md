# Lovart Better Design Benchmark Seed V1

Purpose: benchmark posts that teach judgment, redesign logic, and visual quality upgrades rather than generic inspiration.

## Category definition

Better Design explains why one visual direction works better than another and how to upgrade weak output into stronger output.

## Five core topics

1. Visual hierarchy fixes
2. Layout and composition upgrades
3. Typography and spacing upgrades
4. Texture, lighting, and material realism upgrades
5. Design teardown and before/after redesign logic

## Benchmark traits to absorb

- names the visual problem before prescribing style
- explains why the current design underperforms
- uses contrast pairs: flat vs layered, noisy vs focused, polished vs human
- gives concrete redesign levers, not taste-only language
- links design choices to trust, clarity, conversion, or memorability

## What to avoid

- inspiration boards with no critique
- moodboard language with no execution logic
- "make it modern" advice with no definition
- trend commentary with no redesign moves

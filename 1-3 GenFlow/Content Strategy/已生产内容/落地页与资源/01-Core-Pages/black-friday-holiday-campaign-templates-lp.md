## Seasonal Campaigns Covered

### Black Friday and Cyber Monday

The Super Bowl of retail. Templates engineered for the specific psychology of BFCM shoppers: limited-time urgency, doorbuster pricing visibility, countdown mechanics, "while supplies last" scarcity cues. Categories include:

- **BFCM Email Headers:** Discount-announcement designs with prominent offer callouts, countdown timer placements, and urgency-driven CTAs.
- **Social Media Posts and Stories:** Flash sale announcements, sneak-peek reveals, early-access promotions, hour-by-hour deal countdowns.
- **Web Banners and Pop-Ups:** Site-wide sale announcements, category-specific promotions, cart-abandonment recovery designs.
- **Ad Creative:** Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok ad templates sized and styled for BFCM performance — high contrast, bold typography, motion-ready compositions.

### Christmas and Holiday Season

November through December: gift guides, holiday collections, shipping deadline announcements, last-chance messaging. Templates cover the full narrative arc of holiday marketing:

- **Gift Guide Carousels:** Multi-slide Instagram and email designs organized by recipient (Gifts for Him, Gifts Under $50, Stocking Stuffers).
- **Holiday Shipping Deadlines:** Clear, urgent designs communicating last order dates for Christmas delivery.
- **New Year Sale:** Post-Christmas clearance, New Year-New-You campaigns, resolution-themed promotions.
- **General Holiday Cheer:** Non-commercial templates for brand holiday greetings, year-in-review content, and community appreciation posts.

### Valentine's Day

Romance, friendship, and self-love campaigns. Gift guide templates, date-night inspiration designs, couple and singles-focused promotions. Soft palettes, heart motifs, elegant typography — the visual language of February 14th across email, social, and web.

### Mother's Day and Father's Day

Sentiment-driven campaigns that celebrate the person, not just push the product. Gift guide templates organized by interest (Gifts for the Chef Mom, Gifts for the Outdoors Dad). Last-minute shopper designs with expedited shipping callouts. Templates that balance emotional resonance with commercial conversion — the hardest balance in seasonal design, and the most effective when done right.

### Halloween

Costume guides, decor inspiration, party promotion, and seasonal product features. Templates that embrace the fun of the holiday — playful typography, spooky-but-not-scary visuals, candy-bright color accents — while driving commercial outcomes.

### Back to School

July through September: school supplies, dorm essentials, student tech, first-day outfits. Templates organized by education level (K-12, college, teacher appreciation) and product category. Designs that speak to both parents (practical, value-driven) and students (trend-aware, aspirational).

### Additional Seasonal Events

Easter, St. Patrick's Day, July 4th, Pride Month, Women's Day, Thanksgiving, Giving Tuesday, New Year's Eve. The collection covers every major commercial and cultural event on the annual marketing calendar.

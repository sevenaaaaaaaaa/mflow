# AI Resume Builder — Create ATS-Friendly Resumes That Get Interviews

Your resume has one job: get you past the gatekeeper. But there are two gatekeepers now, not one. First, the Applicant Tracking System — an algorithm that parses, scores, and filters your resume before any human sees it. Second, the recruiter who spends an average of six to seven seconds scanning what makes it through. If your resume fails either test, your application is dead on arrival.

Most resume builders optimize for one or the other. Pretty templates that choke ATS parsers. Or text-dense documents that bore recruiters into clicking away. Lovart's AI Resume Builder optimizes for both — designs that parse flawlessly through every major ATS while looking sharp enough to hold a recruiter's attention past the seven-second mark.

Lovart's parsing engine tests every resume against real ATS scoring algorithms to ensure your document does not get silently filtered out.

Every resume comes with a matching cover letter — same visual identity, same professional tone. Lovart generates cover letters customized to the job description, addressing the hiring manager's stated needs with specific examples from your experience.

Download as ATS-optimized DOCX (what most employers and systems expect), designer-quality PDF (for direct recruiter emails and portfolio sites), and plain-text (for application forms that require pasting).

**Step 4 — Review and Refine.** Touch Edit lets you adjust individual bullet points, reorder sections, or tweak the layout. The AI scores your resume against the target job description and highlights gaps.

**Step 5 — Export and Apply.** Download in your preferred format. Apply with confidence knowing your resume will survive the ATS and impress the recruiter.

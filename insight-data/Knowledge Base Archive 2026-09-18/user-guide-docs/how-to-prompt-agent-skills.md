Lovart Skills
One-click, ready-to-use workflows for logos, product listings, social assets, video ads, and more.
What It Does
Lovart Skills are intelligent workflows tuned for specific creative tasks. When you pick a Skill, the Agent knows what it needs to produce, asks you for anything missing, and moves through the steps automatically to deliver a finished output. Unlike a free-form prompt, a Skill encodes prompt best practices, professional design guidelines, and multi-step chains, so you can follow the Agent&#x27;s lead.
Open the Skill Book.
When you open a new project, Skills appear in the empty input box.
At any other time, click the Skills icon in the bottom-left of the input box.
Pick a Skill.
Browse by category and select the one that best matches your task.
Describe what you need.
Type a short prompt with your goal.
Upload any references.
Follow the Agent&#x27;s guide.
The Agent asks for any missing details (preferences, goals, assets, etc.) and runs the workflow to produce the final deliverables.
Available Skill Categories
: Build logos, brand systems, and campaign visuals for new or existing brands.
: Generate ad creative and campaign assets tailored to specific platforms and objectives.
Social Media
: Design Instagram posts, YouTube thumbnails, RedNote covers, and other platform-native content.
: Produce product shots, listing images, and mockups for online storefronts.
: Turn briefs into finished videos with shot lists, consistent characters, and smooth transitions.
When in doubt, ask the Agent. The Agent will recommend or explain a Skill to you.
Upload references and describe your intent to guide the Agent.

: Build your own reusable Skill from conversations.
Custom Skills
Save a successful project conversation as a reusable Skill.
What It Does
Custom Skills distill any conversation into a reusable Skill, so you can rerun the same workflow on new inputs without prompting from scratch. Use it to lock in a process you&#x27;ve already proven, and build a personal library of workflows over time.
Create a successful project.
Thinking Mode
Talk to the Agent until you are happy with the design output(s).
Save it as a Skill.
Open the Skill Book in the input box and click Create a Skill.
Save the new Skill under My Skills.
In a new chat, open the Skill Book and select your saved Skill.
You can edit your custom Skill&#x27;s instructions, examples, or other details as your workflow evolves.
Follow the Skill&#x27;s guidance to rerun the workflow on new inputs.
Custom Skills capture the conversational path, so a clean, focused session makes a cleaner Skill than one with detours and corrections.
Custom Skills let you create personal versions of
Lovart Skills
. Use Lovart Skills for general jobs and My Skills for workflows specific to your brand or process.
Limitations
Custom Skills are only available in
Thinking Mode
, and only after at least one Agent conversation in the session.

## FAQ

**Q: What does MCoT stand for?**

A: Mind Chain of Thought—Lovart's proprietary reasoning engine before generation.

**Q: Is MCoT only in Thinking Mode?**

A: Thinking Mode shows more of the chain; Fast Mode still uses routing and checks with less narration.

**Q: Can I bypass MCoT?**

A: You cannot disable safety and routing entirely—doing so would recreate generic generator chaos.

**Q: Does MCoT cost extra credits?**

A: Visible planning may add steps; it usually saves net credits by reducing failed generations.

**Q: How does MCoT relate to Brand Kit?**

A: Brand Kit constraints inject into decomposition so sub-tasks inherit palette, type, and references.

**Q: How do I learn MCoT without engineering docs?**

A: Run real briefs in Thinking Mode, read plans before approving, and compare failures to sub-tasks. Pair with ChatCanvas 101 and Brand Kit setup guides linked in this article.

Weddings are the highest-stakes design project most people ever commission. The invitations set expectations. The signage guides guests. The timeline anchors the day. And everything must cohere into a single visual theme — elegant, personal, and flawless.

Lovart generates wedding materials that feel custom-designed, not template-generated.

**Invitation suite:** *"Wedding invitation. 'Emma & James.' Elegant script for the names. Classic serif for the details. Botanical watercolor border — eucalyptus and white roses. Cream paper texture. The design should feel romantic but not saccharine. Include matching RSVP card and details card. Print-ready."*

**Day-of signage:** *"Wedding welcome sign. 'Welcome to the Wedding of Emma & James.' Large elegant script. Floral watercolor accents matching the invitation suite. Easel-stand format. Clean, readable from 10 feet away. Gold foil accents if simulated."*

**Seating chart:** *"Seating chart. 'Find Your Table.' Tables 1-12 listed with guest names in small elegant type. Floral watercolor header. Gold trim border. Matching the invitation design language. Large format — 24×36 inches."*

For building a complete visual system that unifies every wedding touchpoint, the Brand Kit locks your color palette (blush, sage, gold) and typography (elegant script + classic serif) across every generation.

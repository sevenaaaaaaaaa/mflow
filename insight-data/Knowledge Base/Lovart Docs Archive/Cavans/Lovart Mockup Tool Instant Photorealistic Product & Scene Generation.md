Canvas

## Mockup

Transform flat designs into photorealistic product mockups with Lovart. Features smart perspective matching and texture adaptation for packaging, apparel, and more.

### What is Mockup

The Mockup feature allows you to instantly place your design or image into realistic, professional scene templates. This transforms a flat design into a photorealistic presentation by wrapping it onto real-world objects (phones, laptops, billboards, packaging, apparel, etc.), making it look like the design is already implemented in a real product or environment.

### Step-by-Step Usage Guide

1. **Select Your Image/Design Layer**
	- Click on the layer containing the design you want to mockup
2. **Access the Mockup Feature**
	- In the top toolbar, click the **"Mockup"** button
3. **Apply the Mockup**
	- Lovart will render the photorealistic mockup (processing may take a few seconds)
	- The mockup will replace or layer over your original design on the canvas

### After Creating a Mockup

Once your mockup is generated, you can:

1. **Export for Presentations**: Save as high-res PNG or JPG for client decks or portfolios
2. **Create Variations**: Apply the same mockup to multiple design versions for A/B testing
3. **Layer & Compose**: Combine multiple mockups into a single composition
4. **Post to Social Media**: Export sized versions for Instagram, LinkedIn, or other platforms

### FAQ

**Q: Can I use any design for mockups?**
A: Best results with flat, well-designed graphics. Photos work too, but flat designs show off mockup effects better.

**Q: How long does mockup generation take?**
A: Usually 5-15 seconds depending on complexity. Larger or more detailed designs may take longer.

**Q: Can I edit the mockup after generation?**
A: Yes, use all standard canvas tools to adjust, enhance, or modify the generated mockup.

**Q: What's the best format for mockup designs?**
A: PNG with transparency works best. High resolution (2K+) produces cleanest results.

**Q: Can I batch generate multiple mockups?**
A: Apply mockup to each design individually. For large batches, consider template workflows.

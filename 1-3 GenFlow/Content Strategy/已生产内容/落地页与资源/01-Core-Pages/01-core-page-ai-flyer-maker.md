## Flyer Design Best Practices

**One message per flyer.** The most common flyer mistake is trying to communicate everything. A flyer should have one primary message — the event, the offer, the announcement. Supporting details support; they do not compete.

**High contrast for readability.** Flyers are often viewed in less-than-ideal conditions — dim lighting, from a distance, while walking by. Lovart generates flyers with strong visual contrast by default, but you can specify "high contrast" or "bold" in your prompt for extra emphasis.

**QR codes drive digital action.** Include a QR code for ticket links, menu pages, or contact forms. Lovart generates QR-ready composition space — just add your code. Pro tip: use `@flyer ... include-qr: true` and Lovart generates a placeholder QR code position that integrates naturally into the layout.

**Print on quality paper.** The design is half the flyer. The paper is the other half. Lovart's print-ready PDFs include bleed for full-color edge-to-edge printing — use a professional print service or a good color printer for best results.

### Add to Canvas

> Place files onto your Canvas workspace for editing.

**What It Does**

Import your images and videos directly onto the Canvas for selection and editing.

**How to Use**

1. **Upload from the toolbar.**
    - Click the Upload icon in the Canvas toolbar to upload images or videos.
2. **Drag and drop.**
    - Drag and drop assets directly onto the Canvas.

**Related Features**

- [Upload Files](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/how-to-prompt/adding-references#c2157e7fb209): Import assets into the chat conversation.
- [Select](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/customize-your-canvas#cdcd787e2a83): Pick and manipulate elements after adding them to the Canvas.
- [Hand](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/customize-your-canvas#2a9fd81bb3c9): Pan and zoom the Canvas without moving elements.

### Frame

**Shortcut:** `F`

> Create rectangular containers on the Canvas to organize and group related elements.

**What It Does**

Frames act as containers on the infinite Canvas — use them to structure multi-panel layouts, group related elements, or define artboards for specific export sizes. Add images, videos, text, or shapes inside a frame to keep related content together.

**How to Use**

1. **Select the Frame tool.**
    - Click the Frame icon in the toolbar.
2. **Draw the frame.**
    - Drag on the Canvas to define the frame size, or click to create a default 1024×1024 frame.
3. **Add content.**
    - Place images, videos, text, or shapes inside the frame for organized design layouts.

**Quick Tips**

- Use frames to organize multi-panel layouts and group related elements.

**Related Features**

- [Add to Canvas](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/canvas-objects#e922b61f4714): Upload images and videos into a frame.
- [Shapes](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/canvas-objects#7917206b9097): Add geometric elements to your Canvas.

### Shapes

> Add rectangles, ellipses, lines, and other geometric elements to your Canvas.

**What It Does**

Shapes are flexible building blocks for your layout. Use them as masks, backgrounds, or graphic elements.

**How to Use**

1. **Select the Shapes tool.**
    - Click Shapes in the toolbar.
2. **Draw a shape.**
    - Choose a base shape (rectangle, ellipse, line, etc.) and draw it on the Canvas.
3. **Adjust appearance.**
    - Set size, corner radius, stroke, and fill.

**Quick Tips**

- Duplicate and align Shapes to build grids, cards, or repeated structures.
- Follow smart guides when aligning and arranging Shapes.

**Related Features**

- [Pencil](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/canvas-objects#fb8e83cbf4ef): Draw custom shapes freehand.
- [Text](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/canvas-objects#da4ec3b9cfcb): Add text boxes to the Canvas.

### Pencil

**Shortcut:** `P`

> Draw freehand strokes on the Canvas.

**What It Does**

Draw freehand vector lines on the Canvas with the Pencil tool.

**How to Use**

1. **Select the Pencil tool.**
    - Click Pencil in the toolbar or press `P`.
2. **Adjust appearance.**
    - Set stroke width, opacity, and color.
3. **Draw anything.**
    - Draw freehand on the Canvas.

**Quick Tips**

- Use Pencil for rough sketches and organic shapes.

**Related Features**

- [Shapes](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/canvas-objects#7917206b9097): Use pre-built geometric shapes alongside freehand drawing.
- [Text](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/canvas-objects#da4ec3b9cfcb): Combine vector artwork with text elements.

### Text

**Shortcut:** `T`

> Add headlines, body copy, and labels as independent layers on the Canvas.

**What It Does**

The Text tool places editable text layers on the Canvas — headlines, body copy, labels, and more. Each layer sits independently on top of your design and supports font selection, sizing, weight, alignment, and hierarchy.

**How to Use**

1. **Select the Text tool.**
    - Click Text in the toolbar, then click the Canvas to create a text layer.
2. **Enter and format your text.**
    - Type or paste your content, then set font, size, weight, and alignment.
3. **Integrate it into your layout.**
    - Combine text with shapes, images, and grids to build clear, readable content.

**Related Features**

- [Edit Text](https://www.lovart.ai/docs/edit-your-design/advanced-ai-editing#b26a423b73fd): Modify text inside AI-generated images.

## Questions Nobody Answers

### What restaurant branding elements can AI design?

Complete restaurant identity system:

**Core Identity:**
- Logo (primary, icon, wordmark, horizontal lockup)
- Color palette inspired by cuisine type and atmosphere
- Typography system (menu fonts, display fonts, signage fonts)

**Menu Design:**
- Food menu (single page, bi-fold, tri-fold, multi-page)
- Drink menu (bar menu, wine list, cocktail menu)
- Daily specials inserts
- Digital menu boards and QR code menus

**Physical Environment:**
- Interior design visualization
- Signage design (exterior, interior, wayfinding)
- Uniform and packaging mockups
- Table settings and presentation styling

**Marketing:**
- Social media templates and content
- Promotional posters and flyers
- Loyalty cards and gift certificates
- Website and online ordering interface mockups

### How does AI handle menu design specifically?

AI menu design is one of the strongest restaurant applications. AI handles:

**Layout and hierarchy:** Sections organized logically (appetizers → mains → desserts), with visual hierarchy guiding the eye to high-margin items.

**Menu psychology:** Placing high-profit items in "sweet spots" (top-right of the menu, first and last items in each section), removing currency symbols, using descriptive language.

**Cuisine-appropriate styling:** A sushi menu looks fundamentally different from a BBQ menu. AI understands cuisine-specific design conventions.

**Dietary iconography:** Gluten-free, vegan, vegetarian, spicy, and allergen indicators with clear, consistent icon design.

**Pricing display:** Clean price alignment, appropriate font sizes, and strategic formatting that doesn't emphasize price.

### Can AI generate appetizing food photography?

Yes — and this is a game-changer for restaurants. Professional food photography costs $50-200 per dish. For a menu with 30 items, that's $1,500-6,000 before you've sold a single plate.

AI food photography generation produces:
- **Realistic dish photos:** Describe the dish in detail and AI generates an appetizing image. Quality is "good enough for menus and social media" — not indistinguishable from professional photography, but dramatically better than no photos or bad phone photos.
- **Consistent style:** All AI-generated food photos share the same lighting, angle, and aesthetic — creating menu cohesion that's hard to achieve even with professional photography.
- **Seasonal variations:** Generate the same dish with different garnishes, plating, and seasonal presentations.

Caveat: AI-generated food photos should be labeled as "illustrative" if they don't accurately represent your actual dish presentation. False advertising of food appearance has legal implications.

### How does AI help with restaurant interior design?

AI interior visualization for restaurants:
- **Style exploration:** Generate your space in 5 different interior design directions (industrial, bohemian, minimalist, classic, themed)
- **Lighting simulation:** Preview how different lighting setups (warm ambient, dramatic spot, natural daylight) transform the space
- **Seating layout:** Optimize table arrangements for capacity, flow, and ambiance
- **Brand integration:** Visualize logo, colors, and brand elements applied to the physical space

Upload photos of your actual space; AI generates the redesigned version while preserving architecture. This is invaluable for renovation planning, investor pitches, and pre-opening marketing.

### What's the best AI approach for restaurant naming and concept development?

AI assists with:
- **Name generation:** 100+ name candidates based on cuisine, concept, and target audience
- **Tagline/slogan:** Catchy, memorable phrases that communicate the restaurant's unique value
- **Concept description:** Investor-ready concept summaries that articulate the vision
- **Menu concept:** Menu structure, signature dish ideas, and pricing strategy suggestions

AI generates ideas. The restaurateur validates against: domain availability, trademark conflicts, cultural appropriateness, pronunciation ease, and memorability. AI accelerates ideation; human judgment makes the final call.

### How do I ensure health code and ADA compliance in AI-generated designs?

AI does NOT understand regulatory requirements. Manual verification is essential for:
- **Menu allergen disclosure:** Legal requirements vary by jurisdiction
- **Health code signage:** Required postings in restrooms and kitchen areas
- **ADA accessibility:** Font sizes for visually impaired, wheelchair accessibility in layouts
- **Alcohol labeling:** Legal requirements for drink menus and promotional materials
- **Calorie disclosure:** Mandatory in chain restaurants, varies by jurisdiction

Use AI for creative design. Use human expertise (or legal counsel) for compliance verification.

### What's the most common AI restaurant branding mistake?

**Designing for your taste, not your customer's expectations.** A fine-dining French restaurant should look like fine-dining French, not like the owner's favorite edgy design blog. AI will generate whatever you prompt it to generate. Prompt for your target customer's expectations, not your personal aesthetic preferences.

Other common fails: AI-generated menus with inaccurate prices (verify every number), food photos that don't match actual portions (label as illustrative), and brand inconsistency across touchpoints (use Brand Lock to enforce consistency).

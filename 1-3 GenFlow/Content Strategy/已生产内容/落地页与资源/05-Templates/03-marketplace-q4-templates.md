## The Q4 2027 Holiday Design Trends

Before diving into the templates, here are the design trends shaping holiday visuals this year:

**Halloween 2027:** Moving beyond orange-and-black clichés toward "elevated spooky" — deep purples, muted oranges, vintage gothic typography, and sophisticated horror references. Less cartoon ghosts, more editorial darkness.

**Thanksgiving 2027:** The maximalist "gratitude + gravy" aesthetic is giving way to warm minimalism. Clean tablescapes, intentional negative space, focus on food as art. The message is abundance through curation, not excess.

**Christmas 2027:** Two parallel trends: (1) "Quiet Luxury Christmas" — champagne, cream, gold, minimalist decor, sophisticated restraint; and (2) "Maximalist Nostalgia" — vintage ornaments, colorful lights, childhood-memory aesthetic, unabashedly festive. Our collection includes templates for both lanes.

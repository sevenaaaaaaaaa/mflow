## Scenario 2: By-the-Glass Menu Redesign

Your by-the-glass list rotates every 4-6 weeks. Ten whites, ten reds, two rosés, three sparkling. Each needs varietal, region, producer, vintage, and a one-line tasting note — all fitting on a single elegant page that patrons can read in candlelight. Lovart's AI understands menu psychology: high-margin pours get prime placement, sparkling anchors the top-left, and descriptive notes ("Brambly blackberry, cracked pepper, worn leather") sell more wine than technical specs.

The **Brand Kit** stores your wine bar's identity — perhaps a deep aubergine and gold palette with an engraved-style logo — and every menu refresh inherits that DNA automatically.

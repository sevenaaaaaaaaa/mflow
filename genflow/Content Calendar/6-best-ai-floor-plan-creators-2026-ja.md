## AI Floor Plan Generators All Claim to Understand Architecture. Most Understand Rectangles and Nothing Else.

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Type "3-bedroom floor plan with open kitchen" into any AI floor plan tool and you'll get something that looks like a floor plan — rooms, doors, windows, maybe some furniture blocks. What you won't get: a plan that respects structural reality. Load-bearing walls in impossible locations. Bathrooms that don't connect to plumbing. Doorways that open into staircase voids. Dimensions that don't add up to the total square footage listed.

Real floor plan design is a constraint satisfaction problem: structural requirements (load paths, plumbing stacks, HVAC runs), code requirements (minimum room dimensions, egress paths, accessibility standards), and livability requirements (room adjacencies, natural light, traffic flow) all constrain the design space. AI that generates floor plans by pixel pattern alone violates all of these. The tools that work use AI for what AI does well — generating design options, rendering styles, and automating repetitive drafting — while respecting the constraints that make a floor plan buildable.

---

## The Spec Sheet Lie: "AI-Generated Floor Plans" — By What Standard?

Floor plan tools generate plans in two fundamentally different ways that marketing materials rarely distinguish:

1. **Pixel-based generation.** The AI "draws" a floor plan image — pixels that look like walls and rooms. These plans are visually plausible but dimensionally meaningless. Measure the distance between two walls on the image and it won't match any real-world measurement.

2. **Vector/parametric generation.** The AI creates a data model of the floor plan — walls with length attributes, rooms with area calculations, doors with swing arcs. These plans are dimensionally accurate and can be exported to CAD/BIM software.

The first category is for visualization. The second is for actual construction. Know which you're getting.

### 1. Planner 5D — Best for Interior-Focused Floor Plans

Planner 5D combines AI floor plan generation with a rich interior design platform. Its AI creates floor plans from text descriptions or uploaded sketches, then the platform enables full furnishing, 3D visualization, and rendering.

**What it does well:** Interior design integration — the floor plan is the starting point for a complete room-by-room design with furniture, materials, and 3D walkthroughs. AI recognition of hand-drawn sketches works well for rough layouts. Furniture library is extensive with real product models. The 3D rendering produces convincing visualizations for client presentations. Good for residential interior planning.

**Where it falls short:** Floor plan generation is interior-focused — structural elements (foundations, roof, framing) are ignored. No architectural drawing export for construction. The AI can produce dimensionally inconsistent layouts. Subscription required for full features. Not for architects producing construction documents.

**Key takeaway:** Best for interior designers and homeowners planning room layouts and furnishings. Not for architectural construction documents.

## FAQ

### Can AI generate a floor plan that's ready for construction?

No. Construction-ready floor plans require structural engineering, code compliance verification, MEP coordination, and professional architectural stamping — none of which current AI tools provide. AI can generate conceptual floor plans that accelerate the design process, but every AI-generated plan needs review, refinement, and documentation by a licensed professional before construction.

### What's the difference between a floor plan drawing and a floor plan visualization?

A drawing (CAD/BIM) contains precise dimensional data — every wall has a measured length, every room has a calculated area, every door has a specified width. A visualization looks like a floor plan but is a rendered image — it conveys the design idea visually but contains no dimensional data. AI tools for drawing generate data; AI tools for visualization generate images. Most AI "floor plan generators" are visualizers, not drafting tools.

### Can AI convert a hand-drawn sketch into a digital floor plan?

Yes, with varying quality. Planner 5D and RoomSketcher can interpret hand-drawn sketches and convert them to digital plans. The sketch needs to be reasonably clear — straight-ish lines, labeled rooms, distinguishable wall thickness. The AI will clean up the geometry and suggest improvements. For very rough sketches ("a circle for the living room and squares for bedrooms"), expect significant AI reinterpretation.

### Do AI floor plan tools understand building codes?

No. Building codes are jurisdiction-specific, frequently updated, and require professional interpretation. AI floor plan tools operate on visual pattern matching, not code compliance logic. A generated plan might show a bedroom without a window (violating egress requirements) or a bathroom that's too small to meet accessibility standards. Always have a professional review AI-generated plans for code compliance.

### Can I export an AI-generated floor plan to AutoCAD or Revit?

Depends on the tool. RoomSketcher offers DWG export. Floorplanner exports as image files (not CAD). Planner 5D exports as images or its own format. Maket offers some CAD export. Check the specific tool's export options — "export" often means image export, not CAD data export. For BIM workflows, look for tools that specifically offer DWG, DXF, or IFC export.

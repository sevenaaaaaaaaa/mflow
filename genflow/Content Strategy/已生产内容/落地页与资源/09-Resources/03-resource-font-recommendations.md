# Design Resource: Font Recommendations by Industry — The Definitive 2027 Guide

Typography is the most underestimated lever in brand design. A Monotype study found that **78% of consumers** form an opinion about a brand's credibility based on its typography alone — before reading a single word of copy. Yet font selection remains largely intuitive rather than strategic for most teams.

This guide provides **industry-specific font recommendations** backed by performance data from over 10,000 Lovart-generated designs. For each industry, we recommend a headline font, a body font, and an optional accent font — along with the reasoning behind each choice.

---

## 1. Technology & SaaS

Tech brands need to balance innovation with approachability. The trend in 2027 favors geometric sans-serifs that feel precise but not sterile.

### Primary Recommendation

**Headline: Inter Display Bold**
Why: Inter is the most widely adopted typeface in SaaS interfaces for good reason. Its tall x-height and open apertures make it exceptionally legible at both large display sizes and small UI labels. The Display variant adds refined optical adjustments for headline use.

**Body: Inter Regular**
Why: Using the same family for body and headline creates visual cohesion. Inter's body text rendering has been optimized by Rasmus Andersson specifically for screen reading.

### Alternative Pairing

**Headline: Switzer Bold** | **Body: IBM Plex Sans**
Switzer brings a slightly warmer, more humanist quality compared to Inter's geometric precision. IBM Plex Sans adds a touch of personality without sacrificing professionalism.

### Accent Font: JetBrains Mono
For code snippets, terminal output, and technical specifications — the monospaced companion that resonates with developer audiences.

**Lovart Prompt:** `typography: geometric sans-serif, Inter Display headlines, clean and modern, tech-forward but approachable`

## 2. Fashion & Luxury

Fashion typography in 2027 is trending toward elegant contrast — pairing high-fashion serifs with minimal sans-serifs to create tension between heritage and modernity.

### Primary Recommendation

**Headline: Cormorant Garamond Bold**
Why: High-contrast serifs with dramatic thins evoke the editorial sophistication of fashion magazines. Cormorant Garamond's distinctive character — especially in its italic cuts — signals luxury without feeling dated.

**Body: Jost Light (300 weight)**
Why: The light weight of Jost creates the delicate, airy spacing that luxury brands use to convey exclusivity. Ample letter-spacing (tracking: +50) enhances this effect.

### Alternative Pairing

**Headline: Bodoni Moda Bold** | **Body: Lato Light**
Bodoni's extreme stroke contrast is the quintessential fashion typeface. Pair with Lato Light for a softer sans-serif counterpoint.

### Accent Font: Cormorant Garamond Italic Display
For pull quotes, testimonials, and editorial features — the italic cut brings dramatic flair.

**Lovart Prompt:** `typography: luxury fashion, high-contrast serif headlines, light sans-serif body, editorial magazine aesthetic, generous whitespace`

## 3. Finance & Banking

Financial typography must communicate trust, stability, and competence. The 2027 trend shifts from the cold, purely geometric sans-serifs of the 2010s toward humanist sans-serifs that add warmth to institutional authority.

### Primary Recommendation

**Headline: DM Serif Display**
Why: Serif headlines immediately signal tradition and reliability — traits consumers seek from financial institutions. DM Serif Display's moderate contrast makes it more approachable than Didot or Bodoni, while still carrying institutional weight.

**Body: DM Sans Regular**
Why: DM Sans is the sans-serif companion to DM Serif Display, designed in the same proportional system. This creates a seamless pairing where the fonts share DNA but play different roles.

### Alternative Pairing

**Headline: Newsreader Bold** | **Body: Source Sans 3**
Newsreader's newspaper heritage evokes old-guard credibility. Source Sans 3 provides excellent screen readability for dense financial content.

### Accent Font: IBM Plex Mono
For numerical data, rate tables, and financial figures — monospaced alignment aids comprehension.

**Lovart Prompt:** `typography: financial trust, serif headlines for authority, clean sans-serif for data, institutional credibility`

## 4. Healthcare & Wellness

Healthcare typography demands accessibility above all else, with warmth as a close second. Patients across age groups and abilities must read and understand critical information.

### Primary Recommendation

**Headline: Fraunces Bold**
Why: Fraunces brings soft, organic curves to the headline role — conveying care and humanity rather than clinical coldness. Its variable font axis lets you dial warmth up or down.

**Body: Atkinson Hyperlegible**
Why: Developed by the Braille Institute, Atkinson Hyperlegible was designed specifically for low-vision readers. Its exaggerated letterform differentiation (e.g., the distinct shapes of lowercase 'i', 'l', and '1') dramatically reduces reading errors. This is the most accessible font recommendation in our entire guide.

### Alternative Pairing

**Headline: Lexend Deca** | **Body: Lexend Peta**
Lexend fonts are variable and were developed specifically to improve reading speed and fluency. They're ideal for health information that must be consumed quickly.

**Lovart Prompt:** `typography: accessible healthcare, warm and human, hyper-legible body text, inclusive design, clear hierarchy`

## 5. E-Commerce & DTC

E-commerce typography must perform at small mobile sizes on product cards while commanding attention in hero banners. The 2027 trend favors compact, space-efficient typefaces.

### Primary Recommendation

**Headline: Outfit Bold**
Why: Outfit's compact letterforms allow more characters per line — crucial for product names and benefit headlines that must fit in mobile viewports. Its rounded terminals add approachability to purchase decisions.

**Body: Nunito Sans Regular**
Why: Rounded geometric letterforms create a friendly browsing experience. Nunito Sans's generous x-height ensures product descriptions remain legible at small sizes.

### Alternative Pairing

**Headline: Bebas Neue** | **Body: Manrope**
Bebas Neue's condensed all-caps styling is iconic for sale announcements and category headers. Manrope's modern geometric build complements its impact.

### Accent Font: Inter Tight
For pricing tables, discount badges, and product specs — Inter Tight's condensed proportions maximize information density.

**Lovart Prompt:** `typography: e-commerce friendly, rounded and approachable, compact headlines for mobile, clear product hierarchy`

## 6. Education & EdTech

Educational typography walks a line between authoritative and encouraging. The goal is to make learning feel accessible, not intimidating.

### Primary Recommendation

**Headline: Fredoka Bold**
Why: Fredoka's rounded, friendly letterforms reduce the intimidation factor of educational content. Its generous spacing makes headlines feel open and encouraging — especially important for K-12 audiences.

**Body: Andika Regular**
Why: Andika was designed for literacy use by SIL International. Its clear, unambiguous letterforms help early and struggling readers distinguish similar characters.

### Alternative Pairing

**Headline: Young Serif** | **Body: Charis SIL**
For higher education and academic content, Young Serif provides a scholarly yet warm headline voice. Charis SIL's familiarity to book readers enhances sustained reading comfort.

**Lovart Prompt:** `typography: educational friendly, rounded and warm, highly legible for all ages, encouraging and accessible`

## 7. Food & Beverage

F&B typography is having a rustic-meets-modern moment. Hand-drawn qualities, organic curves, and artisanal character dominate the space.

### Primary Recommendation

**Headline: Playfair Display Bold**
Why: Playfair Display balances classic serif elegance with a warmth that suits both fine dining and casual food brands. Its distinctive 'f' and 'y' terminals add signature character.

**Body: Karla Regular**
Why: Karla's slightly unconventional letterforms (note the distinctive lowercase 'k' and 'g') add personality without sacrificing readability for menu descriptions and ingredient lists.

### Alternative Pairing

**Headline: Amatic SC Bold** | **Body: Lora**
Amatic SC brings hand-lettered charm for farm-to-table, craft, and artisanal brands. Lora's calligraphic roots maintain the organic feel in body text.

**Lovart Prompt:** `typography: food and beverage, artisanal warmth, distinctive character, organic and handcrafted feel`

## 8. Real Estate

Real estate typography must project professionalism while differentiating in a crowded market. The 2027 trend splits between luxury and approachable lanes.

### Primary Recommendation

**Headline: Montserrat SemiBold**
Why: Montserrat's geometric clarity and wide letterforms project confidence and transparency — exactly what buyers and sellers seek in an agent. It reads well on yard signs, listing cards, and digital ads alike.

**Body: Open Sans Regular**
Why: A reliable workhorse that handles dense property descriptions, legal disclaimers, and comparative market analyses with consistent legibility.

### Alternative Pairing

**Headline: Yeseva One** | **Body: Nunito**
For luxury real estate, Yeseva One's elegant serif brings a premium, curated feel. The contrast with Nunito's rounded sans-serif creates a sophisticated tension.

**Lovart Prompt:** `typography: real estate professional, geometric and trustworthy, clear and spacious, premium but accessible`

## 10. Media & Publishing

Publishing typography must support extended reading sessions while establishing editorial authority. The 2027 trend elevates typefaces with literary heritage.

### Primary Recommendation

**Headline: Newsreader Bold**
Why: Designed specifically for long-form reading in digital publications, Newsreader revives the newspaper type tradition for modern screens. Its optical sizes ensure appropriate contrast at every scale.

**Body: Source Serif 4 Regular**
Why: Adobe's open-source serif offers excellent readability at small sizes and long passages. Its moderate contrast prevents eye strain during extended reading.

### Alternative Pairing

**Headline: Libre Baskerville Bold** | **Body: Literata Regular**
Libre Baskerville brings classic book typography to the web. Literata was Google's custom commission for Play Books, optimized specifically for long-form digital reading.

**Lovart Prompt:** `typography: editorial publishing, literary serif, optimized for extended reading, authoritative and comfortable`

**Body: Public Sans Regular**
Why: Consistency across headlines and body reinforces the unified, transparent message that donors and supporters expect from impact organizations.

### Alternative Pairing

**Headline: Merriweather Bold** | **Body: Lato**
Merriweather's warmth suits community-focused messaging. Lato's semi-rounded details add humanity to statistics and impact reports.

## The Font Stack Decision Tree

Still unsure? Use this decision framework:

1. **Does your brand prioritize trust?** → Serif headlines + humanist sans-serif body
2. **Does your brand prioritize innovation?** → Geometric sans-serif throughout
3. **Does your brand prioritize accessibility?** → Hyper-legible fonts (Atkinson, Lexend)
4. **Does your brand target luxury buyers?** → High-contrast display serif + light sans-serif
5. **Does your brand need personality?** → Distinctive display font + neutral body companion

When in doubt, start with the **Inter family** (Inter Display for headlines, Inter for body). It's the safest, most versatile foundation — and the default in Lovart's Free plan.

# Seedance Video Production Workflow — A Real 30-Video Campaign Case Study

In February 2026, a DTC skincare brand faced a content crisis. Their spring product launch needed 30 videos — product demos, ingredient spotlights, founder stories, customer testimonials, and platform-specific social cuts — across 4 platforms in 2 languages. Their existing video production pipeline (agency + freelancers) quoted $68,000 and a 7-week timeline. The launch was in 3 weeks.

They used Lovart's Seedance video workflow instead. They shipped all 30 videos in 8 days. Total cost: $1,196 ($149 Enterprise plan + $1,047 in stock footage and music licensing). Here's exactly how they did it — and what the campaign produced.

## 2. The Traditional Timeline (Why They Couldn't Use It)

Their agency's 7-week timeline broke down as:
- Week 1–2: Creative brief, concept development, storyboarding (agency)
- Week 3: Client review and revision (1 round)
- Week 4–5: Production — filming, voiceover recording, asset gathering
- Week 6: Post-production — editing, color grading, sound design, motion graphics
- Week 7: Final review, revisions, platform formatting, delivery

This timeline was industry-standard. It was also impossible given the 3-week launch window.

## 3. The Seedance Workflow

### Day 1–2: Creative Direction and Asset Preparation (Human)

The brand's creative director defined the campaign visual direction. The team gathered:
- Product photography (white background, lifestyle, ingredient close-ups)
- Brand video footage (existing B-roll from previous shoots)
- Licensed stock footage (lifestyle, nature, texture clips from Artgrid)
- Music tracks (licensed from Epidemic Sound)
- Voiceover scripts (written by the brand's copywriter)
- Text-to-speech voice selection (chosen from Lovart's TTS library)

### Day 3–4: Video Generation (Seedance)

For each video type, the team used a specific Seedance approach:

**Product Demos (6 videos, 30 sec each)**
Prompt: *"30-second product demonstration video. Open with product hero shot on white background, brand name animation top-center. Cut to ingredient close-up macro shots with text overlays: 'Hyaluronic Acid', 'Vitamin C', 'Ceramides.' Transition to application footage. Close on product with 'Shop Now' CTA. Modern clean aesthetic. Brand colors: sage green #87A96B, cream #FFFDD0. Background: minimal, light. Typography: clean sans-serif, on-screen for 3–4 seconds per ingredient. End card with logo and website URL. 9:16 vertical format."*

Seedance interpreted the prompt, assembled the visual sequence, applied text animations, color-graded to brand palette, synced to the selected music track, and rendered the output. Generation time: 3–5 minutes per video.

**Ingredient Explainers (8 videos, 15–20 sec each)**
Prompt: *"15-second ingredient spotlight video. Ingredient name animation top-center: 'Squalane.' Split screen: left side shows ingredient source (olive photography), right side shows benefit text: 'Deep Hydration.' Transition to product with ingredient callout overlay. End on brand tagline: 'Science-Backed Skincare.' 9:16 format. Educational but sleek."*

These 8 videos would have required 8 separate motion graphics builds in After Effects. Seedance generated all 8 in under 45 minutes total.

**Social Cuts (12 videos, 15–30 sec each)**
These were multi-platform variants of the main videos — recut for TikTok vertical, Instagram square, YouTube landscape, and website horizontal. Auto-Resize handled aspect ratio conversion. Seedance handled recutting — trimming to platform-optimal lengths, adding platform-specific text treatments, adjusting music pacing.

### Day 5–6: Human Review and Refinement

The creative team reviewed all 30 videos:
- Checked product color accuracy (corrected 3 videos where grading was slightly off)
- Verified text accuracy on all overlays
- Checked music sync and adjusted timing on 4 videos
- Confirmed platform-specific formatting
- Reviewed Spanish translations (external translator verified, 2 corrections made)

### Day 7: Client Review and Final Delivery

Internal team approved all 30 videos. Final exports rendered. Platform-specific files organized and delivered.

### Day 8: Buffer Day

Used for 1 unexpected revision (founder wanted a different opening shot for her story video — regenerated and replaced in 20 minutes).

## 6. What Seedance Does Well

- **Motion graphics assembly:** Turning a prompt into a composed, animated, color-graded video sequence
- **Text animation:** Kinetic typography that would take hours in After Effects rendered in seconds
- **Multi-format output:** Platform-optimized variants from a single creative direction
- **Music synchronization:** Matching visual pacing to music tempo and structure
- **Batch production:** 30 videos from a defined creative direction in days, not weeks

---

## 7. What Seedance Doesn't Do

- **Original cinematography:** You still need cameras, lighting, and someone to operate them. Seedance edits and enhances footage — it doesn't capture it.
- **Complex narrative editing:** Documentary-style storytelling with interview clips, B-roll coverage, and narrative arc construction still benefits from human editing.
- **Lip-sync dubbing:** Seedance does not generate talking-head avatars or lip-sync dubbing. For presenter-driven content, supplement with Synthesia or HeyGen.
- **Custom 3D animation:** Complex 3D product renders, character animation, and VFX compositing require specialized tools.
- **Client-relationship management:** The creative brief, revision conversations, and approval process remain human-led.

---

## 8. When Seedance Replaces an Agency (and When It Doesn't)

**Seedance replaces an agency when:**
- Your video content is design-driven (motion graphics, text animation, product showcases, social cuts)
- Your timeline is compressed (days/weeks, not months)
- Your budget is limited (hundreds/thousands, not tens of thousands)
- You have in-house creative direction capability
- Your content volume is high (20+ videos per campaign)

**You still need an agency when:**
- Your content requires original cinematography with professional crews
- Your campaign needs celebrity/influencer talent coordination
- Your narrative requires documentary-level editing craft
- Your brand platform is being defined (the first campaign that establishes visual identity)
- Compliance and legal review require specialized oversight (pharma, finance, regulated industries)

## FAQ

### Do I need video editing experience to use Seedance?
No. Seedance works through text prompts — same as ChatCanvas. Describe the video sequence you want, and Seedance assembles it. The learning curve is prompt articulation, not video editing tool operation. Video editing experience helps with refinement but isn't required for generation.

### Can I use my own footage in Seedance videos?
Yes. Upload your footage as reference clips. Seedance will incorporate them into the generated video sequence — applying color grading, adding text overlays, and synchronizing with music.

### How does Seedance handle music licensing?
Seedance includes a library of licensed music tracks for commercial use (included in your Lovart plan). You can also upload your own licensed music. Seedance does not provide unlicensed music — all tracks are cleared for commercial distribution.

### What video formats does Seedance output?
MP4 (H.264 and H.265), MOV (ProRes on Studio and above), and GIF. Resolution up to 4K on Agency and Enterprise plans; 1080p on Pro and Studio.

### How does Seedance compare to Runway or Pika?
Runway specializes in AI video generation and editing (text-to-video, image-to-video, video inpainting). Pika specializes in short-form AI video clips with stylistic effects. Seedance specializes in design-driven video production — assembling footage, text, motion graphics, and music into platform-ready marketing videos. The tools serve different stages of video production.

### Can Seedance handle multi-language video production?
Yes. Generate the base video in your primary language. Regenerate with translated text overlays and TTS voiceover in the target language. The visual framework stays consistent; the language layer updates. Professional translation review of text content is recommended.

### What about video SEO and metadata?
Seedance generates video files. SEO metadata (titles, descriptions, tags, captions files) must be added through your publishing platform. Lovart does not currently auto-generate video metadata, though TTS output can export as .SRT caption files.

### Is there a limit to how many Seedance videos I can generate?
Pro plan includes 20 Seedance generations per month. Studio includes 50. Agency includes 150. Enterprise is unlimited. Generation counts reset monthly. Video length and complexity do not affect generation count — each generation is one video, regardless of duration.

Lash studios own the ultra-feminine luxury space. Think champagne wishes and caviar dreams — but make it modern.

## 5 Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Should my brand match the lash style I specialize in?**
Yes. If you're known for natural classics, your brand should be soft and understated. If you do dramatic Russian volume, bolder branding fits. Your brand is a preview of your aesthetic eye.

**Q2: What's the most important branded touchpoint?**
The mirror reveal. Place a subtle branded decal on your studio mirrors — it's the first thing clients see when they open their eyes. Lovart generates mirror decal templates perfectly sized for lash station mirrors.

**Q3: How do I brand my lash style menu?**
Use consistent face illustrations showing different lash maps. Color-code by style: Classic = soft pink, Hybrid = rose gold, Volume = deep rose, Mega Volume = plum. Lovart creates illustrated lash map templates in your brand colors.

**Q4: What aftercare materials should I brand?**
Lash shampoo bottles, spoolie brushes, aftercare cards, and satin eye masks. Each is a daily touchpoint. Lovart's product template pack covers all standard lash aftercare items.

**Q5: How do I make my booking page feel luxurious?**
Match it exactly to your brand kit. When clients move from Instagram to your booking flow, the experience should feel seamless. Inconsistent design = friction = abandoned bookings.

**Q6: What social media templates do lash artists need most?**
"Fresh Set" reveal template (before/after split), "Lash Mapping" educational carousel, "Client Love" review highlight, and "Available Appointments" story template. Lovart's lash studio social pack includes all four.

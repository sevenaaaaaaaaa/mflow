## Step 1: Launch a New Project with the YouTube Thumbnail Preset

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Open Lovart's ChatCanvas. In the prompt bar, type: **"YouTube thumbnail for a tech review channel, 1280x720, bold sans-serif title area on the left, subject portrait cutout space on the right, dark gradient background with neon accent lines."** Lovart generates a 1280×720 canvas pre-configured with the correct aspect ratio — no manual dimension entry, no DPI confusion. The AI positions a text-safe zone and a focal-point grid overlay automatically. Select the generated layout closest to your vision or iterate with **"make the accent color electric blue instead of purple."**

## Step 2: Import and Cut Out Your Subject

Upload a photo of yourself or your video subject directly onto the canvas. Use Lovart's **Touch Edit** to tap the subject and select **Remove Background.** The AI edge-detection isolates your figure in under three seconds — hair strands, uneven lighting, and all. Drag the cutout into the designated portrait zone from Step 1. If the edges look harsh, tap **Feather Edge** and set it to 3px. Touch Edit lets you resize with pinch gestures and reposition with drag; no pen tool, no layer masks, no frustration.

## Step 3: Add the Title Text Overlay

Double-tap the text-safe zone on the left. Type your video title — keep it to 4–6 words maximum; thumbnail text that exceeds six words loses legibility at mobile size. Lovart's **Brand Kit** pulls your channel's typeface automatically (set it once in Settings → Brand Assets). If you haven't set one yet, Lovart defaults to Inter Bold at 72pt, which renders cleanly at all device breakpoints. Apply a subtle text shadow — black at 40% opacity, offset 2px right and 2px down — to make white text pop against any background.

## Step 4: Apply Color Grading and Effects

Tap the background layer and select **AI Enhance.** Lovart analyzes the color histogram of your subject photo and suggests a complementary background treatment. For a tech channel, it might push a cool cyan-to-deep-blue radial gradient with a subtle grid overlay. Accept the suggestion or refine with natural-language commands: **"increase contrast by 15%, add a subtle vignette, and desaturate the background by 10% to make the subject stand out."** Every effect renders non-destructively — meaning you can undo individual adjustments without starting over.

## Step 5: Export and A/B Test Variants

[IMAGE 3 PLACEHOLDER — Real UI Screenshot]

Tap **Export** and select **PNG (recommended for YouTube).** Before finalizing, use the **Variant Generator** to produce two additional versions — one with the subject on the left and text on the right, and one with a different accent color. Download all three. Upload them to YouTube's thumbnail A/B testing tool (available in YouTube Studio for eligible channels). Track CTR over 7 days and keep the winner. Lovart automatically saves your project layers so you can return and tweak for your next video in 90 seconds.

**Q: How do I make sure my thumbnail text is readable on mobile?**
A: Lovart includes a mobile preview toggle in the top toolbar. Tap it to simulate a 100×75px rendering (the approximate size of a thumbnail on a 6.1-inch phone screen). If you can read the text in that view, it works.

**Q: What font should I use for gaming channel thumbnails?**

**Q: Does Lovart support non-English text for thumbnails?**

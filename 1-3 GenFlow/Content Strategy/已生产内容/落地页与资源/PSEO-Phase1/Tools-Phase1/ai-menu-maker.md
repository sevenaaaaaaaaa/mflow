## Menu Types Lovart Generates

### Fine Dining Menu

Elegant, restrained, typography-forward. Fine dining menus communicate sophistication through simplicity — generous white space, considered type pairing, subtle color palettes. Lovart generates menus that match the dining experience: high-end, curated, intentional.

```
@menu type:fine-dining restaurant: "The Ember Room" cuisine: modern American courses: starters, mains, desserts, wine style: elegant, dark, gold accents
```

### Casual Dining and Bistro Menu

Warm, inviting, personality-driven. Casual dining menus balance readability with character — clean organization with touches of personality through illustration accents, warm color palettes, and approachable typography.

```
@menu type:casual restaurant: "Harper's Bistro" cuisine: comfort food courses: breakfast, lunch, dinner, drinks style: warm, rustic, chalkboard-inspired
```

### Cafe and Coffee Shop Menu

Quick-scan, item-focused, atmosphere-rich. Cafe menus need to communicate a lot of options fast — coffee varieties, milk options, sizes, prices — while maintaining the cafe's aesthetic. Lovart generates cafe menus optimized for readability above the counter.

```
@menu type:cafe cafe: "Daylight Roasters" offerings: espresso, pour-over, cold brew, pastries, sandwiches style: minimalist, warm, Scandinavian
```

### Fast-Casual and Digital Menu Board

Bold, visual, optimized for screens. Digital menu boards in QSR and fast-casual environments need high-contrast designs visible from across the room, with food imagery that drives impulse ordering.

```
@menu type:fast-casual restaurant: "Green Bowl Co" cuisine: build-your-own bowls sections: bases, proteins, toppings, sauces, drinks style: bold, fresh, colorful
```

### Bar and Cocktail Menu

Moody, atmospheric, experience-driven. Cocktail menus sell an experience as much as a drink. Lovart generates bar menus with dramatic lighting, inventive drink names, and tasting-note descriptions — the kind of menu people photograph and share.

```
@menu type:bar bar: "The Lowlight" offerings: signature cocktails, wine, beer, bar snacks style: speakeasy, moody, Art Deco
```

### QR Code and Mobile Menu

Optimized for phone screens. With QR code ordering now standard, Lovart generates mobile-first menu designs — single-column layouts, finger-friendly tap targets, optimized image sizes for fast loading.

## Menu Design Principles Lovart Encodes

You do not need to know these. But Lovart does.

**The Golden Triangle:** Eye-tracking studies show that customers scan menus in a pattern — center, then top-right, then top-left. Lovart places your highest-margin items along this path.

**Dollar sign omission:** Research consistently shows that menus without dollar signs next to prices generate higher average check sizes. Lovart formats prices as clean numbers by default (18) rather than currency-marked ($18.00). You can override this in settings.

**Section anchoring:** The first and last items in each section get disproportionate attention. Lovart structures sections with anchor items at these positions — your signatures at the top, your profitable standards at the bottom.

**Visual weight balance:** Lovart distributes food imagery evenly across the menu layout. No image-heavy section next to an image-barren section. Visual rhythm matters.

## FAQ

**Q: Can I upload my own food photos instead of using AI-generated ones?**
A: Yes. Upload any photo to ChatCanvas and Lovart places it into the menu layout with professional framing and color matching to the overall design.

**Q: How do the AI-generated food images compare to real photography?**
A: For menu use — especially printed at typical menu sizes — Lovart's food-styling AI produces images that are effectively indistinguishable from photographed dishes. The AI understands food plating, lighting, and styling conventions. For marketing photography (full-page magazine ads), professional photography may still be preferred for complex, multi-element dishes.

**Q: Can I print the menu at a professional print shop?**
A: Yes. Export as PDF with CMYK color profile, 300 DPI, and bleed marks. Any professional printer can work with these files.

**Q: What if my menu is very long — 100+ items?**
A: Lovart handles multi-page menus naturally. Generate the full menu; Lovart paginates it with consistent headers, footers, and section continuation marks.

**Q: Can I create a digital menu that customers view on their phones via QR code?**
A: Yes. Export the mobile-optimized version, host it on your website or a menu hosting service, and link your QR code to it. The menu is designed for phone-screen readability out of the box.

**Q: Does Lovart support dietary and allergen labeling?**
A: Yes. Include dietary markers in your dish descriptions — "(V)" for vegetarian, "(GF)" for gluten-free — or enable the automatic icon system that places standardized dietary icons next to applicable dishes. Icons are generated in your menu's visual style.

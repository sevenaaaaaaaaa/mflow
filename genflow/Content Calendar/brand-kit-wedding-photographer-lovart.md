## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Wedding photographer branding must feel editorial, timeless, and emotionally resonant. The palette should complement your photography—never compete with it. Your brand frames your work, so the frame must be impeccable.

- **Primary**: `#D4AF37` — Elegant champagne gold, the color of wedding bands and sunset receptions
- **Secondary**: `#FEF9F3` — Warm alabaster for website backgrounds and pricing guides
- **Accent**: `#8B7731` — Antique gold for typography, watermarks, and premium cues
- **Shadow**: `#4A4A4A` — Charcoal for body text; less harsh than pure black on light wedding imagery
- **Highlight**: `#D4C5B9` — Dusty rose-bronze for "Investment" CTAs and inquiry buttons

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create a brand that doesn't overpower my photography style?**
Lovart's "photographer-first" mode analyzes your portfolio's dominant tones, contrast levels, and emotional register, then suggests a palette that complements your work. Dark-and-moody photographer? Deep tones, minimal gold. Light-and-airy? Soft pastels, generous whitespace.

**Q2: How do I create a pricing guide that actually converts?**
Lovart's pricing-guide module uses conversion-optimized layout patterns: lead with the middle-tier package (anchoring), show value-per-item rather than total price first, and include an "Inquire" button after every collection. The AI A/B tests layouts for you.

**Q3: Can Lovart generate my client-facing contracts or questionnaires?**
While Lovart doesn't provide legal documents, it generates beautifully formatted templates for your existing contract PDF and creates branded questionnaires (couple's story, shot list, family-formal groupings, vendor contacts). Pairs with HoneyBook and Dubsado.

**Q4: Does Lovart support video content for wedding filmmakers?**
Yes. Lovart generates title-card templates, lower-thirds, and end-cards for wedding films. Describe your video style, and the AI produces a motion-graphics package with logo sting, chapter titles, and credits. Export as .mogrt for Premiere Pro or .fcpxml for Final Cut.

**Q5: What tier do established photographers with associates need?**

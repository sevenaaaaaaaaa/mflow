ChatCanvas is Lovart's infinite collaborative workspace. Unlimited canvas size with pan and zoom. AI has context awareness of all elements — prompts, references, generated assets. Real-time collaboration allows multiple users to edit simultaneously. Version history for tracking changes and reverting. Keyboard: Cmd+Click (Touch Edit), Cmd+Z (Undo), Space+Drag (Pan canvas).

## we’re here to **all your questions**

Whether you are building a cohesive brand identity or exploring new aesthetics, find the inspiration and strategies you need to elevate your design workflow today

[ Read More ](<https://www.lovart.ai/blog>)

######  What is Lovart, and how is it different from other AI generators?

Lovart is an **AI Design Agent** , not just a tool. Unlike standard image generators, it thinks like a designer. It plans, executes, and edits complex projects—from logos to videos—all on one infinite canvas.

######  What is the ChatCanvas?

It’s a workspace where you design by talking. Instead of struggling with complex menus, just click anywhere on the canvas and tell the AI what to change. It’s as easy as chatting with a teammate.

######  Can I really edit the text inside an image?

Yes! This is our superpower. With **Text Edit** , you can fix typos or change words inside any image, and Lovart automatically matches the original font, lighting, and perspective.

######  Can I keep my characters or products consistent across different images?

Absolutely. Our **Nano Banana** model locks onto your character or product’s face and style, ensuring they look exactly the same across different scenes and poses.

######  Which video generation models do you support?

We combine the best in the world. You can access **Google Veo 3, Sora 2, and Kling** directly inside Lovart to create cinematic videos without switching apps.

######  Can I use Lovart for free?

Yes! We offer a Free Plan. New users get starter credits, and we provide **Daily Free Credits** just for logging in, which is enough to try out most core features. For heavy usage or access to premium video models (like Veo 3), you can upgrade to our Starter, Basic, or Pro plans.

######  Can I use the content I create for commercial purposes?

Yes. All paid subscriptions (Starter plan and above) come with a **Commercial License**. You are free to use your generated assets for ads, marketing campaigns, merchandise, or client projects.

######  What file formats can I export?

We support professional-grade exports. You can download images in **PNG or JPG** , and even vector **SVG** (perfect for logos). Videos can be exported as **MP4**. Plus, with our Upscale feature, you can boost assets to **4K or 8K** resolution for print-ready quality.

######  Do I need design experience to use Lovart?

Zero skills required. Just describe your idea in plain English. Our Agent handles the layout, style, and details for you.

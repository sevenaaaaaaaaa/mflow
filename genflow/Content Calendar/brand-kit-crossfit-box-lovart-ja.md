## Font Recommendations

- **Primary Logo:** Anton — heavy condensed sans-serif, pure power
- **Headlines:** Bebas Neue — bold condensed for WOD boards and event posters
- **Body Text:** Roboto — clean, readable for workout programming and waivers
- **Accent:** Permanent Marker — whiteboard-style for daily WOD announcements
- **Digital:** Montserrat — versatile for social media and website

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Should my CrossFit box look tough or welcoming?**
Both. The brand attracts with community and retains with results. Use dark, powerful colors for external marketing (outdoor signage, Instagram) and warmer tones for internal materials (member communications, welcome kits).

**Q2: What's the most important branded item in a box?**
The WOD board — it's photographed and shared on social media by members daily. A well-designed WOD template becomes free marketing. Lovart generates WOD board templates in your brand style.

**Q3: How do I balance CrossFit affiliate branding with my own box brand?**
CrossFit HQ requires specific affiliate branding. Place their mark in designated locations and build your visual identity around it — complementary colors, not competing. Lovart's template system respects affiliate guidelines.

**Q4: What photography style works for CrossFit?**
Gritty and authentic. Gym lighting (not studio). Action shots (not posed). Sweat and effort (not glamour). Black-and-white with selective color on your brand accent is a signature look.

**Q5: Should I brand my programming (app, beyond the whiteboard)?**
Yes — if you use BTWB, SugarWOD, or TrainHeroic, match your box colors in their settings. Members interact with these platforms daily. Consistent branding across physical and digital touchpoints matters.

**Q6: How do I create branded merchandise that members actually wear?**
Design 3 tiers: daily training gear (shirts, shorts, socks), lifestyle wear (hoodies, hats, joggers), and limited drops (competition kits, event tees). Lovart generates apparel mockup templates for all three tiers.

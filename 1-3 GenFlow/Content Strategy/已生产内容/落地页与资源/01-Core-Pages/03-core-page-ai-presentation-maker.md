## What Lovart's AI Presentation Maker Actually Does

### ChatCanvas: Presentations by Conversation

You don't drag boxes onto a blank canvas. You talk to Lovart.

"I need a 12-slide pitch deck for a B2B SaaS product targeting HR teams. Clean, modern, with a blue-and-teal palette. Include a problem slide, solution slide, market size, traction metrics, team, and ask."

ChatCanvas interprets the brief and generates a full presentation structure. Each slide gets a purpose. Each slide gets a layout that matches that purpose. No starting from zero. No staring at a blank slide with a blinking cursor.

The AI understands presentation logic: title slides anchor, problem slides create tension, solution slides release it, data slides prove it, and CTA slides convert. You don't need to teach it storytelling. It already knows.

### Touch Edit: Precision Control Without the Learning Curve

AI generation gets you 90% there. But sometimes you need to tweak the last 10% — a different chart type, a repositioned logo, a swapped image.

Touch Edit is Lovart's direct-manipulation layer. Tap any element on any slide to adjust it. Drag to reposition. Pinch to resize. Swipe through layout alternatives for any slide. You get the speed of AI generation with the control of manual editing, except you're never digging through nested menus or hunting for the right panel.

### Brand Kit: Every Slide, Every Deck, Always On-Brand

You define your brand once. Upload your logo. Set your primary and accent colors. Pick your headline and body fonts. From that moment forward, every presentation Lovart generates — whether it's a sales deck, an investor pitch, or an internal report — uses those brand assets automatically.

No more copy-pasting the logo onto every slide. No more checking if the blue is #1a73e8 or #1a73e9. Brand Kit locks in consistency across every output.

### Smart Layout Engine

Lovart's layout engine understands slide types and their optimal structures:

- **Title Slides:** Full-bleed background, centered headline, subtle branding
- **Text + Image Slides:** Split layouts that balance copy and visuals
- **Data Slides:** Auto-formatted charts that pull data from your description
- **Comparison Slides:** Side-by-side layouts for before/after, pros/cons, competitor analysis
- **Timeline Slides:** Horizontal or vertical progressions for roadmaps and milestones
- **Team Slides:** Photo + bio layouts with consistent spacing and alignment
- **CTA Slides:** High-impact closing slides with clear next steps

Each layout type respects typographic hierarchy, color contrast, and negative space by default. You can override anything, but you rarely need to.

## Why an AI Design Agent Beats a Template Library

Templates solve one problem: you don't start from nothing. But they create three new problems:

1. **The Fit Problem:** No template perfectly matches your content. You end up forcing your message into a layout designed for someone else's message.
2. **The Same-As-Everyone Problem:** Popular templates get used by thousands of people. Your deck looks like everyone else's deck.
3. **The Maintenance Problem:** Templates don't evolve. When your brand updates or your content strategy shifts, you're back to hunting for new templates.

Lovart's AI approach sidesteps all three. Every deck is generated for your specific content. No template library can produce "a presentation about quantum computing for a non-technical audience with a warm, approachable visual style" — but Lovart can, because it understands the intent, not just the format.

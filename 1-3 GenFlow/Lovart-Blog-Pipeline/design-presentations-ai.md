The average professional spends 4 hours per week in presentations. Most of those presentations use the same 12 default templates. The result is a sea of visually identical slide decks that communicate nothing about the brand behind them.

Lovart generates custom presentation slides — pitch decks, webinars, reports, educational lectures — with visual identity built in. Brand Kit ensures every slide belongs to the same presentation.

---

## Step 1: Define Your Slide Master

*"Presentation slide master design. Clean, modern. Brand Kit colors. Header bar with logo top-left, slide title top-center. Content area with generous whitespace. Footer with page number and company name. 16:9 format."*

This master becomes the visual anchor for every subsequent slide. Generate it once, reference it for all slide variants.

---

## Step 2: Generate Slide Types

**Title slide:** *"Presentation title slide. Bold headline: 'Q3 Growth Strategy.' Subtitle: 'Market Expansion & Product Launch.' Date and presenter name. Dramatic but professional. Brand Kit gradient background. Large centered text."*

**Content slide:** *"Content slide: 'Market Opportunity.' Bullet points: 3 key markets identified, $4.2B TAM, 18% YoY growth. Clean layout. Icons for each bullet. A simple data visualization in the right column showing market growth curve."*

**Data slide:** *"Data slide: 'Revenue Projections 2026-2028.' Clean bar chart showing growth across 3 years. Annotated with key numbers. Minimal text — let the chart speak. Brand Kit colors for the chart elements."*

**Quote slide:** *"Pull quote slide. Large serif quote text centered: 'The best time to expand was last year. The second best time is now.' Attribution below. Dramatic negative space. Brand Kit accent color for the quote."*

**CTA slide:** *"Closing slide. Bold CTA: 'Let's Build.' Below: website, email, LinkedIn. Logo centered at bottom. Brand Kit. Clean, confident, minimal."*

---

## Step 3: Export and Assemble

Export each slide as a high-resolution PNG. Import into PowerPoint, Keynote, or Google Slides as full-slide background images. Add your actual content (text, data) in the presentation software on top of the AI-generated design.

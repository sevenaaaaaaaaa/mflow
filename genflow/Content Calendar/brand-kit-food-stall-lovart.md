## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Food stall branding must work at two scales: from across a food court (3-second attention) and on a delivery app thumbnail (1-inch square). Boldness wins. Nuance loses.

- **Primary**: `#D62828` — Fiery food red, the universal appetite-stimulant and high-visibility color
- **Secondary**: `#FFF0E6` — Warm eggshell cream for menus and packaging backgrounds
- **Accent**: `#F77F00` — Spicy tangerine for "Bestseller" badges and price highlights
- **Contrast**: `#1A1A1A` — Deep charcoal for typography on bright backgrounds; maximum readability
- **Signal**: `#FFD166` — Golden saffron for "Fresh Batch" indicators and time-sensitive promos

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart design for a very small physical stall space?**
Yes. Lovart's "micro-footprint" mode calculates your stall dimensions and suggests optimal signage placement. The AI won't generate a 6ft banner if your frontage is 4ft. Vertical stacking, hanging signs, and countertop displays are prioritized.

**Q2: How do I get on delivery apps looking professional?**
Lovart generates delivery-platform-optimized assets: profile photos (288×288px), cover images (1200×628px), and menu item photos with consistent styling. The same brand kit applies to Uber Eats, DoorDash, Grubhub, and Deliveroo simultaneously.

**Q3: Can Lovart create a menu that's easy to update daily?**
The dynamic chalkboard template generates a laminated base design with writable areas. Print the base once, then use the matching digital app to overlay daily specials. Customers scan a QR to see today's exact menu with real-time updates.

**Q4: What about packaging—takeout boxes, paper wraps, sauce cups?**
Lovart generates branding for standard packaging formats. Describe your packaging (e.g., "brown kraft box, 6×4×2 with clear lid"), and Lovart provides a sticker/label design with placement guides. Custom wrap-paper patterns also available.

**Q5: What's the fastest tier for a single-stall operation?**
The Free tier gives you the banner and price board. $19/month Creator adds loyalty card design, social templates, and delivery platform assets. Most stalls find $19 sufficient. Upgrade when you open a second location.

### Overview

AI-powered video generation tool for creating video content from text descriptions and keyframes

### How to Use

1. Click the Video Generator icon (video play icon) in the left toolbar
2. Enter your video description in the "What are we creating today" input field
3. Configure generation settings:
4. **Creation Mode**:
	- **Frames**: Add start and end frames (keyframes) to define video beginning and ending states. Click the "+" button to add frames that control video progression and transitions
	- **Motion Control**: Advanced motion path and camera control
		- **Add Video**: Use existing video clips as motion reference or base content
		- **Add Image**: Import static images to animate or use as keyframes
5. **Model**: Select from available AI video models

	- Sora 2 Pro (Premium)
	- Veo 3.1 (Premium)
	- Veo 3.1 Fast (Premium)
	- Kling O1 (Premium)

	- Seedance Pro 1.5
	- Kling 2.5 Turbo (Premium)
	- Wan 2.6

	- Vidu Q2
	- Veo 3 (Premium)
	- Veo 3 Fast (Premium)
	- Kling 2.1 Master (Premium)
	- Seedance Pro 1.0
	- Kling 2.0 Master (Premium)
	- Kling 2.1 (Premium)
	- Kling 1.6 (Premium)
	- Seedance Lite
	- Vidu Q1
	- Hailuo 02
	- Hailuo 2.3

6. **Output Settings**:
	- **Mode**: std / pro

7. Click generate button to create your video
8. Generated videos automatically appear on canvas with playback controls

### Multi-Frame Control Deep Dive

**Keyframe System Explained:**
Keyframes define the start and end states of your video. The AI generates the intermediate frames automatically.

**Creating Effective Keyframes:**
1. Start Frame: Define the initial scene composition
2. End Frame: Define the final scene state
3. AI analyzes transition requirements
4. Generates smooth interpolation between states

**Best Practices for Keyframes:**
- Use contrasting poses/positions for dynamic transitions
- Match lighting direction for realistic interpolation
- Keep subject consistent between frames
- Ensure sufficient visual difference for meaningful change

### Motion Control Features

**Adding Motion Reference Video:**
- Upload existing video to define motion style
- AI extracts motion patterns and applies them
- Perfect for: Consistent motion across scenes, character animation
- Format: MP4, MOV supported

**Adding Motion Reference Image:**
- Use a static image to define camera movement
- AI interprets implied motion (pan, zoom, tilt)
- Perfect for: Cinematic camera movements, dynamic reveals
- Works well with: Product showcases, landscape footage

### Output Settings Explained

**Mode Selection (std / pro):**
- **std (Standard)**: Faster processing, good quality
- **pro (Professional)**: Enhanced quality, longer processing time
- Choose pro for final deliverables, std for iterations

**Orientation Options:**
- **Image mode**: Vertical content (9:16) for Reels/Stories
- **Video mode**: Horizontal content (16:9) for YouTube/presentations

**Format and Duration:**
- Default 16:9 (1920×1080) for standard video
- 5 second clips are standard; AI generates complete motion
- Adjust prompts for optimal 5-second storytelling

### Crafting Effective Video Prompts

**Structure Your Prompt:**
1. Subject: What or who is in the video
2. Action: What they do (walk, dance, explore)
3. Environment: Where it happens
4. Mood: Lighting, atmosphere, style
5. Camera: Movement type (static, pan, zoom)

**Example Prompts:**
- Basic: "A coffee cup steaming on a rustic table, morning light"
- Advanced: "Close-up of coffee being poured into a white cup, golden hour lighting, slow motion splash, cinematic feel, gentle camera zoom"
- Complex: "A person walking through autumn park, colorful leaves floating, golden sunlight, warm color grading, tracking shot following from behind"

**Motion-Specific Keywords:**
- "Slow motion" / "Time-lapse" - Speed control
- "Cinematic" / "Documentary style" - Visual treatment
- "Dynamic" / "Energetic" - Pace indicators
- "Ethereal" / "Dreamlike" - Mood descriptors

### FAQ

**Q: Which model is best for beginners?**
A: Veo 3.1 Fast offers good balance of quality and ease of use. Start here and experiment.

**Q: Can I use custom keyframes from my own footage?**
A: Yes, upload any image as a keyframe reference. The AI uses it as a visual guide.

**Q: How do I get consistent characters across videos?**
A: Use the same reference image for keyframes and add to prompt: "Same character as reference image"

**Q: What's the maximum duration?**
A: Standard clips are 5 seconds. For longer content, generate multiple clips and assemble.

**Q: Can I regenerate specific sections?**
A: Yes, re-use keyframes with modified prompts for variation generation.

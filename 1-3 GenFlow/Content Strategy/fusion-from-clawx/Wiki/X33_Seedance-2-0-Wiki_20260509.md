Seedance 2.0 is LiblibAI's self-developed AI video model. Key features: multi-shot batch system (12-slot parallel generation), character consistency across all shots, native audio generation, up to 2K resolution. @ Directives allow simultaneous control of image style, video motion, and audio. Best for: brand storytelling, social media content, product demonstrations, advertisements, educational content.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: How minimal should my architecture brand be?**
Clean, yes. Sterile, no. The best architecture brands communicate philosophy through curated choices — a single accent color, a distinctive paper stock, a signature photography style. Every element should feel intentional but not sparse.

**Q2: What is the most important piece of branded collateral?**
The portfolio — print or digital. It's what wins projects. Every project sheet should follow an identical layout template. Consistency in presentation signals consistency in delivery. Lovart's portfolio templates enforce this discipline.

**Q3: How do I present unbuilt work in my portfolio?**
Renderings, models, and drawings are your product. Present them as beautifully as finished buildings. Include process sketches — they demonstrate how you think. Clients hire architects for thinking, not just drawing.

**Q4: What photography style works for architecture?**
Strong natural light, human scale reference (a person in the frame), and consistent angles (hero shot, detail, context). Avoid HDR over-processing. The photography should match the architecture — a baroque church and a glass box require different treatments.

**Q5: Should my firm be named after me or a concept?**
Sole practitioners: use your name. It builds personal reputation. Multi-partner firms: either names (SOM) or concept (BIG). If you plan to grow beyond yourself, a non-personal name offers more flexibility.

**Q6: How do I brand my construction documents?**
Title blocks are branding opportunities. Design one title block template used consistently across all drawing sets. Subconsultants notice. Contractors notice. A beautiful drawing set commands respect on the job site.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q1: Should my vintage shop brand feel old or modern?**
The sweet spot is modern-vintage — clean, contemporary layouts with vintage-inspired colors and typography. Your operational brand (website, booking, payment) should feel seamless and modern. The aesthetic layer carries the nostalgia.

**Q2: How do I brand my pricing and tags?**
Create a tag system that adds value. Include era, brand, material, and a brief styling suggestion on each tag. A beautifully designed tag justifies higher pricing and educates customers. Lovart generates tag templates with all fields.

**Q3: What photography style works for vintage clothing?**
Flat-lay on textured backgrounds (aged wood, linen, concrete). Natural light. Show garment details — labels, buttons, unique stitching. For modeled shots, style complete looks that inspire customers to buy multiple pieces.

**Q4: How do I communicate sizing across eras?**
Vintage sizing differs dramatically from modern. Create a visual size guide explaining the difference. Include actual measurements on every tag. Lovart's size guide template makes this information beautiful and digestible.

**Q5: Should I have a separate brand for my online shop vs. physical store?**
No — it should be the same. But your online brand needs more photography, more detailed descriptions, and more trust signals (reviews, return policy). Lovart's dual-format templates work for both environments.

**Q6: How do I make sustainability a brand pillar?**
Vintage is inherently sustainable. Make this explicit in your branding — a circular fashion badge, a carbon-saved counter on your website, packaging that avoids plastic. Lovart generates sustainability badge systems for vintage retailers.

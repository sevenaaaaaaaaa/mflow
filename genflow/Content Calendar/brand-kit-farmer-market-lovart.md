## Brand Color Palette

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

Farmers market branding should evoke the sensory experience of a Saturday morning market—sunlight through tent canopies, the smell of fresh basil, wooden crates of color. The palette is rooted in soil, sunshine, and seasonal abundance.

- **Primary**: `#7CB342` — Farm-fresh green, the color of market tent canopies, fresh produce, and growing things
- **Secondary**: `#FFFDE7` — Sun-warmed buttercream for maps, directories, and market-tote backgrounds
- **Accent**: `#558B2F` — Deep basil-green for typography, wayfinding headers, and organic certification badges
- **Sunshine**: `#FFB300` — Golden marigold for "Today's Market" callouts, special events, and "in season" indicators
- **Earth**: `#5D4037` — Rich soil-brown for body text and secondary information

## FAQ

**Q1: Can Lovart create a brand that represents 35 different vendors without looking generic?**
Yes. Lovart's "market umbrella" approach creates a unifying visual system that's vibrant enough to be recognizable but flexible enough to let vendor personalities shine. The market provides the frame; vendors provide the art. A consistent map, signage, and social media presence lift all vendors.

**Q2: How do I get vendors to actually use the market's branding?**
Lovart generates a "Vendor Branding Toolkit": tent-sign templates, price-tag sheets, social-media overlay stickers, and a "Proud Vendor of Willow Creek Market" badge graphic. Make it easy and free for vendors to adopt cohesive branding—they will, because it makes their stall look better.

**Q3: Can Lovart generate seasonal market branding (summer, fall harvest, winter holiday market)?**
Absolutely. Generate seasonal sub-brands with temporary accent colors while preserving the core `#7CB342` identity. Fall harvest: `#FFB300` and pumpkin-orange accents. Holiday market: festive reds and pine-greens. Spring: cherry-blossom pinks. The market brand evolves without losing recognition.

**Q4: Does Lovart create EBT/SNAP and food-access program signage?**
Yes. Lovart generates accessible, dignified EBT-acceptance signage, "Market Match" doubling-program posters, and SNAP-education materials in your market's brand style. Food-access equity is a core farmers-market value—brand it prominently and positively.

**Q5: What about market merch (totes, t-shirts, water bottles)?**
Lovart's merch module generates "Willow Creek Farmers Market" tote-bag designs, volunteer t-shirts, and vendor aprons. Merch is a revenue stream AND free advertising—every tote walking through town is a mobile billboard for Saturday.

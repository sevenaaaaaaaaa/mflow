## The Chat-Generate Facebook Ads Workflow

### Step 1 — Identify Your Creative Format

Facebook supports multiple ad formats. Your prompt must specify which one:

- **Single Image Ad**: 1080x1080 (square) or 1200x628 (landscape link). One hero visual, one message.
- **Carousel Ad**: 1080x1080 per card, 2-10 cards. Each card tells part of a story or showcases one product in a collection.
- **Collection Ad**: Cover image (1200x628 or 1080x1080) plus product tiles. The cover sets the brand tone; the tiles pull from your catalog.
- **Video Thumbnail**: The static frame that represents your video ad before it plays. 1080x1080 or 1200x628.

Start every prompt with the format: "Facebook single image ad, 1080x1080..."

### Step 2 — Write a Product-Hero Prompt with Social Proof

Effective Facebook ad creatives share a common DNA: product as hero, benefit as message, social proof as trust signal. Your prompt should embed all three.

```
Facebook single image ad, 1080x1080. A sleek white skincare bottle on a textured stone surface, 
surrounded by fresh aloe vera leaves and a single dew droplet on the bottle cap. 
Soft natural light from upper left, casting gentle shadows. 
A small gold badge in the top right corner: '4.9 ★ 12,000+ Reviews'. 
At the bottom, space for a white text overlay with a headline. 
Clean, premium, editorial beauty photography style. 
Lifestyle-adjacent — aspirational but believable.
```

Key components:
- Product as hero (white skincare bottle, prominently placed).
- Benefit implied through lifestyle context (fresh aloe, dew — freshness, purity).
- Social proof badge (star rating, review count).
- Layout accommodation for text overlay (headline space at bottom).
- Style guardrail ("aspirational but believable" — avoiding uncanny-valley AI perfection).

### Step 3 — Generate Format Variants

From your hero prompt, generate platform-specific variants:

**Landscape Link Ad (1200x628)**:
```
Same product scene, but reformatted to 1200x628 landscape. 
Product on the left third, text overlay space on the right two-thirds. 
Facebook link ad format — wider composition, less vertical density.
```

**Carousel Card Set (1080x1080 each)**:
```
Card 1: Product hero shot, same style. Card 2: Ingredient close-up — aloe texture, macro detail. 
Card 3: Before/after split — left side dull skin, right side radiant skin (no face, neck-down only). 
Card 4: Lifestyle scene — product on bathroom shelf, morning light, aspirational.
Card 5: Review quote card — 'I've tried everything. This is the one.' — in elegant typography, 
brand colors, with the product visible in the corner.
```

**Dark Mode Variant**:
```
Same product scene but on a dark charcoal background instead of stone. 
Darker mood, more dramatic lighting. For placements that appear in dark-mode feeds.
```

### Step 4 — Apply Facebook's Creative Guidelines

Before exporting, verify your creative against Facebook's ad policies:

- **Text in image**: Facebook no longer enforces the 20% text rule strictly, but ads with less than 20% text coverage still perform better. If your image is text-heavy, it will reach fewer people.
- **Before/After claims**: Avoid "miraculous" transformation imagery. Keep comparisons grounded and believable.
- **No misleading CTAs**: A "Shop Now" button on the image should not imply functionality the platform's native button already provides.
- **Safe zone**: Facebook crops ads differently across placements (Feed, Stories, Marketplace, Audience Network). Keep critical elements within the center 80% of the canvas.

Prompt for policy-safe output: "No misleading before/after claims, minimal text on image, keep the center zone clear of edge-critical elements."

### Step 5 — Build a Creative Testing Matrix

The real power of AI-generated ad creatives is not speed — it is testing volume. With manual design, testing five creative angles means five commissions or five hours. With an AI agent, it means five prompts.

Generate a testing matrix for every campaign:

| Angle | Visual Type | Hook | Test Variable |
|-------|-------------|------|---------------|
| Product Hero | Studio shot, clean background | "The serum 12,000 people switched to" | Image clarity vs lifestyle |
| Lifestyle Context | Product in-use, real setting | "Your morning routine, upgraded" | Authenticity vs polish |
| Social Proof | Review quote card + product | "4.9 stars. 12,000 reviews." | Text-heavy vs image-heavy |
| Problem/Solution | Split composition — problem left, solution right | "Before Lovart. After Lovart." | Emotional contrast |
| Founder Story | Founder photo or avatar + product | "I made this for my own skin" | Personal vs corporate |

Run all five in the same ad set. Let Facebook's algorithm optimize delivery to the winning creative. Kill underperformers. Use the winner as the base for your next round of variants.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the correct Facebook ad image sizes?**

Feed ads: 1080x1080 (square) or 1200x628 (landscape). Stories ads: 1080x1920 (vertical). Carousel: 1080x1080 per card. Collection cover: 1200x628 or 1080x1080. All should be JPG or PNG, under 30MB.

**Can the agent generate ads for Facebook's Advantage+ placements?**

Yes. Specify "Facebook Advantage+ creative, multiple aspect ratios needed" and the agent will generate versions optimized for Feed, Stories, and Reels placements from the same base scene.

**How many ad variants should I generate per campaign?**

Minimum three — one product-hero, one lifestyle, one social-proof. For higher-budget campaigns ($500+/day), generate five to seven variants to give the algorithm more signals to optimize against.

**Will Facebook penalize AI-generated ad creatives?**

No. Facebook does not distinguish between AI-generated and human-designed creatives in its ad review or delivery system. The algorithm evaluates creative performance based on engagement signals, not origin.

**How do I avoid the "stock photo" look in my ad creatives?**

Avoid prompts that are too generic ("business person smiling, white background"). Use specific scene descriptors, brand-specific props, and texture cues ("film grain," "natural light," "slightly underexposed"). The more grounded your scene description, the less stock-photo the output.

**Can I generate video ads with the agent?**

The Lovart agent generates static images and can produce video content from image sequences and scene descriptions. For video ads, describe the scene progression frame by frame and specify the video ad format.

**How do I maintain brand consistency across multiple ad campaigns?**

Save your brand kit in Lovart with your logo, color palette, font pairings, and approved image style references. Every generated ad will pull from the same visual rulebook, even across different campaigns and formats.

## Executive Summary

Lovart's English SEO gap is not just a keyword gap. It is a page-shape gap.

中文：Lovart 的英文 SEO 缺口不只是关键词缺口，更是页面形态缺口。

The 2026-05 SEO report shows Google brand click dependence at 97.4% and Bing brand click dependence at 99.9%. Core competitor coverage is 63.9% on Google and 5.6% on Bing, with missing core terms such as `ai logo generator`, `ai ad generator`, `ai design generator`, `ai commercial`, `brand video`, and `character consistency`.

中文：2026-05 SEO 月报显示，Lovart 在 Google 的品牌点击占比为 97.4%，Bing 为 99.9%；核心竞品词覆盖率 Google 为 63.9%，Bing 为 5.6%。缺失的核心词包括 `ai logo generator`、`ai ad generator`、`ai design generator`、`ai commercial`、`brand video`、`character consistency` 等。

High-ranking English pages do three things better than generic AI pages:

中文：英文高排名页面比普通 AI 页面强在三件事：

1. They map one search intent to one page promise. Looka owns logo generation by making the first-screen promise about business name, style inputs, 100s of options, file formats, and brand kit. Creatify owns marketing video by starting with product URL, avatars, scripts, voice, A/B tests, and real results.
2. They convert abstraction into workflow. Strong pages do not say "AI-powered creative platform" and stop. They show an input path, a production path, an editing path, and an export/use path.
3. They use proof in the body, not only in logos. Proof appears as tested criteria, benchmark frameworks, G2 ratings, output formats, commercial rights, pricing, limitations, use-case fit, and "who should choose what" decision rules.

中文：

1. 它们把一个搜索意图对应到一个清晰页面承诺。Looka 用 business name、style input、100s options、file formats、brand kit 承接 logo 需求；Creatify 用 product URL、avatar、script、voice、A/B test 和真实结果承接 marketing video 需求。
2. 它们把抽象能力写成具体工作流。强页面不会只说 “AI-powered creative platform”，而是展示输入路径、生成路径、编辑路径和导出/使用路径。
3. 它们把证据放在正文里，而不只是放 logo wall。证据可以是测试标准、benchmark、G2 评分、输出格式、商用权、价格、限制、适用场景和“谁该选什么”的决策规则。

Lovart should not copy the exact language of these pages. Many pages still contain AI-sounding claims. The reusable advantage is structural: precise intent, concrete workflow, visible trade-offs, FAQ coverage, and conversion moments placed where users actually hesitate.

中文：Lovart 不应该照抄这些页面的具体措辞。很多高排名页面仍然有 AI 味或营销空话。真正值得复用的是结构：精准意图、具体工作流、可见取舍、FAQ 覆盖，以及在用户犹豫点放置转化信息。

### Priority Logic From The Keyword Folder

- High-competition, broad terms such as `AI Image Generator`, `AI Video Generator`, `Background Remover`, `Graphic Design`, and `Logo Design` should not be Lovart's first standalone attack unless the page has a specific wedge. They are useful as hub context and comparison targets.
- Medium/opportunity terms such as `AI Design Generator`, `Marketing Video AI`, `AI Commercial`, `AI Ad Generator`, `Brand Video`, `Product Video`, `Character Consistency`, and `Motion Control` are better suited to Lovart's current differentiation.
- Model terms such as `Sora`, `Kling`, `Veo 3`, `FLUX`, `Seedance`, `Seedream`, `Midjourney`, and `DALL-E` should be used for comparison, model-routing explainers, and "which model for which job" content, not as shallow traffic pages.
- Audience terms should be used inside page sections and title variants. A page for marketers should not read like a page for designers.

中文：

- `AI Image Generator`、`AI Video Generator`、`Background Remover`、`Graphic Design`、`Logo Design` 这类高竞争泛词，不适合 Lovart 一开始就用薄页硬打。它们更适合作为 hub 背景、对比对象或内链支持。
- `AI Design Generator`、`Marketing Video AI`、`AI Commercial`、`AI Ad Generator`、`Brand Video`、`Product Video`、`Character Consistency`、`Motion Control` 更贴近 Lovart 当前差异化。
- `Sora`、`Kling`、`Veo 3`、`FLUX`、`Seedance`、`Seedream`、`Midjourney`、`DALL-E` 这类模型词，应服务于模型对比、模型路由解释和“哪个模型适合哪个任务”的内容，不适合做浅层蹭流量页。
- 受众词应进入页面 section 和标题变体。写给 marketer 的页面，不应该读起来像写给 designer。

## Best Page Breakdowns

中文：以下页面拆解关注“为什么体验/排名可能更好、Lovart 可学什么、不能照抄什么”。英文保留给全球团队复用，中文补充策略判断。

### 1. Looka AI Logo Generator

**Source URL:** https://looka.com/ai-logo-generator/

- Why it works: the page does not sell "AI creativity" in the abstract. It sells a founder workflow: enter business name, choose styles/colors/symbols, review 100s of logos, customize, download files.
- Strong copy move: "3 simple steps" turns a complex branding decision into a safe path.
- Conversion design: free-to-start lowers entry risk; file formats and ownership FAQ handle the real purchase objections.
- EEAT/trust: mentions award-winning designers, live support, copyright ownership, commercial use, and exact deliverables.
- Lovart can learn: a logo page should not lead with "MCoT" first. It should lead with what a founder gets: logo options, brand kit, editable variations, files for web/social/print.
- Do not copy: Looka is logo-centric. Lovart should position logo as the first asset in a broader brand campaign, not as an isolated download.

中文要点：Looka 强在把 logo 生成写成创始人能理解的安全流程：输入公司名、选择风格、看大量方案、编辑、下载文件。Lovart 不应把 logo 页写成技术介绍页，而应把 logo 放进更大的品牌系统和 campaign 资产里。

### 2. Canva Magic Design

**Source URL:** https://www.canva.com/magic-design/

- Why it works: it connects AI generation to a familiar editing and asset ecosystem.
- Strong copy move: "Go from idea to design with ease" is generic, but the page improves by showing prompts, media upload, Brand Kit, video, presentations, collaboration, sharing, storage, and safety.
- Conversion design: multiple entry points for personal, teachers, and work use cases.
- EEAT/trust: safeguards, terms, creator attribution, commercial usage, device/browser support.
- Lovart can learn: broad tools pages need audience segmentation. One "AI design generator" page should contain user paths, not only features.
- Do not copy: Canva's broad template library is its moat. Lovart's moat is brief-driven reasoning, editable canvas, multi-format campaign continuity.

中文要点：Canva 的优势不是一句 “AI design generator”，而是模板、编辑器、Brand Kit、协作、导出和安全机制组成的生态。Lovart 不能复制模板库路线，应强调 brief-driven reasoning、可编辑画布和多格式 campaign 连续性。

### 3. Figma AI Design Generator

**Source URL:** https://www.figma.com/solutions/ai-design-generator/

- Why it works: it speaks to product teams and designers who care about iteration, prototypes, and staying inside the design/build workflow.
- Strong copy move: "Start with prompts, then refine in the visual editor, adjust the code for more control, or prompt again" makes AI a workflow component, not a magic box.
- Conversion design: product examples and blog links reinforce credible adoption by designers.
- EEAT/trust: "built by real designers" and real Figma Make examples.
- Lovart can learn: for professional audiences, "control after generation" matters more than "instant creation".
- Do not copy: Figma's page is sparse and can rely on brand authority. Lovart needs more proof and clearer before/after use cases.

中文要点：Figma 面向专业设计/产品团队时，重点不是“生成很快”，而是生成后还能迭代、控制、进入设计/开发工作流。Lovart 面向专业人群时，也要把“生成后如何编辑和协作”说清楚。

### 4. Venngage Best AI Ad Generators

**Source URL:** https://venngage.com/blog/best-ai-ad-generator/

- Why it works: it begins with the campaign pain: moving from rough idea to live creative is manually heavy, especially when testing multiple campaigns.
- Strong copy move: the testing criteria are explicit: quality, formats, integrations, tracking, prompting assistance, brand consistency, pricing, scalability.
- Conversion design: the page uses comparison tables and "best for" labels before detailed reviews, helping scanners decide quickly.
- EEAT/trust: claims to have tested tools, explains criteria, lists limitations.

Use for: `ai logo generator`, `ai commercial generator`, `brand video maker`, `image to video ai generator`.

## Cross-Page Anti-Slop Lessons

中文：这一节把 SERP 调研反哺到 Anti-slop 规范，说明好页面和 AI 味页面的差别。

### What Good Pages Do

- They name the buyer: founder, marketer, agency, ecommerce team, product designer.
- They name the input: business name, product URL, photo, script, prompt, brand kit, reference image.
- They name the output: SVG/PNG, 9:16 video, 16:9 cut, social post, business card, campaign kit, editable prototype.
- They name the constraint: no design skills, no shoot, no production team, no signup, no watermark, commercial use.
- They handle objections in FAQ: ownership, editing, file formats, pricing, commercial license, output quality, platform fit.
- They show limitations or selection criteria when writing comparisons.

中文：好页面会明确买家、输入、输出和限制，并在 FAQ 中处理真实疑虑，例如版权、编辑、格式、价格、商用、质量和平台适配。写 comparison 时还会说明限制和选择标准。

### What Still Feels Like AI Slop

- "Stunning", "effortless", "unleash", "revolutionize" without concrete workflow.
- Model-name dumping without explaining why the user benefits.
- Repeating the same title phrase in many modules.
- CTA copy that ignores user hesitation.
- Generic persona lines such as "for creators and businesses" without scenario detail.

中文：AI 味通常来自抽象形容词、模型名堆叠、重复标题、无视用户顾虑的 CTA，以及“for creators and businesses”这种没有场景细节的泛人群写法。

## Appendix: Template Snippets

中文：以下是可直接复用的英文结构公式，保留英文便于全球团队写页面时直接套用。

### Tool Landing Hero Formula

`Turn [input] into [specific output] for [audience/use case] in [time/effort frame], then [edit/export/test] it for [channel/business goal].`

Example for Lovart logo:

`Turn a business idea into an editable logo and brand launch kit. Generate directions from your brief, refine the mark on ChatCanvas, and export web, social, and print-ready files from the same project.`

### Comparison Verdict Formula

`Choose [competitor] if [their legitimate strength]. Choose Lovart if [workflow where Lovart is structurally stronger]. Use both when [hybrid workflow].`

Example:

`Choose Canva when a template library and quick manual editing are enough. Choose Lovart when the brief needs to become a connected campaign system: logo, mockups, ads, and video directions that stay editable in one canvas.`

### Best-of Review Criteria

For AI design/ad/video lists, use these criteria:

- Output quality.
- Editing control after generation.
- Brand consistency.
- Format coverage.
- Workflow fit.
- Commercial rights.
- Cost and free plan limits.
- Team or agency scalability.
- Real limitations.

### FAQ Bank for Lovart Missing Core Pages

- Is the output commercially usable?
- Can I edit the design after generation?
- What files can I export?
- Can I keep a brand style consistent across multiple assets?
- Can I create platform-specific ad/video sizes?
- Do I need design or video editing skills?
- How is this different from Canva, Looka, or an AI image generator?
- Can I generate multiple variants for testing?
- Can I use my own product photo, logo, or brand kit?
- What happens if I need a logo plus ads, mockups, and videos?

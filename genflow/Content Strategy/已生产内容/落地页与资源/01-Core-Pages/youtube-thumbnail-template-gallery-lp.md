## Template Categories by Niche

### Gaming Thumbnails

High-energy compositions with dramatic lighting, character close-ups, bold sans-serif typography with stroke outlines, and color grading that pops on dark-mode YouTube. Templates for Let's Plays, walkthroughs, challenge videos, tier lists, and reaction content. Each template leaves clear zones for face-cam reactions — the single most important element in gaming CTR.

### Tech and Tutorial Thumbnails

Clean, high-contrast designs that signal competence and clarity. Product close-ups on gradient backgrounds, step-number overlays for tutorials, before-and-after splits for comparison videos, "X vs Y" face-off layouts. Typography is clean, modern, and legible at mobile thumbnail size — typically 150px wide.

### Beauty and Lifestyle Thumbnails

Soft lighting, warm color palettes, elegant typography, and generous face-forward compositions. Templates for makeup tutorials, skincare routines, hauls, try-ons, morning routines, and room tours. The visual language conveys aspiration and relatability simultaneously — the sweet spot for lifestyle CTR.

### Finance and Business Thumbnails

Authoritative, minimal designs with structured layouts. Data visualization elements (charts, numbers, arrows), professional headshots, confident color schemes (navy, charcoal, emerald, gold). Templates for market analysis, investing guides, business case studies, and personal finance tips. These thumbnails signal credibility before the viewer reads the title.

### Education and Explainer Thumbnails

High-clarity designs that promise learning. Question marks, lightbulb moments, diagram overlays, step-by-step visual cues. Vibrant but not chaotic color palettes. Templates for how-to videos, explainers, study guides, language lessons, and academic content.

### Vlog and Entertainment Thumbnails

Expressive, personality-driven designs with bold emotional cues — surprise, laughter, curiosity. Templates optimized for face-driven CTR with space for expressive reaction shots, location B-roll, and episode numbering for series content.

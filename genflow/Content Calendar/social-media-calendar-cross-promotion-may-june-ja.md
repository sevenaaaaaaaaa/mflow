## Calendar Conventions

[IMAGE 2 PLACEHOLDER — Conceptual Diagram]

- **BLOG:** Long-form blog content publish date. Social promotion begins same day and continues for 72 hours.
- **PROD:** Product feature launch or update. Coordinated with Engineering and Product Marketing.
- **COMM:** Community content (Spotlight features, UGC reposts, #BuiltWithLovart roundups).
- **TREND:** Reactive content — jumping on AI design conversations, news, or cultural moments. Not pre-scheduled; team monitors and activates.
- **PROMO:** Promotional content (feature highlight, case study, pricing announcement).
- **EDU:** Educational content (tips, tutorials, prompt guides, workflow examples).

**Posting cadence targets:**
- X/Twitter: 2–3 posts/day (mix of original + replies/engagement)
- LinkedIn: 1 post/day + 1 article/week
- Instagram: 1 post/day (static or carousel) + 3–5 Stories/day
- TikTok: 1 video/day
- YouTube Shorts: 3 videos/week (repurpose TikTok content)

## Cross-Promotion Rules

### Blog → Social Workflow
1. Blog publishes at 9:00 AM ET Monday–Thursday (optimal for US + EU overlap).
2. Primary social post within 60 minutes of publish.
3. Secondary angle post within 24 hours (different hook, same link).
4. Visual derivative (carousel, infographic, short video) within 48 hours.
5. Community discussion prompt within 72 hours (poll, question, "hot take").

### Product Launch Coordination
1. Product Marketing confirms launch date ≥7 days in advance.
2. Content team prepares: announcement post, feature demo video, tutorial carousel, FAQ Stories.
3. Social team schedules coordinated cross-channel blast at launch hour.
4. 24-hour monitoring for community response; amplify positive feedback.
5. 48-hour follow-up: educational content showing feature in practice.

### Community Content Pipeline
1. #BuiltWithLovart monitored daily; best submissions saved to shared folder.
2. Tuesday #BuiltWithLovart roundup is standing weekly feature.
3. Monthly "best of" compilation published first weekend of following month.
4. Deep-dive Spotlight features (one per Digest) require user permission and interview.

## Channel-Specific Notes

**X/Twitter:**
- Thread format outperforms single posts 3:1 for educational content.
- Best posting windows: 10 AM–12 PM ET, 6–8 PM ET.
- Reply to relevant conversations in AI/design/startup space to build presence beyond broadcast.

**LinkedIn:**
- Articles (long-form) drive 5–8× more profile views than posts.
- Carousel PDFs are the highest-engagement post format (40%+ engagement rate vs 15% for static images).
- Tag relevant people/companies sparingly — only when genuinely referenced.

**Instagram:**
- Carousels with 6–10 slides consistently outperform single images.
- Stories should include one interactive element per day (poll, question, slider, quiz).
- Reels are the growth format; prioritize over static posts for reach.

**TikTok:**
- Screen recordings + voiceover is our best-performing format.
- Hook must appear in first 1.5 seconds.
- Use trending audio where natural; don't force it.
- Caption overlay on all videos (accessibility + sound-off viewing).

**YouTube Shorts:**
- Repurpose TikTok content; minimal additional production needed.
- Titles should include target keyword.
- Post 3×/week to maintain algorithmic momentum.

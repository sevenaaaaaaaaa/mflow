## Why an AI Poster Generator?

Posters are the original visual marketing format. They work in the physical world — store windows, bulletin boards, conference halls, street posters — and they work digitally — social media shares, email attachments, website hero images. A well-designed poster communicates authority, effort, and professionalism before anyone reads a single word.

The problem is that poster design has historically required one of three things: professional graphic design skills, expensive software licenses, or tolerance for template-based tools that make every poster look like every other poster. Lovart's AI Poster Generator eliminates all three barriers. You do not need design skills. You do not need software. Lovart generates high-contrast promotional posters with clear visual hierarchy — offer first, product second, urgency elements third.

## How the AI Poster Generator Works

### Step 1: Describe Your Poster

Open Lovart and type `@poster` in ChatCanvas. Describe what you need in natural language. The more detail you provide, the more precisely Lovart can render your vision:

- What is the poster for? (event, promotion, educational, etc.)
- What is the main message or headline?
- What supporting information should appear? (date, location, price, website)
- What visual style or mood do you want? (minimalist, bold, vintage, futuristic, elegant)
- Any specific colors, imagery, or references?

Lovart's MCoT engine parses your description, maps it to the optimal generation model from its 9+ image models, and produces a composition plan before generating the first pixel.

### Step 2: Select Your Size

Lovart supports all standard poster dimensions:

| Format | Dimensions | Best For |
|---|---|---|
| A4 | 210 × 297 mm | Office posters, handouts, small displays |
| A3 | 297 × 420 mm | Retail windows, event signage, medium displays |
| 18×24 | 18 × 24 inches | Standard poster frame size (US) |
| 24×36 | 24 × 36 inches | Large format, maximum visual impact |
| Custom | Any dimensions | Trade show booths, custom wall spaces |

All sizes are generated at 300 DPI for print-ready quality. Digital-only posters can be exported at 72 DPI for smaller file sizes.

### Step 3: Generate and Refine

Lovart generates 2-4 poster variations in approximately 30-60 seconds. Review them, pick your favorite, and refine:

- **Touch Edit:** Adjust individual elements. Move the headline, resize the imagery, shift the color palette — all with direct manipulation, no layer panel required.
- **Text Edit:** Click any text element to edit the copy directly. Lovart reflows the layout to accommodate changes. Change the date, update the speaker lineup, swap the venue — the composition adapts.
- **@poster Regenerate:** Not satisfied? Provide feedback — "make the title larger," "switch to a dark background," "use a serif font" — and Lovart regenerates based on your guidance.

### Step 4: Export and Use

Export options:
- **PDF (print-ready):** CMYK color profile, 300 DPI, bleed marks and crop marks for professional printing
- **PNG/JPEG (digital):** RGB, optimized for web and social media
- **Social media crops:** Lovart auto-crops your poster into Instagram (1:1), Facebook Event (16:9), and LinkedIn (1.91:1) formats

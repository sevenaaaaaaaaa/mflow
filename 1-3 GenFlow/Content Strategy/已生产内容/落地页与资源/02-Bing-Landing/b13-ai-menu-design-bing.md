## How Does AI Menu Design Apply Menu Psychology?

Professional menu design incorporates principles of menu psychology — the study of how menu layout and presentation influence customer choices. Lovart's AI Menu Design tool applies these principles automatically:

**The Golden Triangle:** Eye-tracking studies reveal that customers scan menus in a predictable pattern — first the center, then top right, then top left. The AI positions high-margin items within this visual path to maximize their visibility and selection rate.

**Strategic Item Placement:** The first and last items in each menu section receive disproportionate attention. The AI places target items (high-profit or signature dishes) in these prime positions while distributing standard items through the middle of sections.

**Price Anchoring:** Placing a premium-priced item at the top of a section makes subsequently listed items appear more reasonably priced by comparison. The AI structures sections with appropriate anchor items to establish favorable price perception.

**Decoy Pricing:** Including a mid-range option priced close to the premium option (but slightly lower) makes the mid-range item appear as a better value, steering customers toward profitable middle-tier selections.

**Visual Emphasis Techniques:** The AI uses subtle visual cues — boxes, color highlights, icons, or chef recommendation markers — to draw attention to specific items without overtly pushing them.

**Descriptive Language Optimization:** AI-generated or AI-enhanced item descriptions use sensory language ("slow-roasted," "handcrafted," "artisan") that increases perceived value and ordering likelihood.

**Price Presentation:** The AI removes currency symbols and minimizes price visual weight (smaller font, less contrast) to reduce price sensitivity while maintaining required information.

**Takeout and Delivery Menus:** Compact designs formatted for print (trifold, bifold) or digital viewing on mobile devices. Clear item categorization, modifier selection clarity, and optimized for off-premise ordering behavior.

**Catering and Event Menus:** Structured designs presenting package options, per-person pricing, and service details with professional presentation standards appropriate for corporate and special events.

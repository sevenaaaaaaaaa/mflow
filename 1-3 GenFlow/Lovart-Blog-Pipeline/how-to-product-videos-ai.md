Product video converts. Studies consistently show that e-commerce listings with video see 40-80% higher conversion rates than image-only listings. But traditional product video is expensive — studio rental, lighting rigs, camera operators, editing hours. A single 15-second product clip can cost $500-2,000.

Lovart's AI video tools — **Seedance 2.0** and **Veo 3** — eliminate the camera. Describe your product and the shot you want, and the **Design Agent** generates professional video in seconds. This guide covers five essential product video types you can create today.

---

## 1. The Turntable Spin

The most requested product video format: a 360-degree rotation of your product on a clean background. Customers want to see the product from every angle before buying.

**How to generate:**
1. Generate a photorealistic product image first. Use Nano Banana Pro for best material rendering.
2. Use **Edit Elements** to isolate the product on a transparent background. Place it on the ChatCanvas.
3. Prompt: *"Veo 3: 360-degree turntable rotation of this product. Slow, smooth spin. Studio lighting from above at 45 degrees. Clean white infinity background. 10 seconds. Keep the product at center frame throughout."*

**Refining:** If the rotation speed is wrong, say *"Slow the rotation by 40% — it should feel contemplative, not rushed."* If the lighting creates unwanted reflections, use Touch Edit on a paused frame to adjust.

## 2. The Lifestyle Scene

Place your product in a contextual environment — the coffee machine on a kitchen counter, the running shoes on a trail, the candle on a bedside table. This is the most effective format for brand storytelling.

**How to generate:**
1. Keep the isolated product on your canvas (from Step 1 above).
2. Prompt: *"Seedance 2.0: lifestyle product video. The coffee machine from the canvas, on a sunlit kitchen counter. Morning light streaming through a window. A hand enters the frame and presses the brew button. Steam rises. Warm, aspirational atmosphere. 8 seconds. Audio: gentle ambient kitchen sounds."*

**Pro tip:** Seedance 2.0's audio-visual sync adds ambient sound — footsteps, steam, button clicks — making the video feel professionally produced without external audio editing.

---

## 3. The Macro Detail

Extreme close-ups on product details — watch movements, fabric textures, ingredient textures. For luxury and high-consideration products, these communicate quality that static images cannot.

**How to generate:**
1. Prompt: *"Veo 3: macro close-up video of a wristwatch movement. Slow orbit around the dial, extreme shallow depth of field. Focus shifts from the hour markers to the date window to the second hand. Metallic surfaces catch light. 6 seconds."*

2. Refine: *"The focus shift at 3 seconds is too abrupt — make the rack focus smoother, more gradual."*

---

## 4. The Demo Video

Show the product in use — how it works, how it feels, what it does. Essential for products where function is the primary selling point.

**How to generate:**
1. Prompt: *"Seedance 2.0: product demonstration. A person unboxing a pair of wireless earbuds. Clean, well-lit desk surface. Hands open the case, remove an earbud, place it in their ear. Smooth, unhurried pacing. The product is the focal point throughout. 12 seconds."*

2. Refine: *"The unboxing feels too fast — slow down the case opening. Add a brief pause when the earbud is revealed before the hand picks it up."*

---

## 5. The Multi-Angle Showcase

Combine wide shots, close-ups, and detail shots into a single product video. This is the format to use when you need one video that tells the complete product story.

**How to generate:**
Generate each segment individually on the same ChatCanvas (for visual consistency), then combine:

1. *"Wide establishing shot of product on a pedestal. Dramatic key light."*
2. *"Medium shot orbiting the product. Studio lighting."*
3. *"Close-up detail of the product's texture/finish."*
4. *"Final wide shot: product with brand logo fade-in."*

Export each as a clip. Assemble in your video editor of choice, or prompt Lovart to generate transitions between clips.

## FAQ

**Q: What resolution do Lovart product videos output at?**
A: 2K native, upscalable to 4K on paid plans. Sufficient for e-commerce listings, social media, and digital advertising. For broadcast television, consult a professional post-production service for upscaling.

**Q: Can I generate video from an existing product photo?**
A: Yes. Upload your photo to the ChatCanvas. Use Edit Elements to isolate the product. Then prompt for video using the isolated product as the canvas reference.

**Q: How long can product videos be?**
A: Seedance 2.0: up to 15 seconds. Veo 3: up to 60 seconds. Most e-commerce platforms recommend 15-30 second product videos.

The color system revolves around a deep navy primary (`#0B1120`) with an electric teal accent (`#14B8A6`) that pulses through the brand like a digital heartbeat.

**Logo Suite Includes:** Full horizontal lockup, vertical lockup, standalone hex mark, hex mark with wordmark overlay, monochrome white-out version

### Template 2: "Solène" — Luxury & Lifestyle Brand

**Style:** Elegant, minimal, aspirational
**Best for:** Fashion labels, beauty brands, premium services

Solène features a refined wordmark set in Cormorant Garamond Bold with custom letter-spacing of +80 for an ultra-luxurious, airy feel. The secondary mark — a minimalist crescent — serves as a subtle brand signature. The color palette is dominated by warm creams and a single dramatic accent: Deep Rose (`#8B2252`).

**Logo Suite Includes:** Primary wordmark, crescent icon mark, monogram version (interlocking 'S' characters), foil-stamp-ready gold version

**Brand Voice Keywords:** Gritty, honest, skilled, enduring

**Guidelines Highlights:** Texture library (leather grain, brushed metal, raw wood), photography style (workshop environments, natural light, hands-in-frame), typography for product specs

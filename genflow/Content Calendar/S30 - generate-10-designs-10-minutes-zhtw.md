## Why Speed Matters

Some people hear "10 designs in 10 minutes" and think: "Okay, but will they be any good?"

Here's the reframe: speed isn't about rushing. Speed is about **iteration volume.** When creating a design takes you 30 minutes, you make one version and hope it's right. When it takes 60 seconds, you make five versions and pick the best one. Quality emerges from quantity of attempts, not slowness of execution.

Speed also kills the procrastination tax. How many marketing campaigns, product launches, or content ideas have died on the vine because "we don't have time to create the visuals"? When visual creation drops from hours to minutes, the bottleneck disappears. You publish more. You test more. You learn more. Speed compounds.

## Remove background

Instantly create transparent backgrounds with Lovart AI. Isolate subjects for product mockups and composites in one click directly on the Infinite Canvas.

### What Remove BG Does

The Remove BG feature automatically detects and removes the background from your image or layer, leaving only the main subject (person, product, object, etc.) with a transparent background. This is useful for creating clean product images, isolating subjects for compositing, or preparing graphics for overlay use.

### Step-by-Step Usage Guide

1. **Select Your Image**
	- Open an existing project with an image or upload a new image to the canvas
	- Click on the image layer to select it
2. **Access the Remove BG Feature**
	- In the top toolbar, click the **"Remove bg"** button.
3. **Wait for Processing**
	- Lovart will analyze the image using AI to detect the subject and background
	- Processing time depends on image size and complexity (typically 2-10 seconds)
	- Avoid editing other elements during processing to ensure optimal results
4. **Review the Result**
	- Once complete, the background will be removed and replaced with transparency
	- Inspect edges around your subject—they should be clean and sharp
	- If the removal includes unwanted parts of the subject, you can refine using other tools
5. **Refine If Needed** *(Optional)*
	- Use the **"Edit Elements"** or **"Remover"** tools if you need to manually fix any areas
	- Manually erase or restore parts that were incorrectly removed or kept
	- Use feathering or edge smoothing options if available for a softer transition
6. **Export or Continue**
	- With a transparent background, you can now:
		- Export as PNG (preserves transparency)
		- Place the subject over new backgrounds
		- Layer it with other design elements
		- Apply effects or filters to the isolated subject

### Refinement Techniques

**When Basic Removal Needs Work:**

1. **Using Remover Tool:**
   - Click "Remover" in toolbar
   - Paint over unwanted background areas
   - AI fills with appropriate content
   - Great for touching up edges

2. **Using Edit Elements:**
   - Apply "Edit Elements" to split layers
   - Manually adjust subject/background separation
   - Fine-tune edge boundaries
   - Reassemble as needed

3. **Using AI Remover:**
   - Erase small artifacts at edges
   - Clean up spillover areas
   - Perfect edge transitions

### Best Practices

**For Cleanest Results:**
- Use images with clear subject separation
- Good lighting produces better isolation
- Higher resolution = cleaner edges
- Avoid images with very similar foreground/background colors

**Common Issues and Fixes:**
- Color spill: Use Remover to clean up
- Hair edges: Apply subtle feathering
- Shadows: Remove before extraction or add back manually
- Transparency gaps: Fill with transparent pixels as needed

### Export Options

**PNG Format:**
- Preserves transparency
- Highest quality for graphics
- Larger file size
- Ideal for: Overlays, compositions, templates

**JPG Format:**
- White background instead of transparent
- Smaller file size
- Universal compatibility
- Ideal for: Direct upload, social media

**SVG Format (if available):**
- Vector-based transparency
- Infinite scaling
- Best for: Logos, icons, simple graphics

### FAQ

**Q: What happens to the background?**
A: It's replaced with transparency, allowing you to place the subject over any background.

**Q: Can I remove complex backgrounds like trees or patterns?**
A: Yes, AI handles various backgrounds including natural textures and patterns.

**Q: What about hair or fine details?**
A: AI attempts to preserve fine details. For best results, use high-resolution images with clear subject separation.

**Q: Can I undo background removal?**
A: Yes, Cmd/Ctrl+Z undoes the action. You can reload the original image at any time.

**Q: Does this work on all image types?**
A: Works best with clear subject/background separation. Results may vary with complex scenes or low-quality images.
